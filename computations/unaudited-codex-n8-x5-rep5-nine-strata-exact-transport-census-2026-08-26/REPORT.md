# Exact transport census for the nine corrected rep5 strata

The exact transport partition has nine singleton classes. A solve of the selected open84 `k=2,t=1` input transports to that input only; it does not close any of the other five open84 or any of the three complement86 strata. Consequently nine exact representatives are required: six open84 and three complement86.

This conclusion is stronger than a support-graph census. All 40,320 site permutations were checked against the source-labelled support, fixed identity blocks, and eliminated edge; only the identity survives. For that site map, all six simultaneous color permutations were tested on every same-shape ordered source pair. An arbitrary ring-variable bijection would have to preserve the multiset of exact variable-occurrence vectors across the 6,561 source-labelled amplitude generators. Byte-exact comparison leaves only each source's literal identity map. Every distinct same-shape pair has an explicit deficit witness; open/complement pairs already disagree in ring and generator counts.

For the nine positive self-maps, the complete 6,562 or 6,570 generator sequence was replayed exactly, not just its graph support. The result is design-only: no Singular or ideal computation ran, and no rep5 closure is claimed.
