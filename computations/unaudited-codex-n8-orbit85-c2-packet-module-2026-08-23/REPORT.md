# Orbit-85 quadratic coefficient versus the frozen carrier-tail module

Status: **bounded exact source-provenance audit; no Gröbner computation.**

## Outcome

The quadratic coefficient `C2` is nonzero in the extended orbit-85 branch
ring and it is not in the module generated by the frozen `7+1`, `6+1+1`,
`3+3+2`, or `380 x 12` carrier-tail packets over their actual coefficient
ring.  The separator is the exact cross-colour multigrading: `C2` has
`T`-degree two and even parity in each of the three unordered colour-pair
degrees, while every quadratic `611/332` term has path parity and every
frozen carrier column has `T`-degree one.

This does not separate `C2` from the all-even `620/440/422` second-polar
packet.  In fact those are precisely the source row types occurring in
`C2`; this is the load-bearing scope guard.

## Compact exact representation

Write `Tail2_w` for the matching sum with exactly two cross-colour edges.
Reverse the frozen proof DAG from its root.  If `alpha_n` is the root
adjoint of node `n`, then a resolution

```text
P_z = m_left P_left + m_right P_right
```

adds `alpha_z*m_left` to `alpha_left` and
`alpha_z*m_right` to `alpha_right`; weakening is the one-input version.
The exact source-labelled coefficient is

```text
C2 = sum_(tail-sensitive compiler c) alpha_c D2(compiler_c).
```

There are only 36 tail-sensitive compiler inputs:

| schema | compiler nodes | source-row references |
|---|---:|---:|
| `A2` | 25 | 25 |
| `C0` | 5 | 160 |
| `XF` through its reduced-Laplace `C0` derivations | 6 | 384 |
| total | 36 | 569 |

For `A2`, `D2` is the stored guarded-inverse multiplier times one literal
`Tail2_w`.  For `C0` it is

```text
p_x u_x sum_j r_j Tail2_(w_j).
```

The `XF` terms use exactly those same labelled `C0` rows in the two displayed
compiler identities; they introduce no new source-row type.  Thus the
existing 2,159-node proof graph plus this 36-input/569-reference interface is
an exact straight-line representation—there is no expansion of its degree
398 multipliers.

## Exact separator

Grade a `T`-monomial by

```text
(total T-degree; deg_01 mod 2, deg_02 mod 2, deg_12 mod 2).
```

Every orbit-85 `Tail2` leaf uses a doubled colour pair, hence has grade
`(2;000)`.  Exact matching enumeration gives

```text
71:  degree 1 only
611: degree 1:15, degree 2:90; every degree-2 term has path parity
332: degree 1:9, degree 2:18, degree 3:42, degree 4:36;
     every degree-2 term has path parity.
```

Here path parity is one of `011,101,110`.  The frozen `380 x 12` carrier
matrix consists of degree-one coefficients.  Its coefficient ring is the
epsilon-constant diagonal/selector/branch ring, so multiplication cannot
change `T`-degree or cross-pair parity.  Projection `Pi_dbl` onto `(2;000)`
therefore annihilates the whole frozen packet module, while the unique marker
above gives `Pi_dbl(C2)=1` after an exact coefficient extraction.

An all-even `620` hostile row does contain `(2;000)`, and the checker requires
that mutation to fire.  The conclusion is consequently a precise packet
nonmembership theorem, not a claim that `C2` is a new consequence beyond all
mixed `X5` rows.
