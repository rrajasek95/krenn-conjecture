# Direct degree-nine X5 test for a carrier minor

Status: **exact nonmembership with a one-monomial separator.**  The selected
nonzero maximal minor of the canonical carrier-aligned `8 x 3` matrix is not
in the degree-nine span generated by all full mixed-X5 rows with degree-five
monomial multipliers.

## 1. Canonical target

Fix triangle `012`, internal-edge columns `01,02,12`, residual pure colour
zero on sites `0,...,5`, and cap rows

```text
01, 11, 21.
```

The target is

```text
Delta = det C_0[{01,11,21},{01,02,12}].                 (1)
```

Each cofactor entry has degree three, so `Delta` has degree nine.  Exact
collection of its six determinant packets gives 6,900 nonzero source
monomials.  Its site-colour fine degree is

```text
site 0  (1,0,0)       site 1  (1,0,0)       site 2  (1,0,0)
site 3  (3,0,0)       site 4  (3,0,0)       site 5  (3,0,0)
site 6  (1,1,1)       site 7  (0,3,0).                    (2)
```

The exact target-polynomial digest is
`dae00b5e44a807a407571a8a12a73017f6ce0cc0b60ac43a8f027282a15a5965`.

## 2. Fine grading makes the full test small

An amplitude row `F_w` contributes one port at every site.  For its fine
degree to divide (2), the word is forced to be one of

```text
00000001, 00000011, 00000021.                           (3)
```

No other one of the 6,558 mixed words can enter this grade.  After subtracting
the word degree from (2), a degree-five multiplier has no port on sites
`0,1,2` and two ports on each of sites `3,4,5,6,7`.  Exact pairing of those
ten labelled ports gives 40 distinct multiplier monomials for each word in
(3).  Thus the exhaustive Macaulay block is only

```text
candidate words                                      3
degree-five multipliers per word                    40
translated rows                                    120
translated-row monomial columns                  9,456
target/row column intersection                   4,968
target-private columns                           1,932
total columns                                   11,388
exact row rank                                      120.     (4)
```

The three pure normalization rows have incompatible top fine degree and do
not enter this degree-nine homogeneous block.

## 3. Singleton characteristic-zero separator

Let `lambda` be zero on every source monomial except

```text
mu = A_03[0,0] A_14[0,0] A_26[0,0] A_35[0,0]^2
     A_47[0,1]^2 A_56[0,1] A_67[2,1],
lambda(mu)=-1.                                         (5)
```

Literal expansion gives

```text
lambda(m F_w)=0  for all 120 rows in (4),
lambda(Delta)=1.                                       (6)
```

There is also a short support proof.  The monomial `mu` contains only one
edge incident to each of sites `0,1,2`, namely `03,14,26`.  Any perfect-
matching monomial dividing `mu` is therefore forced to use all three.  The
remaining sites are `5,7`, but `mu` contains no edge `57`.  Hence no
perfect-matching term from any amplitude row divides `mu`; multiplying by a
degree-five monomial cannot create it.  On the other hand its collected
coefficient in (1) is `-1`, proving (6).

The one-entry integer separator digest is
`914e217aaac6685d874c5ff2da78ed19db644c8e5f522da50d3f8e8251df5f22`.

Therefore

```text
Delta notin span_Q { m F_w : deg(m)=5, w mixed },       (7)
```

and equivalently it is not in the degree-nine component of the homogeneous
mixed-amplitude ideal.

## 4. Scope

This is the direct finite prerequisite requested after the holonomy audit.
It is exhaustive at degree nine and uses the literal full-X5 word universe,
reduced soundly by fine grading to (3).  It rules out a linear/degree-five
certificate for carrier rank drop.

It does **not** prove that `Delta` is outside the radical or a saturation of
the normalized X5 ideal.  A higher-degree identity could multiply (1) before
using X5, and normalized pure equations can participate only through such a
higher filtered cancellation.  The next valid target is therefore a bounded
search for a higher-degree multiplier or a radical counterguard—not another
degree-nine orbit-span calculation.
