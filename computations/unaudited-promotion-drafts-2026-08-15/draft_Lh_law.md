# The L_h law for the cap error, and the degree-h blocking taxonomy

**Theorems W13.1, W13.2, W13.5, W13.7 and the taxonomy T1–T4, with the
hypotheses repaired by audit A5.  Proposed status on promotion: `[P]` for
W13.1/W13.2/W13.5/W13.7 and T1; `[V]` (verified-exact on stated strata, not
proved) for the repaired T2, the corrected two-colour law, and T3; `[open]`
for T4.**

## 0. Notation and conventions

**The `h` convention** (`notes/2026-08-15-conventions-and-hazards.md` item 3;
two conventions differing by one are in circulation).  Throughout this
document

```text
B = {p, q} u U,   |U| = 2h,   N = |B| = 2h + 2,   h = N/2 - 1,
```

so `h = 3` corresponds to `N = 8`.  In code `p = 0`, `q = 1`,
`U = (2, ..., 2h+1)`.

A source assigns endpoint-ordered blocks `A_uv` in `C^{3x3}` to the pairs of
`B`; `A_pq` is the **pair block**.  A **cap covector** is
`K in (V_p (x) V_q)^*`, with nine coordinates `K_ij` (`i` the `p`-slot
colour, `j` the `q`-slot colour).  Set

```text
s      = <K, A_pq> = sum_{i,j} A_pq[i][j] K_ij            (linear in K)
kappa_c = K(e_c^{(p)}, e_c^{(q)}) = K_cc
x      = sum_{{a,b} subset U} A_ab
R_ab   = K |- ( A_{p|a} A_{q|b} + A_{p|b} A_{q|a} ),   r = sum_{{a,b}} R_ab
```

and let `E_pq(K)` be the homogeneous cap error of
`proofs/clean-pair-cap-exact-descent.md` / `notes/clean-pair-cap-exact-descent-target.md`
eq. (4):

```text
E_pq(K) = sum_{k=2}^{h} s^{h-k} [ r^k / k! * exp(x) ]_U
        = sum_{k=2}^{h} s^{h-k} r^k x^{h-k} / ( k! (h-k)! ),          (4)
```

all products taken in the site-square-zero algebra on `U` and the second
display denoting the full-`U`-support component.  `E_pq(K)` is homogeneous of
degree `h` in `K`.  For a **word** `w in {0,1,2}^U` write `E_w(K)` for the
coefficient of `(x)_{u in U} e_{w_u}` in `E_pq(K)`: a degree-`h` form in the
nine `K_ij`.  Then `E_pq(K) = 0` iff `E_w(K) = 0` for all `3^{2h}` words.

**L-monomials and blocking.**  An **L-monomial** of degree `h` is
`m = s^a kappa_0^{b_0} kappa_1^{b_1} kappa_2^{b_2}` with `a + |b| = h`; there
are `C(h+3,3)` of them (10, 20, 35, 56 at `h = 2,3,4,5`).  The descent of
`proofs/clean-pair-cap-exact-descent.md` needs an **active clean cap**: a `K`
with `s kappa_0 kappa_1 kappa_2 != 0` and `E_pq(K) = 0`.  If an L-monomial
`m` lies in the ideal generated by `{E_w}` then `m(K) = 0` at every `K` with
`E_pq(K) = 0`, while an active clean cap has `m(K) != 0`; such an `m` is a
**degree-`h` blocking certificate** at the pair, and its existence excludes
the descent there.  This document is about which L-monomials can be blocking
certificates.

**Terminology guard** (ledger items 1–2).  "Clean" is used here only in the
*slice-clean* sense of §4 (the colour-`c` pair-slice error vanishes) and in
the fixed phrase "active clean cap"; not in the cut-clean or word-clean
senses.  A "witness" in the sense of the current fleet is an active clean cap
`(p,q,K)`; no other sense appears here.

**The two representation spaces.**  Let `V` carry the `p`-slot (row) index
and `W` the `q`-slot (column) index, both `C^3`, so degree-`h` forms in `K`
live in `S^h(V (x) W)`.  For `k >= 0` put `Sigma_k = S^k V (x) S^k W` and let

```text
iota_k( e_mu (x) f_nu ) = sum_{sigma in S_k} prod_{t=1}^{k} K_{mu(t), nu(sigma(t))}
```

be the **Cauchy inclusion** onto the `lambda = (k)` isotypic component of
`S^k(V (x) W)` — a *permanent*, not a determinant: `iota_k` applied to the
`k x k` array `[K_{mu(a) nu(b)}]` is its permanent.  (Replacing it by the
alternating sum collapses the whole picture to the determinant line; that is
a fired mutation control, not a remark.)  `dim Sigma_k = C(k+2,2)^2` = 36,
100, 225, 441 at `k = 2,3,4,5`.

## 1. Theorem W13.1 (configuration expansion)

> **Theorem W13.1.**  With the notation above, for every source, pair and
> word,
>
> ```text
> E_w = sum_{M in PM(U)} sum_{J subset M, |J| <= h-2}
>            s^{|J|} prod_{e in J} x_e prod_{f in M \ J} R_f,
> ```
>
> where `x_{ab} = A_ab(w_a, w_b)` is a scalar and
> `R_{ab} = P_a (x) Q_b + P_b (x) Q_a` is a linear form in `K` with
> `P_a[i] = A_{p|a}(i, w_a)`, `Q_b[j] = A_{q|b}(j, w_b)`.

This is eq. (4) with the factorials cleared: the sum over `k` of eq. (4)
becomes the sum over the subsets `J` of a perfect matching, `|J| = h - k`.
Audit A5 evaluated eq. (4) **literally, with the factorials in place** — the
first run of the equation exactly as written in the descent note — and
compared it against its own configuration expansion and against the source
lane's two routines: **508 exact instances at `h = 2,3,4`, zero mismatches**,
with a recorded assertion that every coefficient of the factorial form is
integral.

## 2. Theorem W13.2 (the law)

> **Theorem W13.2.**  For every source, every pair and every word,
>
> ```text
> E_w  in  L_h(A) := Sigma_h + s Sigma_{h-1} + ... + s^{h-2} Sigma_2,
> ```
>
> where `s^j Sigma_k` abbreviates
> `{ s(K)^j f(K) : f in iota_k(S^k V (x) S^k W) }` inside `S^h(V (x) W)`, and
> `s = <K, A_pq>`.  The sum is direct at full rank, and for a generic source
> with pair block `A` the span of the error components is exactly `L_h(A)`.

| `h` | `N` | ambient `dim S^h` | `dim L_h(A)`, full rank | decomposition | codim |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 6 | 45 | 36 | 36 | 9 |
| 3 | 8 | 165 | 136 | 36 + 100 | 29 |
| 4 | 10 | 495 | 361 | 36 + 100 + 225 | 134 |
| 5 | 12 | 1287 | 802 | 36 + 100 + 225 + 441 | 485 |

**What is verified, and how.**

* *Containment*: exhaustive over all words at `h = 2` (81) and `h = 3` (729);
  at `h = 4` audit A5 checked **all 6,561 words** by exact integer
  orthogonality against a CRT-certified annihilator of `L_4(A)`, for three
  sources of different degeneracy, with zero violations.
* *Tightness*: the span of the error components fills `L_h(A)` exactly for
  generic sources — 36/36, 136/136, 361/361 at `h = 2,3,4`, reproduced
  independently by the audit.  Degenerate pair blocks give strictly smaller
  spans (and a strictly smaller `L_h(A)`), so the law is *stronger* there,
  not weaker.
* *Directness* is Theorem W14.3 and now has a general-`h` proof; it is stated
  and proved in `draft_evaluation_principle.md` §3, together with its failure
  at `rank A_pq <= 2` (`dim L_3 = 136 / 131 / 125` at ranks 3 / 2 / 1).
* *The `A`-independent part* of the law has dimension
  `C(3,h)^2` = 9, 1, 0 at `h = 2,3,4`: it is `Lambda^h V (x) Lambda^h W`.
  This is the exact sense in which the determinantal obstruction (the
  `Lambda^3 (x) Lambda^3` pairing, "FACT 1" of the `h = 3` probe layer) is
  only the `GL x GL`-equivariant **shadow** of W13.2: it is the part of the
  law that survives averaging over `A`, and it vanishes identically from
  `h = 4` on, while the `A`-dependent law persists at every order.

**Degeneracy.**  `dim L_3(A)` = 100 / 125 / 131 / 136 as `rank A_pq` = 0/1/2/3;
`dim L_4(A)` = 225 / 315 / {315, 346} / 361.

## 3. Theorem W13.5 (apolarity form)

Use the **apolar pairing** on degree-`h` forms in the nine `K_ij`:

```text
<phi, F> := phi(d/dK) F = sum_e e! phi_e F_e
```

(the multinomial factors are part of the pairing; the plain coefficient dot
product is *not* the right form — audit A5's first pass used it, got a
mismatch, and the corrected run is the one recorded below).  Under this
pairing `<phi, (u^T K v)^k> = k! phi(u v^T)`.

> **Theorem W13.5.**  A degree-`h` form `phi` is apolar to `L_h(A)` if and
> only if, for every rank-one matrix `R = u v^T`, the univariate
> `t |-> phi(R + tA)` vanishes to order at least `h-1` at `t = 0` — i.e.
> `phi` vanishes to order `>= h-1` along the direction `A` at every point of
> the Segre variety of rank-one matrices.

**Verification** (exact, both directions, at `h = 2,3,4`): `dim perp(L_h)` =
9, 29, 134, in each case equal to the dimension of the solution space of the
order-`(h-1)` conditions, with zero forward violations and a fired control (a
random form fails the order condition).  Also verified exactly: `Sigma_k`'s
apolar perp is the degree-`k` part of the Segre ideal (dims 9, 65, 270 at
`k = 2,3,4`).

**Consequences.**

* At `h = 3` the space of `A`-independent obstructions is one-dimensional and
  spanned by `det`; that is FACT 1 in one line.
* At `h = 4` the perp is 134-dimensional and its intersection over varying
  `A` is zero: **no `A`-independent degree-4 obstruction exists**.  (The
  audit's report says the intersection was taken over six matrices; the
  script uses four.  The verdict — dimension 0 — is unaffected.)
* The apolar algebra of `det_3` has Hilbert function `(1, 9, 9, 1, 0, ...)`,
  computed both from the catalecticants and from the ideal generated by
  `Sigma_2`.  The source lane's prose describes a hand proof of `I_4 = S^4`
  by a cofactor-independence argument; that proof text exists nowhere in the
  corpus, and what is machine-verified is `dim I_4 = 495 of 495`.  Promote
  the computation, not the claimed hand proof.

## 4. Theorem W13.7 (the slice bridge)

For a colour `c`, the **colour-`c` slice** of the source at the pair `(p,q)`
is the scalar data `s_c = A_pq[c][c]`,
`r_{ab}^{(c)} = A_{p|a}(c,w_a) A_{q|b}(c,w_b) + A_{p|b}(c,w_b) A_{q|a}(c,w_a)`,
`x_{ab} = A_ab(w_a,w_b)`, and its **level-`h` error** is eq. (4) evaluated on
that scalar data.  The slice is **slice-clean** when that error vanishes at
every word.

> **Theorem W13.7.**  For every word `w`, the coefficient of `kappa_c^h` in
> `E_w` equals `E_w(E_cc)` — the evaluation of `E_w` at the elementary cap
> covector `K = e_c (x) e_c` — and equals the level-`h` cap error of the
> colour-`c` slice at `w`.  At the monochrome word `c^U` it is exactly the
> colour-`c` slice error of the `h = 3` probe layer's law F1, so F1 holds
> verbatim at every `h`.

Verified exactly at `h = 3` and `h = 4`: 18 instance rows (three trials x
three colours x two orders), each comparing the `kappa_c^h` coefficient
against the scalar formula on 30 words including the monochrome word, all
agreeing.  The consequence used downstream — *a slice-clean colour `c`
forces `kappa_c^h` out of the error span* — is the degree-`h` case of the
degree-free evaluation principle of `draft_evaluation_principle.md` §2.

## 5. The degree-h blocking taxonomy

Because `span{E_w} ⊆ L_h(A)` always and `= L_h(A)` for generic sources,
membership in `L_h(A)` is exactly the degree-`h` blocking question:

```text
m not in L_h(A)  =>  m is in no source's degree-h error span at that pair
                     (universal exclusion);
m in L_h(A)      =>  m is in the degree-h error span of a generic source
                     with that pair block.
```

Classify an L-monomial `m = s^a kappa_0^{b_0} kappa_1^{b_1} kappa_2^{b_2}` of
degree `h` by `a` and by the number of distinct `kappa`-colours occurring.

### T1 (monochrome, `a <= h-2`) — proved, no hypothesis

> **T1.**  For every pair block `A` and every colour `c`, the monomials
> `s^a kappa_c^{h-a}` with `0 <= a <= h-2` lie in `L_h(A)`.

Audit A5 confirms T1 **with no hypothesis on `A`** — it holds across the full
battery including matrices with zero rows or columns, single-cell blocks, the
zero matrix, and complex (Gaussian) entries.  These `3(h-1)` monomials are
exactly the survivors at a generic pair block: 3 of 10, 6 of 20, 9 of 35, 12
of 56 at `h = 2,3,4,5`, checked as an equality of lists at each order.  The
survivor fraction `3(h-1)/C(h+3,3)` tends to 0, so relative to the number of
degree-`h` monomials, blocking becomes rarer as `N` grows.

### T2 (two or more colours) — **repaired hypothesis**

The statement as originally issued — *"for `A_pq` with no zero row and no
zero column, no L-monomial with two or more `kappa`-colours lies in
`L_h(A)`"* — is **false**.  Promote only the repaired form, and record the
corrected law beside it.

> **T2 (repaired).**  If `A_pq` has **no zero entry** (all nine cells
> nonzero), then no L-monomial with two or more distinct `kappa`-colours lies
> in `L_h(A)`.
>
> Status `[V]`: verified exactly on 220 full-support pair blocks at `h = 3`,
> over *all* multi-colour L-monomials, with zero violations; and on the
> `h = 2,3,4` battery.  Not proved for all `A`.  At `h = 5` only partially
> certified.

> **The corrected law (audit A5), for the shape that fails.**  For `c != c'`
> and `h >= 3`,
>
> ```text
> kappa_c kappa_{c'}^{h-1} in L_h(A)
>    <=>  A_cc != 0  AND  A_ij = 0 for every (i,j) with i != c' and j != c',
>         except (i,j) = (c,c).
> ```
>
> Here `i` is the row (`p`-slot) index and `j` the column (`q`-slot) index,
> `c` is the colour occurring once and `c'` the colour occurring `h-1` times.
> Since `c != c'`, the condition names exactly **three forced zeros**: the
> cells of the `2x2` block on rows `!= c'` and columns `!= c'`, other than
> `(c,c)`.
>
> Status `[V]`: 0 mismatches over the 265 `0/1` matrices with no zero row or
> column, times the 6 ordered colour pairs, at `h = 3`.  Not swept at
> `h >= 4`, and **not asserted for pair blocks with a zero row or column**,
> where it demonstrably fails (e.g. the single cell `E_22`).  The source
> report's phrase "three weight spaces that cannot cancel" is an unverified
> prose gloss on this rule, not a proved mechanism.

**The refutation, in closed form.**  Take

```text
A_pq = [[1, 0, 0],
        [0, 0, 1],
        [0, 1, 0]]      (the permutation matrix of the transposition (1 2)),
```

which has full rank, determinant `-1`, no zero row and no zero column — every
hypothesis of the original T2.  Then `s = <K, A_pq> = K_00 + K_12 + K_21`,
and since `iota_3(e_1 e_1 e_1 (x) f_1 f_1 f_2) = 6 K_11^2 K_12`,
`iota_3(e_1 e_1 e_2 (x) f_1 f_1 f_1) = 6 K_11^2 K_21` and
`iota_2(e_1 e_1 (x) f_1 f_1) = 2 K_11^2`:

```text
kappa_0 kappa_1^2 = K_00 K_11^2
                  = (s - K_12 - K_21) K_11^2
                  = s kappa_1^2
                      - (1/6) iota_3(e_1 e_1 e_1 (x) f_1 f_1 f_2)
                      - (1/6) iota_3(e_1 e_1 e_2 (x) f_1 f_1 f_1).
```

The first term lies in `s Sigma_2` (as `kappa_1^2 = (1/2) iota_2(e_1e_1 (x)
f_1f_1)`) and the other two lie in `Sigma_3`, so
`kappa_0 kappa_1^2 in Sigma_3 + s Sigma_2 = L_3(A)` — a two-colour monomial
inside `L_3` at a full-rank, no-zero-line pair block.  The identity is
checkable by hand and was verified as an exact polynomial identity.

It is not a formal artefact: for three aggregate sources carrying that pair
block, `kappa_0 kappa_1^2` lies in the actual error span `span{E_w}` (of
dimension 136, i.e. tight), so it is a genuine two-colour degree-3 blocking
certificate.  The failure is specific to the shape `kappa_c kappa_{c'}^{h-1}`:
the controls `kappa_0 kappa_1 kappa_2` (at `h = 3,4`) and `s kappa_0 kappa_1`
(at `h = 3`) stay outside.

**Size of the failure set.**  45 of the 265 `0/1` matrices with no zero row
or column violate the original T2 at `h = 3` (**17%**); 9 of 109 orbit
representatives over `{-1,0,1}` (modulo simultaneous permutation and row and
column sign flips).  At `h = 2` there are no violations, since `L_2 = Sigma_2`
carries no `A`-dependence.

**Consequences of the repair, to be carried by every consumer.**

* The pair blocks of the residual family at `N = 8` are full nine-cell blocks,
  so they satisfy the repaired hypothesis automatically: **T2 is available
  there**.
* T2 must **never** be applied at a sparse pair block.  The source lane's
  own caveat — that single-cell pair blocks degenerate the taxonomy, to be
  handled pair by pair on monomial sources — is a strictly weaker statement
  and does **not** cover the failure set, which contains full-rank matrices.
* The `N = 8` sharpening "`kappa_c^2 kappa_c'` can never block" is exactly
  the clause that fails; it survives only under the repaired hypothesis.  The
  `h = 3` probe layer's inability to exclude that monomial was therefore
  correct, not a gap.
* Open, and to be recorded rather than hidden: whether an **exact** source
  can carry a T2-violating sparse pair block is untested.  The refutation
  stands against the theorem as stated regardless.

### T3 (`a = h`) — verified-exact

> **T3.**  For `h >= 3`: `s^h in L_h(A)` iff `det A_pq = 0`.  For `h = 2`:
> `s^2 in L_2(A)` iff `adj A_pq = 0` (equivalently `rank A_pq <= 1`).
>
> Status `[V]`: confirmed on the full battery at `h = 2,3,4`, including
> matrices with zero lines and complex entries; not proved for all `A`.

### T4 (`a = h-1`, monochrome) — measured, not characterised

> **T4.**  `s^{h-1} kappa_c in L_h(A)` is conditional on `A`, and the
> condition weakens with `h`.  At `h = 2` it is exactly "the `2x2` block
> complementary to `(c,c)` vanishes".  At `h = 3,4` membership also occurs at
> every rank-one `A`, and at `h = 4` the triple
> `(rank A, cof_cc(A) = 0, complementary block = 0)` no longer determines it.
>
> Status: **open**.  Do not promote a closed form.

## 6. Scope warning: this is a degree-`h` taxonomy only

The taxonomy constrains degree-`h` certificates.  It says nothing at higher
degrees, and the higher degrees do **not** behave the same way: in the
measured degree-`(h+1)` layer, **314 of 316 pairs whose minimal blocking
degree is `h+1` have multi-colour minimal certificates**.  Any chain that
quantifies over degrees — in particular any argument that concludes "the
certificate must be monochrome" — must consume that fact.  The related record
correction also travels with this document: the earlier claim that blocking
degrees `2..5` were observed is not reproducible (every runner used
`max_degree = 4`), so degree-5 blocking is unestablished
(`notes/2026-08-15-conventions-and-hazards.md` item 7).

Two further scope lines:

* `span{E_w} = L_h(A)` is a genericity statement; for special sources the
  span is smaller, and every "not in the span" verdict must be read against
  the source actually used.
* Nothing here asserts the existence of an active clean cap at any pair; the
  taxonomy says which monomials *could* obstruct one.
