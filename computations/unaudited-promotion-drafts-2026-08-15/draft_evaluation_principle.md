# The evaluation principle, and directness of the L_h decomposition at every order

**Theorem W14.5 (one-directional), with Lemmas W14.1 and W14.2, and Theorem
W14.3 (directness) with the general-`h` proof supplied by audit A6.
Proposed status on promotion: `[P]`.**

## 0. Notation

Notation is that of `draft_Lh_law.md` §0: `B = {p,q} u U`, `|U| = 2h`,
`N = 2h + 2`, cap covector `K` with coordinates `K_ij`, `s = <K, A_pq>`,
`kappa_c = K_cc`, cap error `E_pq(K)` of eq. (4) with word components
`E_w(K)`, and `Sigma_k = S^k V (x) S^k W` embedded by the permanental Cauchy
map `iota_k` into `S^k(V (x) W)`.

Two further objects:

* **`E_cc`**, the elementary cap covector `e_c (x) e_c` — the point of the
  nine-dimensional cap space with `K_cc = 1` and all other coordinates 0.
* **`I_pq`**, the ideal of `C[K_00, ..., K_22]` generated by the word forms
  `{E_w}`, and `V(I_pq)` its zero locus.  The degree-`h` graded piece of
  `I_pq` is `span{E_w}`, which equals `L_h(A)` for generic sources
  (`draft_Lh_law.md` §2).

**Slice-cleanness, in the form used here** (ledger item 1: this is the
*slice-clean* sense, not cut-clean or word-clean).  The colour-`c` slice is
**clean** when `E_w(E_cc) = 0` for every word `w` — equivalently, by Theorem
W13.7, when the level-`h` cap error of the colour-`c` scalar slice vanishes
at every word; equivalently, `E_cc in V(I_pq)`.

## 1. The supporting structure: W14.1 and W14.2

For a rank-one cap `K = u (x) v` write

```text
alpha       = sum_{i,j} u_i v_j A_pq[i][j],
P_a[i]      = A_{p|a}(i, w_a),      Q_b[j] = A_{q|b}(j, w_b),
R_ab^{(u,v)} = <u,P_a><v,Q_b> + <u,P_b><v,Q_a>,
```

and for a subset `S` of `U` of even size `2k` let `Haf^{(u,v)}(S)` be the sum
over perfect matchings of `S` of the products of `R^{(u,v)}` over their edges.

> **Lemma W14.1 (hafnian closed form).**
> `Haf^{(u,v)}(S) = k! * sum_{T subset S, |T| = k} prod_{a in T} <u,P_a>
> prod_{b in S - T} <v,Q_b>.`
>
> *Proof.*  Expand `Haf = sum_M prod_{(a,b) in M} (<u,P_a><v,Q_b> +
> <u,P_b><v,Q_a>)`.  A term is indexed by a matching together with an
> orientation of each edge, i.e. by the set `T` of endpoints contributing
> their `<u,P>` factor; `|T| = k`, and for each such `T` the matchings
> compatible with it are exactly the `k!` bijections `T -> S - T`. ∎

> **Theorem W14.2 (level law).**  With
> `G_j^{(u,v)}(w) = sum_{|J| = j} (prod_{e in J} x_e) Haf^{(u,v)}(U - V(J))`,
> the sum running over partial matchings `J` of `U` with `j` edges,
>
> ```text
> E_w(u (x) v) = sum_{j=0}^{h-2} alpha^j G_j^{(u,v)}(w).
> ```
>
> Specialising to `u = v = delta_c` (so `alpha = s_c := A_pq[c][c]`):
> the colour-`c` slice error at `w` is `sum_j s_c^j G_j^{(c)}(w)`.
>
> *Proof.*  Regroup the configuration sum of Theorem W13.1 by `J`:
> `sum_M sum_{J subset M} = sum_{J partial matching} sum_{M superset J}`, and
> the inner sum is the hafnian over `U - V(J)`. ∎

Both were re-derived and re-verified by audit A6 from primary definitions:
456/456 exact checks of W14.1 (orders `h = 2,3`, subsets of size 2, 4, 6,
direct hafnian against the closed form) and 108/108 of W14.2 (54 rank-one
expansions plus 54 colour slices).  A6 additionally ran **eq. (4) literally,
factorials included, in the square-free algebra** and matched it against the
matching-sum expansion coefficient by coefficient (45 word-level identities
at `h = 2,3`); dropping the `1/k!` breaks the identity, and an antisymmetric
`R` breaks the level law — both are fired controls.

## 2. Theorem W14.5 (the evaluation principle) — one direction

> **Theorem W14.5.**  Suppose the colour-`c` slice at the pair `(p,q)` is
> clean, i.e. `E_cc in V(I_pq)`.  Then every element of `I_pq` vanishes at
> `E_cc`.  Consequently no L-monomial `m` with `m(E_cc) != 0` lies in
> `I_pq` — **at any degree**.  Since
> `(s^a kappa_c^b)(E_cc) = s_c^a` with `s_c = A_pq[c][c]`, and any monomial
> carrying a colour other than `c` vanishes at `E_cc`:
>
> * `kappa_c^d` lies in no degree-`d` part of `I_pq`, **unconditionally**;
> * `s^a kappa_c^b` and `s^d` lie in no part of `I_pq` **whenever
>   `A_pq[c][c] != 0`**;
> * monomials carrying another colour: **no information**.
>
> *Proof.*  Evaluation at a point is a ring homomorphism, so it kills every
> element of an ideal all of whose generators it kills. ∎

The proof is one line; the content of the theorem is entirely in what it does
**not** exclude.

**The converse is false — the "iff" must be dropped.**  The implication
"`m` is excluded `=>` `A_pq[c][c] != 0`" fails.  Audit A6's witness: at
`h = 3`, take a source in which the colour-`0` row of **every** `p`-block
vanishes (`A_{p|a}(0, *) = 0` for all six sites of `U`) and whose pair block
is full rank with `det = -14` and `A_pq[0][0] = 0`.  Then

* the colour-`0` slice is clean (indeed every level function `G_j^{(0)}`
  vanishes identically — strictly stronger than cleanness);
* `s(E_00) = A_pq[0][0] = 0`, so the evaluation principle says nothing about
  `s kappa_0^2`;
* yet exact rational membership testing shows `s kappa_0^2` is **not** in
  `span{E_w}` (span dimension 58 of the ambient 165).

The exclusion there has a cause outside the principle's reach: by the graded
criterion (Corollary W14.4), `s^a kappa_c^{h-a} in span{E_w}` requires
`G_a^{(c)}` not identically zero, and in this construction all `G_j^{(c)}`
vanish.  So the correct promoted form is the single implication above.

**What this refutes, and what replaces it.**  The originally conjectured
*transfer lemma* — "a clean colour-`c` slice removes every
`s^a kappa_c^{h-a}` from the error span" — is **false**.  The counterexample
regime is exact and forced: with `a >= 1` and `A_pq[c][c] = 0`,
`s kappa_c^{h-1}` re-enters the span (4 of 4 constructed instances, span
dimensions 126 and 135 for the two construction shapes, with 12 of 12
explicit exact membership certificates whose level projections are the
predicted ones).  The regime is non-vacuous at `h = 4` as well.  The
hypothesis `A_pq[c][c] != 0` is exactly what makes those slices dirty, which
is why the repaired statement is the evaluation principle and nothing more.
The earlier 6-of-6 evidence for the false lemma is explained: those sources
satisfied the strictly stronger condition that all `G_j^{(c)}` vanish.

**The honest reading**, in audit A6's words: *the transfer lemma is
unprovable without exactness* — every counterexample so far uses a source
that is not exact, and the open question is whether a globally exact source
can realise the regime.  Nothing in this document assumes it cannot.

## 3. Theorem W14.3 (directness), with the general-`h` proof

> **Theorem W14.3.**  If `rank A_pq = 3` then the sum
>
> ```text
> L_h(A) = iota_h(Sigma_h) (+) s iota_{h-1}(Sigma_{h-1}) (+) ...
>                                (+) s^{h-2} iota_2(Sigma_2)
> ```
>
> is **direct**, at every `h >= 2`.

At `rank A_pq <= 2` it is not: at `h = 3` the dimension drops from
`136 = 36 + 100` to 131 (rank 2) and 125 (rank 1), so the full-rank
hypothesis is load-bearing.

### 3.1 Reduction to a key lemma

**(a) `GL x GL` invariance.**  The substitution `K |-> g^T K h` sends `s_A` to
`s_{g A h^T}` and preserves each `iota_k(Sigma_k)`, which is a
`GL(V) x GL(W)` submodule of `S^k(V (x) W)`.  Hence `dim L_h(A)` depends only
on the `GL x GL` orbit of `A`, i.e. only on `rank A`; at full rank we may
take `A = Id`, so that `s = tr(K) = K_00 + K_11 + K_22`.  (Machine-checked:
the substituted `iota_3` generators lie in the exact rational row space of
the originals; the substitution law for `s_A` holds exactly; and a control
confirms the same substitution *does* move a random subspace.)

**(b) The key lemma.**  Write `C_j = iota_j(Sigma_j)`.

> **Key Lemma.**  `C_j n tr * S^{j-1} = 0` for every `j >= 2`.

*Directness from the key lemma.*  Suppose
`sum_{k=2}^{h} tr^{h-k} c_k = 0` with `c_k in C_k`.  Every term except
`k = h` is divisible by `tr`, so `c_h in tr * S^{h-1}`, whence `c_h = 0` by
the key lemma at level `h`.  Then `tr * ( sum_{k=2}^{h-1} tr^{(h-1)-k} c_k )
= 0`, and `tr` is a nonzerodivisor in the polynomial ring, so
`sum_{k=2}^{h-1} tr^{(h-1)-k} c_k = 0` — the same statement one level down.
Induct.  (Note the lemma is needed at *every* level `j <= h`, not only at
`j = h`.) ∎

### 3.2 The key lemma via apolarity

Work with the apolar pairing `<phi, F> = phi(d/dK) F` on forms of equal
degree, and write the dual variables as `y_ij`.  Two standard facts, both
verified exactly here:

1. `iota_h(Sigma_h) = span{ (v^T K w)^h }`, the span of `h`-th powers of
   rank-one linear forms (dims 36, 100, 225 at `h = 2,3,4`, equal to
   `dim Sigma_h` in each case);
2. that span is the apolar perp of `(I_Segre)_h`, where `I_Segre = I_2` is
   the ideal generated by the nine `2x2` minors `y_ij y_kl - y_il y_kj` of
   the dual matrix `(y_ij)` — the homogeneous ideal of the Segre variety of
   rank-one `3x3` matrices.  (Dimensions add up exactly: `36 + 9 = 45`,
   `100 + 65 = 165`, `225 + 270 = 495`; orthogonality checked exactly.)

Let `T := sum_i d/dy_ii`, the derivation obtained by substituting `d/dy` into
the linear form `tr`; it maps `S^h -> S^{h-1}`.  The adjunction
`<l g, D> = <g, l(d) D>` for a linear form `l` gives, for `l = tr`:

```text
tr * g in C_h  =  ((I_2)_h)^perp
   =>  0 = <tr * g, D> = <g, T(D)>  for every D in (I_2)_h
   =>  g perp T( (I_2)_h ).
```

So the key lemma follows from

```text
(*)   T( (I_2)_h ) = S^{h-1}     for every h >= 2,
```

because then `g perp S^{h-1}` forces `g = 0`, i.e. `C_h n tr * S^{h-1} = 0`.

### 3.3 Proof of (*)

**Base case `h = 2`.**  For `c != d` choose `e` outside `{c,d}` and take the
minor `m = y_ee y_cd - y_ed y_ce`.  Then `T(m) = y_cd` exactly: only `y_ee`
is a diagonal variable in the first term, and neither `y_ed` nor `y_ce` is
diagonal.  This produces every off-diagonal variable.  For a principal minor
`y_aa y_bb - y_ab y_ba` one gets `T = y_aa + y_bb`, and the three such sums
span the diagonal 3-space.  Hence `T((I_2)_2) = S^1`, a 9-dimensional space.
(Machine-checked: rank 9, exactly over `Q`.  Two controls fire: replacing `T`
by the single partial `d/dy_00` gives rank `< 9`, so the sum over the
diagonal is essential; using only the three principal minors gives rank 3, so
the off-diagonal minors are load-bearing.)

**Inductive step `h-1 -> h`.**  Put `X = T((I_2)_h)`.  Let `L` be the ideal
generated by `{ y_ab (a != b) } u { y_aa - y_bb }`, and let
`phi : S -> k[t]` be the quotient by `L`, so `phi(y_aa) = t` and
`phi(y_ab) = 0` for `a != b`; the degree-`(h-1)` part `(L)_{h-1}` has
codimension 1 in `S^{h-1}`.  For `z in (I_2)_{h-1}` — so that `z y_ab` and
`z y_aa` lie in `(I_2)_h`, `I_2` being an ideal — the derivation `T` gives

```text
T( z y_ab ) = T(z) y_ab            (a != b, since T(y_ab) = 0),
T( z y_aa ) = T(z) y_aa + z        (Leibniz, since T(y_aa) = 1).
```

By the inductive hypothesis `T((I_2)_{h-1}) = S^{h-2}`, so `T(z)` ranges over
all of `S^{h-2}`.  The first line gives `S^{h-2} y_ab subset X` for every
`a != b`.  Subtracting the second line for two different diagonal variables
with the same `z` gives `S^{h-2} (y_aa - y_bb) subset X`.  Together these
generate `(L)_{h-1}`, so `X` contains a subspace of codimension 1.

It remains to escape `(L)_{h-1}`.  Take `m = y_00 y_11 - y_01 y_10` and
`u = y_00^{h-2}`, so `m u in (I_2)_h` and `T(mu) = T(m) u + m T(u) in X`.
Since `T(m) = y_00 + y_11`, `phi(m) = t^2` and
`T(u) = (h-2) y_00^{h-3}`,

```text
phi( T(mu) ) = phi(y_00 + y_11) t^{h-2} + t^2 (h-2) t^{h-3}
             = 2 t^{h-1} + (h-2) t^{h-1}
             = h t^{h-1},
```

which is nonzero in characteristic 0.  Hence `X` is not contained in
`(L)_{h-1}`; as `X` contains that codimension-one subspace, `X = S^{h-1}`. ∎

This is the only place where characteristic 0 is used.

*Note on modular arithmetic.*  Rank over `F_p` is a lower bound for rank over
`Q`, and the a-priori upper bounds are the generator counts; hitting the
maximal possible value from below therefore **proves** the statement over
`Q`.  Two mutation controls guard the rank computations (a duplicated row and
a row that genuinely lies in `tr * S^{h-1}` must not raise the rank).

## 4. Scope

* Directness needs `rank A_pq = 3` and characteristic 0.  Its failure at
  lower rank is measured, not conjectured.
* `span{E_w} = L_h(A)` for generic sources is an input from
  `draft_Lh_law.md` §2; the directness theorem itself is about `L_h(A)` and
  is source-independent.
* W14.5 needs no rank hypothesis and no degree bound; it is the evaluation
  homomorphism.
* Neither theorem asserts the existence of a clean slice, a blocked pair, or
  an active clean cap at any pair.

## 5. Recorded, not promoted here

* **The degree-`(h+1)` layer.**  Universal (`A`-only) exclusion exists only
  at degrees `h` and `h+1`; the degree-`(h+1)` perp is one-dimensional at
  full rank, spanned by `det K` at `h = 2` and by
  `phi_A = q_A^2 - 4 <K, cof A> det K` at `h = 3`.  Audit A6 upgraded
  `phi_A perp J_4(A)` from verified to **proved symbolically in `Z[A]`**
  (1,224 pairings vanish identically, with two fired symbolic mutation
  controls), and corrected the characteristic-polynomial description: with
  `N = K adj(A)` and `e_1, e_2, e_3` the elementary symmetric functions of
  the eigenvalues of `N`,
  ```text
  e_1(N) = shat_A,   e_2(N) = det(A) q_A,   e_3(N) = det(A)^2 det K,
  hence  e_2(N)^2 - 4 e_1(N) e_3(N) = det(A)^2 phi_A
  ```
  — the factor `det(A)^2` was missing from the source lane's statement.  This
  material belongs to a separate promotion (the "Gap B" closed forms); it is
  recorded here so the corrected identity is on file.
* **The rank-one re-basing motivation.**  The claim that rank-one caps lose
  nothing is not promoted: the measured support is 18 informative witness
  pairs from 3 sources at `h = 2` (11 of 29 pairs are vacuous, and 9 sources
  were run, not the 70 the source docstring implies).  Later, unaudited work
  found the re-basing to be lossy.  Nothing in this document depends on it.
