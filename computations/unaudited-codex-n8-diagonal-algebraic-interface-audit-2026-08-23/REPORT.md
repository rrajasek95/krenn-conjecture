# N8-DIAGONAL algebraic-interface audit

## Verdict

The certified `N8-DIAGONAL` proof contains **no characteristic-zero or
integral source-polynomial Nullstellensatz multipliers**, and none can be
cheaply exported from its frozen proof objects.  The proof of record is an
87-orbit Boolean CNF/DRAT refutation of a sound nonvanishing-pattern
relaxation.  Consequently the maximal degree and support of diagonal quotient
multipliers are **unknown**, not zero.

This does not weaken the certified theorem.  It only means the theorem
currently has no literal algebraic interface suitable for taking the first
off-diagonal tail coefficient.

## What is actually certified

The encoder introduces Boolean atoms

```text
p(c,S) = 1  iff  haf(t^c|S) != 0
```

and Laplace auxiliaries `g(c,S,w,u)`.  Its clauses express field-valid
implications among zero/nonzero predicates.  The shipped packet has 87 orbit
CNFs and 87 DRAT traces.  Every orbit CNF has 5,592 Boolean variables; the
CNFs have 13,737--13,932 clauses, and the DRAT traces total 429,178 nonempty
lines (5,771,259 bytes).  The certificate tree contains only CNF, DRAT, core,
encoder, ledger, and replay material.  It contains no Gröbner basis, syzygy,
Macaulay matrix, multiplier list, or rational/integer polynomial payload.

This agrees with the certified proof and supersession audit: algebraic
corroboration exists only at `N=4,6`; the sole `N=8` case-ideal attempt had 81
variables and 1,932 generators, timed out after 3,000 seconds in
characteristic 32003, and has no verdict.  The DRAT proof is therefore not a
compressed representation of a source-polynomial Gröbner calculation.

## Exact algebraic statement and normalization guard

Let `D` be the diagonal quotient (all endpoint-off-diagonal edge cells set to
zero) and let `J_D` be the normalized `N=8` diagonal GHZ ideal: the 1,638
nontrivial all-even mixed rows together with three pure rows `F_(c^8)-1`.
The certified any-field theorem implies abstractly that `J_D` is the unit
ideal over `Z`: a proper ideal of the finite-type `Z`-algebra would have a
maximal-ideal residue field and hence a forbidden point over a field.
Therefore an integral certificate exists, but the proof package gives neither
its coefficients nor a degree bound.

If the pure rows are not normalized and only required to be nonzero, the
literal identity `1 in <mixed rows>` is impossible (the origin is a common
zero).  The correct algebraic form is then either a Rabinowitsch unit
certificate after adjoining an inverse of the product of the three pure
amplitudes, or a power-membership certificate

```text
(F_(0^8) F_(1^8) F_(2^8))^m in <mixed rows>.
```

Thus any claimed identity `1 = sum P_w F_w + sum V_e T_e` must explicitly
include the normalized pure equations among the `F_w`, or include the
Rabinowitsch relation.

## Why the DRAT traces do not export the requested multipliers

There are four distinct losses of algebraic provenance.

1. A Boolean atom records only whether a hafnian vanishes; it is not a source
   polynomial or a monomial in the source ring.
2. Faithfully algebraizing `p(c,S)` requires a selector and an inverse, for
   example `b^2-b`, `(1-b)h`, and `b(hu-1)`.  Resolution/DRAT multipliers would
   initially live in this much larger selector--Rabinowitsch ring.
3. The clause derivations use domain logic, Laplace support implications,
   factor-zero implications, and the free-set case split.  The DRAT records do
   not retain polynomial-combination witnesses for those implications.
4. The 87 refutations are branch certificates for 4,096 support cases.
   Eliminating branch selectors and inverse variables, then gluing all cases,
   can introduce localizer powers and large degree.  Neither DRAT line count
   nor minimal-core size bounds the resulting source multiplier degree or
   support.

One could compile the CNFs into polynomial calculus in an extended Boolean
ring, but that would not yet be the requested source identity.  The hard step
is eliminating the selectors/inverses and recovering literal source-row
provenance; this is essentially a new algebraic elimination, not certificate
format conversion.

## Tail lift interface

If an explicit quotient certificate

```text
1 = sum_w pbar_w * Fbar_w
```

were obtained in `D`, lifting representatives to the full endpoint-colour
ring would be mechanical: the difference
`1 - sum_w P_w F_w` maps to zero in `D`, hence lies in the ideal generated by
the off-diagonal tail cells `T_e`, and can be collected as
`sum_e V_e T_e`.  This is exactly the desired first-order-tail interface.
The missing object is therefore sharply localized: **one explicit normalized
N8 diagonal-quotient unit certificate**, with source-row labels.  Nothing in
the certified packet supplies it.

The nearby degree-18 calculation in `computations/test_diagonal_power2.py`
does not fill this gap: it proves `P^2` membership only for the **six-site**
diagonal quotient (874 by 5,530 Macaulay map, 11,682 nonzeros), and it freezes
rank/membership evidence rather than exact rational multipliers.  It must not
be cited as an `N=8` interface.

## Smallest honest next computation

Build the direct normalized diagonal quotient with 84 edge-colour variables,
the 1,638 all-even mixed rows, and three pure normalizations.  Before any
Gröbner run, exploit `S_8 x S_3` multigrading to profile the lowest possible
Macaulay degree and search for an equivariant linear-system certificate over
two large primes.  Only a finite degree profile common across primes should
trigger rational reconstruction.  Re-running the 81-variable/1,932-generator
case Gröbner calculation would repeat the known extraction barrier and would
not preserve a compact source-row-labelled multiplier interface.
