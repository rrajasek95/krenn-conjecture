# Independent dangerous-chart degree-four audit

## Outcome

For each **zero-based** dangerous pure-matching chart 25--28, the raw
source-labelled computation gives an exact integral lift

\[
                 H_0H_1H_2\in I_{\rm mix}+K^5,
\]

where `K` is generated by the 240 endpoint cells outside the twelve named
pure anchors.  The certificates use literal mixed hafnian columns and replay
on all rows of `K`-degree at most four.  No orbit quotient, anchor
specialization, or finite-field membership inference appears in the final
replay.

This is finite K-adic membership.  Localizing the displayed congruence at an
anchor monomial preserves this finite membership, but it does **not** prove
membership in the full localized ideal, saturation, or radical membership.
No localized repair was needed or tested because the unmultiplied finite
lift exists on every chart.

## Independent construction

`audit_d4.py` rebuilds the 252 endpoint cells in raw order

```text
(u,v,a,b), with (u,v) lexicographic and then a,b in 0,1,2,
```

and independently enumerates the 105 perfect matchings of eight labelled
vertices.  A source column retains the exact mixed word and its eight-cell
multiplier; its 105 output terms are never merged by source label.

The corrected degree-three certificates are frozen by payload digest and
replayed through raw definitions.  The independently rebuilt lower
components have the following dimensions, agreeing with but not importing
the producer computation:

| zero chart | d3 certificate | lower rows | lower source columns |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 25 | 282 | 1,013 | 5,262 |
| 26 | 211 | 917 | 3,836 |
| 27 | 205 | 1,013 | 5,262 |
| 28 | 207 | 917 | 3,812 |

At degree four the audit first quotients by literal singleton leading
columns.  It then includes every non-singleton row in the degree-four tails
of **all** lower source columns, not only the tail of one chosen degree-three
certificate.  The remaining core is solved using two exact source families:

1. minimum-degree-four columns `B`; and
2. literal differences of minimum-degree-three columns having the same sole
   degree-three leading row.

Every family in (2) is visibly in the complete lower kernel before its
degree-four tail is read.  A finite-field echelon only selects a nonzero
minor.  That minor is then solved over `Fraction`, expanded back to literal
source columns, and replayed exactly.  All final coefficients happen to be
integers.

## Numbering and the legacy chart-25 scope guard

The old certified `n8-chart25-degree4` calculation is **legacy one-based
chart 25 = zero-based orbit 24 (W40)**.  It is not current zero-based chart
25.  `audit_chart24_filtration_scope.py` independently aligns the old and new
representatives by

```text
old -> new vertices: (0,3,1,2,4,7,5,6)
old -> new colours:  (2,0,1)
```

and proves that their twelve pure-anchor sets agree after transport.  The
apparent conflict with the new zero24 cutoff-five lift is a filtration
change:

```text
old K36: outside a 36-cell carrier = 12 anchors + 24 extras
new K12: outside only the 12 anchors
```

The old four canonical dual rows have degrees

```text
K36: (2,2,2,4)
K12: (5,6,6,6).
```

Hence that obstruction is invisible modulo `K12^5`.  The independent replay
of the new 2,222-term zero24 cutoff-five certificate succeeds exactly and its
truncated pairing with the old cochain is zero.

There is an additional guard: the transported 20-row affine cochain does not
annihilate all raw minimum-`K36`-degree-zero columns.  At K12 cutoffs six and
seven it has respectively 44 and 52 source-column violations.  Therefore it
cannot be promoted to a K12 obstruction without constructing the missing
degree-zero Schur lift.  The scope-audit ledger digest is
`94ab74547f58b6da4025f64d81a1857d72780015b8a4959bb22eea4b30a3d78c`.
