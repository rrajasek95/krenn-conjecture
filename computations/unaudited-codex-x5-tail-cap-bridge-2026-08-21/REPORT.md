## The exact missing algebraic lemma

Let

```text
R = Q[g^c_uv, t^ij_uv : c=0,1,2, i!=j, 0<=u<v<8],
```

with 84 diagonal variables and 168 cross-colour variables.  Let `I5` be
generated by all 6,558 mixed matching polynomials `F_w`, with the three pure
Hafnians fixed to one.  On a no-cap branch, for every carrier `C` choose a
blocker `i_C`, a rank `r_C`, and an `r_C` minor `m_C` of `L_C`; localize at
the product of the `m_C` and impose all `(r_C+1)` minors of
`[L_C;ell_i_C]`.

The missing **carrier-to-tail entry, lifting, and termination lemma** must
prove on every such nonempty branch that, after a site/colour transport:

1. a tail order puts all columns discarded by the frozen 12-column slice in
   the previously eliminated ideal;
2. one of the exact 126 minors is live, or a zero factor enters one of the
   four support recursions;
3. the associated-graded elimination lifts to the actual localized source
   ideal and strictly decreases a well-founded tail/support measure; and
4. termination yields an active clean cap on the same source (or, for the
   minimum-source strategy, a coefficient-preserving strict support
   reduction).

The relevant tail localizers are the remaining `6+1+1` Hafnian cofactors
and the support monomial of the selected rescue determinant.  Closing the
four support recursions proves item 2 only; it does not prove items 1, 3, or
4.

## Conditional theorem

**Conditional X5-to-cap.**  Assume the carrier-to-tail lemma above, including
same-source termination, and assume the four support-factor recursions are
closed.  Then every `X5` source has an active clean cap.

Indeed, assume no active clean cap.  The exact carrier criterion supplies a
blocker branch for each of the 728 carriers.  The entry lemma selects a tail
quotient.  The frozen 126-orbit theorem eliminates its next twelve variables;
the four-orbit hypothesis covers the only rank-boundary alternative.  The
well-founded measure permits only finitely many repetitions, and the terminal
clause produces an active cap, contradicting the chosen blocker branch.

This is a formal implication, not a proof of its entry/lifting hypothesis.
If termination is allowed to replace the source by a strictly smaller exact
source, the same argument proves the minimum-source version sufficient for
the global existence theorem, not literal same-source `X5-to-cap`.
