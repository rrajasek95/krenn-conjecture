orbits and 148,176 labelled monomials.  Its square's post-unary survivor has

```text

```

## 3. Anchor-product radical containment is impossible

The logical 31-chart cover is correct only with a pure-target factor.  The
stronger proposal that every twelve-anchor product itself lies in
`sqrt(I_mix)` is false on all 31 charts.

For a chart, choose a physical edge `e` outside its at-most-twelve selected
physical edges and put

```text
x_uv^{ab}=z_uv,   z_e=-6,   z_f=1 for f!=e.
```

Ninety perfect matchings avoid `e` and fifteen contain it, so every word
hafnian equals `90+15*(-6)=0`.  Every selected anchor is nevertheless one.
This supplies a rational common zero of the mixed ideal with anchor product
one (and all three pure hafnians zero) on every chart.  Therefore it does not
refute the valid goal

```text
a_chart^r * (H0*H1*H2)^m in I_mix.
```

The all-chart three-mode countercontrol has result SHA
`73d0157147b3628f6cd575cb1b2cbfdf6fca6cefb191c0cf0c4e357ca5944998`.

## 4. Orbit-0 anchor telescoping raises the target to K-degree 14

For the maximally symmetric orbit-0 chart, let `a` be the product of its
twelve anchors.  Choose the three mixed pair-constant words

```text

```

Their unique anchor terms `t0,t1,t2` partition `a`.  Write `Hi=ti+Ei`.
Each error has K-profile `{2:12,3:32,4:60}`.  The exact identity

```text
t0*t1*t2 + E0*E1*E2
  = H0*t1*t2 - E0*H1*t2 + E0*E1*H2
```

shows that `a*F` is congruent to `-F*E0*E1*E2` modulo the mixed ideal.
Applying this to the exact remainder `T-S`, whose minimum K-degree is eight,
proves

```text
a*T in I_mix + K^14.
```

At K-degree eight, every one of the 120 `R8'` quotient rows has three literal
unary pivots (360 exact checks).  The K14 leading residual is the factored
packet `-R8'*E0_2*E1_2*E2_2`.  The three 12-term layers have 1,728 distinct
products and 22 orbits under the order-384 factorization stabilizer; `R8'`
splits from 120 full-G orbits to 485 factor-stabilizer orbits.  There are only
10,670 orbit-pair types before double-coset splitting.

This is a much more structured leading target than the 1,578,292-orbit
factored `T^2` residual, although it begins at K14 rather than K16.  It is

## 5. Port-gauge normalization is lossless

The coloured-port torus acts by

```text
x_uv^{ab} -> lambda_(u,a)*lambda_(v,b)*x_uv^{ab}.
```

The twelve selected anchors form a perfect matching of the 24 coloured
ports.  Their value map therefore has rank twelve and an integral Laurent
section: on each anchor edge set one endpoint scale to the inverse anchor
value and the other to one.  No root extraction or compatibility equation is
required.

Every term of `H_w` uses exactly the ports `(v,w_v)`, hence

```text
H_w -> (product_v lambda_(v,w_v))*H_w.
```

The character is a Laurent unit, so all mixed-zero and pure-nonzero
conditions are preserved.  Consequently, after setting the anchors to one,
membership `T_A in J_A` in the 240-variable normalized ideal is equivalent
to target membership in the anchor localization.  Clearing Laurent
denominators and equalizing anchor exponents gives `a_A^r*T in I_mix`.

The all-31 audit verifies 688,905 word-term covariance instances per chart.
Orbit0 is legacy chart 1 and has 78 mixed generators with constant term one;
legacy chart26 has only two.  No prior artifact decides `T_A in J_A` for the
full 240-variable ideal:

- the chart26 six-column normalized contraction leaves 564 invariant / 2,240
  actual tail monomials in degrees 2--7;
- the 73-generator positive identity uses only a 60-edge restriction (48 of
  the 240 nonanchors);
- the chart25 positive identity uses only a 36-coordinate carrier; and
- the known normalized common zero has pure tuple `(0,0,1)`, so it is not a
  target-membership counterexample.

The three-mode gauge-referee result SHA is
`2bfcdf8c1993b9cc5ab5388446a711883ea93a013bd0d9cfb065305617eb9d38`.

## 6. Recommended attack order

1. Work in the normalized orbit0 ideal `J_A`, exploiting its 78 constant
   mixed generators and the K14 telescoped residual.  This is the smallest
   currently sound exponent-one chart problem.
2. Build a well-founded inhomogeneous contraction or a homogenized
   `t`-saturation certificate for the K14 packet.  A bounded ordinary-degree
   nonmembership result is not terminal because high-degree S-pairs can
   descend.
3. Determine which of the other 30 charts admit a three-mixed-factor anchor
   partition and transport the telescoping identity.  Treat exceptional
   charts through their normalized critical contractions.
4. Retain full `T^2` closure as fallback.  Its compact clean binary shortcut
   is now exactly excluded.
