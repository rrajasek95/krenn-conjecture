exact characteristic-zero obstruction to `t^6` membership in the homogeneous

## Exact reconstruction and theorem

For each branch `lift_characteristic_zero.py` reparses the original and
second-prime providers and first proves that their variables and integer
polynomials are identical.  It then:

1. takes the union of the two final modular supports;
2. enumerates every literal generator/multiplier column incident to that union;
3. solves `L(t^6)=1` and `L(column)=0` by exact `Fraction` RREF;
4. clears denominators and primitive-normalizes the result;
5. re-enumerates every column incident to the nonzero integer support and
   verifies every pairing exactly over the integers.

Every other degree-six source column is support-disjoint and hence has pairing
zero.  Thus each emitted integer functional `L` satisfies

```text
L(t^6) = 1,
L(m * g_i) = 0
```

for every literal source generator `g_i` and every monomial multiplier `m`
making total degree six.  If a rational source combination equalled `t^6`,
applying `L` would give both zero and one.  Therefore `t^6` is not in the
degree-six homogeneous source span over characteristic zero.

`validate.py` independently reparses both literal providers, reloads each
integer dual, reconstructs the complete incident-column set, checks the exact
integer pairings, and checks equality after reduction to both producer duals.
Standard and isolated Python executions agree.  Optimized Python is rejected
fail-closed because it disables assertions.  Five hostile mutations are
rejected.

## Diagnosis and next gate

There is no prime-specific rank failure and no target-pairing anomaly:

- both primes produce the same support and column trajectory sizes;
- the exact rational systems are consistent and full rank on their support;
- every primitive target coefficient is exactly `1`;
- the same integer dual reduces to both modular producer duals.

Accordingly, no further degree-six prime sampling or reconstruction is useful.
The next justified experiment is a separately bounded homogeneous degree-seven
membership CEGAR with exact rational acceptance.  This package neither designs
nor launches that search, and it does not read or modify any active D12 cache.
