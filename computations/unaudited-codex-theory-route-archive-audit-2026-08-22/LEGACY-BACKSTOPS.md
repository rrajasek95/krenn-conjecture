# Legacy and backstop route audit

Status: archive-only audit, 2026-08-22. No solve was launched and no source
note was edited. This supplements [the conceptual route audit](REPORT.md) by
classifying the older named routes in the chronological
[route registry](../../notes/route-registry.md) and
[parallel attack board](../../notes/parallel-proof-attack-board.md).

The authority order used below is the repository's own: accepted
[baseline/supersession records](../../certification/BASELINE.md) first;
independently audited exact notes next; then checked but uncertified route
notes; and finally the later `unaudited-codex-*` reports. In particular, the
later X5, minimum-norm, and tail reports sharpen scope and task allocation,
but do not silently promote an old local lemma into the certified spine.

## Supersession summary

The families split cleanly into four buckets.

1. **Current only as certified inputs:** the square-zero source calculus as
   consumed by `SP-DESCENT`, and the `S1` slice-cover lemma as consumed by
   `SP-K6`.
2. **Absorbed outputs:** U1/S1/E1/E2/pair-fan/multi-pair mechanisms whose old
   purpose was to select a physical line or define a descent. Certified
   `SP-CURVATURE` and `SP-DESCENT` already do those jobs.
3. **Retired restricted routes:** C1, B1, and D1 as standalone programmes;
   the regular-fan construction of a zero shore; and bare one-/two-/three-cut
   OC1 implications.
4. **Genuinely unresolved backstops:** OC1 only after adding a fourth cut and
   full mixed coupling; K4C/PF1/T2 only after an arbitrary-source entry
   theorem; the E1/E2 physical overlap complex; conditional zero-shore
   common-power algebra; and multi-pair saturated incidence.

No later X5, minimum-norm, or tail result closes any item in bucket 4. X5 is
the correct complete `N=8` coefficient scope, minimum norm supplies exact
block normality/isotropy, and the tail theorem supplies a strong 380-row
rank packet. Their common remaining gap is source-faithful transport to an
active clean cap: the [tail bridge](../unaudited-codex-x5-tail-cap-bridge-2026-08-21/REPORT.md)
still lacks entry/lift/termination, while the older backstops lack a theorem
placing an arbitrary X5 source in their special chart.

## Name and scope traps

- `zero-shore` and `zero-tail` are not renamed copies; they impose different
  vanishing objects.
- A second “full nine” pair system is not extra information: it is an
  exchange chart on the same top tensor. Only the pre-power source overlap
  identities are new structure.
- `X4-to-cap` is stale terminology for the frozen 380-row tail packet: 360
  rows have off-count five, so its literal consumer is X5.
- The 728 star/triangle blockers are a sound finite sufficient-support
  surrogate, not the definition of absence of every projective clean cap.
- “Audited local theorem” does not mean “certified proof-spine dependency.”
