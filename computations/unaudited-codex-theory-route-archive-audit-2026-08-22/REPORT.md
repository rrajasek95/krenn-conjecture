# Archive audit of conceptual/theory routes

Status: archive-only audit, 2026-08-22.  No solve was launched and no prior
note or computation was edited.  This ledger groups mechanisms rather than
filenames: many apparent “new ideas” in the archive are renamed instances of
the same functor or loss of source provenance.

The scope is the remaining `N=8,d=3` implication from the exact GHZ fibre to
an active clean cap/strict descent.  The standing hostile fact is that GHZ
is a border point of the matching image, so a top-output polynomial,
flattening, or regular rational invariant cannot separate exact membership
from border membership.  See the [existing-theory survey](../unaudited-codex-existing-theory-survey-2026-08-21/REPORT.md)
and [proof-spine review](../unaudited-codex-proof-spine-review-2026-08-21/REPORT.md).

## 1. Invariants, representation theory, apolarity, Pfaffians and Wick

### Squarefree/Wick/apolar matching algebra

The literal source-faithful positive piece is

`q^4/4! = H_8(A)`

in the site-square-zero algebra.  Its Hilbert sectors and the scalar matching
apolar algebra are known exactly; Numata’s strong-Lefschetz structure is
recovered.  The target GHZ apolar quotient itself has maximal multiplication
ranks and nonzero higher Hessians.  First lower multiplication has rank 24 on
cap-active, all-blocked, and remote controls.

Decisive counterguard: differentiating a universal apolar identity is not a
pointwise fibre equation.  Top Wick/cumulant/catalecticant data are also
blocked by the Laurent boundary.  The only untested version is a **relative,
source-labelled identity for `(q,q^2,q^3,q^4)` coupled to a carrier block and
the full mixed equations**.  Sources:
[global Wick/apolar survey](../unaudited-codex-global-wick-apolar-survey-2026-08-21/REPORT.md),
[zeon hierarchy](../unaudited-codex-zeon-contraction-hierarchy-2026-08-21/REPORT.md),
[bosonic Gaussian annihilator](../unaudited-codex-bosonic-gaussian-annihilator-2026-08-21/REPORT.md).

### Three-copy alternating invariant

Exact positive pieces: the invariant equals 6 on GHZ; it has a
vertex-determinant expansion; a nonzero term supplies a rainbow triple whose
local vectors are bases and whose repeated edges have sufficient rank.  The
31 `S8 x S3` matching geometries and their first two-cell completion shell
are fully enumerated.

Counterguards: all 31 geometries admit rank-respecting witnesses; the global
Hadamard/nuclear/Frobenius inequalities are strict even at the exact `n=4`
global GHZ-fibre minimum.  A dense rank-respecting source destroys every
support-only singleton potential.  Untested: a **coefficient-valued matching
exchange complex/local system**, not another support invariant.  Sources:
[alternating invariant norm](../unaudited-codex-alternating-invariant-norm-2026-08-22/REPORT.md),
[31-orbit audit](../unaudited-codex-three-copy-31-rank-audit-2026-08-22/REPORT.md),
[exchange complex](../unaudited-codex-three-copy-exchange-complex-2026-08-22/REPORT.md).

### Pfaffian/Kasteleyn/matchgate/Wick identities

Exact positive piece: the genus-two embedding gives the 105-term hafnian as
an exact signed sum of 16 Pfaffians; principal deletion and Arf twists are
tracked source-faithfully.

Counterguard: Plücker/Jacobi/Buchsbaum--Eisenbud rows are either ordinary
four-site Laplace identities, leave the squarefree physical grade, or cancel
identically after the unique two-step repair.  Nontrivial deletion twists do
not descend through the same Arf aggregate.  Untested: a genuinely new
target-augmented cross-word attaching map resolving both grade and Arf
character.  Sources: [genus-two terminal audit](../unaudited-codex-genus2-pfaffian-terminal-2026-08-21/REPORT.md),
[Q/matchgate referee](../unaudited-codex-Q-matchgate-referee-2026-08-21/REPORT.md).

### Brauer, partition algebra, characters and equivariant syzygies

Exact positive piece: the perfect-matching permutation module is the
multiplicity-free sum `[8]+[6,2]+[4,4]+[4,2,2]+[2^4]`; all relevant row
modules and stabilizer closures have exact character decompositions.

Counterguard: amplitude rows occupy only the invariant matching line.  Other
idempotents annihilate them and encode unconstrained monomial differences,
while full symmetry closure of the 270-row master assumes the missing 1,638
rows.  Degree-two equivariant Macaulay compression still has prohibitive
target blocks and no new low-isotypic identity.  Untested: only a nonlinear,
source-labelled response/reinsertion map.  Sources:
[Brauer screen](../unaudited-codex-brauer-partition-screen-2026-08-21/REPORT.md),
[equivariant syzygy compression](../unaudited-codex-equivariant-syzygy-compression-2026-08-21/REPORT.md).

### Phase hyperfields, delta-matroids and characteristic two

The characteristic-two/delta-matroid shadow and several integral product
formulas are real positive structure.  They do not lift through complex
105-term phase cancellations: the phase-hyperfield Wick axioms already fail
on lower-arity controls.  Any lift needs coefficient phases, not support
orthogonality.  Sources: [phase-hyperfield screen](../unaudited-codex-phase-hyperfield-wick-screen-2026-08-21/REPORT.md),
[characteristic-two audit](../unaudited-codex-char2-product-formula-audit-2026-08-21/REPORT.md).

## 2. Tensor networks, PEPS, MPS, connection matrices and Fourier

### One-hot PEPS/Holant and virtual symmetry

Exact positives: the fixed one-hot site projector is exact; Fourier exposes a
local `Z3` covariance; blocked maps have exact rank `3^r`.

Counterguards: no proper `K4` or `K8` block is normal/injective or
`G`-injective, and the target symmetry does not imply edge pull-through.
The invisible chord changes internal data while preserving the complete top
tensor.  Untested: no standard PEPS theorem remains; a new source-relative
kernel-lifting theorem would be required.  Sources:
[one-hot PEPS/Holant](../unaudited-codex-onehot-peps-holant-2026-08-21/REPORT.md),
[Fourier-Z3 PEPS](../unaudited-codex-fourier-z3-peps-2026-08-21/REPORT.md).

### Minimal MPS / weighted automata / Hankel connection matrices

Exact positive: reachable/observable minimization gives the three-state GHZ
copying automaton and exact cut ranks.

Counterguard: minimization forgets literal matching edges, arbitrary Laurent
valuations, and reachable-unobservable states.  Minimum norm deletes the
single inactive invisible chord but not general Hankel radicals; the exact
`n=4` global minimum already contains one with all star/triangle blocks
injective.  Untested: a **source-relative radical-to-intersecting-block
lifting lemma**.  Sources: [global minimal MPS](../unaudited-codex-global-minimal-mps-2026-08-21/REPORT.md),
[graph connection/Hankel audit](../unaudited-codex-graph-connection-hankel-audit-2026-08-21/REPORT.md).

### Fourier, charge conservation and uncertainty

Exact positives: sitewise Fourier transforms the source edgewise and the
output tensor exactly; GHZ becomes the sum-zero indicator and saturates
Donoho--Stark/Hausdorff--Young.  Projective charge lifting is explicitly
understood.

Counterguards: normalized Fourier is an isometric bijection of source fibres,
preserving minimum norm, isotropy and cap existence.  The exact `n=4` global
minimum has six dense rank-one Fourier edges and violates edgewise character
conservation/common-matching conclusions.  In fact an exact Fourier-GHZ
source cannot be made edgewise charge homogeneous by vertex scalars.  This is
the same obstruction as the PEPS pull-through failure, not a distinct route.
Untested: only a new source-relative inequality coupling individual matching
summands.  Sources: [Fourier uncertainty](../unaudited-codex-fourier-uncertainty-2026-08-22/REPORT.md),
[Fourier root lifting note](../../notes/fourier-root-symmetry-lifting.md).

## 3. GIT, torus, Kempf--Ness, norm, ED/conormal and Jacobians

### Torus/Hilbert--Mumford/tensor scaling

Exact positives: the site-colour character lattice, balanced supports,
one-parameter degenerations, and moment equations are explicit.  Minimizing
over the moving GHZ orbit gives full port isotropy.

Counterguards: pure normalization plus balance is noncompact; support faces
and moment polytopes do not encode phase cancellations.  Tensor
scaling/capacity and geometric Brascamp--Lieb reduce to the same moment data.
The phased Fourier/anchor source is fully isotropic without GHZ output.
Untested: none at moment-map level; a theorem must add full mixed coefficient
equations.  Sources: [Kempf--Ness audit](../unaudited-codex-t0-kempf-ness-2026-08-21/REPORT.md),
[X5 torus/HM audit](../unaudited-codex-x5-torus-hm-audit-2026-08-21/REPORT.md),
[minimum-preimage literature survey](../unaudited-codex-minimum-preimage-literature-2026-08-22/REPORT.md).

### Minimum norm and block normality

This is a genuine positive reduction, not retired: a hypothetical exact fibre
has an attained global minimum; every intersecting edge block (stars and
triangles) is the least-norm solution of its exact affine system.  Moving the
target orbit gives full isotropy.

Its limits are exact.  The `n=4` minimum and phased `n=6` local minimum have
injective star/triangle maps, so block normality is vacuous there.  Least-star
trace/SOS, star-dual overlap and one-site CP uniqueness all fail to locate a
common edge.  The live hypothesis is **minimum norm plus all mixed GHZ rows
implies a source-labelled cap/descent**; no archived theorem proves it.
Sources: [minimal-norm gauge note](../../notes/minimal-norm-gauge.md),
[star-dual overlap](../unaudited-codex-star-dual-overlap-2026-08-22/REPORT.md),
[star CP slice](../unaudited-codex-star-cp-slice-2026-08-22/REPORT.md),
[Hermitian star/SOS](../unaudited-codex-hermitian-star-sos-2026-08-22/REPORT.md).

### ED correspondence, conormality and Jacobian rank

Exact positives: at a smooth minimum, `A=D Phi_A^* lambda`; exact blockwise
versions remain valid without smoothness.  The GHZ stabilizer supplies a
21-dimensional tangent and the candidate global contradiction is the sharp
bound `rank(dPhi)-stab <=231`.

Counterguards: bare ED/conormality is machinery, not a cap theorem; regular
KKT is automatic at full rank and Fritz--John has a huge vacuous kernel.
Carrier response matrices are not Jacobian blocks, and blocker covectors are
not global conormals.  Untested: a **word-degree >=3 global conormal, joint
X5 algebraic-matroid/Jacobian theorem, or the singular normal cone of the
exact GHZ fibre**.  Sources: [ED/conormal framework](../unaudited-codex-ed-conormal-gap-framework-2026-08-21/REPORT.md),
[existing-theory Jacobian section](../unaudited-codex-existing-theory-survey-2026-08-21/REPORT.md).

### Doubled norm, Gram/SOS and association-scheme SDP

Exact positives include the diagonal matching bound and several exact
balanced algebraic families.  Counterguards are decisive: `P_mixed>=2` is
false even on exact balanced no-carrier families; elementary negative Hessian
directions integrate.  Five matching-scheme sector variables admit a PSD GHZ
witness and impose no useful isotropy relation.  Untested: at most a new
coefficient-sensitive fibre-relative inequality; Bessel, Cauchy, ordinary
SOS and invariant-sector SDP are exhausted.  Sources:
[doubled norm/Gram screen](../unaudited-codex-doubled-norm-gram-screen-2026-08-21/REPORT.md),
[sharp mixed-norm bound](../unaudited-codex-sharp-mixed-norm-bound-2026-08-21/REPORT.md).

### Rees graph, blow-up and boundary arcs

Exact positive: the matching map’s graph/Rees viewpoint identifies GHZ as a
boundary point over the base locus; explicit Laurent valuations and leading
outputs are frozen.  Counterguard: valuation types are not finitely many
symmetry orbits, so a finite tropical-orbit classification is false.
Untested: a source-labelled exceptional-fibre theorem with more structure
than valuation support.  Source: [GHZ Rees boundary](../unaudited-codex-ghz-rees-boundary-2026-08-21/REPORT.md).

### Projective cap-error algebra

Exact positive: the cap question for a fixed pair is a homogeneous ideal in
`P^8`, with activity separated by saturation.  Counterguard: phased `n=6`
is basepoint-free on the active open despite isotropy/local minimality; W25
has only three independent cubic sections but its base locus lies entirely
on the activity divisor.  Chern/degree/span counting alone has no leverage.
Source: [cap-error projective audit](../unaudited-codex-cap-error-projective-2026-08-22/REPORT.md).

## 4. Rigidity, matroids, secants and flattenings

### Carrier/tail matroids and rigidity stresses

Exact positives: response rows define honest linear matroids; blocker
membership is equivalent to fundamental circuits/stresses; the tail matrix
has exact rank and stress space.

Counterguard: a dense rational source has every carrier circuit while no cap
conclusion follows, and initial tail rigidity does not control nonlinear
remote points.  Separate carrier/tail matroids discard the shared polynomial
coupling.  Untested: a **joint X5 incidence algebraic matroid or source
identity carrying tail Fitting minors to carrier augmented minors**.  Source:
[carrier matroid/stress](../unaudited-codex-carrier-matroid-stress-2026-08-21/REPORT.md).

### CP/Kruskal/secant/slice rank

Exact positive: the GHZ target has rank-three CP structure and the one-site
star decomposition is explicit.  Counterguard: at `n=4` the three channels
route through three different neighbours, so CP uniqueness does not recover
a common physical edge.  Generic Segre/secant statements forget matching
provenance.  Genuinely untested: a **two-site or matching-compatible secant
circuit theorem stable under perfect-matching deletion/exchange**.  Source:
[star CP slice](../unaudited-codex-star-cp-slice-2026-08-22/REPORT.md).

### Flattenings and connection ranks

Top-output flattenings are fixed by GHZ and cannot distinguish exact from
border realizability.  Boolean three-cut and matching-Hankel ranks give useful
diagnostics but have explicit flattening-multiplicity/invisible-state
countermodels.  Untested only when augmented with literal source labels;
plain rank/subrank is retired.  Sources:
[Boolean three-cut](../../notes/boolean-three-cut-flattening.md),
[flattening countermodel](../../notes/flattening-multiplicity-countermodel.md).

## 5. Renamed or overlapping routes

The following names should be treated as duplicates unless a new
source-labelled hypothesis is stated:

1. **Wick / zeon / squarefree Artinian / bosonic Gaussian / apolar moment
   tower** — the same `q,q^2,q^3,q^4` algebra at different presentations.
2. **Brauer / partition algebra / matching association scheme / equivariant
   syzygy compression** — projections of the same perfect-matching
   permutation module.
3. **PEPS virtual `Z3` / Fourier pull-through / Fourier root symmetry /
   Hausdorff--Young uncertainty** — the same sitewise Fourier covariance;
   output symmetry does not lift edgewise.
4. **Minimal MPS / weighted automaton / Hankel / connection matrix** — the
   same reachable-observable quotient and the same loss of literal edges.
5. **GIT / Kempf--Ness / tensor scaling / capacity / moment polytope /
   Brascamp--Lieb normalization** — the same port-moment equations unless
   full mixed rows are added.
6. **Minimum norm / ED criticality / conormal / singular-vector tuple** —
   related stationarity formalisms; only exact intersecting-block normality
   survives singular fibres unconditionally.
7. **Carrier rigidity / response matroid / fundamental stress / linear tail
   stiffness** — the same first linear incidence data without joint source
   coupling.
8. **CP uniqueness / Segre slice / secant / ordinary flattening** — target
   decomposition data that do not by themselves preserve physical matching
   provenance.
9. **Alternating-rainbow support / fourth-matching switch / support exchange
   potential** — one invariant route; the dense rank-respecting counterguard
   kills every support-only all-shell version.

## 6. Deduplicated genuinely untested hypotheses

These are the only broad conceptual hypotheses found without a decisive
archived counterguard:

1. A coefficient-valued matching exchange/gain local system with a finite
   circuit-basis theorem, extending the exact gain CEGAR mechanism.
2. A source-labelled relative `(q,q^2,q^3,q^4)` apolar/HPL identity coupled
   simultaneously to X5 rows and one carrier.
3. A joint X5 carrier-tail algebraic matroid/Jacobian theorem, or a
   word-degree-at-least-three global conormal.
4. A matching-compatible two-site secant/circuit theorem forcing a shared
   edge, tight cut or clean selector.
5. The singular normal cone/local ideal of the full exact GHZ fibre at its
   attained minimum, beyond regular KKT and associated-graded tail ranks.
6. A degree-controlled global six-site Nullstellensatz certificate whose
   localizer powers give a quantitative inequality compatible with the
   block-normal equations.
7. A source-labelled exceptional-fibre/Rees theorem controlling leading
   coefficients, not just valuations or support faces.

The [fresh-idea survey](../unaudited-codex-fresh-idea-survey-2026-08-22/REPORT.md)
independently ranks the first five in essentially this order.  Everything
else found in the requested four theory families is either an exact positive
lemma already absorbed into the proof spine, a renamed duplicate above, or a
route with a literal hostile control.
