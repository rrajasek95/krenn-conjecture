## Headline

For an ordered pure-matching chart, retain its twelve selected pure cells as
named, symbolic, nonzero anchors and let `K_anchor` be the ideal generated by
the other 240 endpoint cells.  Exact source-labelled certificates prove on
each of the 27 zero-based charts outside `25,26,27,28` that

```text
H0 H1 H2 belongs to I_mix + K_anchor^3,
H0 H1 H2 belongs to I_mix + K_anchor^4.                 (1)
```

All 54 identities replay literally over `Q`; in fact every coefficient is
integral.  Modular ranks were used only during discovery and are not part of
the proof.  At both cutoffs the complete truncated component contracts by
iterated literal one-output source columns, leaving no irreducible core.

Thus the first-failure clusters through this range are

```text
first failed at K^3:       0 charts
first failed at K^4:       0 charts
no failure through K^4:   27 charts.
```

There is no exact obstruction representative in degrees below four.

## 1. Raw charts and stable numbering

The checker reads only the ordered matching triples from the pinned raw

canonical keys.  The scanned charts split as follows:

* 24 first-layer leading-contractible types: legacy charts `1` through `24`;
* three first-layer critical types: zero-based `24,29,30`, which are legacy

* four excluded all-disjoint types: zero-based `25,26,27,28`, which are

This agrees with the exact rank-24 acyclic first-layer contraction in

## 2. Literal source model

There are 252 endpoint-cell variables.  A row is a degree-twelve monomial,
stored as its twelve coordinate IDs.  For every nonpure word `w` and every
degree-eight multiplier `m`, a source column is the literal polynomial

```text
m H_w = sum over the 105 vertex perfect matchings of m times term(w,M).
```

The `K_anchor` degree of a row is the number of its factors outside the
twelve anchors.  For cutoff `d`, the checker starts with the complete
degree-`<d` expansion of `H0 H1 H2` and closes every incident mixed source
column and every degree-`<d` output until no row or column remains unseen.
This is the complete connected source component, not a selected packet.

No anchor is assigned the value one.  Multipliers and coefficients retain
the anchor names, so gauge-product relations are never assumed.

## 3. Exact contraction and certificates

A column with exactly one active output is an integral unit pivot.  Removing
its row may expose another unit column.  The checker iterates this operation,
then, if a core remains, selects a finite exact minor over `Q`, solves it,
and cancels the peeled rows in reverse order.  Finally it expands every
chosen `m H_w` and compares the complete truncated row counter with the raw
target counter.  A coefficient-sign mutation must fail.

For all 27 charts the `K^3` and `K^4` cores peel completely, so their exact
certificates do not use the discovery prime at all.  The certificate sizes

Every denominator is one.  Full chartwise ledgers, words, multipliers, and
coefficients are in `results.json`.

## 5. Reconciliation with the old legacy-chart25 dual

The apparent contradiction with
`notes/n8-chart25-degree4-exact-four-row-dual.md` is purely a filtration
scope mismatch, augmented by a lower-Schur typing issue.

The raw representatives are isomorphic but not byte-identical.  The exact
new-to-old relabeling is

```text

colors (1,2,0),
```

whose inverse is `(0,3,1,2,4,7,5,6)` and `(2,0,1)`.  After transport, the
old support is exactly the new twelve-anchor set.  But the old filtration
retains 36 coordinates: those twelve anchors plus 24 carrier extras.  Hence

```text
K_old    = ideal outside 36 coordinates,
K_anchor = ideal outside 12 coordinates,
K_old is strictly contained in K_anchor.
```

The old four quotient rows regrade exactly as

```text
K_old degrees:       (2,2,2,4)
K_anchor degrees:    (5,6,6,6).
```

Their twenty actual-row lifts have histograms `{2:16,4:4}` for `K_old` and
`{5:8,6:12}` for `K_anchor`.  Thus the old cochain is identically zero below
anchor degree five and cannot obstruct the new `K_anchor^5` identity.

An independent raw matching engine replays all 2,222 terms of the new
legacy-chart25 certificate.  Pairing the *full*, untruncated columns against
the old cochain finds four certificate columns of minimum `K_old` degree zero;
their weighted pairing is `-3/4`, whereas the truncated `K_anchor^5` pairing
is zero.  Exhausting the transported cochain's support gives:

| anchor cutoff | retained cochain rows | incident columns | nonzero pairings | target pairing |

| 5 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

At cutoff seven all 52 violations have minimum `K_old` degree zero.  The old
four-row object therefore must **not** be advertised as a `K_anchor^7` dual:

## 6. What this does and does not prove

Equation (1) and the five `K^5` probe identities are exact finite polynomial
memberships, and remain valid after anchor localization.  They do not imply
membership in the completed or saturated localized ideal, do not control an
infinite degree-raising tail, and do not close any chart.  In particular:

* the 24 leading charts still rely on the separate global first-layer
  contraction;
* the formal lifts on legacy charts 25--27 do not by themselves repair the
  global Morse complex;
* the first possible anchor-filtration interaction with the old chart25
  cochain occurs in degrees five and six, but requires its full Schur lift.
