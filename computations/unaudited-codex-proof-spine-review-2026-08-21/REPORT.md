### How the solved six-site proof actually scales

The formal `(6,3)` proof is not a support-only SAT argument.  Its SAT layer
is repeatedly strengthened by exact local algebraic no-goods: monomial
units, signed Laurent identities, and small rational target-product
certificates.  The Boolean solver only assembles those coefficient-level
lemmas into an exhaustive cover.

The corresponding eight-site architecture should therefore be hybrid:

1. use the 31 pure-matching-triple orbits only as the finite outer cover;
2. add exact no-goods coming from response-carrier caps, chart unit ideals,
   and arbitrary-mate certificates;
3. ask SAT for a surviving support only after each no-good is proved as a
   literal source identity on its antecedent support;
4. feed each survivor back to algebra, as in the six-site certificate, until
   the branch is closed or a genuine coefficient component is exposed.

The already-run base CNF establishes only that step 1 is insufficient.  It
does not refute this lazy algebra/SAT loop.  In particular, the universal
mate certificates now being extracted from positive-dimensional chart
components are precisely the sort of high-coverage local clauses the
six-site proof used.

## Existing-theory compression: four-qubit invariant theory

The sixteen transversal values should be treated as a binary quadrilinear
form, not merely as sixteen unrelated support coordinates.  If the six
same-colour edge blocks are `M_ij`, then literally

`Q_ijkl = M_01[i,j] M_23[k,l] + M_02[i,k] M_13[j,l]
          + M_03[i,l] M_12[j,k]`.

Thus `Q` is a `2 x 2 x 2 x 2` tensor.  The classical invariant algebra for
such a tensor under `SL(2)^4` is freely generated in degrees `2,4,4,6` by
the Luque--Thibon invariants `H,L,M,D` (with the third flattening determinant
`N` linearly dependent on `L,M`).  This is the first pre-existing framework
that has produced a nontrivial exact match with our computations:

- the exact support-6 point has quadratic invariant `H_Q=4` and flattening
  determinants `(-32,-32,0)` in the current index convention;
- the whole support-8 family has the same values, despite having a different
  coordinate support;
- the two positive-dimensional branch-1 lines again have `H_Q=4` and a
  permutation/sign-convention version of `(32,0,-32)`, independent of their
  parameter `T`.

This explains why support size and the chart parameter fail to distinguish
the recurring components: they move inside one invariant fibre (or fibres
identified by qubit permutation).  It does **not** yet prove that the points
are in one `SL(2)^4` orbit; equality of invariant values only identifies
closed-orbit data in the GIT quotient.  The degree-6 invariant and an
explicit local transformation/stabilizer calculation are the next tests.

The nearby matchgate/pure-spinor theory does not apply directly.  The
support-6 tensor is parity-even but violates the arity-4
Grassmann--Pluecker/Cartan quadric by `4`; the support-8 tensor has mixed
parity.  Moreover, the unrestricted six-block-to-`Q` map has an exact
rank-16 Jacobian minor, so there is no universal matchgate equation on `Q`.
Matchgate identities are useful here as an exclusion test, not as the proof
spine.

If the full invariant tuple and orbit calculation succeeds, the replacement
for much of the chart enumeration is:

1. classify constrained one-colour `Q` tensors by the finite list of
   Luque--Thibon invariant fibres and Verstraete normal forms;
2. express a right colour as a local `SL(2)^4` transform of the appropriate
   normal form;
3. solve the sixteen coordinatewise complementary-product equations in the
   local-transformation parameters;
4. pull the entry/cofactor packet back as four-qubit covariants and exclude
   the surviving transformation components.

The important covariance guard is that the original permanent/triangle and
coordinatewise cross-colour equations are not known to be invariant under
arbitrary local `SL(2)^4`.  A normal-form reduction is sound only after the
entry/cofactor data are transported as covariants, or after an explicit
source-level lift of the local transformation is proved.

### 6. Universal mate-incidence elimination

There is a second way to avoid complete one-colour component classification.

- both directional entry/cofactor products;
