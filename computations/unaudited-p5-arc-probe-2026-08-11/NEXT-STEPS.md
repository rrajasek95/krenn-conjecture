## What the prototypes established (exactly, over Q, at two points)

1. `W4, W5, W6` hold — the three-step recurrence fixes `r4, r5, r6` exactly.
   (`W6` is new; the repo had only `W4, W5`.)
2. `W7` **fails**.  The conjectured transfer polynomial
   `(1+z0 T)(1+z30 T)(1+z52 T)` is not the all-order law.
3. Principality **holds** anyway: solving `M30` for the newest bend kills all
   38 other mixed rows and both pure rows `H0, H1` at every tested order.
4. The raw linearized response already deviates at relative order 4
   (`c_4 != C*u*e1`); `W4/W5/W6` survive only modulo the centre ideal.  So no
   time-homogeneous `(A,B,C)` Markov sequence has the conjectured cubic
   transfer — route A as stated is measuring the wrong object.

## The computation that should now be done symbolically

Replace the transfer/Cayley–Hamilton target by the **all-order colon
stabilization** target, which the point data says is the true phenomenon:

> For every `n >= 7`, the order-`n` compatibility row `Q_n^{M30}` is monic in
> the newest bend `r_{n-3}` with the fixed localized unit
> `C*u = (1/2) z11 z16^2 z41 (z26+z45)`, and every other compatibility row
> `Q_n^{M_i}`, `Q_n^{H0}`, `Q_n^{H1}` lies in the ideal generated by
> `L, F1, F2, G, Q_7^{M30}, ..., Q_n^{M30}`.

Monicity is already an all-order theorem
(`notes/n8-p5-newest-bend-uniform-coefficient.md`).  The open half is the
membership, and the right finite certificate is the affine-bend Wronskian of
`notes/affine-bend-wronskian-principalization-criterion.md`:

```
Omega_i = (d/dr_{n-3}) Q_n^{M30} * Q_n^{M_i}
        - (d/dr_{n-3}) Q_n^{M_i}  * Q_n^{M30}      (bend-free)
```

Because the newest-bend coefficient of `M30` is the *same* unit at every
order, `Omega_i` is a single bend-free expression, not one per order.  The
finite task is to show `Omega_i` lies in the graph/centre ideal.  The
prototypes above give the exact numeric oracle to test any candidate
identity at zero cost before spending a symbolic run.
