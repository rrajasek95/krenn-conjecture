# Sharp boundary of the current X5 support-cap induction

Status: **the existing support-only certificate calculus closes exactly the zero-through-six added-block layers, but it does not induce over all layers.** Its first minimal stuck state occurs at seven added blocks. This is a counter-obligation to the present induction strategy, not a counterexample to the conjecture.

## The valid decreasing argument

For an added support `S`, let `mu(S)=|S|`. The formal cap-`67` guard gives a unit-minor deletion whenever a nonzero outside block `A_r6` or `A_r7`, `r in {3,4,5}`, lacks its complete switched partner pattern. Each such deletion is an exact coordinate-zero conclusion and strictly lowers `mu`; deleting a block can only remove further switched products, so iteration terminates and the resulting lower-support residual is literal, not sampled or quotient-derived.

This gives a sound induction step whenever a deletion exists. Terminal branches are supplied by a supported-carrier triangle/star response graph or, for a nonidentity cap, a response kernel on which the three diagonal activities and the carrier pairing are all nonzero functionals. The sealed chain proves that these reductions and terminal certificates cover every support with at most six additions.

What fails is completeness of the rule set. A decreasing measure cannot help at a state with neither a deletion nor a terminal certificate.

## First minimal stuck support

Take the canonical full-family support

```text
S* = {06,13,17,24,26,56,57},
variable cycle = {04,12,35,67} all nonzero.
```

The fixed-cap loads in the order `03,16,27,45` are `(2,5,4,3)`. At outside site `5`, the four edges `17,26,56,57` form the complete switched rectangle, so the formal guard forces no block to zero and `mu` cannot decrease.

For cap `67`, the source-neighbour criterion gives exactly

```text
H_67 = {01,02,05,12,15,25} = K4 on {0,1,2,5}.
```

Thus the response support fits neither a triangle nor a star. The pinned seven-block boundary record further marks every supported carrier as failing the triangle/star test. The recorded `K4` support certificate does not establish `rank(L_C)<=8`, so it does not supply the nonzero kernel needed by the hyperplane-avoidance argument. Hence none of the existing certificate rules applies to `S*`.

Its image under `(1 2)(6 7)` is the second member of the exact orbit. Altogether the full-family boundary has six such orbits, twelve supports. Minimality is rigorous relative to this calculus: the sealed manifest chain closes every support of size at most six.

## Exact limitation and next lemma

The result does **not** show that `S*` contains an actual inactive common zero or that no coefficient-dependent cap exists. The precise missing statement is the complete-switched-rectangle `K4` response-minor lemma: on each of the six representatives, the source/residual/guard equations must either force a nominally nonzero block to zero, or produce a carrier `C` with `rank(L_C)<=8` such that `K00`, `K11`, `K22`, and `<K,A_C>` all restrict nontrivially to `ker(L_C)`.

That lemma would close the first stuck layer: the forced-zero branch descends to the sealed six-layer theorem, while the kernel branch is closed over `Q` by avoiding four proper hyperplanes. No theorem currently pinned in the support-cap chain proves this coefficient-level dichotomy, so extrapolating the six-layer pattern would be circular.

The machine-readable result records the six representatives, the exact census, the rule calculus, and the nonpromotion scope. All five sealed manifests from two through six blocks replayed successfully. The seven-block result is pinned as boundary evidence but had no standalone manifest; this referee verifies only its recorded combinatorial/certificate interface and does not promote a full seven-block theorem.
