The open issue is exact cancellation at arbitrary size. Failure of one six-site construction is only an example. A general impossibility proof must exclude every allowed graph and every assignment of complex weights.

These examples use same-color endpoints and small integer weights. The conjecture allows bicolored edges and complex weights too. The cancellation example is one scalar matching sum, not a GHZ solution or a conjecture counterexample. All enumeration here is exact for the displayed integers.
