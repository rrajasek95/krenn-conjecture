# All-order status and the remaining closure theorem

This is an editorial synthesis of independently reviewed research, not
a new proof or a formal-registry promotion. The general complex ordinary
ternary Krenn–Gu conjecture remains OPEN. The current internally reviewed
general frontier is even n>=12; the allowed four-site case remains.

The main completed reduction is general in n. What remains is a uniform
nonexistence theorem for the resulting weighted system, rather than an
unchecked final calculation in an otherwise complete proof.

## What is resolved at arbitrary order

1. **Exact diagonal reduction.** Every full ternary source is diagonal
   in the original target bases. Its three actual scalar color matrices
   obey M_h C_h=tau_h I, where C_h consists of actual deleted-pair
   hafnians and tau_h!=0. This is neither an assigned inverse nor a
   restriction to positive or support-valid graphs.
   [Source and complete audit](../unaudited-codex-higher-common-power-bridge-2026-09-05/UNIFORM-GLOBAL-EDGE-MONOCHROMATICITY-AND-COFACTOR-INVERSE.md).

2. **One matching color is enough for a contradiction.** At every even
   n>=6, no full ternary source can have even one color consisting of
   a perfect matching. The proof uses whole mixed cubic responses to
   force the other colors into matchings. Thus forcing one matching
   color would now close the conjecture.
   [All-order theorem](../unaudited-codex-higher-common-power-bridge-2026-09-05/UNIFORM-ONE-MATCHING-COLOR-EXCLUSION.md).

3. **Strong universal structural restrictions.** At forbidden orders
   n>=6, every color must have a nonmatching component. Those components
   have minimum degree at least three and are 2-vertex-connected; every
   color has a non-edge component with an internal zero actual cofactor.
   Color degrees are one or at least three, with upper bound n/2-2. Every
   pair-color union is 2-vertex-connected. These statements constrain
   every remaining source, without a dense-family ansatz.
   [Current reviewed synthesis](../unaudited-codex-higher-common-power-bridge-2026-09-05/CURRENT-UNIFORM-FRONTIER.md).

4. **Exact two-vertex-cut structure.** A pair-color two-cut leaves exactly
   two pieces of the same parity. The actual inverse equations force
   both participating colors to split into separate even blocks, in
   the specified opposite pattern. Consequently a pair containing a
   connected spanning color is 3-vertex-connected. This does not rule
   out all two-cuts when both participating colors are disconnected.
   [Classification](PAIR-COLOR-TWO-VERTEX-CUT-PARITY-AND-EVEN-INTERFACE-SPLIT-NEW-SOURCE.md),
   [odd decoupling](SCALAR-ODD-TWO-TERMINAL-DECOUPLING-AND-CONNECTED-COLOR-PAIR-CONNECTIVITY-NEW-SOURCE.md),
   [classification acceptance](ROOT-MATH-ACCEPTANCE-PAIR-COLOR-TWO-VERTEX-CUT-CLASSIFICATION.json),
   [decoupling acceptance](ROOT-MATH-ACCEPTANCE-ODD-TWO-TERMINAL-DECOUPLING.json).

   Both parity cases now also have explicit smaller binary constructions
   preserving the whole two-target top and all four whole cubic identities.
   Odd pieces use one new boundary vertex; even pieces use a specified
   unit edge. These are proved coefficient factorizations, not presumed
   inheritance under deletion. They do not preserve a third active target.
   [Odd lifts](ODD-SEPARATOR-BINARY-LIFTS-INHERIT-WHOLE-CUBIC-RESPONSES-NEW-SOURCE.md),
   [even lifts](EVEN-SEPARATOR-VIRTUAL-EDGE-BINARY-LIFTS-AND-CUBIC-INHERITANCE-NEW-SOURCE.md).

5. **Original-core and pair rigidity.** Original deleted-root row maps
   are injective; their diagonal preimages are exactly the original
   row span. Every whole original deleted-pair tensor is nonzero, and
   its single-pure-target case is exactly an existing mutual anchor.
   Same-color exterior rows of a pair are either both zero or linearly
   independent. These close several proposed local normalization and
   repair arguments, but do not force a usable pair or a smaller source.
   [Reviewed statements and dependencies](../unaudited-codex-higher-common-power-bridge-2026-09-05/ALL-ORDER-RESULTS-AND-CLOSURE-ROUTES.md).

6. **Global coefficient certificates under explicit conditions.** Joint
   component-cut restrictions and arbitrary-length selected-pair cycle
   arguments turn suitable configurations into forbidden mixed words.
   The new equal-color-corner argument also applies within a connected
   component and forces actual higher complementary hafnians to vanish.
   The existence of a suitable configuration with a nonzero simultaneous
   complement is not part of those theorems.
   [Cycle certificate](GLOBAL-SELECTED-PAIR-ORIENTATION-CYCLE-CERTIFICATE-NEW-SOURCE.md),
   [corner propagation](EQUAL-COLOR-CORNER-AND-ACTUAL-HIGHER-COFACTOR-ZERO-PROPAGATION-NEW-SOURCE.md).

At forbidden orders n>=6 there are also results for specified component families.
A union of qualifying complete multipartite cofactor components, including
odd-prime Fourier blocks, cannot carry a supported matching of another
color; arbitrary exterior components are retained. An entire color made
only of qualifying components is excluded. Separate theorems handle
specified common-balanced binary component families. Exactly-half-size
components with diameter at most two and complete multipartite actual
cofactor support are excluded. These hypotheses are essential; one
qualifying component with arbitrary exterior is not itself ruled out.

## The precise missing step

For a partition V=S_a disjoint_union S_b disjoint_union S_c, the actual
coefficient of its color word is

    haf(M_a[S_a]) haf(M_b[S_b]) haf(M_c[S_c]).

A full source demands that this product vanish for EVERY partition with
at least two nonempty parts, while all three full hafnians are nonzero.
The remaining task is to prove that these demands cannot coexist at
arbitrary forbidden order, using the derived compatibility identities.
Equivalently, one must force a nonzero mixed coefficient or supply a
valid descent covering every remaining source. Forcing one matching
color is a particularly clean sufficient intermediate theorem.

The hard gap is universal coverage: components can have complicated
internal cofactor zeros, selected deletions can cancel, and pair unions
can remain highly connected. Neither an admissible cycle nor a useful
separator is known to exist in every remaining case.

## Which route is best supported

The exact diagonal weighted formulation remains the best-supported
starting point. It uses the all-order reductions already established
and keeps the matching sums that cause the central difficulty.

A concrete sufficient target is **binary rigidity**: prove that every
actual diagonal two-color source with a full two-target top and all four
whole cubic monomials vanishing at every root has a matching color.
The accepted one-matching-color binary argument would then make
both colors matchings. A pair projection of a ternary source has these
premises, so the target would close the ternary conjecture. The reviewed
controls considered here do not refute it, but it is stronger in domain
than necessary: it also concerns binary sources that have no third-color
extension.
General binary rigidity, including its common-balanced subclass, is open.

The new [minimum-source reduction](MINIMUM-NONMATCHING-BINARY-CUBIC-SOURCE-HAS-THREE-CONNECTED-PAIR-UNION-NEW-SOURCE.md)
now proves that a smallest nonmatching member of this exact binary class,
if one exists, has a 3-vertex-connected pair-color union. Both smaller
separator constructions remain in the class, and matching behavior in
both smaller pieces would reassemble to matching behavior in the original.
The two- and four-site bases are also proved. Thus it suffices to settle
binary rigidity in the 3-vertex-connected case. Neither individual color
is asserted connected, and the reduced object is not a smaller ternary
counterexample. The [complete independent audit](MINIMUM-NONMATCHING-BINARY-CUBIC-SOURCE-HAS-THREE-CONNECTED-PAIR-UNION-NEW-SOURCE-AUDIT.md)
and [root acceptance](ROOT-MATH-ACCEPTANCE-MINIMUM-NONMATCHING-BINARY-THREE-CONNECTIVITY.json)
record this precise scope.

The practical direction is to combine exact separator reductions where
cuts exist with global coefficient and cycle arguments for the remaining
components. If binary rigidity proves too strong, the additional third
color must be used explicitly. No argument establishes that one research
route is uniquely optimal or guarantees that its remaining theorem is easy.

The scope controls explain what to avoid. Nonzero individual cofactors
do not ensure a nonzero simultaneous complement; even a prescribed
perfect matching can fail every deletion ordering. A viable deletion
need not inherit an actual scalar inverse. For every fixed defect radius,
there are controls passing those near-pure coefficient checks and the
listed local identities while retaining a longer nonzero mixed word.
Thus finite-radius checks and unproved complement nonvanishing cannot
replace the global step.
