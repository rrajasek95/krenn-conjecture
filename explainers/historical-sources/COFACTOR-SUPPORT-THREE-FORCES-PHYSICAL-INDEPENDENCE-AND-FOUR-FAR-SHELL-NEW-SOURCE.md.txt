## 1. Actual full-source hypothesis and theorem

Let an original diagonal ordinary ternary quadratic A on V, |V|=2m>=6,
have whole top A^[m]=sum_h tau_h h^V with all three tau_h nonzero.
The physical-site algebra is commutative and kills any repeated site;
A^[j]=A^j/j!. Fix one color M, write tau=haf(M), and let K be its
ACTUAL deleted-pair hafnian cofactor matrix with defined zero diagonal.
The reviewed [global theorem](../unaudited-codex-higher-common-power-bridge-2026-09-05/UNIFORM-GLOBAL-EDGE-MONOCHROMATICITY-AND-COFACTOR-INVERSE.md)
gives M K=K M=tau I.

**Theorem.** If supp K[p,*]=S={q,s,t} has exactly three sites, then
S is independent in the ENTIRE physical support graph: every color
matrix vanishes on all three pairs inside S. No assumption about
how many original M edges at p meet these cofactors is needed.

## 2. A nonzero cubic on the actual core retained off the triple

Write a=K[p,q], b=K[p,s], c=K[p,t], so abc!=0. The accepted
[support-three calculation](FOUR-FAR-EQUALITY-AND-UNIQUE-ACTIVE-COFACTOR-INTERSECTION-NEW-SOURCE.md#1-a-scalar-cofactor-support-triple-is-independent-in-the-original-matrix)
gives M[S,S]=0: the p row of K M on q,s,t reduces, after nonzero
scalings, to x+y=x+z=y+z=0.

Put T=V\S, and let Q be the ACTUAL M quadratic on T. For i in S
write the original M row, now wholly supported on T, as

    R_i=sum_(v in T) M[i,v] e_v,
    F_q=a R_q, F_s=b R_s, F_t=c R_t, P=abc.

Define the symmetric trilinear form

    Phi(L1,L2,L3)=[e^T] L1 L2 L3 Q^[m-3].             (1)

The exponent is nonnegative at m>=3. The actual inverse row gives

    F_q+F_s+F_t=tau e_p.                              (2)

Since M[S,S]=0, expanding the full hafnian forces all three sites
q,s,t to match into T. Therefore

    Phi(F_q,F_s,F_t)=P tau.                            (3)

For distinct i,j in S, let k be the remaining site. The off-diagonal
entry (M K)[i,k]=0 expands the ACTUAL cofactor K[v,k] by the two
retained independent sites i,j. Summing over v in T gives exactly

    Phi(R_i,R_i,R_j)=0,
    hence Phi(F_i,F_i,F_j)=0.                          (4)

The two repeated rows remain raw, so no factorial is removed.
Equation (2) and e_p^2=0 imply Phi(sum F,sum F,F_i)=0. Expanding
and using (3)--(4) leaves, for EVERY i in S,

    Phi(F_i,F_i,F_i)=-2 P tau !=0.                     (5)

Thus each original row R_i has a nonzero cube on this actual
retained odd core. No scalar inverse is assigned to Q, and no
identity is iterated on K.

## 3. The full ternary cubic removes the other-color edges

Fix i in S and write S\{i}={j,k}. For either other color N, the
reviewed [three-active-color higher-response theorem](../unaudited-codex-higher-common-power-bridge-2026-09-05/UNIFORM-THREE-ACTIVE-COLOR-HIGHER-PURE-RESPONSE-VANISHING.md)
makes the original-root response R_i^3 (A restricted off i)^[m-2]
zero on EVERY word using only M,N.

Select N at precisely j,k and M at every site of T. The M row R_i
has no entry at j,k. The N core edge j--k is therefore forced; after
its selection the remaining core is exactly Q and its exponent is
m-3. The selected coefficient is

    N[j,k] Phi(R_i,R_i,R_i)=0.                          (6)

Equation (5), divided by the nonzero scaling of R_i, proves
N[j,k]=0. Applying this to every i and both other colors proves
the theorem. This step uses the FULL original ternary cubic,
not scalar-class closure or a response frame on an induced source.

## 4. Four-far equality: an exact shell and component consequence

Use the accepted [four-far equality theorem](FOUR-FAR-EQUALITY-AND-UNIQUE-ACTIVE-COFACTOR-INTERSECTION-NEW-SOURCE.md).
Thus p is nonmatching in B,C and F_H(p)={q,r,s,t}, with

    supp K_B[p,*]={q,s,t}, supp K_C[p,*]={r,s,t}.

Both triples are now physically independent. Accordingly, the only
possible physical edge within F_H(p) is q--r. In particular s--t,
q--s,q--t,r--s,r--t vanish in ALL colors.

There are further zeros on that remaining pair. The p,r entry of
K_B B is zero and, writing a=K_B[p,q]!=0, gives

    0=a B[q,r]+K_B[p,s] B[s,r]+K_B[p,t] B[t,r]
      =a B[q,r].                                      (7)

Thus B[q,r]=0. The p,q entry of K_C C similarly gives C[q,r]=0.
The entire four-set F_H(p) is therefore independent in BOTH B,C;
its sole permitted physical edge is H[q,r].

Let D_2(p)={v:dist_H(p,v)=2}. Every H neighbor of s or t lies in D_2(p):
their H-far status excludes p and N_H(p), and the just-proved physical
zeros exclude all of F_H(p). All other sites have H-distance at most
two from p. Since haf(H)!=0, both H rows are nonempty. Hence

    N_H(s),N_H(t) subset D_2(p),
    dist_H(p,s)=dist_H(p,t)=3.                          (8)

Thus s,t lie in p's same NONMATCHING H component. Every site outside
that component belongs to {q,r}. Each H component has even order
and nonzero local hafnian, by factorization of the nonzero full pure
hafnian. Therefore H is either spanning connected, or has exactly
two components: the component on V\{q,r} and the isolated edge q--r.
The latter edge, if present as that isolated component, is private.

The equality is not excluded: no argument here kills its required
pure-cube exterior correction, gives universal routing coverage,
or establishes general pair-union 3-connectivity.
