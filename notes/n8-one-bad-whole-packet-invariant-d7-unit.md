# The unrestricted fixed-star one-bad packet has a degree-seven unit

## Result

For either sharp fixed-star orientation, retain all 135 endpoint-coloured
cells of the six-site residual quadratic `q`.  The normalized packet is

\[
 H_{0124}=X_0^4,\qquad H_{0135}=X_1^4,\qquad
 H_U=H_V=0,\qquad H_{012345}=X_2^6,
\]

with `(U,V)=(0125,0134)` or `(0145,0123)`.  Its ordinary scalar coefficient
ideal contains `1` with multiplier degree at most seven over `Q`.

This closes the degree-seven frontier left open in
[the bounded whole-packet calculation](n8-one-bad-whole-packet-macaulay-frontier.md).
It is an independent algebraic closure of the same fixed-star packets already
excluded more conceptually by the permanent-null cap theorem.  It does **not**
concentrate arbitrary multisite endpoint stars.

## Reynolds reduction

The constant-connected degree-seven Macaulay component has 70,398 target
monomials and 110,898 literal source-row multiples.  The first sharp packet
has an order-eight automorphism group generated by endpoint swap, the two
compatible residual-pair swaps, and the induced exchange of colours `0,1`.
The checker verifies all eight actions directly on the 1,053 coefficient
generators.

The unit is fixed.  Over characteristic zero, Reynolds averaging therefore
shows that it lies in the full source image exactly when it lies in the
invariant source image.  Target monomials have 9,411 orbits, with orbit-size
histogram

\[
 1:40,\qquad 2:139,\qquad 4:944,\qquad 8:8288.
\]

After primitive normalization and duplicate removal, the invariant source
image has 14,651 rows and 62,323 nonzero entries.

## Exact characteristic-zero certificate

Sparse elimination modulo `1,000,003` gives rank 8,959.  This is a lower
bound for the rank over `Q`.  Exact rational nullspace computation returns
452 independent annihilators.  Every returned vector is checked against
every primitive Reynolds row, and every one has zero coefficient at the
constant-monomial orbit.  Hence

\[
 \operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb Q}=9411-452=8959,
 \qquad [1]=0\text{ in the rational cokernel}.
\]

Equivalently, the matrix and the matrix augmented by `1` both have rational
rank 8,959.  The site permutation `(2 4)` transports the conclusion to the
second sharp orientation.

## Scope and proof impact

This is a whole-`q` ideal theorem, not a sparse-support computation or a
localization.  Its role is nevertheless bounded: the fixed-star one-bad
packets were already closed by a cleaner cap/descent argument.  The remaining
conjecture-level extraction problem is arbitrary multisite-star concentration
or a direct active-clean cap, neither of which follows from Reynolds averaging
after the ports have been fixed.
