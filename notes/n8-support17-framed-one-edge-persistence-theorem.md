# N=8 framed one-edge persistence theorem

## Result

The branch-complete support-17 calculation admits a theorem-level
compression.  It is not necessary to regard its millions of charts as
independent accidents.

Let (G) be one of the support-16 graphs in the two-RRX frontier, let (e)
be a live added edge, and retain the directed cap/block incidence (X).  The
three normalized pure target rows determine a labelled colour frame

\[
  \ell_0\sqcup\ell_1\sqcup\ell_2.
\]

For every resulting support-17 local link pattern, one of the following
holds.

1. **Contraction-ideal persistence.**  The new response increment lies in
   the same source-star contraction ideal (I_X) as the old response, so the
   old active private-cap kernel still annihilates it.
2. **Uncovered matching debt.**  An inherited singleton decorated matching
   word is absent from the added-edge link and therefore remains a nonzero
   singleton coefficient.
3. **Missing pure row.**  The proposed block completion omits one of the
   normalized pure-supported rows.
4. **Complementary binary landing.**  The remaining two-colour link is the
   crossed (2\times2) permanent face and has an exact active rank-two cap
   kernel.

There are no necessary guards.  Up to source-labelled graph isomorphism, the
nonprivate part consists of exactly 502 directed local link patterns.  Thus
the result closes all one-edge descendants of the 281 support-16 directed
frontier types: the 905 private incidences close by (1), and all 502
nonprivate local types close by (2)--(4).  This is the finite (N=8)
exhaustion required after the high-high deletion reduction.

## Algebraic proof

### 1. The private-cap mechanism is ideal-theoretic

Let (I_X) be the ideal generated by the contraction slots through the
directed block (X).  Write the augmented cap response as

\[
  C_{G+e}=C_G+\Delta_e C.
\]

If (C_G,\Delta_eC\in I_X), a left/right kernel (K) annihilating the
generator (X) annihilates both summands.  Adding (e) does not change the
old diagonal activity readouts.  Hence the same (K) is still an active
private-cap witness.  This explains 905 of the 1320 complete-private parent
augmentations.  It is basis-covariant on the contracted factor: changing
coordinates carries (I_X) and its annihilator together.

The other 415 augmentations are exactly the cases in which a response
residue leaves (I_X).  They do not invalidate the lemma; they enter the
matching-link alternatives below.

### 2. Singleton persistence is a link-support lemma

Let (S_G) denote the inherited singleton decorated matching words, and let
(L_e) be the decorated one-edge link, i.e. the new matching occurrences
that contain (e).  All new perfect matchings contain (e), so coefficients
outside the word support of (L_e) are unchanged.  Consequently

\[
  S_G\not\subseteq\operatorname{supp}_{\rm word}(L_e)
  \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
  \text{an inherited nonzero singleton survives}.
\]

The useful basis-free combinatorial corollary is

\[
  |S_G|>|L_e|\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
  S_G\not\subseteq\operatorname{supp}_{\rm word}(L_e).
\]

No coefficient genericity enters: every declared support component is live,
so a one-occurrence product is nonzero.  This is the uniform part of the
argument and applies at arbitrary support whenever the displayed hypothesis
holds.

### 3. The only framed (N=8) residue is binary

After the ideal and link-support tests, the support-17 table leaves only the
complementary crossed binary face.  If (a) is the cap's direct colour and
(b,c) are the other frame lines, its response is

\[
  P_{bc}(K)=K_{bb}K_{cc}+K_{bc}K_{cb}.
\]

On each of the 12 left charts, the denominator-cleared rank-two matrix from
the support-16 binary-cover calculation satisfies

\[
  w^T K=0,\qquad P_{bc}(K)=0,
  \qquad K_{00}K_{11}K_{22}\ne0.
\]

Transposition supplies the 12 right charts.  This proves an active clean-cap
landing.  Unlike the preceding two mechanisms, this conclusion uses the
three distinguished target lines.

## Scope and basis-free limit

The exact statement is a **GHZ-framed one-edge persistence theorem**.  It is
equivariant under source-labelled permutations of the three frame lines and
under their nonzero rescalings.  The contraction-ideal and cardinality
sublemmas are intrinsically basis-covariant.

An arbitrary-(\mathrm{GL}_3) formulation with the target held fixed is not a
valid strengthening: a general change of basis moves the three pure GHZ
lines and hence moves the normalized pure equations.  What has been proved
uniformly beyond (N=8) is therefore the conditional ideal/link lemma.  The
claim that every residual link is complementary binary is exhausted here
only for the 502 (N=8), support-17 directed types.  No support-18
counterguard is needed because that finite exhaustion has zero guards.
