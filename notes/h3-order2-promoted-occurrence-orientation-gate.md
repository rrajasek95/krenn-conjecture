# The promoted-occurrence totalization stops first at one endpoint orientation line

## Exact outcome

For either marked order-two cut, the lower response occurrence module has
12 coordinates: an ordered `p` endpoint, an ordered `s` endpoint, and the
single residual `q` edge.  Endpoint-role transposition

\[
 \tau(p_i s_j q_{kl})=p_j s_i q_{kl}
\]

pairs the 12 occurrences freely.  If `f` is the marked occurrence and

\[
                         c_f=12e_f-\mathbf 1,
\]

then its integral endpoint-parity decomposition is

\[
 \boxed{
 c_f^+=6(e_f+e_{\tau f})-\mathbf 1,\qquad
 c_f^-=6(e_f-e_{\tau f}).}                            \tag{1}
\]

The complete response row is endpoint-even.  The logarithmic projector on
the residual `q` edge retains exactly `f` and `tau f`, so it is also even.
Thus the primitive orientation

\[
                            o_f=e_f-e_{\tau f}          \tag{2}
\]

is already the first missing class.  Its primitive coordinate dual kills
both the complete row and the selected `q` pair, and reads 12 on `c_f^-`.

## Why direct Cartan/KS does not construct it

Physical site covariance and colour-root Cartan/KS operations do not
exchange the `p` and `s` roles.  Hence they commute with `tau`.  Starting
from the endpoint-even complete response row `R`, every such transported
row remains even and

\[
                       (1-\tau)H_{\rm root}(R)=0.      \tag{3}
\]

The formal endpoint-odd Cartan prism is nonzero only when it is applied to
an endpoint-oriented seed.  At order two, producing that seed is exactly
the promoted-occurrence construction being sought.  Applying the prism to
the complete row therefore does not bypass `W`; it assumes its first face.

The constant involution combining `P<->S` with response-head transpose does
transport (2) to the conjugate response object.  But its first principal-
parts diagonal is zero, `d theta=0`.  It is a flat two-object isomorphism,
not a chain whose boundary is (2).  A group-bar symbol on the selected
occurrence would again presuppose occurrence localization; the group bar on
the complete row has zero boundary.

## The two lower cuts are one cell type

The two marked faces isolated previously are

```text
lower word 0112, reinsert q23:21,
lower word 0121, reinsert q45:12.
```

They are not two unrelated construction problems.  The physical top-word
stabilizer

\[
                           \sigma=(2\ 5)(3\ 4)
\]

fixes `01211222`, sends `q23:21` to `q45:12`, and commutes with endpoint
role.  Therefore one source-valid cell for the `0112` orientation line,
together with its `sigma` transport, supplies the orientation face in both
marked cuts with the same sign.  This is the exact covariance needed by the
two equal `-5/8 c_lower` debts.  It reduces the construction to one
representative cell plus coherence, not two independent cells.

This reduction does not fill the endpoint-even part in (1).  That part is
the selected residual-`q` pair relative to the complete response row and
still needs its scalar/target correction.  Thus one enriched comparison
family may carry both parity faces, but the odd face is the first place the
direct complete-row construction fails.

## Active-clean/Fredholm scope

The coordinate dual to (2) is not yet a physical terminal.  It distinguishes
two tagged terms inside one complete response polynomial; it has not been
identified with physical `q`, anchor incidence, ordinary residue,
eta/sigma, `W`, or an oriented clean-line coordinate.

The existing active-clean alternative becomes applicable only after a
complete augmented comparison makes that identification:

```text
nonzero oriented clean-line projection -> active-clean exit,
zero projection with nonzero kernel q   -> relative generator,
zero kernel q                           -> Fredholm separator.
```

Consequently the sharp positive theorem is one physically typed
endpoint-odd occurrence cell with boundary `o_f`, correct scalar/target
correction, exact word/fine/repeated grade, and all physical augmented
readouts.  Its `sigma` translate handles the second cut.  If this column is
not in the full augmented physical image, a cokernel dual is exact by
finite-dimensional duality, but calling it terminal requires the same
physical typing; the coefficient orientation dual alone is insufficient.

The complementary endpoint-parity theorem at commit `7206f28` identifies
the next branch exactly.  If the typed odd projection is visible, it enters
the oriented active-clean/terminal fork.  If it is dark, the survivor is
the five-dimensional endpoint-even centered quotient containing `c_f^+`.
Coefficientwise that quotient is generated by the quadratic endpoint
projector

\[
 c_f^+=-{1\over24}(B+6I)(B-4I)c_f^+,
\]

but no occurrence-local physical lift of `B-4I` is known.  Thus the present
direct-Cartan no-go and the parity fork fit together without adding another
branch: construct one fully typed endpoint-occurrence family (odd face plus
even quadratic product-rule face), or promote its first augmented dual.

## Effect on the Gamma1 attack

The coefficient cancellation remains exact.  Each marked restriction of
the unscaled promoted cell is

\[
                  \frac{15}{2}c_{\rm lower}
                   +\frac{13}{2}H_0.
\]

Adding `1/12` of that cell to the shifted raw packet cancels its
`-5/8 c_lower` face and leaves `13/24 H0` on each cut.  The present theorem
shows that the missing lower construction is one endpoint-oriented
representative (plus its symmetry transport), while the common `H0` line
handles the remaining constants.  It does not assert that the scalar,
target, reduced-Eq, residue, or terminal faces of this cell have already
been built.
