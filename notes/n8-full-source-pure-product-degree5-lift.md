# The full pure product lifts through filtration degree five

## Exact outcome

Let (R) be the full polynomial ring in the 252 arbitrary endpoint-colour
coordinates for eight vertices, let (I_{\rm mix}) be generated by all
6,558 mixed hafnian coefficients, and let (J) be the coordinate ideal of
the 240 variables outside the twelve-coordinate expanded-prism boundary
support.  In the balanced degree-twelve, 24-port Macaulay component, an
exact rational certificate proves

\[
  \boxed{H_0H_1H_2\in I_{\rm mix}+J^6.}
\]

Equivalently, 7,861 orbit-sum columns with mixed generator words and
balanced degree-eight multipliers satisfy

\[
  H_0H_1H_2-\sum_w Q_wH_w\in J^6.
\]

The frozen checker streams the certificate and replays the identity over
\(\mathbf Q\) with zero residual.  The coefficient denominator lcm is only
four, and the largest numerator is fifteen.

This remains a truncated theorem.  It is not the unqualified membership
\(H_0H_1H_2\in I_{\rm mix}\), so it does not by itself prove Krenn's
conjecture.

## Why the degree-four solution could not simply be extended

The exact degree-four certificate from `bd1b3e4` leaves 17,370 degree-five
residual row orbits.  Closing only those rows under columns whose minimum
off-support degree is five gives

\[
  49{,}688\text{ rows}\times153{,}422\text{ columns}.
\]

Over \(\mathbf F_{1009}\), this leading block has rank 49,454 and the fixed
tail is inconsistent.  That is not a full obstruction: the degree-four
system has a 14,148-dimensional column kernel, and changing the earlier
solution along this kernel changes its degree-five tail.

The correct calculation retains all 19,721 columns and 5,576 rows through
degree four, attaches every one of their degree-five tails, and then closes
the new degree-five rows under minimum-degree-five columns.  The new tails
add 17,527 row orbits directly and 194 more in the next closure layer.  The
coupled block is

\[
  72{,}985\text{ rows}\times224{,}153\text{ columns},
\]

with 471,894 nonzero invariant entries.  It is the block-triangular Schur
system

\[
  \begin{pmatrix}A_{\le4}&0\\T_5&B_5\end{pmatrix},
\]

formed without ever constructing a basis for \(\ker A_{\le4}\).

Sparse elimination over \(\mathbf F_{1009}\) gives rank 72,904 and left
nullity 81.  All 81 cokernel directions annihilate the pure-product target,
and back-substitution gives a 7,861-column modular solution.  Rational
reconstruction produces coefficients in \(\tfrac14\mathbf Z\); direct
rational replay, rather than the modular discovery, is the final proof.

## Scope and next frontier

The result removes the first degree-five obstruction in characteristic zero
and shows why fixed-lift continuation is too restrictive.  At every later
degree the coupled system must again retain the kernel freedom from all
earlier layers; failure of the tail of one chosen certificate is not a
nonmembership proof.

The next exact frontier is degree six.  One should stream the degree-six tail
of the 7,861-column certificate, adjoin the degree-six tails of all columns
through degree five, close under minimum-degree-six columns, and solve the
coupled Schur system.  A positive modular solve can again be rationally
reconstructed and replayed; a negative solve is rigorous only if its dual
annihilates every earlier and degree-six column.
