# The retained `Hasse[2]` packet is a second-fundamental obstruction, not a differentiated source row

## Verdict

There is no global source-valid map from the mixed `Hasse[0]` GHZ equation
to the retained-pair `Hasse[2]` packet inside the old cotangent/principal-
parts complex.  The latter is not an equation obtained by differentiating
the former on the source fibre.

Let `R` be the complete response hafnian on `2h+2` augmented vertices and
let `a,b` be two compatible, disjoint direction edges.  Put

\[
                    H_{ab}=D_aD_bR.
\]

Then

```text
degree R       = h+1,
degree D_e R   = h,
degree H_ab    = h-1.
```

The mixed source is `A=P/(R)`.  Its hypersurface deformation obstruction
module is

\[
                     T^1_A=A/J_R,
\]

where `J_R` is generated by the first cofactors `D_eR`.  Since `H_ab` is a
nonzero homogeneous polynomial of degree `h-1` and `(R,J_R)` has no nonzero
component below degree `h`,

\[
                         [H_{ab}]\ne0\quad\text{in }T^1_A.       \tag{1}
\]

## The exact jet equation

Use two square-zero parameters and write

\[
 x'=x+\epsilon\xi+\delta\eta+\epsilon\delta\zeta,
 \qquad \epsilon^2=\delta^2=0.
\]

The source equation expands as

\[
\begin{aligned}
R(x')={}&R(x)
 +\epsilon J_R\xi+\delta J_R\eta\\
 &+\epsilon\delta\bigl(J_R\zeta+operatorname{Hess}_R(\xi,\eta)\bigr).
\end{aligned}                                                       \tag{2}
\]

The mixed coefficient is one, not two.  Hence the precise conditions are

\[
 J_R\xi=0,\qquad J_R\eta=0,\qquad
 J_R\zeta+\operatorname{Hess}_R(\xi,\eta)=0.             \tag{3}
\]

For the coordinate retained pair `xi=e_a, eta=e_b`, the Hessian term is
exactly `H_ab`.  Thus a corrected two-jet exists if and only if

\[
                        -H_{ab}\in\operatorname{im}J_R.             \tag{4}
\]

If physical corrections are restricted by a source map `a:L->T_source`,
the correct condition is stronger: replace `im J_R` by `im(J_R a)`.

This separates two failures that were previously conflated:

1. the coordinate operators `D_a,D_b` do not themselves descend to
   derivations of `A`, because `D_aR,D_bR` are not in `(R)`;
2. even after first-jet tangent corrections are supplied, (1) obstructs
   the mixed lift.

## A literal source guard

Choose one perfect matching of the complement of `a union b`, set its
`h-1` edges equal to one, and set every other edge variable to zero.  Then

\[
                       R=0,\qquad J_R=0,
                       \qquad H_{ab}=1.                         \tag{5}
\]

The point lies on the mixed response fibre and both coordinate directions
are tangent there.  Nevertheless the mixed obstruction is one, while every
possible normal acceleration has value zero under `J_R`.  No choice of
`zeta` solves (3).

At `h=3`, `R` has 105 terms and the selected lower packet is

\[
 q_{23}q_{45}+q_{24}q_{35}+q_{25}q_{34}.
\]

Taking `q23=q45=1` and every other displayed cell zero gives exactly
`R=J_R=0` and lower value one.  This is the cotangent explanation of the
earlier physical polynomial counterguard.

## What principal parts actually says

In the second principal-parts algebra, `delta R=0` is one filtered Taylor
relation.  Its degree-one initial term is `dR`.  Its mixed degree-two term
is coupled to the first term by (3).  Extracting the coefficient of the
retained direction pair sends `delta R` to `H_ab`, which is nonzero.

Therefore retained-pair coefficient extraction is not a map of the
principal-parts quotient.  A splitting/connection `zeta` is exactly what is
needed to make it one.  The associated graded does not turn `H_ab` into a
new zero row; it records the first conormal relation and leaves (1) as the
second-fundamental class.

This is also why arbitrary differentiation of the point equation was
invalid.  The correct output of jet formalism is the membership equation
`J*zeta+H=0`, not `H=0` by itself.

## The only immediate positive construction is local

On a Jacobian open `D(J_c)`, after both first jets have already been made
tangent, one may choose

\[
                         \zeta_c=-H/J_c.                         \tag{6}
\]

Then (3) holds and the original `Hasse[0]` equation is preserved through
mixed order.  This is a genuine local source two-jet.  It has three limits:

- it uses a normal source-coordinate correction, so it expresses `H` as a
  Jacobian boundary rather than proving the separate row `H=0`;
- it carries denominator, chart-transition, and product-rule faces; and
- it cannot extend across the singular guard (5).

Thus a proof branch already localized at an admitted nonzero cofactor may
use (6), provided the correction is physically typed and all proper faces
are retained.  There is no global polynomial restriction/algebraization
from `H0` alone.

## Exact remaining source-side option

A global construction would need additional source equations whose
Jacobian/Tate columns kill the class (1), or a new Tate/Spencer generator
with boundary representing it.  The latter is not automatically a harmless
resolution enlargement: because (1) is nonzero for the old source, adjoining
such a boundary changes its derived fibre unless the extra physical source
relation is proved independently.

This result is uniform for `h>=2`, a compatible retained direction pair,
and a mixed target-zero response word.  It isolates the obstruction from
`H0` alone and does not claim that the complete augmented source supplies no
additional normal column.
