\[

## Exact remaining scope

\[
 a_{02}F+M_{02}=0,\qquad a_{01}F+M_{01}=0,
 \qquad F=\operatorname{haf}(Q_\omega).                 \tag{9}
\]

Eliminating (F) gives the general cross-head condition

\[
                         a_{02}M_{01}-a_{01}M_{02}=0.    \tag{10}
\]
