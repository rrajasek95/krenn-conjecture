# Independent audit of the mixed-endpoint one-site Laurent closure

## 1. Verdict and scope

The restricted lower bounds in
[the support-frontier note](mixed-endpoint-one-site-support-frontier.md) and
[the cost-32 closure note](mixed-endpoint-one-site-laurent-closure.md) are
sound.

The audit was reconstructed directly from the coefficient definitions.  It
does not import the primary support-search module or either primary checker.
Within the stated class of coordinate aggregate cells and one-site coordinate
response rows, it independently proves

\[
 |\operatorname{supp}q|\geq 33
 \quad\hbox{for path--edge rows},\qquad
 |\operatorname{supp}q|\geq 34
 \quad\hbox{for matching rows}.                         \tag{1}
\]

These are necessary lower bounds, not constructions at 33 or 34.  They do
not apply to non-coordinate edge blocks or multi-site response rows.

## 2. Response-row reduction

Write the one-site response rows as

\[
 p_i=e_i^{(a_i)},\qquad s_i=e_i^{(b_i)},\qquad a_i\ne b_i.
\]

The clean-room checker generates all \(30^3=27{,}000\) triples of directed
edges.  A breadth-first orbit calculation uses adjacent site transpositions,
adjacent colour transpositions, and simultaneous reversal of all arrows.  It
finds 17 orbits with the advertised multiset of orbit sizes.

There are exactly two kinds of literal four-site block conflict:

1. two diagonal missing pairs coincide, so the same block is required to be
   two different pure-colour targets; or
2. an off-diagonal missing pair \(\{a_i,b_j\}\), with \(a_i\ne b_j\),
   coincides with a diagonal missing pair, so the same block is required to
   vanish and to be nonzero.

Testing these conditions on every orbit leaves precisely

\[
 ((0,1),(1,2),(3,4)),\qquad ((0,1),(2,3),(4,5)),          \tag{2}
\]

with orbit sizes 4,320 and 720.  Thus no one-site row geometry is omitted
before the support calculation.

## 3. Boolean support reconstruction

The audit allocates one bit to each of the 135 endpoint-ordered aggregate
cells.  For every sampled four-site coordinate coefficient it reconstructs
the three matching monomials; for every six-site coordinate coefficient of
\(q^{[3]}\) it reconstructs the fifteen matching monomials.  Every term bit
is constrained by a full equivalence with the conjunction of its cell bits.

Only necessary consequences over \(\mathbb C\) are imposed:

- a required nonzero coefficient has a supported matching term;
- a required zero coefficient does not have exactly one supported matching
  term.

Thus coefficients with at least two supported terms retain unrestricted
complex cancellation.  The independent encodings have the following sizes:

| row geometry | variables | hard clauses | zero quadratic coefficients |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| path--edge | 13,014 | 62,445 | 645 |
| matching | 13,257 | 63,417 | 726 |

Exact weighted MaxSAT gives minimum 30 in both cases.  Projected-support
enumeration with an independent exact-cardinality encoding reproduces every
layer used in the first theorem:

| geometry and cost | projected supports | odd HNF relation found |
|---|---:|---:|
| path--edge, 30 | 8 | 8 |
| path--edge, 31 | 18 | 18 |
| matching, 30 | 5 | 5 |
| matching, 31 | 0 | 0 |

This independently confirms the support-frontier exhaustion below 32.

## 4. Exact sign lattice

On a fixed support, a forced zero quadratic with exactly two active terms
gives

\[
 x^{d_k}=-1.                                             \tag{3}
\]

For active-cell exponent columns, form the row lattice generated by

\[
 (d_k,1)\quad\hbox{and}\quad(0,2).                       \tag{4}
\]

The equations are inconsistent precisely when this lattice contains
\((0,1)\).  Indeed, membership gives integers \(r_k,t\) with

\[
 \sum_k r_kd_k=0,\qquad \sum_k r_k+2t=1,                 \tag{5}
\]

and multiplying (3) to the powers \(r_k\) gives \(1=-1\).  Conversely, if
there is no such odd relation, the prescription \(d_k\mapsto-1\) defines a
character on the generated exponent lattice and extends to the ambient free
lattice because nonzero complex numbers have roots of every order.  Hence
the criterion is exact in the Laurent torus, rather than only a necessary
parity test.

The checker computes row Hermite normal form with its unimodular
transformation matrix.  When \((0,1)\) is a member, it reconstructs the
integer coefficients in (5), checks every exponent column is exactly zero,
and checks the total sign is odd.  Every support in the table above is
rejected by such a reconstructed relation.

## 5. Third-term-aware CEGAR audit

For every binomial used with \(r_k\ne0\), let \(T_{k,0},T_{k,1}\) be its two
active cell monomials and let \(Z_{k,2}\) be the exact conjunction bit for
the inactive third matching.  The independently learned clause is

\[
 \bigvee_{r_k\ne0}\left(
   \bigvee_{z\in T_{k,0}\cup T_{k,1}}\neg z
   \ \vee\ Z_{k,2}
 \right).                                                \tag{6}
\]

If (6) is false on a later support, both old terms of every used binomial
still exist and every third term is still absent.  All equations in the odd
relation therefore persist verbatim, contradicting (5).  Consequently (6)
is necessary for every complex weighting.  It explicitly permits both ways
a denser support can escape: remove a formerly active term, or activate the
third matching and turn a binomial into a trinomial.

The clean-room CEGAR run reaches the following minima:

| geometry | independently learned cuts by cost | next minimum |
|---|---|---:|
| path--edge | \(8@30,8@31,15@32\) | 33 |
| matching | \(5@30,19@32\) | 34 |

Its deterministic ledgers are

```text
path-edge:
d9171d5b59d8b81496bb8a1e3dc1550e1e0be8c65b38ffcbed09b4030d7b027d
matching:
5cd60db956a56b31565bce86eb90a25084149a17c72e90a3e731fabbf0fa7e3b
```

The cut counts differ from the primary run because the optimizer can choose
different equal-cost supports and HNF can return a different valid odd
relation.  This is useful independence, not a discrepancy: one sound cut can
remove several supports.  After the last cut, a second SAT engine with an
independently generated totalizer proves UNSAT at support at most 32 for
path--edge and at most 33 for matching.  The optimized formulas are SAT at
33 and 34 respectively.  This proves (1).

## 6. Reproduction

Run

```sh
uv run python computations/audit_mixed_endpoint_one_site_laurent_closure_independent.py
```

The remaining mathematical frontier is exactly the one stated in the
primary note: a support at the new minimum still has to satisfy the full
quadratic and cubic weight equations and the three nonzero target
normalizations.  This audit establishes only that no support below those
minima can do so.
