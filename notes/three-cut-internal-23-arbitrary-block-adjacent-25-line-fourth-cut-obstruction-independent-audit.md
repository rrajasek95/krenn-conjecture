# Independent audit: arbitrary \(A_{23}\) plus the adjacent \(A_{25}\) affine line

## 1. Verdict and exact scope

The local theorem in
[the primary note](three-cut-internal-23-arbitrary-block-adjacent-25-line-fourth-cut-obstruction.md)
passes a clean-room exact reconstruction.  Keep

\[
\begin{array}{c|c@{\qquad}c|c@{\qquad}c|c}
01&E_{00}&45&E_{00}&02&E_{11}\\
14&E_{11}&04&E_{22}&13&E_{22}\\
35&E_{10},
\end{array}
\]

let \(A_{23}=X\) be an arbitrary complex \(3\times3\) matrix, and let

\[
                         A_{25}=E_{00}+tE_{11}.
\]

Even with both boundary stars and \(A_{67}\) completely arbitrary, the
complete quotient identities for cuts \(2,3,4\) cannot coexist with the
identity for cut \(0\), \(1\), or \(5\), while the three diagonal target
fibres remain units.

The independent checker is
[verify_three_cut_internal_23_arbitrary_block_adjacent_25_line_fourth_cut_obstruction_independent_audit.py](../computations/verify_three_cut_internal_23_arbitrary_block_adjacent_25_line_fourth_cut_obstruction_independent_audit.py).
It imports no project module and does not read any matrix, orbit list,
normal, equation list, or certificate from the primary checker or its
helpers.  It reconstructs every object from endpoint-ordered matchings.
No flaw was found.

The scope is narrow and must remain explicit: only this one affine
direction in \(A_{25}\) is free, the other seven internal blocks are fixed,
and this is not an arbitrary two-block theorem or a proof of Krenn's
conjecture.

## 2. Independent matching and linear-algebra routes

Perfect matchings are generated by recursively pairing the largest
remaining vertex, rather than by the primary enumeration route.  Every
edge is stored with its lower endpoint first, so \(35:E_{10}\), every
entry \(x_{ab}\) of \(A_{23}\), and the moving \(25:E_{11}\) retain their
literal endpoint orientation.

For every parameter point the audit independently constructs:

- the six-site matching tensor;
- all fifteen deleted-pair cofactors;
- a fixed list of \(45\) raw insertion columns for each of the six
  cylinders;
- every endpoint-inserted boundary atom;
- all nine boundary-colour slices of the full eight-site tensor.

Projected subspace intersections are computed by the exact dual identity

\[
                 (U\cap V)^\perp=U^\perp+V^\perp
\]

using least-coordinate Gauss--Jordan elimination over \(\mathbb Q\).
This double-annihilator computation is independent of the primary
linear-algebra path.

## 3. Stabilizing torus, normalization, and all support masks

Starting from all \(18\) internal site-colour exponents, the eight fixed
cell equations have rank \(8\), so their kernel has dimension \(10\).
Restriction of that kernel to the nine characters of \(X\) has rank \(5\).
Adjoining the character of the \(E_{11}\) direction on edge \(25\) raises
the rank to \(6\).

The checker also reconstructs the literal six-character action
\((r_0,r_1,r_2,c_0,c_2,\tau)\).  Its \(X\)-weights are

\[
\begin{array}{c|ccc}

1&r_1c_0&r_1r_0&r_1c_2\\
2&r_2c_0&r_2r_0&r_2c_2,
\end{array}
\]

and the moving coefficient \(t\) has the independent weight \(\tau\).
Every fixed cell has weight one.  Extending the action by

\[
g_{6,c}=1,\qquad
g_{7,c}=\left(\prod_{i=0}^{5}g_{i,c}\right)^{-1}
\]

preserves each diagonal target exactly and only rescales the otherwise
arbitrary boundary entries.

Thus \(t\ne0\) is normalized to one without changing an \(X\)-modulus.
For \(t=0\), the system is literally the already independently audited
arbitrary-\(A_{23}\) fixed-interior theorem.

Exhaustive rank calculation over all \(512\) supports of \(X\) gives the

\[

The sole modulus retained by any quotient chart is the rectangle

## 4. The \(T\)-block, its overlaps, and absence of mixed terms

Let \(R_{ab}\) be the output-coordinate block of \(x_{ab}\), and let
\(T\) be the block of the moving \(25:E_{11}\) coefficient.  Fresh
cofactor enumeration gives nine pairwise disjoint \(R_{ab}\), each of
size \(35\), and

\[
\begin{aligned}
 |T|&=35,\\
 |T\cap R_{10}|=|T\cap R_{11}|=|T\cap R_{12}|&=9,\\
 T\cap R_{ab}&=\varnothing\quad(a\ne1).
\end{aligned}
\]

The \(t\)-dependent deleted-pair cofactors are exactly

\[
                          03,\ 04,\ 13,\ 34.
\]

Moreover \(T\) is disjoint from the three-word fixed tensor \(u_+\), and
among the pure targets only \([1^6]\) belongs to \(T\).

Because edges \(23\) and \(25\) share vertex \(2\), no matching contains
both.  The checker verifies, for each of the nine \(X\)-cells, that the
mixed finite difference

\[
 F(x,t)-F(x,0)-F(0,t)+F(0,0)
\]

vanishes coefficientwise for the full tensor, every cofactor/boundary
atom, and all \(270\) raw cylinder columns.  A separate dense rational
\(X\) test checks

\[
                         F(X,2)=2F(X,1)-F(X,0)
\]

for all of the same data.  Thus the argument is genuinely affine in
arbitrary complex \(t\), rather than a zero/one sampling argument.

When a killed \(R_{1b}\) overlaps \(T\), the common coordinates are
killed.  The audit checks the resulting \(T\)-overlap with the killed
space to have sizes \(9,9,9,0\) in the \(x_{10},x_{12},x_{20},x_{22}\)
charts, respectively.  It then verifies term by term that every omitted
arbitrary \(X\)-coefficient disappears from the projected tensor,
boundary map, and every raw cylinder column.

## 9. Conclusion and frontier

The theorem survives an implementation-independent exact audit, including
its most fragile points: torus exhaustiveness, overlap of \(T\) with three
\(R\)-blocks, killing rather than resurrecting overlap coordinates, safe
projected normals, shared ordered fibres, and the exceptional
\(\lambda\)-family.

What is proved remains only

\[
 A_{23}\text{ arbitrary},\qquad A_{25}=E_{00}+tE_{11},
\]

with the other seven internal blocks fixed.  The next local extension must
free another direction in \(A_{25}\), a second affine parameter there, or
an internal edge disjoint from \(23\), where genuine \(Xt\) terms appear.
