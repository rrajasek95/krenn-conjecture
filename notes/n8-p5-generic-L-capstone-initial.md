## What this does and does not prove

This is a complete **initial-form** standard-basis checkpoint at strict order
seven.  Together with the unit determinant it proves unique lifting through
this order and removes every mixed and pure initial obstruction currently
visible on the dense generic-$L$ component.

It is not yet a Mora standard basis for the full completed-local germs.  The
normal-eliminated equations used by the branch recursion are formal series:
the 196 smooth ambient normal variables have already been solved order by
order.  Only their strict-order-seven coefficients, not the entire series,
are present in this finite input.  Treating these coefficients as full germs
would incorrectly turn a filtered statement into an all-orders theorem.

There are two precise ways to close the gap:

1. construct the finite iterated P5/$L$ strict-transform homomorphism from
   the 252 translated ambient variables, retaining the 196 smooth-normal
   variables plus radial and remainder coordinates, and compute a localized
   Mora basis there; or
2. prove a filtered-Rees promotion lemma: the twelve distinct-variable
   Weierstrass initials form a standard basis at every filtration step, and
   the full H0/H1 differentials remain in their conormal span.  The all-row
   reduction and the exact H0 conormal identity verify the first nontrivial
   instance of precisely that induction.

The existing projector history determines the beginning of the finite chart
map without new choices.  If $T$ is the 56-column ambient tangent map and
$\tau$ is the radial parameter, its translated ambient coordinate series is

$$
X=\tau T(a(\tau))-\tau^2W+\tau^3N_2+\tau^4N_3+
  \tau^5Y,
$$

where $W,N_2,N_3$ are exactly the full, identity-safe normal directions
already constructed by `FactorizedP5Projector`, `StrictJetProjector`, and
`ThirdStrictProjector`.  The last vector $Y$ retains the 196 normal
remainders independently; the 196 monic ambient pivot equations then
eliminate it instead of requiring more precomputed normal-graph terms.  The
tangent arc $a(\tau)$ is generated mechanically from the committed
corrections $c_1,\ldots,c_6$, including the free bends $s,t,r$, followed by
eleven independent order-seven transverse remainders.  This supplies the
concrete implementation route
for the missing chart exporter.  Its two checks must be: reconstruction of
all already-frozen coefficients through strict order seven, followed by
exact division of the finite substituted source polynomials by their known
power of $\tau$.

The finite ambient input already exists in
`computations/analyze_n8_ambient_local_standard_basis.py`, but its unlocalized
252-variable Mora computation exceeded the exploratory memory cap.  The
smallest missing computational artifact is therefore the explicit localized
iterated-chart map, not another coefficient expansion.

There is also a plausible equivariant source for the conormal relation, with
an important guard.  Sitewise off-diagonal $GL_3$ vector fields satisfy exact
Ward identities for the universal matching map.  Their first-order shadow is
already certified by `verify_n8_counterexample_pure_tangent.py`, which gives
$dH_0=e\,dH_{00000010}$ and
$dH_1=a^{-1}dH_{11000111}$ on the original torus.  An off-diagonal field is
not itself tangent to the mixed-output ideal: its target action can send a
mixed coordinate to a pure coordinate.  Thus equivariance alone does not
make H0 or H1 constant.  A valid shortcut must construct a Koszul-corrected
combination tangent modulo the twelve chart generators and reduce its exact
differential in their conormal module.  The finite Rees exporter above would
make that test source-faithful.

One useful triangularity fact is already exact.  The tangent coordinate
$z_{46}$ is the combination of ambient cells

$$
-x_{27}^{02}-x_{27}^{12}+x_{37}^{02}+x_{37}^{12}.
$$

Although it is not a single literal cell, all four cells are incident to
site 7.  A perfect matching uses exactly one cell at site 7, so every
universal hafnian coefficient is globally affine in $z_{46}$.  This is the
literal multiaffinity behind the monic newest-bend equation and the site-7
Ward interpretation.  The finite exporter should therefore retain the
eleven newest pivot variables rather than substitute their $r$-dependent
solutions: doing so keeps the $r$ direction supported at one site and rules
out later powers of $r$ in the source transforms.
