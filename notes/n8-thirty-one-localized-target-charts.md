# The full eight-site target is covered by 31 localized matching charts

## 1. Exact localization-cover lemma

Let \(R\) be the polynomial ring in the 252 endpoint-colour coordinates
\(x_{uv}^{ab}\), and let \(I_{\rm mix}\) be the ideal generated by the 6,558
mixed eight-site hafnian coefficients.  For a colour \(c\) and a perfect
matching \(M\) of the eight vertices, put

\[
                 p_{c,M}=\prod_{uv\in M}x_{uv}^{cc}.
\]

Then \(H_c=\sum_M p_{c,M}\), with 105 summands.

**Lemma (31-chart localization cover).**  Suppose that every mixed
coefficient vanishes and \(H_0,H_1,H_2\ne0\).  There are perfect matchings
\(M_0,M_1,M_2\) such that

\[
       P=p_{0,M_0}p_{1,M_1}p_{2,M_2}\ne0.                 \tag{1}
\]

Up to \(S_8\times S_3\), the monomial \(P\) belongs to exactly one of 31
orbits.  Consequently it is enough, for one representative \(P_j\) of each
orbit, to prove a localized identity

\[
 P_j^{N_j}(H_0H_1H_2)^{e_j}\in I_{\rm mix}               \tag{2}

\]

for some \(N_j\ge0\) and \(e_j\ge1\).  The stronger choice \(e_j=1\) is
sufficient but not required.

**Proof.**  A finite sum can be nonzero only if one of its summands is
nonzero, so choose \(M_c\) with \(p_{c,M_c}\ne0\) for each colour.  Their
product gives (1).  Simultaneous vertex and colour relabelling preserves the
mixed ideal and the three-pure condition.  The exact orbit audit gives 31
orbits and reconstructs their sizes; the sizes sum to

\[
                         105^3=1{,}157{,}625,
\]

the number of all labelled triples.  Hence the list is exhaustive.  At a
common mixed zero in the chart \(P_j\ne0\), the left side of (2) is nonzero,
contradicting its membership in \(I_{\rm mix}\).  \(\square\)

Equivalently, one may prove
\(H_0H_1H_2\in\sqrt{I_{\rm mix}R[P_j^{-1}]}\) on each chart.  This is
strictly weaker than global unsaturated membership and is exactly what a
counterexample requires: its three unit pure coefficients automatically
choose one chart.

## 2. Matching-incidence structure of a chart

The twelve factors of \(P_j\) form a perfect matching of the 24
vertex-colour ports.  Forgetting the colours on edges but retaining the
three coloured half-edges at each vertex gives a cubic multigraph.  A mixed
Macaulay column incident to the support chooses four of its twelve edges
which cover all eight vertices and whose selected port colours are not
constant.  Removing that mixed one-factor leaves a two-factor.  Thus the
zeroth layer of a localized contraction is controlled by two finite data:

1. the colour multiplicities of the selected holes; and
2. the cycle partition of the complementary two-factor.

The checker reconstructs these data for all 31 charts.  Every chart has at
least one mixed support one-factor.  Their counts range from 2 to 78:

\[
\begin{array}{c|rrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrr}
\text{count}&2&3&4&5&6&7&9&10&11&12&14&15&21&22&24&42&78\\ \hline
\text{charts}&2&2&3&2&5&1&2&2&1&2&1&1&1&2&2&1&1.
\end{array}
\]

The underlying cubic multigraph is connected in 19 charts.  The remaining
component partitions occur with multiplicities

\[
 (6,2):5,\qquad(4,4):4,\qquad(4,2,2):2,\qquad(2,2,2,2):1.
\]

These facts rule out a support-level dead end: no pure target monomial is an
isolated row of the mixed incidence map at eight vertices.

## 3. The two minimally coupled charts

Exactly two charts have only two mixed support one-factors.

* In chart 25 the support stabilizer has order 8.  Both mixed factors have
  hole multiplicities \((4,4)\), and each complementary two-factor is one
  Hamilton eight-cycle.
* In chart 26 the support stabilizer has order 4.  Both mixed factors have
  hole multiplicities \((4,2,2)\), and each complementary two-factor has
  cycle partition \((5,3)\).

The expanded-prism support used in the exact 60-edge certificate, the full
source filtration, and the exceptional-torus local calculation is precisely
chart 26.  Thus those calculations address one of the two least coupled
localized supports, not a generic easy orbit.  They already prove

\[
 H_0H_1H_2\in I_{\rm mix}+J^6

\]

in the full ring relative to that support and give a complete Laurent
certificate on its 60-edge subchart.

The second extremal chart, chart 25, is now the sharp comparison case.  A
descent proving that every chart with more than two mixed support factors
reduces to charts 25 or 26 would leave only these two hard algebraic models.
No such descent is claimed here; the ledger isolates it as a precise finite
lemma rather than assuming it from the factor count.

## 4. Why this is smaller than full invariant membership

The unlocalized \(S_8\times S_3\)-invariant component already has 388,889
row orbits after three incidence layers.  The localization cover instead
fixes one reference port matching, permits its twelve variables to be units,
and uses only its stabilizer.  Laurent multiplication by those support
variables is the natural translation operation for a chartwise contracting
homotopy.  A proof may therefore use a support power \(P_j^{N_j}\), which is
invisible to the global degree-twelve membership problem but fully valid for
the conjecture.
