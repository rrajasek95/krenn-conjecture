# A seven-row packet with a live diagonal anchor, and the colour asymmetry

Research evidence only.  Krenn's conjecture remains open, `SP-CLEAN-BRIDGE`
is untouched, and no certified dependency changes.

## 1. Outcome

The [star-sector trade](h3-star-sector-anchor-terminal-class-trade.md) proves,
on the audited seven-row guard's frozen colour-2 slice, that a live diagonal
anchor annihilates the direct scalars carrying the terminal class.  That
statement is worth nothing unless a live anchor is actually attainable.  It
is, for one of the two missing colours but not the other.

* **Colour 0 is attainable.**  There is an explicit exact rational packet
  satisfying all \(7\times729\) seven-row coefficients together with
  \(\operatorname{Row}(0,0,0^6)=1\).  It has rank-three stars, every literal
  Segre rectangle, and \(\chi=0\) on every admissible cap — exactly what the
  trade predicts.  So the trade's conditional is **not vacuous**.
* **Colour 1 is not.**  \(\operatorname{Row}(1,1,1^6)\) lies in the ideal
  generated by the seven rows, by an explicit five-term certificate.  It
  therefore vanishes at every common zero of the seven rows and can never
  equal its target \(1\).

The two missing anchors of the guard are therefore not interchangeable,
which the trade note's symmetric treatment of the two colours does not show.

## 2. The witness

The audited guard, with the direct scalar moved and five colour-0 entries
added.  The colour-2 slice is verbatim
(\(q_2=z_0z_1+z_4z_5\); \(p_0=z_0+z_1\), \(p_1=z_4\), \(p_2=z_2+z_3\);
\(s_0=z_5\), \(s_1=z_2-z_3\), \(s_2=\tfrac12(z_2+z_3)\)).  New material:

\[
 q_0=z_1z_5+z_2z_3,\qquad p_0(0,0)=1,\qquad s_0(4,0)=1,\qquad d_{10}=-1,
\]

with \(d_{01}\) removed and the whole colour-1 sector zero.

Its properties, all checked exhaustively:

* all \(7\times729=5103\) seven-row coefficients are correct on every word,
  and \(\operatorname{Row}(0,0,0^6)=1\);
* the complete missing-target ledger is two entries,
  \(\bigl(00,(2,0,0,0,0,0),+1\bigr)\) and \((11,1^6,-1)\) — the same count as
  the guard's, but now with \(X_0\) live;
* both endpoint stars have rank three, the direct block is \(-E_{10}\), and
  all \(81\) literal Segre rectangles hold, the responses being literal
  products \(p_is_j\);
* \(\chi=0\) for every admissible cap on every pure colour.  The structural
  data is untouched — the colour-2 caps \((0,1)\) and \((0,2)\) still have
  \(Q_2=-2\) and \(\tfrac12\) — but the trade has forced
  \(d_{01}=d_{02}=0\), so \(\chi=-2d_{01}\) and \(\chi=\tfrac12d_{02}\) both
  vanish.  The two live diagonal caps have \(Q=(0,1,0,0)\) and so fail the
  source relation, which disqualifies them as selected rows.

The leak is the anchor's own carrier:

\[
 \operatorname{Row}\bigl(0,0,(2,0,0,0,0,0)\bigr)
  =q_0(2,3)\bigl(q_0(1,5)s_0(4,0)+q_0(1,4)s_0(5,0)\bigr),
 \qquad
 \operatorname{Row}(0,0,0^6)=p_0(0,0)\cdot\bigl(\text{the same quantity}\bigr).
\]

This is **not** a counterexample to anything.  It satisfies eight of the
nine GHZ rows minus one coefficient and misses the colour-1 target entirely.
It is consistent with the trade note's result that the seven rows plus the
*complete* colour-0 anchor row are infeasible: the \(00\) row here is correct
on \(728\) of its \(729\) words, which is the most that is available.

## 3. The leaf census

The trade note records that a branch search on "seven rows plus the
pure-word colour-0 anchor" terminates with \(383\) open leaves, and warns
that open leaves are not evidence of feasibility.  All \(383\) are now
decided.  \(373\) are feasible, each satisfied by one of four explicit
rational solutions, whose carriers are

\[
 q_0(1,5)p_0(0)s_0(4),\quad q_0(0,5)p_0(1)s_0(4),\quad
 q_0(1,4)p_0(5)s_0(0),\quad q_0(0,4)p_0(5)s_0(1).
\]

The remaining \(10\) — leaves \(206,211,215,219,348,354,357,360,363,365\) —
are infeasible, each by the identity (that leaf's anchor equation)
\({}+1=p_0(4,0)\cdot\) (another of its equations), forcing \(1=0\).

## 4. The colour-1 certificate

Modulo the \(32\) forced zeros — each of which is itself a degree-one
seven-row equation, hence in the ideal — the bare anchor polynomial is an
explicit combination of five seven-row equations:

\[
 \operatorname{Row}(1,1,1^6)
 =d_{11}q_1(2,3)\,E_3+p_1(0,1)\,E_4+p_1(1,1)\,E_5+A'E_6+C'E_7,
\]

where, with \(c=1\), the five equations are the bare rows at the target-zero
words

\[
\begin{aligned}
 E_3&:\ (2,2)\ \text{row at}\ 2\,2\ \text{on sites }\{2,3\},\ c\text{ elsewhere},\\
 E_4,E_5&:\ (0,1)\ \text{row at}\ 2\ \text{on site }0,\ \text{resp. site }1,\\
 E_6,E_7&:\ (0,1)\ \text{row at}\ 2\ \text{on }\{1,4,5\},\ \text{resp. }\{0,4,5\},
\end{aligned}
\]

and the multipliers are
\(A'=q_1(1,5)p_1(4,1)+q_1(1,4)p_1(5,1)\),
\(C'=q_1(0,5)p_1(4,1)+q_1(0,4)p_1(5,1)\).
\(E_3\) is the four-site permanent
\(q_1(0,4)q_1(1,5)+q_1(0,5)q_1(1,4)\).

Every word used has target zero and every row used is among the seven, so
each \(E_k\) vanishes on the seven-row variety, and hence so does
\(\operatorname{Row}(1,1,1^6)\).

The same combination **provably does not close for colour 0** — the residual
has sixteen monomials — which it could not, since section 2 exhibits a
colour-0 solution.

Two remarks on method.  First, a branch search leaves undecided leaves on
this same colour-1 system while the certificate closes it in one line; open
leaves really are not evidence.  (An exact count is not quoted: the figure
depends on the branch rule and on which substitutions are pre-applied, and no
checker here fixes those.)  Second, this is strictly stronger than
the trade note's "seven rows plus the complete \(11\) row are infeasible",
and it settles that the two pure-word anchors cannot both be restored.

## 5. Why the two colours differ

The observed mechanism, recorded as an explanation rather than a proof — the
proofs are the witness and the certificate, one in each direction.

The two label stars carry their frozen colour-2 weight at different sites:
\(p_0\) at \(\{0,1\}\) and \(s_0\) at \(\{5\}\), against \(p_1\) at \(\{4\}\)
and \(s_1\) at \(\{2,3\}\).  The certificate reaches the anchor's star data
through supplied rows evaluated at words that place colour \(2\) exactly
where a frozen star has weight.  For colour \(1\) those words sit in the
supplied \(01\) row; the corresponding access to the colour-0 anchor's own
second star would need a row that is not supplied.  The asymmetry is between
the two labels' frozen support, not between the two colours as such.

## 6. Scope

1. Monochromatic internal edges throughout, as in the trade note.  The
   cross-colour case is untouched here.
2. Specific to the audited guard's colour-2 slice.  The
   [transport note](h3-star-sector-transport-collapse-general-peel-degenerate.md)
   shows the peel and trade themselves do not survive a change of slice, so
   nothing here should be read as general.
3. The witness satisfies eight of nine rows minus one coefficient.  It is
   not a nine-row packet and does not bear on Krenn's conjecture.
4. What the witness establishes about the trade is exactly that its
   hypothesis is attainable for colour 0.  It does not make the trade a
   theorem about anything larger.

## 7. Audit

The dependency-free checker
[`verify_h3_star_sector_pure_word_anchor_witness.py`](../computations/verify_h3_star_sector_pure_word_anchor_witness.py)
verifies the witness twice — once from an eight-vertex hafnian over
permutation-canonicalised matchings, once from the deleted-pair chart
formula — on all \(6561\) coefficients, plus star ranks, Segre rectangles,
cap layers, the trade, the peel, the \(383\)-leaf decision, the colour-1
certificate together with its failure for colour 0, and five mutation
guards.  Standard library only, exact `Fraction`, about seven seconds,
passing normal, `-O` and `-I -S`, identical across five hash seeds.

Independently reconstructed during audit, from the eight-vertex matching
tensor rather than from the chart formula (no checker in the repository
performs this reconstruction): the witness satisfies the seven rows on all \(729\) words with zero
violations, gives \(\operatorname{Row}(0,0,0^6)=1\), reproduces the exact
two-entry ledger above, has star ranks \(3,3\), satisfies all \(81\) Segre
rectangles, and has \(\chi=0\) on every admissible cap.  Its cap *layers* are
not all zero — the colour-2 caps \((0,1)\) and \((0,2)\) keep \(Q_2=-2\) and
\(\tfrac12\), and the two live diagonal caps keep \((Q_0,Q_1,Q_2,Q_3)=(0,1,0,0)\),
exactly as section 2 says; what vanishes is \(\alpha Q_2+Q_3\), because the
trade has killed \(\alpha\).  The colour-1 certificate was
re-derived independently and closes exactly; the colour-0 combination leaves
a sixteen-monomial residual.
