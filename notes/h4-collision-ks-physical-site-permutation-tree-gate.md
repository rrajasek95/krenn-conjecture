# The `h=4` site symmetry builds only the unpointed orbit; `phi01` has an invariant root debt

## Verdict

The complete physical site, root/Weyl, and restriction/reinsertion operations
do not construct the minimal `h=4` tree.

There is a genuine asymmetry between its two proposed edges:

```text
phi12: p1 -> p2   is a coefficient-level Hasse/Beck--Chevalley overlap;
phi01: p0 -> p1   changes the actual decorated coefficient monomial.
```

The first edge is source-provenant in the intrinsic principal-parts/Hasse
resolution.  Its downstream PP/AugP2 protected comparison is still
conditional.  The second edge is not produced physically:

- the obvious tail-block permutation is an isomorphism to a relabelled
  source object, not a bar in the fixed pointed object;
- raw folding along that isomorphism lowers fixed-source `H0`;
- the connected two-root Weyl path has nonzero GHZ target defect; and
- every target-safe combination of those paths has matching aggregate zero,
  while the desired fixed-tail edge has aggregate one.

Thus the physical tree stops at `phi01`, and there is no compatible physical
`dTau`.  The first new datum must be a pointed, target-corrected word-changing
cell with nonzero invariant matching aggregate.

## 1. Literal block permutations

Write the tail as

\[
                         T=23|45|67.
\]

The three presentation grades are

```text
p0  0121221222 / t_23*q_(v,45|67) / (45|67;23)
p1  0121122222 / t_45*q_(v,23|67) / (23|67;45)
p2  0121122222 / t_67*q_(v,23|45) / (23|45;67).
```

Define physical tail-site permutations

\[
 g_{01}=(2\ 4)(3\ 5),\qquad
 g_{12}=(4\ 6)(5\ 7),\qquad
 g_{02}=g_{12}g_{01}.                                \tag{1}
\]

They preserve the set of edges of `T` and transport every literal tag:

```text
g01: p0 -> p1,
g12: p1 -> p2,
g02: p0 -> p2.
```

This statement is exact on the complete coefficient source, not just on
displayed words.  The checker enumerates all fifteen perfect matchings on
the six tail sites and verifies that every decorated matching monomial is
transported to the corresponding decorated monomial in the moved word.  It
also checks the strict composition in (1).

However, covariance is not yet a pointed-source boundary.  It is important
to inspect the actual monomials at the fixed tail.

## 2. `phi12` is a true factorization kernel

Let `m_i` denote the complete decorated coefficient monomial under `p_i`.
The audit gives

\[
                         m_0\ne m_1,
                 \qquad  m_1=m_2.                    \tag{2}
\]

The equality on the right holds because `p1,p2` have the same word and full
matching; the block swap merely interchanges the two all-colour-2 edge
factors `45` and `67` in a commutative monomial.

Retain the presentation flags and let `mu` be reinsertion into the decorated
top.  In bases `(p0,p1,p2)` and `(m0,m1)`,

\[
 \mu=
 \begin{pmatrix}
 1&0&0\\
 0&1&1
 \end{pmatrix}.                                      \tag{3}
\]

Hence

\[
 \mu(p_2-p_1)=0,
 \qquad
 \mu(p_1-p_0)=m_1-m_0\ne0.                           \tag{4}
\]

The kernel of (3) is one-dimensional and generated by `p2-p1`.  Its two
endpoints retain precisely

```text
t_45*q_(v,23|67); removed/reinserted 45,
t_67*q_(v,23|45); removed/reinserted 67.              (5)
```

The labelled Boolean/Hasse cobar totalization supplies the associativity/
Beck--Chevalley cell between the two restriction/reinsertion factorizations
of the same top.  This is the source-side `phi12`: it fills (4) without
identifying `m0` and `m1` or quotienting coefficient `H0`.

This positive statement has the same scope as the pinned Hasse theorem.  It
does not itself map (5) to the physical PP/AugP2 target, `q`, anchor,
ordinary residue, `W`, or ridge rows.  That augmented comparison remains
the already isolated `U4` obligation.  Conditional equal protected values
give it no readout mismatch, but are not used to claim an unconditional
cap cell.

## 3. Why the site-permutation bar does not give `phi01`

For `phi01`, equation (4) says that restriction/reinsertion does not even
put the requested difference in the coefficient factorization kernel.  The
permutation `g01` sends `m0` to the different coefficient `m1`.

There are two familiar ways to treat this source isomorphism.

1. Retain the source-object label.  Transporting the moved tag back by the
   inverse identification makes the action bar boundary zero.
2. Forget the object label and fold the two coordinates.  This declares
   `m1-m0` a boundary.

The second operation changes fixed-source homology.  In the minimal quotient

```text
fixed coefficient coordinates m0,m1       H0 dimension 2,
after raw relation m1-m0                   H0 dimension 1.
```

The presentation-preserving graph cone instead adds `u01` and uses

\[
                       db_{01}=m_1-m_0-u_{01}.         \tag{6}
\]

It has three coordinates and one relation, so `H0` remains two-dimensional.
The graph coordinate is the proper face deleted by raw folding.  This is the
same pointed-object guard as the complete full-site chart-swap theorem, now
on the exact `h=4` tail-block permutation.

For comparison, raw folding of the two retained fine flags in (5) would
also reduce their two-dimensional pointed `H0` to one.  The difference is
that `p2-p1` is already a genuine coefficient factorization kernel and is
filled by the Hasse source resolution; `p1-p0` is not.

## 4. The connected root/Weyl alternative has a terminal aggregate

The only remaining existing source-provenant route to the changed word is
the local colour action.  Apply the simultaneous signed Weyl operation at
tail sites `2,4`.  It sends

```text
0121221222 -> -0121122222.                            (7)
```

The sign is the standard signed-Weyl orientation: exactly one of the two
sites starts in colour `1`.  Local root covariance is termwise on the
complete perfect-matching source, so (7) is a real source operation.

It is not target-safe.  On the ten-site GHZ tensor

\[
             \Delta=0^{10}+1^{10}+2^{10},
\]

the defect `(w-1)Delta` is a nonzero four-word vector.  It is invariant
under every permutation of the four tail sites `3,5,6,7` disjoint from the
two root sites, exactly as in the uniform physical Cartan-prism theorem.

Now retain all fifteen tail matchings.  Give a local root top at matching
`M` coefficient `a_M`.  Since every copy has the same nonzero target defect,
target safety inside the root/Weyl/restriction family forces

\[
                         \sum_M a_M=0.                 \tag{8}
\]

The space in (8) has rank fourteen.  Its canonical annihilator is

\[
                 \chi_{\rm match}(a)=\sum_Ma_M.       \tag{9}
\]

Every endpoint-odd Cartan prism has matching coefficient
`e_M-e_{sM}`, so (9) kills every such prism.  The checker exhausts the full
`S4` on the sites disjoint from the roots and all fifteen matchings.

The desired fixed-tail `phi01` has coefficient `e_T`, hence

\[
                         \chi_{\rm match}(e_T)=1.      \tag{10}
\]

Equations (8)--(10) are the exact first source-provenance obstruction.  A
target-safe root/Weyl combination can supply matching-odd differences but
cannot isolate the invariant fixed-tail word connection.  The existing
restriction/reinsertion operations are word diagonal and equivariant; they
add the lower Hasse faces but do not change this top matching-aggregate
quotient.

This is stronger than the empty-registry observation.  It grants every
complete root top on every tail matching and every target-safe linear
combination of them, then computes their exact rank and annihilator.

## 5. Triangle consequence

If the raw action-groupoid arrows were admitted, (1) would supply the usual
nerve two-simplex

\[
                   d\tau=\phi_{01}-\phi_{02}+\phi_{12}.\tag{11}
\]

That is an exact statement in the relabelled action groupoid.  It is not a
fixed-pointed-source construction.

In presentation-safe cones the edges instead have

\[
                  db_{ij}=(p_j-p_i)-u_{ij}.           \tag{12}
\]

Substituting (12) into (11) leaves the graph-coherence debt

\[
                         u_{01}-u_{02}+u_{12}.         \tag{13}
\]

The Hasse cell handles the source factorization part of `u12`.  No admitted
operation handles the invariant `u01` component detected by (9).  Therefore
there is no physical tree and no compatible physical `dTau` at present.

## Shortest positive theorem

The minimal new theorem is now one edge sharper:

> Construct a pointed, target-corrected physical word-changing comparison
> from `p0` to `p1` whose fixed-tail projection has
> `chi_match != 0`, while retaining the literal `t*q`, window, removal,
> reinsertion, and operation-family labels.

Once such a normalized `phi01` exists, relabeling naturality supplies its
`phi02` mate, the Hasse/Beck--Chevalley source supplies `phi12`, and the
mixed site/Hasse interchange is the exact remaining test for (11).

The sharp terminal alternative is to prove that the named complete
root/Weyl/Hasse/site-permutation operations exhaust the relevant physical
degree-one top quotient.  Then `chi_match` is a terminal dual.  The present
checker proves terminality only inside that named operation family; it does
not assume exhaustiveness against a new augmented comparison.

## Scope

The audit covers the fixed `23|45|67` fibre, all fifteen tail matchings,
all three presentation words/fine labels, actual decorated coefficient
monomials, the complete disjoint-tail `S4`, the signed two-root GHZ defect,
and the pointed `H0` ranks.  It applies independently to each of the four
collision operation families because all site/root moves fix the operation
ports.

the source-Hasse `phi12` to unconditional augmented cap readouts, or claim
that `chi_match` survives an as-yet-unwritten pointed target-corrected
source family.
