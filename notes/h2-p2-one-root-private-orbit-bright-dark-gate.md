# The eight `P2` one-root debts have three ambient word types but eight fixed-packet grades

## Result

For the literal lower packet

```text
sites          0,1,4,5
word           0 1 1 2
marked ports   0,1
residual       q45:12
reinsertion    q23:21
```

the first one-endpoint Hasse boundary occupies exactly the eight words

```text
0012  0102  0110  0111  0122  0212  1112  2112.       (1)
```

After quotienting the complete response line separately in each word, all
eight classes are nonzero and independent. Moreover every one is even under
ordered-endpoint reversal. Thus the already isolated endpoint-odd
active-clean fork has zero projection on the whole eight-class packet, not
just on the representative `0102` class.

There is useful symmetry, but it is weaker than a one-seed construction.
The site/colour stabilizer of the **unmarked** word `0112` is `V4`. Its
orbits on (1) are

\[
\begin{aligned}
 \mathcal O_{211}&=\{0012,0102,0122,0212\},\\
 \mathcal O_{310}&=\{0111,1112\},\\
 \mathcal O_{220}&=\{0110,2112\}.                    \tag{2}
\end{aligned}
\]

The subscripts are the sorted colour multiplicities. Hence no colour/site
relabeling can mix the three lines of (2). More strongly, once the marked
endpoint pair `01` and residual pair `45` are retained, the site/root
stabilizer is the identity. A nontrivial element of `V4` transports the
marked occurrence too; it gives a cell in a relabelled packet, not another
word block of the original packet.

Consequently:

- a natural theorem parameterized over the marked occurrence needs at least
  three root/site word types;
- without that natural marked-packet theorem, the fixed physical grade still
  asks for eight labelled sections; and
- one `0102` section plus covariance does **not** span the required packet.

## 1. Exact symmetry decomposition

Let `a` exchange the two sites carrying colour `1`. Let `b` exchange the
outer sites and simultaneously exchange colours `0` and `2`. These generate
the unmarked-word stabilizer

\[
                         \langle a,b\rangle\simeq V_4. \tag{3}
\]

On the eight wordwise private classes its permutation character, in the
order `(1,a,b,ab)`, is

\[
                              (8,4,0,0).               \tag{4}
\]

Thus the four rational characters `(a,b)=(+,+),(+,-),(-,+),(-,-)` occur
with multiplicities

\[
                               3,3,1,1.                \tag{5}
\]

Equivalently, the four-element orbit in (2) is the regular `V4` module and
each two-element orbit is induced from the subgroup generated by `a`.

The construction is genuinely covariant if one transports both the word
and the marked occurrence. The checker rebuilds the centered `B-4`
preimage for every transported marked occurrence and verifies the eight
one-root faces coefficientwise. This is precisely why that covariance does
not prove the fixed-packet claim: none of the three nonidentity elements of
`V4` preserves the selected unordered endpoint occurrence

\[
                         \{(0,1;45),(1,0;45)\}.        \tag{6}
\]

Endpoint reversal does preserve (6), but it acts in the twelve occurrence
coordinates within each word. Every private face is `S`-even, so this extra
involution generates no new word block.

## 2. Bright occurrence readout is not coordinate access

Let `r` be the `0102` occurrence vector and `r_priv` its projection off the
constant response line. In the canonical twelve-occurrence order, take

\[
                         m_{\rm bright}=e_0-e_1.       \tag{7}
\]

Then

\[
 \mathbf1^Tm_{\rm bright}=0,
 \qquad
 r_{\rm priv}^Tm_{\rm bright}=-{13\over12}.           \tag{8}
\]

The support of (7) is two, hence it is inclusion-minimal among nonzero
vectors in the kernel of the complete response row. This is the smallest
linear bright guard possible.

It does **not** give the minimum-support deletion/unit theorem used for the
physical endpoint map. That theorem concerns literal domain columns such as
`p_i[u,a]`. The coordinates in (7) are values of individual matching
monomials inside one aggregate response row. There is no pinned map sending
`e_0-e_1` to a literal domain column while preserving the word, fine grade,
target, Eq, anchor, residue, and physical `q` rows.

Thus even at minimum occurrence support,

\[
 \boxed{r_{\rm priv}^Tm\ne0
        \not\Rightarrow
        \text{physical coordinate deletion, unit, or active-clean exit}.} \tag{9}
\]

The obstruction is typing, not a lack of coefficient brightness. A positive
result needs the occurrence-local principal-parts section being sought, or a
comparison from this private quotient to the complete physical endpoint
Jacobian.

## 3. Lower darkness does not make the reinserted cell dark

The same phenomenon is sharper on the dark branch. Let `z_priv` be the
exact coefficient preimage from the preceding gate,

\[
                         (B-4I)z_{\rm priv}=r_{\rm priv}. \tag{10}
\]

Take

\[
                   m_{\rm dark}=-2e_0+15e_1-13e_2.   \tag{11}
\]

Direct calculation gives

\[
 \mathbf1^Tm_{\rm dark}=0,
 \qquad
 r_{\rm priv}^Tm_{\rm dark}=0,
 \qquad
 z_{\rm priv}^Tm_{\rm dark}=-{1\over8}.              \tag{12}
\]

The three displayed columns form a circuit for the two rows
`(1,r_priv)`: every two-column restriction has rank two. Therefore (11) is
support-minimal subject to both the complete response equation and lower
private darkness.

After reinserting `q23:21`, the Hasse product rule is

\[
                 d(q a)=q\,da+(dq)\,a.                \tag{13}
\]

The first two equalities in (12) kill the evaluated lower occurrence debt,
but the final equality leaves the independent

\[
                         dq_{23}^{21}\otimes z_{\rm priv} \tag{14}

\]

face bright. Setting the other seven word evaluations to zero gives an
exact presentation-level packet in which all eight lower private readouts
are dark while (14) survives. Hence the answer to “does lower darkness land
`P2` modulo the physical fibre?” is **no** without one further hypothesis.
One must also kill the forced `dq` readouts.

If both the eight lower readouts and every corresponding reinsertion
conormal readout vanish at a given physical source, then the evaluated
associated-graded `P2` boundary does land modulo the complete response
rows. This is a pointwise landing, not a uniform source chain or a section
valid in a neighbourhood.

## 4. The exact active-clean/terminal fork

Let `J_priv` denote the projection of the **complete augmented physical
source map**, including `dq23`, to the direct sum of the eight private word
quotients and their reinsertion conormals. Let `R_P2` be the displayed
private `P2` boundary packet. Finite-dimensional duality gives the exact
alternative

\[
 \begin{array}{ll}
 R_{P2}\in\operatorname{im}J_{priv}
   &\Longrightarrow\text{a physical private section},\\[2mm]
 R_{P2}\notin\operatorname{im}J_{priv}
   &\Longrightarrow\exists\lambda:\lambda J_{priv}=0,
                                      \ \lambda R_{P2}\ne0.
 \end{array}                                           \tag{15}
\]

The second line is only a private-quotient separator until `lambda` is
shown to extend across every protected/anchor/target/Eq/residue/physical-`q`
row of the full source map. The first placement gate already supplies such
a separator for the displayed three-term cone, but not that extension.

Nor does (15) enter the current active-clean theorem automatically: all
eight classes lie in the endpoint-even sector, while the typed oriented
active-clean carrier is endpoint-odd. The shortest remaining theorem is
therefore one of:

1. construct the endpoint-even occurrence-local one-endpoint PP section,
   including its `dq23` face, in each of the three natural word types; or
2. promote a surviving covector in (15) to a literal full augmented
   physical terminal.

## Scope

Equations (7) and (11) are sharp rational occurrence-fibre counterguards.
They are deliberately **not** claimed to arise from an optical source under
the nonlinear monomial evaluation map. That missing realization is exactly
why the physical minimum-support theorem cannot currently be applied. This
note proves the symmetry, parity, rank, and evaluation implications; it
does not construct the three physical PP cell types or a Fredholm terminal.
