# Cancelling the monic pure-anchor conormal is already a source unit

## Outcome

The missing `h=3` attaching row cannot be a new universal polynomial
syzygy among the existing physical source equations.  There is an exact
equivalence.

Let `u` be the homogenizing coordinate for the selected pure target and

\[
                            F_0=H-u.                    \tag{1}
\]

Let `I` be the ideal generated by all physical Eq rows which have zero
selected-`u` conormal: all mixed full-nine rows, the other normalized pure
anchors, middle-word rows, response companions, and ordinary chart/normal
corrections after their selected-anchor incidences have been separated.
The generators of `I` need not be target-zero individually; their physical
target cancellations remain part of any proposed chain.  Then for every
coefficient `kappa` in the source ring,

\[
              \boxed{\quad \kappa F_0\in I[u]
                     \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
                     \kappa\in I.\quad}                \tag{2}
\]

On the active chart `kappa != 0`, localizing `kappa` turns (2) into

\[
             \kappa F_0\in I[u]
             \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad 1\in I_\kappa. \tag{3}

Therefore an underived source chain which cancels the class
`kappa[F_0]` is already an ordinary localized unit certificate in the
remaining source-row ideal.  It is not a weaker intermediate construction.

This sharply changes the search target.  Another Ward identity, Hasse cube,
matching circuit, or chart Bianchi relation can only work if it already
proves the packet empty.  The alternatives are:

1. derive a packet-conditioned ordinary source unit/saturation; or
2. construct genuinely new resolution data whose differential contains an
   independent `u`-linear lower face.

The second option is not present in the committed physical source module.

## 1. General algebra lemma

Let `A` be any commutative ring, let `I` be any ideal of `A`, and work in
`A[u]`.  For `H,kappa in A`, reduce a proposed membership

\[
                         \kappa(H-u)\in I A[u]          \tag{4}
\]

modulo `I`.  In `(A/I)[u]`, the coefficient of `u` in the left side is
`-kappa`.  Thus (4) implies `kappa=0` in `A/I`, which is exactly
`kappa in I`.  The converse is immediate.  No domain, reducedness, or
genericity hypothesis is used.

Equivalently, `H-u` is monic and hence a non-zero-divisor in `R[u]` for
every coefficient ring `R`.  The coefficient proof is even sharper here
because `kappa` has no `u` dependence.

The same statement allows polynomial multipliers on the other rows.  It
also allows arbitrarily many presentation copies of `F_0`: after chart
differences redistribute their coefficients, only their total coefficient
multiplies the common global polynomial `H-u`.

## 2. Physical application

The exact source-base-change audit constructs

\[
 N=Y\mathcal M-\kappa Y\rho-\kappa T+\kappa r_0
\]

with

\[
 (d,\operatorname{tgt},\operatorname{ores})N
       =(\kappa F_0 e_{\rm Eq}+\kappa Yw,0,0).          \tag{5}
\]

After imposing `F_0=0`, (5) looks like the desired attaching chain.  Before
base change, cancelling its first term with existing physical rows would
give exactly the membership on the left of (2).  The literal `6561`-word
census proves that every mixed row is in `I`; only the pure selected anchor
has nonzero selected-`u` coefficient.

Adjacent-chart comparisons do not evade (2).  With one conormal coordinate
per chart, a complete candidate is `e_c+w`, while a comparison is
`e_d-e_c`.  The functional

\[
                   (x_1,\ldots,x_r,w)\longmapsto
                         \sum_c x_c-w                  \tag{6}
\]

kills every such column and detects the desired conormal-free `w` boundary.
Thus comparisons can move the coefficient of `F_0` between chart labels,
but its total remains the normalized boundary.  The complementary-anchor
covariance audit gives the same invariant across colours.

If a physical construction produces a lower face with total anchor
incidence `-kappa`, zero `w`, target, and ordinary residue, its Eq projection
must violate (6).  By (2), that violation is precisely a localized unit in
the remaining source rows unless the construction adds a genuinely new
`u`-linear generator.

## 3. Consequence for priorities

The rootless attaching problem should no longer be phrased as searching for
an arbitrary universal nullhomotopy of `K`.  In the current source
presentation such a nullhomotopy is theorem-equivalent to packet emptiness.
The useful positive targets are more concrete:

* use the literal two-chart/full-nine equations and activity localization to
  prove `kappa in I` on each normalized structural packet; or
* enlarge the source resolution and define, with full target and residue
  typing, a new generator not built as a polynomial combination of the
  existing rows.

Formal higher Hasse, covariance, and signed-circuit operations which descend
only after setting `F_0=0` remain tautological Tor and cannot satisfy (2).
