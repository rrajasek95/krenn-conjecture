# An exact localized radical counterexample on the eight-site chart

## Result

For the first \(n=8\) vertex-to-triangle expansion of the prism, let
\(I_{\mathrm{mix}}\) be the ideal generated by all non-pure eight-site
hafnian coefficients in the full ring of \(28\cdot9=252\) arbitrary
endpoint-colour variables.  Let \(P_G\) be the product of the twelve
properly coloured boundary coordinates.

There is an explicit five-parameter Laurent family on which

\[
  H_c=0\quad\text{for every mixed word }c,
  \qquad P_G=1.
\]

Consequently

\[
  \boxed{P_G\notin\sqrt{I_{\mathrm{mix}}}.}
\]

This is a statement about the full \(n=8\) mixed ideal, not merely a
one-hot specialization.  It follows by evaluation: if some power
\(P_G^N\) belonged to \(I_{\mathrm{mix}}\), the displayed common zero would
give \(1=0\).

The family has pure coefficient tuple \((0,0,1)\).  It is therefore a
decisive counterexample to this proposed source-ideal radical containment,
but not yet a three-pure GHZ/Krenn counterexample.

## Coordinates

Use the port torus to normalize the twelve boundary-support variables to
one:

```text
0100 0311 0722 1422 1611 2322
2411 2500 3400 5622 5711 6700
```

As in the preceding audits, `uvab` denotes \(x_{uv}^{ab}\).  Let
\(a,b,c,d,e\) be nonzero parameters.  The only other nonzero coordinates
are

\[
\begin{array}{c|c@{\qquad}c|c}
x_{02}^{10}&a & x_{02}^{11}&b\\
x_{03}^{10}&c & x_{15}^{11}&d\\
x_{16}^{10}&e & x_{24}^{00}&a/c\\
x_{24}^{01}&a/b & x_{24}^{10}&b/c\\
x_{25}^{10}&b/a & x_{34}^{01}&c/b\\
x_{34}^{10}&1/c & x_{34}^{11}&1/b\\
x_{35}^{00}&-c/a & x_{35}^{10}&-1/a\\
x_{57}^{10}&-d/e & x_{67}^{01}&-e/d\\
x_{67}^{10}&1/e & x_{67}^{11}&-1/d.
\end{array}
\]

Every unlisted coordinate is zero.  Thus the point uses only 30 nonzero
coordinates: the twelve boundary variables and 18 extras.  All 30 belong
to the audited 60-edge support of the sparse degree-one dual.

The rational specialization

\[
  a=b=c=d=e=1
\]

already suffices.  At that point the first twelve displayed extra
coordinates are \(+1\), followed by

```text
3500=-1 3510=-1 5710=-1 6701=-1 6710=+1 6711=-1.
```

Hence this is a literal rational common zero, not only a point over a
function field.

## Exact verification

For each of the \(3^8=6{,}561\) colour words, the checker expands all 105
perfect-matching terms.  A nonzero coordinate is represented as a sign and
an exponent vector in \((a,b,c,d,e)\).  Terms with equal Laurent exponent
are collected over the integers.  The result is:

* all \(3^8-3=6{,}558\) mixed coefficients are the zero Laurent polynomial;
* \(P_G=1\) identically; and
* the three pure coefficients are \((0,0,1)\).

This replay uses exact integer exponent arithmetic and does not rely on the
Gröbner calculation that led to the family.

The pure values are also visible directly.  On this support,

\[
\begin{aligned}
H_{00000000}
 &=1+x_{24}^{00}x_{35}^{00}
   =1+(a/c)(-c/a)=0,\\
H_{11111111}
 &=(1+x_{02}^{11}x_{34}^{11})
   (1+x_{15}^{11}x_{67}^{11})\\
 &=(1+b/b)(1-d/d)=0,\\
H_{22222222}&=1.
\end{aligned}
\]

## How the family emerged

The previous exact search excluded every support with at most 13 extras.
The same Boolean-support and odd-binomial filters continue to exclude all
admissible supports through 17 extras.  At 18 extras, there are 3,724
Boolean-admissible supports in four symmetry-related families.  All but one
support orbit retain a three-binomial \(1=-1\) obstruction.  The exceptional
representative is

```text
0210 0211 0310 1511 1610 2400 2401 2410 2510
3401 3410 3411 3500 3510 5710 6701 6710 6711
```

Its localized equations instead reduce to a nonempty five-dimensional
torus, parametrized above.  This explains both the sharp sparsity jump and
the five free parameters.

## What this does and does not settle

The source-ideal route cannot prove the desired boundary-product radical
containment: that containment is false already for this \(n=8\) chart.
Any proof strategy that needs

\[
  P_G\in\sqrt{I_{\mathrm{mix}}}
\]

must therefore be abandoned or strengthened with additional hypotheses,
such as requiring all three pure coefficients to be nonzero.

The next meaningful question is geometric rather than ideal-membership:
does the mixed fibre through this torus family have a deformation on which
the two vanishing pure coefficients become nonzero while the third remains
nonzero?  A tangent/Jacobian calculation can decide whether such directions
are visible infinitesimally.  The present family itself does not answer that
question.
