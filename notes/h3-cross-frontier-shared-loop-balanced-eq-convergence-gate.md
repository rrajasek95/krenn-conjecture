# Gate I and the balanced square share a coefficient shadow, not a source column

## Verdict

The apparent cross-frontier convergence is real at the level of one
grade-forgotten coefficient formula, but it is not a source-valid
construction.

For a normalized Gate-I six-label direction `u`, the strongest audited
shared-loop near-hit—already overgranting the unproved labelwise residue
section—has

\[
                   x_u=(B=u,\ Eq=u,\operatorname{ainc}=-1),       \tag{1}
\]

and the missing correction is

\[
                   c_u=(B=0,\ Eq=-u,\operatorname{ainc}=+1).      \tag{2}
\]

After an abstract four-orbit identification, the `delta`-weighted sum of
four copies of (2) is exactly the balanced Eq-only filler `Eq=-delta`.
But the same sum of these strongest near-hits (1) is the tied packet
`B=Eq=delta`, which has

\[
                         \delta\cdot(B-Eq)=0.                       \tag{3}
\]

The shore-sign gauge preserves the tie, and the physical `q` identity has
zero `B/Eq` projection.  Moreover the two packets have different literal
word, fine, repeated-placement, and operation-parent tags.  No committed
chain map supplies the four-orbit identification.  Thus the common datum is
precisely the still-missing source-labelled mixed-incidence `kappa_mix`
column, not a composite of the present near-hit, gauge, and `q` identity.

## 1. The exact coefficient coincidence

The four normalized Gate-I repair directions are

\[
 e_1,\qquad e_4,\qquad {e_0+e_5\over2},\qquad
 {e_2+e_3\over2}.                                      \tag{4}
\]

They are respectively the two fixed repairs and the two paired repairs in
the canonical six-label component.  Define the grade-forgetting linear map

\[
 P(u)=(u_1,u_4,u_0+u_5,u_2+u_3).                       \tag{5}
\]

It sends the four vectors in (4) to the four standard corner units.  Hence
for `delta=(1,1,-1,-1)`,

\[
 u_\delta=e_1+e_4-{e_0+e_5\over2}-{e_2+e_3\over2},
 \qquad \sum_i(u_\delta)_i=0,
 \qquad P(u_\delta)=\delta.                            \tag{6}
\]

Linearly extending (1)--(2) from normalized vectors gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
 P(x_{u_\delta})&=(B,Eq,\operatorname{ainc})
                         =(\delta,\delta,0),\\
 P(c_{u_\delta})&=(0,-\delta,0),\\
 P(x_{u_\delta}+c_{u_\delta})&=(\delta,0,0).           \tag{7}
\end{aligned}
\]

So the external audit found the correct algebraic normal form.  The
load-bearing qualification is that the second line of (7) is a sum of four
copies of the **missing** correction.  It is not generated by the first
line.

## 2. Gauge and physical `q` do not create the missing incidence

In the balanced corner order

```text
DQ[a|b], DQ[b|a], PS[P0,S1], PS[P1,S0],
```

the shore gauge is `diag(1,1,-1,-1)`.  Applying it to both literal blocks
in the near-hit gives

```text
before gauge    B=( 1, 1,-1,-1), Eq=( 1, 1,-1,-1)
after gauge     B=( 1, 1, 1, 1), Eq=( 1, 1, 1, 1).
```

Thus it is a change of corner coordinates, not a new differential.  It
preserves `B=Eq` coefficientwise.

The literal physical row has signature

```text
(M,ainc,q)=(1,-1,-1)
```

and zero `B/Eq` projection.  It can repair scalar `q`/anchor bookkeeping
inside a fixed source grade but cannot change (3), add an Eq incidence, or
transport a column to a different word/operation block.

The checker adjoins all four diagonal cap rows, the four `K2,2` companions,
the `delta` near-hit, and the physical `q` identity.  Every column has
`chi=delta.(B-Eq)=0`; their exact projected rank stays one below the
balanced class.  Adjoining either `(B,Eq)=(delta,0)` or `(0,-delta)` raises
the rank by one.  This is the smallest linear counterguard to the proposed
composition.

## 4. Sharp remaining common theorem

A genuine common column would be a source-labelled
response-to-`AugP2` mixed-incidence cell with

\[
                 \Pi_{B/Eq}(\kappa)=(0,-\delta)
                 \quad\text{or equivalently }(\delta,0),             \tag{8}
\]

and whose four Gate-I restrictions have the complete signatures (2), with
literal `q`/anchor compatibility on the same cell.  It must also provide a
word/fine/repeated/operation-preserving placement chain map realizing (5).

That statement is exactly the unclassified `kappa_mix` family in the
source-grade terminal census.  Constructing it would indeed close both
frontiers.  The present identities do not construct it; conversely, a proof
that every such same-grade mixed cell is `chi`-dark promotes the balanced
terminal.

The smallest logical counterguard is the direct sum of the two committed
source presentations with their literal grade idempotents.  All stated
near-hit, shore-gauge, and physical-`q` identities hold in their respective
summands, while no cross idempotent arrow exists and the balanced class
survives.  Therefore the apparent convergence is only a projection
coincidence until the placement chain map is supplied.
