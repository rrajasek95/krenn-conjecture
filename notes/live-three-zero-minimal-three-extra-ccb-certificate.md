# The three CCB boundary cells are uniformly injective

## 1. Outcome

The complete response has rank \(19\) everywhere on each of

\[
                         CCB,\qquad CBC,\qquad BCC.
\]

Every maximal minor used below avoids source pair \(01\).  The conclusion
therefore allows an arbitrary scale for the direct \(B_{01}\) term.

The three placements are checked independently.  In the common coordinates
\((x,y,z,u,v)\), their actual affine parameters and structural zero are

\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{cell}&(x,y,z,u,v)&\text{structural zero}\\ \hline
CCB&(a,b,c,d,e)&f=0\\
CBC&(a,b,e,f,c)&d=0\\
BCC&(c,d,e,f,a)&b=0.
\end{array}                                                     \tag{1}
\]

After these substitutions, the origin minor has the same squarefree support
in all three cells:

\[
 (y+1)(u+1)TQP,                                                 \tag{2}
\]

where

\[
 T=x(2v+1)+2,\qquad Q=yu+3y+3u+6,                              \tag{3}
\]

and \(P\in\mathbb Q[x,y,z,u,v]\) has total degree eight and is linear in
\(z\).  Equality of the three standardized polynomials \(P\), not merely
equality up to a numerical sample, is checked symbolically.

## 2. Exact branch cover

The four elementary components of (2),

\[
                    y=-1,\qquad u=-1,\qquad T=0,\qquad Q=0,     \tag{4}
\]

have exact restricted maximal-minor ideals equal to \((1)\).  Rational
substitution is used on \(T=0\) and \(Q=0\), and the resulting denominator is
included in the localization.

It remains to work on \(P=0\), away from the factors already closed in (4).
The two exceptional coordinate values factor as

\[
 P|_{y=-2}=-z(u+1)(v+1)F,\qquad
 P|_{u=-2}=-x(y+1)(v+1)G                                    \tag{5}
\]

for explicit residual polynomials \(F\) and \(G\).  After removing the
already-covered \(y=-1\) and \(u=-1\) pieces, the checker proves exact
localized unit ideals on the six components

\[
\begin{array}{lll}
y=-2, z=0,&y=-2, v=-1,&y=-2, F=0,\\
u=-2, x=0,&u=-2, v=-1,&u=-2, G=0.
\end{array}                                                     \tag{6}
\]

On the complement \((y+2)(u+2)\ne0\), the remaining \(P=0\) locus is the
disjoint four-part cover

\[
 x=0;\qquad x\ne0, z=0;\qquad xz\ne0, v=0;\qquad xzv\ne0.   \tag{7}
\]

For every placement and every part of (6)--(7), the ideal generated by
\(P\), the relevant component equation, and the selected exact maximal
minors becomes \((1)\) after localization by precisely the factors declared
nonzero.  Equations (4), (6), and (7) exhaust the zero locus of (2), so at
least one direct-free maximal minor is nonzero at every point of each cell.

## 3. Exact audit

[verify_live_three_zero_minimal_three_extra_ccb_cells.py](../computations/verify_live_three_zero_minimal_three_extra_ccb_cells.py)
reconstructs the response and runs the entire branch cover separately for
CCB, CBC, and BCC.  All determinants are computed by fraction-free
elimination over \(\mathbb Q\), and every final ideal or localized ideal is
checked over \(\mathbb Q\).

Some response-row bases were discovered at points over
\(\mathbf F_{17}\) or \(\mathbf F_{19}\).  Those finite-field points select row
labels only: the checker rebuilds every selected determinant over
\(\mathbb Q\) before it enters a certificate.  The deterministic discovery
data and the origin factorization are recorded in
[explore_live_three_zero_minimal_three_extra_ccb.py](../computations/explore_live_three_zero_minimal_three_extra_ccb.py).
