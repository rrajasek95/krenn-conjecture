# The eta law is affine, not a source character

## Exact conclusion

On the marked clean-C5 open, write

\[
 t=q_{pq}^{00},\qquad u_v=q_{xv}^{00}.
\]

The five normalized physical stabilizers obey

\[
 \eta_z(t)=1,\qquad
 \eta_z(u_v)=-\delta_{zv}u_z/t.                       \tag{1}
\]

The compensating law from `68e1593` integrates exactly, but it is not a
multiplicative torus character.  Its unique affine primitive is

\[
 C=5t-\sum_vu_v,\qquad c_v=t-u_v,                    \tag{2}
\]

and

\[
 \eta_z(C)=5+u_z/t,\qquad
 \eta_z(c_v)=1+\delta_{zv}u_z/t.                     \tag{3}
\]

This determines the value which a physical rootless comparison must carry.
It does not construct that comparison.

## No Laurent/logarithmic character

For a Laurent character

\[
                         M=t^a\prod_vu_v^{b_v},
\]

equation (1) gives

\[
                         \eta_z(\log M)={a-b_z\over t}. \tag{4}
\]

The numerator in (4) is an integer; its Laurent support is only
\(\{t^{-1}\}\).  The required law has support
\(\{1,u_z/t\}\).  Hence no Laurent character has the desired logarithmic
derivative.  The derivative \(\eta_z(M)\) itself is one Laurent monomial
(or zero), so it cannot equal the two-term law either.

The five character-weight rows are

\[
                 a-b_1,\ldots,a-b_5.                 \tag{5}
\]

They have rank five.  Their invariant lattice is generated by

\[
                         K=t\prod_vu_v.               \tag{6}
\]

Consequently every Laurent-polynomial primitive of (3) is

\[
                         C+\Phi(K),                   \tag{7}
\]

for a Laurent polynomial \(\Phi\).  Formula (2) is the unique affine one.

## Why the affine primitive is not the physical lift

First, the full target-stabilizer kernel already excludes a scalar repair.
The stabilizer with weights

\[
                  \lambda_{p,2}=1,\qquad\lambda_{x,2}=-1
\]

fixes every \(t,u_v\), hence fixes (2) and every (7), but it sends the
formally corrected endpoint aggregate to

\[
 d\left(\sum_v\Omega_v+C\right)=5q_{pq}^{22}.         \tag{8}
\]

Thus the marked scalar primitive is not a well-defined terminal functional
on the complete physical source cokernel.

Second, it has the wrong source degree.  In the full endpoint/site grading,

\[
 \deg(t)=e_p+e_q,\qquad \deg(u_v)=e_x+e_v.            \tag{9}
\]

Adding the same internal \(P_3\sqcup K_2\) tail \(N\) to both terms leaves
the difference in (9) unchanged.  The smallest ordinary polynomial
homogenization uses complementary factors and is tautological:

\[
                         t(u_vN)-u_v(tN)=0.            \tag{10}
\]

A nonzero lift must therefore retain the endpoint-bar order/source label
or use distinct labelled matching tails.  It is a relative chain, not a
coefficient-ring character.  This is the precise qualification missing
from the shorthand `(t-u_v)Q_v`: that expression records the required
normalized value, not a homogeneous physical source cell.

Here the endpoint and rootless presentations have the same
