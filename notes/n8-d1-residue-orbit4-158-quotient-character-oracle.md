# O4 158-cell quotient-character oracle

Quotient every Laurent monomial by the exponent lattice generated by the
available plus binomials, carrying along its exact multiplicative character.
A reduced two-class generator is then a signed directed edge between two
quotient characters.  Three graph-local events give exact units:

1. a one-class row is already a Laurent monomial;
2. parallel edges with different constants cancel to a Laurent monomial;
3. a closed walk whose character product is not one is a Laurent unit.

These are ordinary algebraic identities, not solver heuristics: expanding the
edge reductions through their parent binomials and clearing denominators gives
an ordinary `U^k` certificate.  This checker tests the graph on the fourth
158-cell O4 frontier.  It finds 12 one-class rows and 12 opposite-character
parallel pairs.  The selected coefficient-one row expands to a five-generator,
10-term ordinary `U^2` identity valid in every characteristic.

The calculation supports, but does not yet prove for every O4 downset, the
proposed finite signed-group-algebra dichotomy.  The first survivor with none
of the three events must be frozen as a genuine quotient-character graph
frontier rather than declared coefficient-feasible.

```bash
python3 computations/verify_n8_d1_residue_orbit4_158_quotient_character_oracle.py
python3 -O computations/verify_n8_d1_residue_orbit4_158_quotient_character_oracle.py
```
