# The two missing pure coefficients lie in the mixed ideal modulo \(\mathfrak m^5\)

## Exact local statement

Let (p) be the rational point on the exceptional five-parameter mixed
torus, let (I_{\rm mix}) be the ideal generated by all mixed hafnian
coefficients in the translated 252-variable ring, and let
\(\mathfrak m_p\) be the maximal ideal of (p).  The exact
degree-by-degree calculation proves

\[
 \boxed{H_0,H_1\in I_{\rm mix}+\mathfrak m_p^5.}
\tag{1}
\]

This is stronger than fifth-order vanishing on formal arcs.  It gives
literal polynomial combinations of mixed equations whose differences from
the two pure coefficients have no translated homogeneous terms of degrees
zero through four.

## Provenance chain

The certificate retains exact rational provenance at every layer:

1. The 1,312 nonzero mixed gradients have rank 196.  Every conormal pivot
   is stored as a combination of literal mixed gradients.
2. The quadratic second-lift obstruction space has rank 39.  Every
   obstruction basis row used in a quartic reduction is stored as a
   combination of cokernel-coordinate functionals.
3. Back substitution through the mixed Jacobian turns each such functional
   into a rational combination of literal mixed hafnian equations whose
   gradient vanishes and whose quadratic tangent restriction is exactly the
   desired obstruction row.
4. After those literal obstruction equations are subtracted, each remaining
   ambient quartic vanishes on the 56-dimensional tangent space and is
   divided exactly through the 196 conormals, again with literal provenance.

Thus no tangent-space or arc-only assertion is silently promoted to ideal
membership.

## Colour zero

The five quadratic conormal products and the 33-conormal cubic correction
remove all terms below degree four.  The quartic tangent rectangle is a
single multiple of the obstruction

\[
 z_{0400}(z_{3710}+z_{3711}).
\]

Its literal lift is especially small:

\[
 \frac12\left(H_{01000000}+H_{01000001}\right).
\tag{2}
\]

The gradient of (2) vanishes at (p), and its quadratic tangent part is
the displayed obstruction.  Subtracting the corresponding quadratic
multiple leaves an ambient quartic with zero tangent restriction.  Exact
division clears it through the mixed Jacobian, leaving only degree at
least five.

## Colour one

After the nine quadratic conormal products and first cubic division, the
four-term cubic remainder is lifted by the literal mixed equation

\[
 H_{11001001}-H_{11000001}.
\tag{3}
\]

The remaining ambient cubic vanishes on the tangent space and divides
through 12 conormals with 36 multiplier terms.  The new quartic restricts
to a 26-term tangent polynomial.  Exact reduction writes it in the ideal
of the rank-39 quadratic obstruction basis.  Every obstruction row used is
then lifted to literal mixed equations as above, and the final ambient
normal quartic divides through the Jacobian with zero remainder.

This algebraic construction is the full-equation counterpart of the
weighted jet identity

\[
 P_4+z_{3511}K_3\in(\mathcal O_1,\ldots,\mathcal O_{39}).
\]

## Why this is an actual \(\mathfrak m\)-adic certificate

All corrections are polynomial multipliers times full mixed hafnian
coefficients, hence belong to (I_{\rm mix}).  The final differences have
zero homogeneous parts below degree five, hence belong to
(\mathfrak m_p^5).  This proves (1) directly in the translated polynomial
ring.

The five-parameter family is a diagonal port-torus orbit.  Transporting
the rational coefficients by their Laurent characters gives the same
modulo-\(\mathfrak m^5\) statement at every generic point of that orbit.

## Consequence and frontier

The osculation pattern now has an ideal-theoretic form, not merely an arc
interpretation.  Iterating the construction amounts to a local
standard-basis computation: normal-coordinate leading forms are cleared by
the 196 conormal lifts, tangent leading forms by literal lifts of the 39
quadratic obstructions, and any new higher initial generators must arise as
S-polynomials among those tangent quadrics.

The next exact test is therefore to compute the higher initial ideal of the
39 sparse quadrics, retain provenance for its new generators, and continue
the normal form to orders six and beyond.  Stabilization to zero would give
formal local membership; a surviving normal form would identify the first
genuine obstruction to an all-orders proof.
