# Signed matching holonomy: programme note

Date: 2026-08-11.  Status: **programme document — definitions, two
reformulations of proved facts, and named open problems.  No new claims.**
This is the uniform-statement track promised in the
[reformulation survey](2026-08-11-external-theory-reformulation-survey.md)
§1.1–1.2.  It does not replace the A/B/C frontier
([difficulty map](2026-08-11-proof-remainder-difficulty-map.md)); it is the
horizon behind it: the theory of which the certificate corpus (D1, chart 26,
the silent-`C6` odd-holonomy closures) consists of instances.

## 1. Objects

Fix even `N` and palette `{0,1,2}` (general `d` is analogous).  Cells
`a_uv(i,j)`, coefficients `H_chi = sum_M m_chi(M)` with
`m_chi(M) = prod_{uv in M} a_uv(chi_u, chi_v)` — one monomial per
**occurrence** `(chi, M)`.

**Flip groupoid `G_N`.**  Objects: occurrences `(chi, M)`.  Generating
morphisms: `(chi, C)` for `C` an `M`-alternating cycle, with target
`(chi, M xor C)`.  This is fibred over the colourings; each fibre is the
matching flip graph of `K_N`, connected and (for `N = 8`) an expander under
2-switches, and Hamilton-connected [Brenner et al., arXiv:2607.04687].

**Exponent local system `E`.**  On the generator `(chi, C)`, the ratio
`m_chi(M xor C)/m_chi(M)` is a Laurent monomial supported on the cells of
`C` under `chi`.  `E` assigns this class in `Z^{cells}`; composing along a
loop of flips gives the loop's **holonomy exponent**.

**Gauge descent.**  The torus `(C^*)^{V x 3}` acts by
`a_uv(i,j) -> b_{u,i} b_{v,j} a_uv(i,j)`; within a fixed `chi` every
occurrence scales identically, so `E` descends to the gauge-invariant
lattice

```
L = ker( Z^{cells} --unsigned incidence--> Z^{V x 3} ),
```

which is **Zaslavsky's even-cycle (frame) lattice** of the cell graph:
rank `|cells| - |V x 3|` (= 228 at `N = 8`, verified in the survey §2),
generated by even cycles **and odd-cycle handcuffs**.  The handcuffs exist
only because the cell graph is non-bipartite; they are the carriers of
every `1 = -1` certificate in the corpus.

**Supports and signed partial characters.**  A support `S` is a set of
cells allowed nonzero.  A solution with support `S` restricts, on each
mixed fibre, to a vanishing sum of live monomials.  Two-term fibres force
`m = -m'`; more generally the corpus's exact certificates are generated by
relations `rho(lambda) = epsilon(lambda)` for `lambda` in a sublattice
`L_S <= L`, with sign `epsilon: L_S -> {+-1}` — an
**Eisenbud–Sturmfels partial character together with a `{+-1}` central
extension**.  Precise formats: the integral HNF discipline of the
[roadmap](2026-08-09-resolution-roadmap.md) §2, the character graphs of the
D1 orbit-4 notes, and the Laurent group-algebra certificates
`sum h_i E_i = 1` of the external `(6,3)` proof.

## 2. Two reformulations of proved facts

**pi_0 (Bogdanov / three-one-factors).**  Three pairwise edge-disjoint
monochromatic perfect matchings on `>= 6` vertices force a fourth perfect
matching in their union — i.e. *every admissible support has a live mixed
occurrence*.  This is the degree-zero statement of the theory: the mixed
part of `G_N` is nonvoid.  It is why nonnegative weights settle the
conjecture (no cancellation can kill the forced occurrence) and why the
complex case is exactly a holonomy problem: cancellation is possible only
if the forced occurrences can be arranged in nontrivially-signed classes.

**Obstruction mechanisms (the certificate dichotomy).**  Every exact
support kill in the current corpus is one of:

- **(O1) odd holonomy** — `lambda in L_S` with trivial exponent dependency
  and `epsilon(lambda) = -1` (a `1 = -1` certificate);
- **(O2) singleton class** — a mixed fibre whose live terms occupy one
  class of the `L_S`-character quotient (a nonzero monomial cannot vanish;
  the unique-perfect-matching obstruction is the one-term case);
- **(O3) an ordinary integral certificate on a visible-cell atom** (a `U^k`
  unit not of character type).

The empirical content of two years of CEGAR (D1 through the 157/158-cell
layers; chart-26 blocks 0–8: 11,578 supports, 8,523 sign units, 3,055
one-class units, **zero integral-HNF third types**) is that (O1)/(O2)
exhaust the character-visible kills and (O3) covers a finite remainder.

## 3. The open uniform statements

**Problem 1 (propagation lemma / oracle conjecture).**  For `N >= 8`:
every support surviving the necessary support closures carries (O1), (O2),
or one of finitely many (O3) atoms.  This is the repo's oracle conjecture
([roadmap](2026-08-09-resolution-roadmap.md) §2 D1) stated as a theorem
target of the present theory.  The refined form must retain the **full
signed Laurent row matroid**: commit `f3641c7` exhibits two repairs with
identical unsigned data split by (O1) vs (O2), so no unsigned statement
can be correct.

**Problem 2 (H^1 formulation).**  Define the obstruction class of a
support as the class of `epsilon` in the group cohomology of `L_S` (equiv.
the holonomy of the sign local system on the flip complex).  Conjecture:
for `N >= 8` the class is nontrivial on every admissible support — with
Bogdanov as pi_0 and this as the H^1 statement.  Structural input wanted:
Lovász matching-lattice theory (bricks/braces, tight cuts; constructive
basis per Silina arXiv:2511.03863) to control `L_S` across supports, and
the parity-binomial-edge-ideal family [Kahle–Sarmiento–Windisch,
arXiv:1503.00584] as the worked model of the (O1)-vs-char-2 phenomenon.

**Problem 3 (exact image / realizability).**  Characterize
`Im_{N,d} = { H(A) } subset (C^d)^{tensor N}` — the general-graph analogue
of the matchgate-identity theorem (planar case: the spinor variety;
general case confirmed open, the planned Landsberg–Morton characterization
never appeared).  Known here: the closure contains `Delta_{N,3}`
([tensor-route Border theorem](tensor-route.md) §6), so the answer is
necessarily constructible-set, not variety-theoretic; the one-hot source
cycle separator ([frontier](consolidated-proof-frontier.md) §4) is a
candidate first boundary invariant.  Krenn–Gu is the statement
`Delta_{N,3} notin Im_{N,3}` for `N >= 6`; a realizability theory would
settle the family wholesale and give photonic graph experiments their
design rules.

**Problem 4 (signed matching cohomology).**  Develop `H^k` of the flip
complex with phase local systems for its own sake: behaviour under tight
cuts and brick decomposition, a phase-decorated matching invariant,
relation to Norine's k-Pfaffian hierarchy (mu_n-labelings; Pfaffian number
now known unbounded, arXiv:2603.00334, arXiv:2605.21077).  Small-graph
computations are cheap and would calibrate whether `H^{>=2}` carries
content.

**Problem 5 (source-constrained homological perturbation).**  Formalize
transfer along a deformation retract whose contraction and homotopies are
constrained to a subcategory of source-provenant maps, with
zero-indeterminacy as vanishing of one relative `Ext^1`.  The augmented
HPL lemma and Schur–Bockstein criterion are its first two computed pages;
Sköldberg's ADMT-from-HPL (arXiv:1311.5803) is the unconstrained scaffold.
This is the abstract home of Theorem B/C's "physically typed comparison,"
and it is the piece with applications outside matchings (any filtered
setting where certificates must be literal consequences of the equations).

**Problem 6 (export handles).**  For each same-shaped nonexistence target
(MUB dimension 6; Butson/Hadamard families; SIC existence), identify the
analogue of the matching lattice: the gauge group, the invariant lattice,
and the combinatorial move playing the role of the alternating-cycle
exchange.  Honest note: the (O1)/(O2) mechanisms feed on multilinear
matching structure those problems lack; without a structural handle the
export is methodology only (multigraded dual certificates, adversarial
audit discipline).

## 4. Ordering and non-goals

Nearest first: Problem 1 restricted to the chart-26 exact-ten stratum and
the D1 downset (where the corpus already constrains the statement), then
the Problem 2 formalization, then small-`N` computations for Problem 4.
Problem 3 is the field-sized prize and should be attempted after the
clean-point theorem, with the boundary-invariant question as its entry.
This note deliberately makes **no** claim that the A/B/C frontier should
pause: the theory track is priority insurance and generalization, not a
replacement attack.
