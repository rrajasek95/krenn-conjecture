# N = 8 D1: seven-cell anchor normal forms

Date: 2026-08-08.

Checker:
[`computations/verify_n8_d1_m7_anchor_normal_form_cover.py`](../computations/verify_n8_d1_m7_anchor_normal_form_cover.py).

This exact combinatorial reduction sits in front of the frozen
[`m=7 support-SAT problem`](n8-d1-m7-support-sat-frontier.md).  It replaces a
blind search over \(\binom{128}{7}\) off-\(\Sigma\) supports by the complete
monochromatic-anchor normal forms, quotients by D1 symmetry, and applies
field-independent unique-monomial certificates.

## 1. Complete normal forms

For either monochromatic anchor, a supported matching covers the four residue
sites with two, three, or four off-\(\Sigma\) cells.  The 72 three-cell traces
have one residue-residue edge and two small-residue edges.

At total size seven, every candidate is of one of two forms:

1. a three-cell trace in each colour plus one arbitrary additional
   off-\(\Sigma\) cell; or
2. a three-cell trace in one colour and a four-cell support in the other
   which contains no three-cell trace.

The checker independently exhausts all four-subsets of the 22 monochromatic
residue-incident cells.  After requiring an anchor trace and excluding the
unique-monomial residue-purity obstruction, the four-cell/no-three-trace
supports are exactly:

- 24 bijections from the four small sites to the four residue sites; and
- 3 unions of two residue perfect matchings.

In the second case the nonzero anchor chooses one of the three small-site
perfect matchings, giving three necessary small-small unit branches.  Thus
there are 33 four-cell anchor-unit branches per colour.

This produces exactly 637,200 labelled anchor branches on 615,600 labelled
off-supports.  The group generated by \((0\ 1)(2\ 3)\) and \(S_4\) on the
residue reduces them to 14,120 branch-state orbits.

## 2. Exact support certificates

For each orbit representative, let the allowed support be \(\Sigma\) plus
its seven off-\(\Sigma\) cells.  The mandatory nonzero cells are the seven
off-cells, the selected anchor factors, and the six D1 live/harm/(E2) cells.

The checker searches residue, both six-site systems, and the full output for
a target-zero word with exactly one matching on the entire allowed support,
all of whose cells are mandatory.  Such a coefficient is a nonzero monomial
over every field.  The orbit tally is:

| certificate domain | killed orbits |
|---|---:|
| residue (4 sites) | 3,672 |
| one-carrier purity (6 sites) | 8,229 |
| full output (8 sites) | 2,193 |
| branch survivors | **26** |

## 3. Status

This is a complete exact support-cover reduction, not a solver sample.  It
does not decide whether the 26 branches pass the full support-SAT shadow or
whether any corresponding exact polynomial ideal is nonempty.  The next
bounded job is therefore only those 26 branches (22 projected supports), not
the million-variable undifferentiated CNF.

Krenn's conjecture remains open; D1 with \(m=7\) is sharply reduced but not
closed, and denser supports remain untouched.
