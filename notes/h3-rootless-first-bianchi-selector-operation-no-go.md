# The first common Bianchi/selector operation cannot supply the rootless relative cell

## Outcome

The smallest common two-chart operation that could reach the missing
rootless Component III class is now determined, and the entire operation
class before that new cell is excluded.

Selector localization can move a diagonal anchor into the selected endpoint
fine degree.  A strict two-chart principal-parts square in the two marked
directions $a_{xv}^{00},a_{pq}^{00}$ supplies the required Bianchi sector
polar and forces the module shift

\[
                     \sigma=e_{x,0}+e_{p,0}+e_{q,0}.       \tag{1}
\]

But neither operation changes the two load-bearing augmentations.  Selector
entries are scalars on the residual output-word module; chart Bianchi edges
have total pure-anchor incidence zero.  A normalized local-colour bar does
change the output word, but its source companion is uniquely labelled.  If
that companion has a pure coefficient, cancelling it with the complete
full-nine rows leaves the same labelled pure target and conormal.  If target
is zero, every pure companion coefficient is zero separately.

Consequently no operation in the complete **underived, source-labelled
Bianchi/selector closure** has the type required in
[`h3-rootless-component-iii-complete-typed-inventory.md`](h3-rootless-component-iii-complete-typed-inventory.md):

\[
 \boxed{\operatorname{ainc}=-1,\qquad
        \widehat w=\operatorname{tgt}=\operatorname{ores}=0}   \tag{2}
\]

in the selected midpoint response grade.  This remains true after arbitrary
localization at $u$-free selector determinants, star minors, and the
curvature determinant.  The only underived escape is already an ordinary
localized source unit.

Thus the minimal possible new operation is a **selector-localized,
denominator-marked two-edge principal-parts/Bianchi square with an
independent $u$-linear lower face of type (2)**.  The square and its shift
are committed source data; the lower face is not.  Constructing that lower
face, or proving the corresponding packet-conditioned unit, is exactly the
remaining rootless proof task.

This is a sharp no-go for the first source-resolution stage.  It is not a
proof that a larger physical source resolution has no such cell, not a
clean-cap construction, and not a proof of Krenn's conjecture.

## 2. Two independent exact separators

Give each chart $c$ one pure-anchor/conormal coordinate $e_c$ and retain
the normalized cap boundary $\widehat w$.  A complete chart candidate and
a Bianchi edge have columns

\[
                   N_c=e_c+\widehat w,
              \qquad E_{cd}=e_d-e_c.                    \tag{3}
\]

For any number and topology of charts, the functional

\[
               \lambda_{\rm chart}(a,w)=\sum_c a_c-w    \tag{4}
\]

kills (3).  The checker verifies the exact rank statement for one through
eight charts: the available rank is the number of charts, and adjoining a
cell of type (2) raises it by one.  More Bianchi paths only redistribute the
anchor incidence.

Independently, the residual word module remains the literal direct sum

\[
 A_S\langle e_z:z\in\{0,1,2\}^6\rangle                 \tag{5}
\]

after selector localization.  The three pure words and twenty binary
three/three midpoint words are disjoint.  For complete eight-site source
companions, cancellation by the 6,561 full-nine rows is the identity on
word labels.  Target projection reads the three pure labels independently.
Therefore

\[
             \operatorname{tgt}(c)=0
       \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
             c_{0^8}=c_{1^8}=c_{2^8}=0.                \tag{6}
\]

Equation (6) is why a reduced or cyclic bar can have zero coarse
augmentation and still fail: retaining the desired pure endpoint retains
its same-labelled target.  Cancelling that target with the old cap restores
the conormal/cap separator.

In coordinates
$\bigl(u\operatorname{Eq},w,\operatorname{tgt},\operatorname{ores}\bigr)$,
the old columns are

\[
 r_0=(-1,0,1,0),\qquad T=(0,-Y,1,0),\qquad
 \rho=(0,1,0,1).                                      \tag{7}
\]

The primitive functional

\[
                         (Y,1,Y,-1)                    \tag{8}
\]

kills (7) and detects $(-1,0,0,0)$.  The checker replays the rank jump
$3\to4$ at four nonzero values of $Y$.

## 3. Why localization cannot evade the no-go

Let $A$ be the $u$-free coefficient ring after any allowed selector or
curvature localization, and let $I\subseteq A$ be the ideal generated by
the remaining physical rows with zero selected-anchor conormal.  The pure
row is monic:

\[
                              F_0=H-u.                  \tag{9}
\]

For every $\kappa\in A$, coefficient extraction in $(A/I)[u]$ gives

\[
        \boxed{\kappa(H-u)\in I[u]\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
               \kappa\in I.}                          \tag{10}
\]

No reducedness or domain hypothesis is used.  On the active
$\kappa\ne0$ chart, cancelling the pure conormal with the existing
underived source rows is therefore equivalent to $1\in I_\kappa$: an
ordinary packet-closing unit.  Selector inverses do not involve $u$, so
they do not affect the coefficient argument.

This also explains why a formal target/residue-cancelled chart candidate is
not enough.  Its remaining conormal is not a bookkeeping nuisance; removing
it inside the underived module already proves the source packet empty.

## 4. The minimal new operation

For the mixed chart row $c_v$, the strict comparison

\[
             K_v=r_{c_v}^{pq}-r_{c_v}^{pr}              \tag{11}
\]

has zero source boundary and zero target.  Marking $a_{xv}^{00}$ and
$a_{pq}^{00}$ gives the genuine two-chart principal-parts square whose
mixed associated symbol is the face hafnian $h_v$, placed in opposite
direct/two-star sectors.  Pure reinsertion and homogeneity force (1).  This
is the first operation that simultaneously has common two-chart provenance
and the correct endpoint character.

Its strict signature in the four coordinates of (2) is

\[
                              (0,0,0,0).                \tag{12}
\]

The denominator reset changes residual word grade, but its commutator is
$h_vY_0$; in the committed target-augmented complex the defect and ordinary
residue coincide.  The target-augmented filtered $d_2$ is the whole cap
graph, hence zero after the diagonal-anchor indeterminacy, not the desired
target-zero residue.  Combining (4), (6), and (8) proves that no existing
lower face completes (12) to (2).

Therefore a positive construction must adjoin one genuinely new lower face
to the denominator-marked square.  It must be $u$-linear, because every
old mixed/midpoint row has selected-$u$ coefficient zero; it must change
the pure word into the selected midpoint grade; and its physical target and
ordinary residue must vanish before source base change.  Declaring this
face would assume the missing theorem.
