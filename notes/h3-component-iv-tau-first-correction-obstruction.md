# The five face chains stop at a primitive endpoint-ridge obstruction

Research boundary only.  This does not construct the Component-IV chain
(n_c), prove the unified overlap theorem, or prove Krenn's conjecture.

## Maximal source-provenant prefix

For each deletion face (v\in\{1,\ldots,5\}), let (c_v) be the literal
eight-site mixed word and put

\[
 K_v=r_{c_v}^{pq}-r_{c_v}^{pr},\qquad
 u_v=a_{xv}^{00},\qquad t=a_{pq}^{00}.
\]

The two chart rows are labelled presentations of the same global hafnian,
so (dK_v=0).  Applying the ordinary two-direction principal-parts functor
to this polynomial identity gives two genuine relative cycles

\[
 d\Xi_v=0,\qquad d\Eta_v=0.
\]

Their mixed symbol is source-provenant and target-zero:

\[
 (h_v)_{pq,\mathrm{direct}}
       -(h_v)_{pr,\mathrm{two\text{-}star}}.             \tag{1}
\]

Across the five faces the ten first faces have rank ten and the five symbols
(1) have rank five.  Fine degree forces the unique cap shift

\[
                    \sigma=e_{x,0}+e_{p,0}+e_{q,0}.
\]

Thus the two-chart/marked-principal-parts prefix is an actual source
construction, not a declared cap column.

## The denominator attachment does not close

Attach the literal denominator generator (d_{v,m_v}) and apply mixed-word
reset followed by pure reinsertion.  Its Hom differential is

\[
                \omega(d_{v,m_v})=h_vY_0.              \tag{2}
\]

The five components of (2) increase the old pure denominator rank from
five to ten.  More sharply, retain the ten individually labelled `pq/pr`
source columns before identifying their physical sectors.  Their lower
rank is five, with kernel generated by the five (K_v).  The polar
cochains evaluate on that kernel as

\[
                         [\partial]_{v,w}=I_5.          \tag{3}
\]

Therefore none has a full-nine Schur lift.  Equation (3) is the first
attaching obstruction: the denominator-marked face cannot simply be glued
to (1), and an equivariant sum does not cancel it.

## Complete first correction and its physical failure

There is a canonical next correction in the complete squarefree Hasse
prolongation.  For every (v) and each of the three matchings (N) of its
four-site face, use the four physical marked cells

\[
 I=\{a_{pq}^{22},a_{xv}^{0m_v}\}\cup N.
\]

The genuine unary third cofactor and the top of this cube coincide:

\[
 \partial_IH_m=1.
\]

The full 17-term Hasse--Koszul correction, including every proper face,
has a proper-face tail of signature

\[
 (-1,0,0,0)
\]

in anchor-incidence, cap-boundary, target, and ordinary-residue coordinates.
Adding the top and the split cap gives the formal total signature

\[
                         (0,1,0,0),                    \tag{4}
\]

with boundary (Yw) and (d^2=0).  Thus signs and omitted Leibniz faces
are not the problem: (4) is exactly the normalized coarse signature needed
for one face of (n_c).

Physical descent compares this colored endpoint cube with the zero-endpoint
two-chart cube.  Their scalar tops agree, and their two internal ridges
agree, but the two endpoint ridges do not.  The Hom differential of the top
identification is the primitive four-term class

\[
 \boxed{\Omega_v=
   (a_{pq}^{22}-a_{pq}^{00})
   -(a_{xv}^{0m_v}-a_{xv}^{00}).}                       \tag{5}
\]

The complete endpoint-ridge space has rank six, while the five classes
(\Omega_v) have rank five.  Hence every nonzero combination, including
the equivariant sum over (v), retains a nonzero ridge obstruction.  The
same failure appears as source validity: the fourth operator sends the
source equation (H_m) to (1).

Consequently the five (	au_v) are not yet physical chains and their sum
does not supply (n_c), even though the complete formal correction has the
right coarse signature.
