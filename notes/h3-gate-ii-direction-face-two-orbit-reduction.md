# The eighteen Gate-II direction faces need two equivariant seeds

## Result

The direction half of the first-principal-parts boundary of the Gate-II
scalar has eighteen literal faces.  Retaining every physical label, each
face is indexed by

```text
(one of three C4 charts, one of three complementary tail matchings,
 one of the two edges in the chart).
```

Thus the count is `3*3*2=18`.  The exact coefficient is `-2` on the direct
`DQ` chart and `+1` on either endpoint `PS` chart.

The label-preserving action generated by the selected-edge Klein four
group, permutations of the four tail sites, and the endpoint chart swap has
the exact orbit tower

```text
18  ->  9  ->  3  ->  2.
```

The final two orbits are the six direct faces and the twelve endpoint faces.
Consequently an equivariant positive construction needs exactly two natural
restriction/reinsertion seeds: one `DQ` seed and one `PS` seed.  Two is
sharp because the coefficient function has the two distinct values `-2`
and `+1`.

## Literal extraction

Use selected sites `{0,1,6,7}` and tail sites `{2,3,4,5}`.  The three
selected-site perfect matchings are

```text
DQ   = 01|67,
PS01 = 06|17,
PS10 = 07|16.
```

The pinned `dL01` computation has 36 terms.  Eighteen differentiate an edge
in the complementary tail and eighteen differentiate an edge contained in
the selected four-site chart.  For the latter packet, the two undisplaced
edges form one of the three tail matchings.  This gives the product
description above without forgetting a matching or direction label.

The direction marginals are the already committed endpoint-even profile

```text
(-6,-6,3,3,3,3),
```

equivalently three tail copies of `(2,2,-1,-1,-1,-1)` after the global
orientation convention is reversed.  The two-orbit statement is its finer
literal version.

## Orbit reduction

There are three independent, label-preserving actions.

1. The Klein four group generated by `(01)(67)` and `(06)(17)` swaps the two
   selected edges inside each chart.  It sends the 18 faces to nine pairs.
2. Permuting tail sites `{2,3,4,5}` acts transitively on their three perfect
   matchings and reduces nine classes to the three chart classes.
3. The endpoint swap `(01)` fixes `DQ` and exchanges `PS01` with `PS10`,
   leaving two classes.

The checker builds the full groups rather than assuming their orders, acts
on every literal face, and verifies that the coefficient is constant on
every orbit.  The group orders in the four stages are `1,4,96,192`, and the
orbit counts are exactly `18,9,3,2`.

## Consequence for the open comparison

This result removes multiplicity, not provenance.  The open mixed cell must
still supply:

- the response-to-`AugP2` word/fine/repeated arrow;
- a source-valid `DQ` seed and a source-valid `PS` seed;
- the mixed private/reduced-`Eq` incidence measured by
  `delta*(B-Eq)`;
- physical `q`, anchor, `W`, residue, and shifted-ridge readouts; and
- naturality under the very actions used in the quotient.

So the result does not fill the balanced class and does not promote its
dual to a terminal.  It says that the eighteen direction obligations are
not eighteen independent construction problems.  Once a physical
equivariant mapping cylinder exists, two seeds generate all of them, and no
one-seed coefficient-preserving construction can suffice.
