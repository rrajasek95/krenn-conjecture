# The full pure product lifts through filtration degree four

## Exact outcome

Let \(R\) be the full polynomial ring in the 252 arbitrary
endpoint-colour coordinates for eight vertices, let \(I_{\rm mix}\) be the
ideal generated by the 6,558 mixed hafnian coefficients, and let \(J\) be
the coordinate ideal generated by the 240 variables outside the twelve
coordinates of the expanded-prism boundary support.

In the balanced degree-twelve, 24-port Macaulay component, exact rational
elimination proves

\[
  \boxed{
  H_0H_1H_2\in I_{\rm mix}+J^5.
  }
\]

Equivalently, the checker constructs mixed words \(w\) and balanced
multipliers \(Q_w\) such that

\[
  H_0H_1H_2-\sum_w Q_wH_w
\]

has off-support filtration degree at least five.

This is a truncated consistency theorem.  It is **not** the full ideal
membership \(H_0H_1H_2\in I_{\rm mix}\), and it does not yet prove the
conjecture.

## Exhaustive target census

The target through filtration degree four has 745 actual monomials and 226
orbits under the order-four stabilizer of the coloured boundary support:

\[
\begin{array}{c|ccccc}
\text{off-support degree} & 0&1&2&3&4\\ \hline
\text{target row orbits} &1&0&14&27&184.
\end{array}
\]

It is important to include every orbit.  The component containing the
boundary monomial contains only 204 of the 226 target orbits.  The remaining
22 degree-four orbits form a second filtered incidence component.  An
earlier preliminary span test that looked only at the boundary-connected
component was therefore incomplete; the final checker reconstructs the
pure-product support independently and solves both components.

The two exact invariant incidence blocks are

\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c|c}
\text{component} & \text{rows} & \text{columns} & \text{rank}
  & \text{target rows}\\ \hline
\text{boundary-connected} & 5554&19676&5551&204\\
\text{degree-four secondary} &22&45&22&22.
\end{array}
\]

Thus the union has 5,576 row orbits, 19,721 mixed-generator column orbits,
and exact rank 5,573.  All three cokernel directions come from the first
component and annihilate the pure-product target.  In particular, the
known 93-row exact dual separates the boundary monomial \(P_G\) but pairs
to zero with \(H_0H_1H_2\).

Sparse rational back-substitution gives 2,337 nonzero orbit columns.  The
chosen solution has coefficients in \(\tfrac14\mathbb Z\), with small
numerators, and is replayed over \(\mathbb Q\) against every row of both
components, including all dependent equations.

## Degree-five frontier

The degree-five target itself has 2,304 actual rows and 592 row orbits.
After applying the chosen degree-four correction, its full mixed-generator
tails expand the first unsolved residual to 17,370 row orbits.  This large
tail explains why constructing the whole degree-five component by cached
closure is inefficient: a preliminary all-component run exceeded 3.4 GB
before completion.

The next finite task remains well defined.  Starting from those 17,370
residual orbits, one should stream the degree-five incidence rows and
columns, use modular sparse elimination to decide consistency, and then
lift a sparse solution or a target-separating dual exactly.  The present
degree-four theorem makes that route substantially more plausible: the
first full-source obstruction to \(P_G\) vanishes on the actual pure-product
target, and no new obstruction appears through four filtration layers.
