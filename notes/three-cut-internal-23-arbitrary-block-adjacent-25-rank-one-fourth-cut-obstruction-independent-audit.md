# Independent audit: the adjacent rank-one \(E_{10},E_{20}\) fourth-cut obstruction

## 1. Verdict and exact scope

**Split verdict.**  This clean-room reconstruction of
[the primary rank-one note](three-cut-internal-23-arbitrary-block-adjacent-25-rank-one-fourth-cut-obstruction.md)
**confirms every geometric claim** (sections 2-5 of the note and its
equation (12)), the eight radical certificates, and the four line
packets - but **refutes the section-6 claim** that the two
\(D_{\mathrm{full}}\) plane packets have reduced Gröbner basis
\([1]\).  On the sublocus

\[
 \{\,X=a_0E_{00},\ a_0\ne0,\ t=0\,\}\subset D_{\mathrm{full}}
                                                             \tag{F1}
\]

the plane packet with the note's target colours \(\{0,1\}\) is
*satisfiable*, with an explicit rational star witness verified
symbolically (Section 8 below).  The note as written therefore does not
prove the fourth-cut exclusion for cuts \(0,1\) at (F1), and its frozen
ideal ledger cannot contain a faithful \([1]\) result for the plane
programs it describes.  Swapping the packet to target colours
\(\{1,2\}\) removes the (F1) degeneracy but is still not unit over the
full parameter ring (Section 8), so the \(D_{\mathrm{full}}\) case
needs a genuinely stronger certificate.  The \(t=0\) configurations
(F1) are covered by the earlier, independently audited
arbitrary-\(A_{23}\)/five-cell theorems, so no counterexample to the
exclusion *statement* is exhibited - the failure is in this note's
self-contained certificate, which it explicitly claims ("the \(t=0\)
fibre ... is proved here directly").

The audited family: keep the seven internal cells

## 2. Independent reconstruction and deliberately different orderings

Perfect matchings are generated by a greatest-vertex recursion with mates
scanned downward; every edge is stored with its smaller endpoint first, so
\(35:E_{10}\), every \(23:x_{ab}\), and the moving pair
\(25:(0,0)\mapsto1,(c,0)\mapsto t\) keep their literal endpoint order.
Raw cylinder columns are regenerated for each cut in the reversed order
hole decreasing, hole colour decreasing, cut colour decreasing; rows of
every matrix are sorted in reverse lexicographic word order; the three
literal duplicate pairs of \(C_2\) are resolved by keeping the *first*
member seen in this reversed enumeration (the E22 audit kept the last);
Gauss pivots are this audit's own scans; the \([C_4|D]\) minor anchors
are designed independently; Singular rings reverse the variable and
generator orders.  Numerical spot checks use greatest-coordinate sparse
Gauss–Jordan elimination over exact `Fraction`s and the double-annihilator
identity \((U\cap V)^\perp=U^\perp+V^\perp\); no floats appear anywhere.

All nine \(x_{ab}\) and \(t\) remain exact symbols over \(\mathbb Q\) in
every symbolic phase.  No torus normalization, no first-nonzero chart,
and no case split on \(t\) is used anywhere, matching the primary proof
shape.

## 6. The degenerate locus: designed minors give the generators exactly

**On the locus,** \(D\) is the explicit eight-column combination of raw

## 8. Shared-star ideals: the plane packets are NOT unit

The packets retain the note's target colours \(\{0,1\}\), all four
ordered fibres, diagonal fibres normalized to \(1\) on the target word,
the same \(72\) endpoint-ordered star variables, and reversed star,
fibre, word, variable, and generator orders throughout.  The arbitrary
\(A_{67}\) term is absorbed by the \(H\)-scalar family in every packet.

\[
\begin{array}{l|r|r|r|l}
\text{program}&\text{vars}&\text{coords}&\text{gens}&\text{result}\\ \hline
\text{line }E_{10},\ x_{00}\text{ inverted (Rabinowitsch)}&87&336&1345&[1]\\
\text{line }E_{10},\ x_{00}=0&85&310&1240&[1]\\
\text{line }E_{20},\ x_{00}\text{ inverted}&87&338&1353&[1]\\
\text{line }E_{20},\ x_{00}=0&85&312&1248&[1]\\
\text{plane }E_{10}\text{ on }D_{\mathrm{full}}&86&274&1096&
\textbf{not unit}\\
\text{plane }E_{20}\text{ on }D_{\mathrm{full}}&86&287&1148&
\textbf{not unit}
\end{array}                                                  \tag{12}
\]

The four line packets and the eight radical certificates reduce to
\([1]\) over characteristic zero, exactly as the note claims; the
cut-\(5\) exclusion (all \(X,t\)) and the cut-\(0,1\) exclusion off
\(D_{\mathrm{full}}\) are therefore independently certified.  The two
plane packets are **not** unit ideals.

**The witness.**  On the sublocus (F1) the tail identity (5) collapses
to \(H-D=a_0[0^6]\): the pure colour-\(0\) target lies inside the
normal \(\langle H,D\rangle\), so the diagonal \((0,0)\)-fibre condition
loses its teeth.  Concretely, take

\[
 p^1_{3,1}=q^1_{5,1}=1,\quad\text{all other }70\text{ star entries }0,
 \qquad
 g_{00}=-\tfrac1{a_0},\quad k_{00}=\tfrac1{a_0},\quad
 \text{all other scalars }0.                                 \tag{13}
\]

Then \(\beta_{00}=\beta_{01}=\beta_{10}=0\) while
\([0^6]+g_{00}H+k_{00}D=0\), and the only active atom of
\(\beta_{11}\) is the endpoint-ordered pair \((3,5)\) against the
all-ones cofactor \((02)(14)\), giving \(\beta_{11}=[1^6]\) exactly.
Every generator of the note's plane packet vanishes identically in
\(a_0\); the audit script machine-checks all \(504\) equations per
direction as exact symbolic identities.  A satisfiable specialization
forces the parameter-ring ideal to be non-unit (a unit ideal stays unit
under every specialization), so both plane programs fail their claimed
\([1]\).  Singular corroborates: the packets specialized at
\(a=(1,0,0),\ w=u=t=0\) (\(452\) generators, \(80\) star/scalar
variables) have reduced Gröbner bases of size \(1623\) for both
directions, and the full parameter-ring plane packets return reduced
bases far from \([1]\) (size \(1697\) observed); the per-run sizes are
in the reproduction log.

**What it means.**  Equation (12) of the note is *true* (Section 7
above): the four-cut normal at (F1) really is the plane
\(\langle H,D\rangle\).  The gap is only in the last step: for cuts
\(z\in\{0,1\}\) at the points (F1), the note's colours-\(\{0,1\}\)
necessary conditions do not produce a contradiction, so the note as
written does not exclude the fourth cut there.  Because (F1) lives at
\(t=0\) with \(A_{23}=a_0E_{00}\), the exclusion statement itself is
still expected to hold by the earlier, independently audited
arbitrary-\(A_{23}\)/five-cell theorems (which used different target
colours); but this note claims a direct self-contained proof, and that
claim fails.  Either the primary's actual plane programs differ from
what its section 6 describes, or its recorded \([1]\) is wrong; the
clean-room rules prevent this audit from inspecting which.

**A colour swap is not a complete repair.**  No element
\(\alpha H+\beta D\) can equal \([1^6]\) or \([2^6]\) (their words lie
outside the \(e\)-word/\(D\)-word support), so target colours
\(\{1,2\}\) avoid the (F1) mechanism, and the \(\{1,2\}\) plane packets
are indeed unit at the witness point and at nine further sampled
rational points of \(D_{\mathrm{full}}\) (including \(X=0\) at
\(t\in\{0,1\}\), single-parameter slices, and an all-ones point).  But
the supplementary full-parameter-ring run shows they are **also not
unit**: the \(E_{10}\) colours-\(\{1,2\}\) packet has a reduced Gröbner
basis of size \(112\), and this audit verified a concrete satisfiable
specialization: at \(X=E_{21}\) (the column-only slice
\(a=0,\ w=0,\ u=1\) of \(D_{\mathrm{full}}\)) the \(E_{10}\)
colours-\(\{1,2\}\) packet is non-unit for both \(t=0\) and \(t=1\)
(reduced bases of size \(107\)) - a point the ten earlier samples
missed (they included \(w=u=1\) jointly but never \(u\) alone).  A
\(90\)-minute elimination attempt on the full packet did not terminate,
and the mechanism at \(E_{21}\) is different from (F1).  A corrected
proof of the \(D_{\mathrm{full}}\) case therefore needs more than a
colour swap for either direction - plausibly the full three-colour
nine-fibre packet, or an explicit decomposition of the satisfiable
parameter locus with separate certificates.
