# Aggregate cancellation cannot rescue the diagonal silent packet

## Outcome

The silent two-centre `P3 + P3` channel from
[`n8-lemma-e-unary-top-channel-synchronization.md`](n8-lemma-e-unary-top-channel-synchronization.md)
does not survive aggregate cancellation when the internal quadratic is
colour-diagonal and the four response spokes are concentrated at their
ordered holes.

Normalize

```text
(p1,s1) holes = (0,1),       (p2,s2) holes = (2,3),
p1_0=e1, s1_1=e1,            p2_2=e2, s2_3=e2,
```

with every other component of these four stars zero.  Let `Q` have arbitrary
equal-colour cells

\[
                       q_{uv}^{00},q_{uv}^{11},q_{uv}^{22}       \tag{1}
\]

on all fifteen physical pairs, with arbitrary complex coefficients and
arbitrary cancellation.  Then there is no solution of

\[
 Q^{[3]}=X_0,qquad
 p_i s_j Q^{[2]}=\delta_{ij}X_i\quad(i,j=1,2).          \tag{2}
\]

The proof is one exact characteristic-zero ideal-membership identity.  It
does not choose a supported matching term, divide by a cell, or assume a
support torus.  Thus it upgrades the preceding principal-channel census to
an aggregate theorem on the entire colour-diagonal chart.

It does **not** settle the one-bad packet.  Off-diagonal internal cells and
multisite endpoint stars are excluded by the hypotheses above.  Those are
now the precise escape from this identity.

## The three nonzero coefficients

For holes `uv`, write

\[
 F_{uv}(w)=
 \operatorname{haf}\bigl(Q_w|_{\{0,\ldots,5\}\setminus\{u,v\}}\bigr),
                                                               \tag{3}
\]

where `w` records the colours on the four retained sites.  Write `H(w)`
for the six-site coefficient of `Q^[3]`.  The three target equations in the
chosen chart are

\[
 A:=F_{01}(1111)=1,qquad
 B:=F_{23}(2222)=1,qquad
 C:=H(000000)=1.                                      \tag{4}
\]

Every other coefficient of `F01,F23` is zero, every coefficient of
`F03,F12` is zero, and every mixed coefficient of `H` is zero.  The two
cross cofactors are exactly the off-diagonal rows

\[
                     p_1s_2Q^{[2]}=p_2s_1Q^{[2]}=0.   \tag{5}
\]

The desired contradiction is therefore an identity which puts `ABC` in the
ideal generated by those zero coefficients.

## Fine multidegree and exact identity

The coefficient product `ABC` uses the following colour tokens at each
physical site:

```text
site 0: {0,2}       site 1: {0,2}
site 2: {0,1}       site 3: {0,1}
site 4: {0,1,2}     site 5: {0,1,2}.
```

Fine multidegree therefore discards every coefficient equation which cannot
divide `ABC`.  After restricting to the 45 variables (1), the compatible
zero rows give exactly 71 nonzero polynomials:

* compatible mixed coefficients of `H`;
* non-target coefficients of `F01` and `F23`;
* all compatible coefficients of `F03` and `F12`.

Call their ideal `I`.  Exact computation over `QQ` gives a reduced standard
basis with 251 elements and

\[
                         \boxed{ABC\in I}.             \tag{6}
\]

More strongly, the checker asks Singular for a lift back to the original 71
source rows.  The lift has 34 nonzero multipliers and is checked by literal
polynomial expansion:

\[
                   ABC=\sum_{g\in I_{\rm source}}m_g g. \tag{7}
\]

Only four mixed top coefficients occur in this particular lift,

```text
H(001111), H(020112), H(020121), H(220022),
```

together with non-target diagonal and cross-cofactor coefficients.  The
remaining multipliers are recorded exactly by the checker output hash rather
than treated as an anonymous standard-basis membership.

Equations (2) make every source generator on the right of (7) zero, while
(4) makes the left side one.  This proves the theorem over `QQ`, hence over
`CC`.  Because no variable is inverted, zero cells, support degeneration,
and every cancellation stratum of the colour-diagonal chart are included.

## Why this is the aggregate version of the channel split

At the term level, a product of one colour-one near-perfect matching, one
colour-two near-perfect matching, and one pure-zero matching is visible in a
mixed top or cross-cofactor fibre.  The principal-channel argument could not
sum that observation because different perfect matchings can cancel in the
same word.  Identity (7) is exactly the missing signed summation: its
cofactor multipliers transport all exchange tails until only original zero
rows remain.

The identity is genuinely diagonal.  If a word has different colours at
the endpoints of one selected edge, an arbitrary `3 x 3` internal block
contributes an off-diagonal cell.  Those extra terms are absent from (7).
A five-minute modular standard-basis reconnaissance in the full 135-cell
ring did not return a remainder or a membership certificate; it is a
non-result, not evidence that the full identity fails.  The next bounded
calculation should lift (7) through the off-diagonal-cell filtration and
freeze the first nonzero correction class, rather than repeat a raw global
standard-basis run.

Likewise, general endpoint stars replace fixed cofactors by sums over many
ordered holes.  Extending (7) to that case requires keeping the star
coefficients as provenance labels and summing the holewise identities before
specializing the response rows.  One cannot infer that a selected pure
summand uses the line-hitting singular-spoke site.

## Reproduction and scope

Run

```text
.venv/bin/python computations/verify_n8_lemma_e_unary_top_diagonal_aggregate_identity.py
.venv/bin/python -O computations/verify_n8_lemma_e_unary_top_diagonal_aggregate_identity.py
```

The checker requires `/usr/local/bin/Singular` 4.4.x.  It reconstructs all
45 variables and 71 source rows, computes the characteristic-zero standard
basis and source lift, verifies (7), and freezes

```text
source lift SHA-256:
169acdcd3fb8194b43c066cd33207b841af2823cd6a0a40ede444c438d3b3ae6

ledger SHA-256:
257e602cc2fc463f4e8598a1e164e428ec0e90f6d06309ad081ad60dc6494839
```

It proves aggregate emptiness only for the concentrated-spoke,
colour-diagonal internal chart.  It does not cover arbitrary `3 x 3`
internal cells, multisite stars, construct an exact rational source, or
settle Krenn's conjecture at `N=8`.
