# The first four-Hamming chart-25 transport loses the relative anchor

## Outcome

The balanced chart-25 relative cell cannot be turned into the rootless
(C_{\rm rel}) by any literal Hamming-one/four-hole finite difference of the
known first-neighbour source columns.

Every one of the (56) mixed and (32) pure first-neighbour columns has
endpoint fine degree

\[
                         111\mid111 .                    \tag{1}
\]

For either selected off-diagonal endpoint order ((a,b)=(2,1)) or
((1,2)), exactly four endpoint half-labels must change to reach

\[
                         3e_a\mid3e_b .                  \tag{2}
\]

There are two changes at each endpoint.  The checker constructs the literal
four-cube of all (16) partial changes for every source column.  The repeated
selected grade occurs only at the terminal vertex; no proper Hamming face
has degree (2).

The terminal transport is source-labelled and makes every column mixed, so
its target, ordinary-residue and normalized-(w) readouts are zero.  But for
the same reason it has zero pure-anchor incidence.  Applied to all (144)
chart-25 relative bridges, it also retains between (775) and (825)
off-fibre matching rows (signed \(\ell^1\)-norm (832)) and has no row in the
original five-row chart-25 fibre.

The obstruction is the primitive integral character

\[
 \boxed{\quad
  \operatorname{anc}(c)+\sum_m\operatorname{tgt}_m(dc) .
 \quad}                                                  \tag{3}
\]

It vanishes on every literal source column: a mixed output word has neither
term, while a pure output word contributes one pure-anchor column and the
oppositely signed, multiplier-labelled target.  Consequently (3) vanishes
on every Hamming edge, cubical square finite difference, three-face, and
four-hole alternating difference.  It also survives arbitrary rational
linear combinations and cancellation between repeated target multipliers.

The two remaining literal operation classes do not enlarge this image.  The
all-word four-hole equations are replayed on all (729\times9) rows and are
response-affine, grade-zero pairings; they do not change (1).  The ten
committed cap/Bianchi contractions replay as exact zero identities, with no
target, ordinary-residue, or (w) readout.

The lower face required by the complete typed Component-III audit has

\[
 \operatorname{anc}=-1,\qquad
 \operatorname{tgt}=\operatorname{ores}=w=0,             \tag{4}
\]

so (3) evaluates to (-1).  Thus (4) is not in the complete literal
first-four-Hamming image.

This identifies the exact role of a new generator.  It must be a genuinely
source-labelled grade-changing relative cell with the readouts (4), not a
finite difference of the known chart-25 columns.  The calculation does not
rule out such a higher source-resolution cell; it proves that merely
recolouring the balanced relative representative, even with all of its
Hamming/Bianchi faces, cannot supply it.

## Exact scope

“Complete” here means every literal cubical finite difference generated by
the four necessary half-label changes on the committed (56+32)
first-neighbour source inventory, including all proper faces and the full
four-hole difference.  It does not declare an uncommitted higher Hamming
cell or a four-hole homotopy with independent readouts.  Such an object is
precisely the new source generator isolated by (4).
