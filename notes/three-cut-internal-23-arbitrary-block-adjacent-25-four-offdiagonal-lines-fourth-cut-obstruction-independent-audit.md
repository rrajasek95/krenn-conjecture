## 2. Endpoint order and the literal boundary formula

Perfect matchings are generated by recursively pairing the greatest
remaining vertex, not by the primary enumeration route.  Every edge is
stored with its lower endpoint first.  Thus \(35:E_{10}\), every \(23:x_{ab}\),
and every \(25:E_{cd}\) keep their literal ordered colours.

For distinct nonzero rational values in all nine entries of \(X\), a
non-binary rational \(t\), all \(108\) entries of the two boundary stars,
and all nine entries of \(A_{67}\), direct eight-site enumeration agrees in
all nine boundary-colour slices with

\[
\begin{aligned}
H_{ab}&=r_{ab}H_S(X,t)+\beta_{X,t}(p^a,q^b),\\
\beta_{X,t}(p,q)
 &=\sum_{i<j}\sum_{u,v}
 \left(p_{i,u}q_{j,v}+p_{j,v}q_{i,u}\right)
 e_u^{(i)}e_v^{(j)}\otimes H_{S\setminus\{i,j\}}(X,t).
\end{aligned}
\]

This directly tests endpoint orientation, both attachments to sites \(6,7\),
the reuse of each star variable among different fibres, and every entry of
the direct \(67\)-block.

## 9. Conclusion and remaining frontier

endpoint order, torus normalization, all \(512\) supports, overlap of the
moving block with killed \(X\)-coordinates, safe rather than fictitious
projected intersections, target nonabsorption, shared star variables,
arbitrary \(A_{67}\), the full complex cross-ratio line, exact generator

What is proved is still only the four named affine lines in \(A_{25}\).
Directions \(10,20,22\) remain outside this theorem.  The primary
reconnaissance indicates why the same quotient template does not immediately
extend: \(10,20\) have a dependent moving torus character, while \(22\)
places a pure target word in its moving output block.  Those are frontier
failures of this method, not countermodels.
