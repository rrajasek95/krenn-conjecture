# A dark component potential needs two source-typing promotions

## Outcome

Let `M` be a minimal zero-holonomy critical matching block.  Its left
cokernel is one-dimensional, generated by a full-support charge `ell`.  For
a physically typed Cartan connector `g`, the dark branch

\[
                             \ell^Tg=0                 \tag{1}
\]

is equivalent to

\[
                              My=g                    \tag{2}
\]

for a component potential `y`.  Equation (2) is exact linear algebra, but it
does not by itself imply support deletion or a typed exchange.  It lives in
the critical-component projection, whereas both conclusions concern the
complete physical source map.

The exact promotion is as follows.  Let `C` be the matrix of complete lifted
columns whose component projection is `M`, and let `G` be a complete lift of
the Cartan connector.  Define

\[
                              R=G-Cy.                 \tag{3}
\]

Then the component projection of `R` is zero.  Consequently:

* if the projection retains every fine label of the current component and
  `R` is nonzero, a nonzero literal coordinate of `R` is a typed contaminant
  or exchange leaving the component;
* if `R=0` and the kernel vector `(-y,1)` is realized by already occupied
  scalar cells in one fixed `p_i` or `s_j` row, the complete-column theorem
  of `0a965e7` gives an anchor-safe support deletion.

Both typing clauses are necessary.  The smallest two-class critical block
has a dark exact potential with no outside residual, but its columns can be
split among different source row heads and its Cartan column can be a
relative chain generator rather than an occupied scalar cell.  Then neither
branch follows.

## The complete-lift identity

Write `pi` for restriction to all coefficient rows belonging to the current
critical component.  The lift hypotheses are

\[
                         \pi C=M,\qquad \pi G=g.       \tag{4}
\]

Combining (2)--(4) gives

\[
                         \pi R=g-My=0.                \tag{5}
\]

This is the entire formal content of the dark potential.  It is useful
because it locates the first possible failure outside the critical block,
but it does not identify the nature of that failure unless `pi` is saturated
by all current fine labels.

The checker applies (3) to a compatible weighted four-cycle.  In an exact
lift with no complementary coordinates it obtains

\[
                  C(-y)+G=0.                         \tag{6}
\]

In a second lift, `G` carries one additional literal coordinate `E0`; then

\[
                  C(-y)+G=E0.                        \tag{7}
\]

Equation (6) is the kernel branch.  Equation (7) is the typed-exit branch
provided `E0` is labelled outside the saturated component.

The canonical `h=3` endpoint-odd Cartan prism supplies important upstream
data: it is source-provenant in its repeated grade and has zero protected
`D/W/target/anchor/Eq` readout.  What remains open is its **uniform
componentwise lift**: the current theorem does not show that every critical
block admits complete columns `C,G` satisfying the source-typing conditions
below.

## When the zero residual deletes support

Suppose `R=0`, the connector is the complete response column of an occupied
scalar cell `z_e`, and each internal column with `y_i != 0` is the complete
response column of another occupied scalar cell in the same literal endpoint
row.  Equation (6) is then a complete occupied same-row relation with
nonzero coefficient at `z_e`.

The theorem of `0a965e7` applies directly.  If the current scalar
coefficients are `x` and the kernel vector is `k=(-y,1)`, the finite update

\[
                        x'=x-\frac{x_e}{k_e}k          \tag{8}
\]

sets `x'_e=0` and preserves every source tensor.  All changed cells share
one coordinate endpoint, so none is a mutual anchor; every old mutual anchor
survives.  Thus (8) contradicts maximum mutual anchors followed by minimum
support.

The load-bearing phrases are “occupied scalar cell” and “same endpoint
row.”  A Cartan/Spencer chain generator is a genuine source-resolution
object but is not automatically a coefficient of the original scalar
source.  Likewise, an exact kernel spread across different endpoint rows
may delete a mutual anchor and need not improve the lexicographic invariant.

## Smallest type-split counterguard

Take the parallel two-class block

\[
 M=\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 c=(1,1),\qquad \ell=(1,-1).                          \tag{9}
\]

It has rank one, full-support right and left charges, and zero holonomy.  Set

\[
 y=(1,0),\qquad g=My=(1,1).                           \tag{10}
\]

Then `ell^T g=0`, and take the complete lift to have no additional rows, so
`R=0`.  Label the two internal columns as distinct scalar row types `p0` and
`s0`, and label `G` as a `Cartan-chain` generator.  No type class contains
two columns, hence there is no same-row occupied-column dependence to which
`0a965e7` applies.  The two scalar cells may be disjoint mutual anchors, and
the Cartan column is not an occupied scalar cell.  There is also no
complementary coordinate, so there is no contaminant or exchange.

This is not a physical GHZ counterexample.  It proves sharply that

```text
minimal zero holonomy + physically labelled Cartan g + ell^T g=0
```

does not logically contain the required support typing.

## Why component saturation is also necessary

There is a separate, even simpler projection guard.  Extend (9) by one
complete row labelled

```text
current_component:forgotten_fine_row.
```

Give `G` value one there and the internal lifts value zero.  The visible
critical projection still satisfies `pi(R)=0`, but the residual coordinate
does not leave or enlarge the component; it was already an internal label
omitted by `pi`.  Therefore nonzero `R` is a typed exit only when the
component projection contains every current source word, tail, endpoint,
and fine-grade label.

## Exact hypotheses for the desired theorem

A sound dark-potential-to-deletion/exchange theorem needs all of the
following.

1. `C` and `G` are complete source lifts retaining every physical equation
   and protected readout, not merely the critical coefficient block.
2. The component projection retains every current source word, tail,
   endpoint orientation, and fine-grade label.
3. Every nonzero complementary coordinate is a literal typed adjacency or
   exchange label outside the current component.
4. On the zero-residual branch, `(-y,1)` is realized by already occupied
   scalar source cells.
5. Those cells lie in one fixed `p_i` or `s_j` row—or another independently
   proved kernel move preserves the anchor invariant.
6. The Cartan connector is one of those occupied scalar cells, not solely a
   relative principal-parts generator.

Under 1--3, `R != 0` gives the literal typed exit by (5).  Under 1 and 4--6,
`R=0` gives the anchor-safe deletion by (8).  There is no additional matrix
or holonomy case.

The remaining theorem is therefore source-homological:

> Attach the physical Cartan comparison to every minimal critical component
> through a complete fine-label-saturated lift, and prove that its exact
> zero-residual potential is occupied-same-row or admits another explicitly
> anchor-safe well-founded kernel move.

Transverse rank remains downstream.  Same-word matching potentials cannot
repair a local head quotient, as the matching-head invariance theorem
already shows; an escaping word-changing Cartan term may subsequently enter
the double-quotient rank test.
