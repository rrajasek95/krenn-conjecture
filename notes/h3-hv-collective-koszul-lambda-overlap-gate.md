# The five `h_v` overlaps diagonalize the mixed charges only after a new darkness theorem

## Verdict

Treating the five reset--switch/Weyl products collectively does expose one
real conditional relation, but it does not select a terminal value.  Put

\[
 X=T H_W,\qquad Z_i=N_iX,\qquad
 \Psi(Z_i)=\lambda_i h_i,\qquad 1\leq i\leq5.        \tag{1}
\]

The five denominator hafnians form the pinned height-five complete
intersection.  Consequently their ten first relations are exactly the
Koszul relations.  For a carrier `K_ij` with

\[
                 dK_{ij}=h_iN_j-h_jN_i,              \tag{2}
\]

the full product rule gives, in one fixed Koszul orientation,

\[
 d(K_{ij}X)=h_iZ_j-h_jZ_i+K_{ij}dX,                 \tag{3}
\]

where

\[
             dX=(dT)H_W+T(W-1).                     \tag{4}
\]

The last term in (3) is mandatory.  It retains the same operation,
word/fine, repeated-edge, root/`W`, and response labels which made the five
individual products off-grade in the previous audit.

If a physical comparison is constructed and every one of the ten families
`K_ij dX` is proved `Psi`-dark, applying `Psi` to (3) gives

\[
       h_ih_j(\lambda_j-\lambda_i)=0\quad(i<j).       \tag{5}
\]

The coefficient ring is a polynomial domain and every `h_i h_j` is
nonzero.  The ten scalar differences in (5) have rank four.  Hence

\[
          \boxed{\lambda_1=\cdots=\lambda_5=c.}       \tag{6}
\]

This is the strongest conclusion supplied by the collective Koszul tower.
All higher Koszul faces are compatible with every common scalar `c`.  Both
`c=0` and `c=1` satisfy the entire scalar system, so it forces neither a
nonzero aggregate nor vanishing.

Without the new darkness statement for `K_ij dX`, even (6) is not forced.
For arbitrary `lambda_i`, the pair debt is

\[
 r_{ij}=h_ih_j(\lambda_j-\lambda_i).                 \tag{7}
\]

The presently unclassified overlap face may have terminal value `-r_ij`.
These ten compensators automatically satisfy every triple condition:

\[
 h_i r_{jk}-h_j r_{ik}+h_k r_{ij}=0.                 \tag{8}
\]

Indeed, if `a_i=lambda_i h_i`, then `r=delta_K a`, so (8) is just
`delta_K^2 a=0`.  Thus full Leibniz and abstract overlap compatibility do
not by themselves correlate the five values.  This is a counterguard, not
a claimed construction of the missing source-labelled overlap cells.

## 1. What is being compared

For each omitted vertex `i`, `h_i` is the hafnian of the complementary four
vertices in `K5`.  Explicitly,

\[
\begin{aligned}
h_1&=x_{23}x_{45}+x_{24}x_{35}+x_{25}x_{34},\\
h_2&=x_{13}x_{45}+x_{14}x_{35}+x_{15}x_{34},\\
h_3&=x_{12}x_{45}+x_{14}x_{25}+x_{15}x_{24},\\
h_4&=x_{12}x_{35}+x_{13}x_{25}+x_{15}x_{23},\\
h_5&=x_{12}x_{34}+x_{13}x_{24}+x_{14}x_{23}.
\end{aligned}                                       \tag{9}
\]

The fifteen displayed monomials are distinct.  The complete-intersection
checker proves that `(h_1,...,h_5)` has height five in

\[
R=\mathbb Q[x_{12},x_{13},x_{14},x_{15},x_{23},
            x_{24},x_{25},x_{34},x_{35},x_{45}].      \tag{10}
\]

The minimal resolution is therefore the Koszul complex, with Betti numbers

```text
1, 5, 10, 10, 5, 1.
```

In particular there is no extra first relation which could give a
distinguished sum of the five product columns.  The only first relations
are generated by (2).

Equation (1) uses `lambda_i` for the normalized cap choice.  The formal
Eq-only shadow of the preceding mixed-product audit has `lambda_i=1` and
value `h_i`; its tied completion has `lambda_i=0`.  The current literal
physical projection is still zero/off-grade, so (1) is a hypothesis about a
future tensor-to-corner comparison, not a value assigned to the existing
complex.

## 2. The pair-overlap calculation

The point of multiplying (2) by `X` is that one cannot keep only the first
two terms in (3).  The switch--Weyl carrier is not closed.  Its differential
contains the 18 tail and 18 direction face types from `dT`, together with
the root/Weyl face `T(W-1)`.  Therefore each first Koszul square comes with
the extra family

\[
 K_{ij}(dT)H_W+K_{ij}T(W-1).                         \tag{11}
\]

Suppose first that a single source-labelled comparison sends (11) into the
kernel of the balanced terminal covector.  The remaining two terms give

\[
\begin{aligned}
 \Psi(h_iZ_j-h_jZ_i)
   &=h_i(\lambda_jh_j)-h_j(\lambda_ih_i)\\
   &=h_ih_j(\lambda_j-\lambda_i).
\end{aligned}                                       \tag{12}
\]

No localization is used to pass from (12) to equality of the scalars:
`R` itself is a domain and `h_i h_j` is nonzero.  The ten equations are the
edge-difference matrix of complete graph `K5`; that matrix has rank four
and diagonal kernel generated by `(1,1,1,1,1)`.  This proves (6).

This is useful, but conditional.  The previous product audit showed that
the operation-labelled tensor grades in (11) do not currently land in the
corner-resolved `P3+K2` private/`Eq` packet.  Declaring them dark would be a
new physical readout statement, not a consequence of their labels or of
`d^2=0`.

## 3. Why arbitrary values remain coherent without the readout

The counterguard can be stated over `R` without choosing numerical
coefficients.  Let the five `lambda_i` be formal scalar coordinates and set

\[
                         a_i=\lambda_i h_i.           \tag{13}
\]

The `h`-Koszul coboundary is

\[
 (\delta_Ka)_{ij}=h_i a_j-h_j a_i
                  =h_ih_j(\lambda_j-\lambda_i)=r_{ij}. \tag{14}
\]

If the unknown terminal readout of (11) is `-r_ij`, all ten images of (3)
vanish.  On a triple,

\[
\begin{aligned}
(\delta_Kr)_{ijk}
 &=h_ir_{jk}-h_jr_{ik}+h_kr_{ij}\\
 &=h_ih_jh_k\bigl[(\lambda_k-\lambda_j)
                  -(\lambda_k-\lambda_i)
                  +(\lambda_j-\lambda_i)\bigr]\\
 &=0.
\end{aligned}                                       \tag{15}
\]

Thus the pair choices introduce no triple obstruction.  All subsequent
compatibility is the same identity `delta_K^2=0`; there is no higher scalar
equation waiting at Koszul degrees three, four, or five.

The checker verifies this coefficientwise in the ten-edge polynomial ring,
not merely after evaluating the `h_i` at numbers.  It also tests a
non-diagonal sample

```text
(lambda_1,...,lambda_5)=(0,1,-2,5/3,9),
```

for which all ten pair debts are nonzero and all ten triple identities
still vanish exactly.

This construction is deliberately only a terminal-shadow countermodel.
It shows that the proposed inference from “full Leibniz plus overlaps” to
equal or zero charges is invalid while the images of (11) are unspecified.
It does not assert that the compensators possess the required physical
operation, word/fine, repeated-edge, `q`/anchor, residue, ridge, and target
labels.

## 4. Higher faces do not fix the diagonal value

After the harmless specialization `h_i=1`, the five-generator Koszul
boundary ranks are

```text
1, 4, 6, 4, 1.
```

Every consecutive boundary product is zero and the complex is exact in
positive degrees.  Multiplication of a comparison by a scalar `c` commutes
with every one of these boundary maps.  Consequently, once (6) is reached,
the higher faces cannot distinguish `c=0`, `c=1`, or any other rational
value.

This agrees with the strict mapping-cone normalization audit but does not
reprove its missing hypothesis.  That audit says:

```text
literal normalized physical DGA/module product, with no added closed
balanced cap cocycle  =>  B=Eq  =>  lambda_i=0.
```

That implication sets `c=0` one object at a time.  The complete-intersection
or Koszul theorem does not supply the normalized-product premise.

## 5. The five-face aggregate is not a unit landing

If the conditional diagonalization (6) holds, then

\[
             \Psi\!\left(\sum_i Z_i\right)
                    =c\sum_i h_i.                    \tag{16}
\]

The polynomial `sum_i h_i` has fifteen distinct quadratic monomials and no
constant term.  It is nonzero when `c` is nonzero, but it is not a unit in
`R`.  Hence (16) cannot produce the primitive denominator-free terminal
needed by the proof.

On the one-monomial `C5` specialization, the five projected PP switches do
have a saturated rank-four image and the all-ones primitive dual.  The
pinned full-source audit shows why that is not enough: before specialization
the five residuals have disjoint supports, rank five, and zero cycle kernel.
The projected aggregate is therefore not a universal source relation that
could set or normalize `c`.
