## 1. Exact official EqSystem census

For a colour word `w`, let `H_w` be the official sum over all `105` perfect
matchings of `K8`.  Because the two edges in (1) share site `0`, no perfect
matching contains both.  The checker evaluates this on every official
coefficient row:

```text

matchings per word                                     105
words containing a / b / both                    729 / 729 / 243
term occurrences of a / b / both              10935 / 10935 / 0
words with nonzero partial_a partial_b H_w                0.
```

The `243` words in the third entry are compatible with each edge separately;
none has a monomial containing both.  Hence the identity is termwise:

\[
                        \partial_a\partial_bH_w=0      \tag{3}
\]

for all `6561` words.  Target constants also have zero derivative.  Adding a
termwise decomposition of `H_w` cannot alter (3).

This explains why a site-repeating pair is invisible in the shadow of one
physical matching.  It appears only after allowing decomposable coefficient
or product operations.

## 2. The universal mixed collision/Tate operation

Let `e_w` be the first official Tate generator,

\[
                         d e_w=H_w-t_w,                \tag{4}
\]

retains both labels in (1) is

\[
 C_{a,b;w}=x_b\,\iota_a e_w-x_a\,\iota_b e_w.         \tag{5}
\]

Its differential is the literal collision row

\[
 dC_{a,b;w}
   =x_b\partial_aH_w-x_a\partial_bH_w.                \tag{6}

For the compatible pure word `11111111`, each first derivative has `15`
terms.  The two supports in (6) are disjoint, giving

```text

partial_b H_w terms                         15
d C_(a,b;w) support                         30
coefficients                               +/-1

```

Thus (5) is not a formal invented arrow.  It is generated by the official
relation Tate cell, multiplication by an official edge variable, and the
literal Hasse restriction.  Its next primitive mixed Hasse face is (3), so
the collision curvature vanishes.  Since (6) is a polynomial row, `d^2=0`.

This is the strongest positive fact available at the first forbidden pair:
the required site-repeating target coordinate has a source-provenant
*internal* collision model.

## 3. Why its boundary is not `r0`

The present generated dg category has two relevant objects:

```text

  termwise H_w/private rows, Hasse restriction/insertion, C_(a,b;w)

cap:
  r0, E=(H0-u)e_Eq, T/rho normalizers, AugP2 faces.
```

Every displayed source operation is in `End(response)`.  Every constructed
cap operation is in `End(cap)`.  Both differential and composition preserve
the ordered pair of object endpoints.  The free closure has only

\[
       (R,R),\qquad(C,C),                               \tag{7}
\]

and no `(R,C)` path.  This proves (2) in every degree.

An exact finite separator makes the same point without category language.
Grant all `30` termwise collision rows independently, stronger than the
actual single boundary (6).  In the direct sum of their source rows and one


The covector which reads only the cap-`r0` landing coordinate kills every

\[

cross-object image of (5).  Likewise, multiplication by `r0` remains
cap-internal; it cannot change the endpoint pair in (7).
