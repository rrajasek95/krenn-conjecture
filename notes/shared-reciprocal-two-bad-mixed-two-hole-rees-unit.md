# The mixed two-hole unit has a finite tilted/Rees lift

## 1. Result

The 18-row characteristic-zero unit from the normalized binary two-hole
square is formally rigid against a general tilted kernel row as long as
three target components remain nonzero.

> **Finite tilted-unit theorem.**  Let `q` be the arbitrary ternary
> five-site internal quadratic and let
>
> \[
> U=\sum_{x=0}^2\tau_x e_t^{(x)}
>     +\sum_{y,d\ne t}\nu_{y,d}e_d^{(y)}+\cdots
> \]
>
> be a literal common-cofactor kernel row.  Localize at
> `T=tau0*tau1*tau2`.  For the nine binary coefficients used by the
> 18-row certificate, replace `K_x(beta)=0` by their exact tilted kernel
> rows.  Together with the nine displayed defects of `K3=X0,K4=X1`,
> the initial Rees system is still the unit ideal.  More precisely, the
> lifted source identity is finite and expresses its unit as the exact
> transgression/bright-defect combination below; no infinite geometric
> series is required.

Consequently a tilted arc cannot approach the normalized three-centre
two-hole chart with all three `tau_x` units and every transgression/bright
defect of positive Rees order.  Any escape must already do one of two
things in its leading term:

1. lose at least one target coefficient `tau_x`; or
2. carry a nonzero leading target-transgression or bright-cofactor defect
   satisfying the explicit affine escape equation below.

This closes the off-diagonal formal neighbourhood of the target-axis
stratum, including non-target components of the kernel row.  It does not
close the boundary on which one of the `tau_x` vanishes.

## 2. Literal tilted kernel rows

Fix `x in {0,1,2}` and a bright word `beta` on the four sites outside
`x`.  Consider the full five-site word having target colour `t` at `x`
and the colours `beta_y` elsewhere.  The coefficient of `Phi_q(U)=0` at
that word is exactly

\[
 \tau_x K_x(\beta)+R_x(\beta)=0,                       \tag{1}
\]

where

\[
 R_x(\beta)=
 \sum_{y\ne x}\nu_{y,\beta_y}
 K_y\bigl(t\text{ at }x,\ \beta\text{ off }\{x,y\}\bigr). \tag{2}
\]

There are four terms in (2), one for each possible non-target insertion
site.  This is a literal coefficient expansion of the common hafnian; no
abstract replacement of a cofactor is used.  The checker records all 36
source addresses for the nine rows needed below.

If `nu=nu^(0)+epsilon nu^(1)+...` and a target-containing cofactor slice is
`L=L^(0)+epsilon L^(1)+...`, then the first Rees correction is

\[
 [\nu L]_1=\nu^{(0)}L^{(1)}+\nu^{(1)}L^{(0)}.          \tag{3}
\]

The minimal mixed-parity guard is the special case in which the first
summand is the whole correction: a fixed non-target row is transgressed by
one first-order `(t,a)` internal cell.

## 3. The cleared 18-row identity

Write `M_(x,beta)` for the pinned multipliers in the 18-row certificate.
For holes `3,4`, write

\[
 \Delta_h(\beta)=K_h(\beta)-
 \begin{cases}
 1,&(h,\beta)=(3,0000)\text{ or }(4,1111),\\
 0,&\text{otherwise}.
 \end{cases}
\]

The ordinary source identity from `4c9da58` is

\[
 1=\sum_{x=0}^2\sum_\beta M_{x,\beta}K_x(\beta)
   +\sum_{h=3}^4\sum_\beta M_{h,\beta}\Delta_h(\beta). \tag{4}
\]

Substitute (1) and clear the three target coefficients.  With
`T=tau0*tau1*tau2`, (4) becomes the exact polynomial identity

\[
 T=-\sum_{x=0}^2\sum_\beta
       {T\over\tau_x}M_{x,\beta}R_x(\beta)
   +T\sum_{h=3}^4\sum_\beta
       M_{h,\beta}\Delta_h(\beta).                    \tag{5}
\]

Thus, in the quotient by the nine literal kernel rows (1), the ideal
generated by the nine `R_x(beta)` and the nine displayed bright defects
contains `T`.  After localizing at `T`, that defect ideal is the unit
ideal.  Equation (5), not a continuity assertion, is the promised finite
unit lift.

Modulo any proposed positive Rees filtration of all `R` and `Delta`, the
right-hand side of (5) has positive degree while the left-hand side is a
unit in degree zero.  Hence no such filtered solution exists.  If some
defects have degree zero, their initial forms must satisfy (5); that is the
exact leading escape equation to be carried into the next chart.

## 4. Relation to the two-kernel repair charts

The canonical first Hubble/tilted bridge is

```text
U=e_t@0-e_a@1.
```

Its target-coefficient vector on the three-centre chart is `(1,0,0)`, so
`T=0`.  The minimal rational two-kernel repair has the same feature for
each individual tilted kernel direction.  Therefore those charts do not
contradict (5): they are minimal realizations on the target-coefficient
boundary where the cleared unit loses its invertible left side.

The whole `T=0` boundary is larger than those Hubble charts.  It consists
of kernel rows with at most two nonzero **target** coefficients and may
still have arbitrarily many non-target-only centres.  No classification of
that larger boundary is inferred here.

This sharply locates the remaining proof branch.  A mixed two-bad escape
cannot be a small off-diagonal deformation of the large target-axis
square.  It must first fall to at most two target-coefficient centres, or
change the bright allocation at leading order.  The committed Hubble
private-row and second-kernel repair lemmas address only minimal portions
of that boundary.

## 5. Scope

The theorem is over characteristic zero because the pinned 18-row unit was
computed over `QQ`.  It applies to arbitrary mixed-colour internal cells;
only the three named target coefficients are localized.  It does not claim
that every tilted two-bad kernel has three nonzero target coefficients,
and it does not close the one-/two-centre boundary.
