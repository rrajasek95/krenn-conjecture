# Johnson-window path independence is all-order; higher Hasse coherence starts at `h=6`

## Verdict

Let `m=h-1` be the number of tail edges in a full order-`h` tail matching.
The `h=3` windows are the two-subsets of those `m` edges.  Their one-edge
overlap graph is

\[
                         J(m,2)=L(K_m).                \tag{1}
\]

Attach one inherited `h=4` triangle for every three tail edges and the three
disjoint-edge Beck--Chevalley squares for every four tail edges.  Over the
characteristic-zero field used by the proof, the resulting two-complex
`X_m` satisfies

\[
 H_1(X_m;\mathbf Q)=0,                               \tag{2}
\]

and

\[
 \dim H_2(X_m;\mathbf Q)
 =3\binom m4-2\binom m3+\binom m2-1.                 \tag{3}
\]

Thus triangle and square faces give path independence at every order, but
they do not give all higher coherence.  The first nonzero `H2` occurs at
`m=5`, hence `h=6`, and has dimension four.  It is the sign-twisted standard
`S5` representation.  Its minimal natural filler has five oriented labelled
three-cells with one sum relation.

The exact conditional consequence is:

> One natural source-labelled `Phi_KS,r0/P_f` schema, together with a strong
> action of the full oriented Hasse species on every physical and protected
> row, has a coherent fixed-tail prolongation for every `h`.

This still is not full `PAComp(h)`.  A fixed four-site `h=3` partition covers
only `3(2m-5)!!` of the `(2m-1)!!` full tail matchings when `m>=3`.  Descent
over the remaining matching cover is a separate physical/exhaustivity datum.

## 1. The two-complex `X_m`

Write `v_ab` for the window consisting of tail edges `a,b`.  The vertices
of `X_m` are all `v_ab`, and two vertices are adjacent when their windows
share one tail edge.  Hence

```text
V = C(m,2),
E = 3*C(m,3).
```

For `a<b<c`, the inherited triangle is

\[
                  T_{abc}=(v_{ab},v_{ac},v_{bc}).      \tag{4}
\]

Every graph edge occurs in exactly one triangle (4), so the triangle
boundaries have disjoint edge supports and rank `C(m,3)`.

For `a<b<c<d`, use the three Beck--Chevalley squares

\[
\begin{aligned}
 Q^0_{abcd}&=(v_{ab},v_{ac},v_{cd},v_{bd}),\\
 Q^1_{abcd}&=(v_{ab},v_{ad},v_{cd},v_{bc}),\\
 Q^2_{abcd}&=(v_{ac},v_{ad},v_{bd},v_{bc}).            \tag{5}
\end{aligned}
\]

These are precisely the three disjoint-insertion interchange cycles on a
four-edge subset.  Thus the two-face count is

```text
F = C(m,3)+3*C(m,4).
```

## 2. Proof that `H1=0`

For any connected graph `G`, the cycle space of `L(G)` is generated by the
line-graph images of cycles of `G` and triangles inside the clique of edges
incident to one vertex of `G`.  To see this, take a closed line-graph walk
and record a meeting vertex of consecutive edges in `G`.  Segments with the
same meeting vertex reduce to triangles in one incident-edge clique.  After
removing them, the meeting vertices form a closed walk in `G`.

Now set `G=K_m`.  Its cycle space is generated by `K3` cycles, whose
line-graph images are exactly (4).  It remains only to reduce an
incident-edge star triangle.  On labels `0,1,2,3`, orient

\[
 S_0=(v_{01},v_{02},v_{03}).
\]

Direct substitution of (4)--(5) gives

\[
 2S_0=T_{012}-T_{013}+T_{023}+T_{123}
       +Q^0_{0123}-Q^1_{0123}+Q^2_{0123}.             \tag{6}
\]

Every star triangle is a relabeling of (6).  Since `2` is invertible over
`Q`, every Johnson cycle is in the declared two-face span.  This proves (2)
for all `m`.

The factor two is real.  The checker finds a one-dimensional `H1` over
`F2` for every tested `m>=4`.  No characteristic-two statement is used in
the Krenn proof.

## 3. `H2` and the first genuine higher-coherence layer

The graph is connected, so its incidence has rank `V-1`.  By (2),

\[
 \operatorname {rank}d_2=E-V+1
   =3\binom m3-\binom m2+1.                           \tag{7}
\]

There are no three-cells in `X_m`, hence

\[
\begin{aligned}
 \dim H_2(X_m;\mathbf Q)
  &=F-\operatorname {rank}d_2\\
  &=3\binom m4-2\binom m3+\binom m2-1,               \tag{8}
\end{aligned}
\]

which proves (3).  The first values are

```text
m                         3   4   5   6   7    8
h=m+1                     4   5   6   7   8    9
dim H2                     0   0   4  19  55  125.
```

Thus `d483380` is the last order at which triangles and squares alone form a
complete coherence complex.  At `h=6`, they make all one-edge paths
homotopic, but four relations among those homotopies require three-cells.

The checker constructs all matrices through `m=9`.  Face rank modulo the
odd prime `1000003` equals the rational cycle-space upper bound, certifying
the displayed ranks without numerical approximation.  Equation (6), not
finite extrapolation, supplies the all-`m` proof of `H1=0`.

## 4. Exact `S5` type of the first three-cell family

On labels `0,1,2,3,4`, define

\[
\begin{aligned}
 B_0={}&-T_{123}+T_{124}-T_{134}+T_{234}\\
 &-Q^0_{0123}+Q^1_{0123}-Q^2_{0123}\\
 &+Q^0_{0124}-Q^1_{0124}+Q^2_{0124}\\
 &-Q^0_{0134}+Q^1_{0134}-Q^2_{0134}\\
 &+Q^0_{0234}-Q^1_{0234}+Q^2_{0234}.                 \tag{9}
\end{aligned}
\]

The checker verifies `d_2 B_0=0`.  Let `p_a` exchange labels `0,a` and set

\[
                         B_a=\operatorname {sgn}(p_a)p_aB_0.       \tag{10}
\]

Then, exactly over the 25 two-faces of `X_5`,

\[
 d_2B_a=0,
 \qquad \sum_{a=0}^4B_a=0,
 \qquad \operatorname {rank}(B_0,\ldots,B_4)=4.       \tag{11}
\]

The checker exhausts all 120 permutations and obtains

\[
                    \sigma B_a=\operatorname {sgn}(\sigma)B_{\sigma(a)}.
                                                                    \tag{12}
\]

Therefore

\[
                         H_2(X_5;\mathbf Q)
                  \cong \operatorname {sgn}\otimes\mathrm {Std}_5. \tag{13}
\]

The orientation twist in (13) matters.  The minimal natural physical filler
is a family of five oriented three-spectator cells `K_a` satisfying

```text
d K_a = B_a,
sum_a d K_a = 0,
sigma K_a = sgn(sigma) K_sigma(a),
```

up to the corresponding one relation among the five cells.  Formula (9)
identifies their complete required triangle/square boundary.  It does not
construct them in the decorated physical `PP/AugP2` complex.  That is the
first new higher-coherence clause in strong physical Hasse-linearity.

## 6. Conditional all-`h` fixed-tail prolongation theorem

Assume the following.

1. There is one pointed, normalized, source-labelled
   `Phi_KS,r0/P_f` schema, natural in every labelled two-edge `h=3` window.
   It carries selected `db01` and every target, private `B`, reduced `Eq`,
   `M`, anchor/`q`, `W`, ordinary-residue, `P_f`, ridge, `eta`, and `sigma`
   row.
2. The physical source complexes form a strong symmetric-monoidal module
   over the full oriented Boolean/matching Hasse species.  This includes the

   Beck--Chevalley, the cells (9)--(13), every higher alternating deletion
   coherence in (14), and associativity/unitality.
3. The `PP/AugP2` comparison and every protected row named above are linear
   for this Hasse action.

Then, for every `h>=3` and every fixed full tail matching `M`, the one
`h=3` schema has a coherent prolongation `Phi(M)` independent of the chosen
two-edge window.

Indeed, (2) identifies all one-edge transport paths.  Equations (8)--(15)
show exactly which higher homotopies must be retained; Hypothesis 2 supplies
them as the full Hasse action.  The Leibniz rule gives all spectator faces

This is an exact conditional fixed-tail theorem.  A literal isolated `h=3`

## 7. Fixed-partition coverage and the separate descent datum

There are `(2m-1)!!` perfect matchings on the `2m` tail sites.  Fix four
sites for the `h=3` window.  They admit three internal pairings, and the
remaining `2m-4` sites admit `(2m-5)!!` matchings.  Hence, for `m>=3`,

\[
\begin{aligned}
 \#\text{all tails}&=(2m-1)!!,\\
 \#\text{fixed-partition tails}&=3(2m-5)!!,\\
 \#\text{uncovered tails}&=(2m-1)!!-3(2m-5)!!,\\
 \text{coverage fraction}&=\frac3{(2m-1)(2m-3)}.       \tag{16}
\end{aligned}
\]

Every full matching has `C(m,2)` two-edge window presentations.  For the
first orders beyond the base:

```text
h   m   all tails   fixed-partition tails   uncovered   windows per tail
4   3       15                 3                12             3
5   4      105                 9                96             6
6   5      945                45               900            10
7   6    10395               315             10080            15.
```

Thus fixed-tail coherence does not establish full-source coverage.  The
remaining datum is a normalized, source-labelled Čech/coequalizer descent
over all `C(m,2)` window presentations of every full matching, retaining
word, fine, repeated, removal/reinsertion, operation, protected rows, and
the actual source-terminal/Macaulay block.

Over `Q`, summing all windows and dividing by `C(m,2)` corrects numerical
multiplicity.  It does not construct the labelled comparison maps or prove
that the result lands in the complete physical source rather than a
tail-divisible/orbit-relative sector.  Those are the separate physical
descent and exhaustivity clauses still required for `PAComp(h)`.

## Scope

The result proves the all-`m` rational `H1` and `H2` formulas and exactly
identifies the first required higher-coherence representation and boundary.
It verifies the local five-set tower through `m=9` and its dimension Euler
identity through `m=14`.

It does not construct `Phi_KS,r0/P_f`, the decorated physical `K_a` cells,
protected-row Hasse-linearity, or complete matching-cover descent.  These
are hypotheses of the conditional prolongation theorem, not conclusions of
the Johnson rank computation.
