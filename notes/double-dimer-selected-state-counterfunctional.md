# A selected-state counterfunctional to termwise double-dimer straightening

## 1. Exact scope

Let \(R\) be the six-site source-coordinate ring, let

\[
 F_c=\sum_{M\in\operatorname {PM}(6)}
       \prod_{uv\in M}x_{uv}^{c(u)c(v)}
\]

for \(c\in\{0,1,2\}^6\), and let \(I_{\mathrm{mix}}\) be generated by the
726 nonconstant \(F_c\). Choose one constant-colour matching monomial
\(u_a\) from each \(F_{a^6}\), and put

\[
                         U=u_0u_1u_2.                    \tag{1}
\]

There is an exact rational evaluation on the nine-edge triangular-prism
chart such that

\[
             F_c=0\quad\text{for every one of the 729 colourings},
 \qquad                         U=1.                     \tag{2}
\]

Consequently

\[
             U^m\notin\sqrt {I_{\mathrm{mix}}}
                     \quad\text{for every }m\ge1.        \tag{3}
\]

In particular, evaluation at this point is an exact dual functional on the
degree-18 Macaulay space: it annihilates every column \(Q_cF_c\) but takes
value one on \(U^2\).

The selected square \(U^2\) is a singleton fixed-double-dimer-union class.
For each colour \(a\), the monomial \(u_a^2\) comes only from the ordered
pair \((P_a,P_a)\); no other pair of matching terms has the same decorated
monomial. Thus (3) rules out either of the following proposed square-level
steps:

1. straighten every monomial in the expansion of \(P^2\) separately by the
   mixed-fibre ideal;
2. group terms only by the union of the two same-colour matchings and
   straighten each fixed double-dimer class separately.

The obstruction survives localization at all cells of \(U\), since every
one of them evaluates nonzero.

This does **not** prove

\[
                         P^2\notin I_{\mathrm{mix}},
 \qquad P=F_{0^6}F_{1^6}F_{2^6}.                        \tag{4}
\]

At the counterfunctional point all three constant \(F_{a^6}\) also vanish,
so \(P^2\) evaluates to zero. A collected certificate for \(P^2\) may, and
must, couple distinct double-dimer union classes. The result is precisely a
negative statement about termwise/component-local recursion, not about the
full coefficient-aware square problem.

## 2. The nine-edge evaluation

Use

\[
\begin{aligned}
 P_0&=04\mid12\mid35,\\
 P_1&=05\mid14\mid23,\\
 P_2&=03\mid15\mid24.
\end{aligned}                                             \tag{5}
\]

Their underlying union \(E\) is the triangular prism. It has exactly one
additional perfect matching,

\[
                         R=04\mid15\mid23.                \tag{6}
\]

Put zero blocks outside \(E\). On an edge of \(E\), set

\[
 A_{uv}(a,b)=h_{uv}\lambda_{u,a}\lambda_{v,b},            \tag{7}
\]

where

\[
 h_{12}=-3,\qquad h_e=1\ (e\ne12),\qquad
 \lambda_{0,0}=-\frac13,\qquad
 \lambda_{v,a}=1\ ((v,a)\ne(0,0)).                       \tag{8}
\]

Every displayed scalar cell is nonzero. For a colouring \(c\), every
supported matching term has the common endpoint factor

\[
                         \Lambda_c=\prod_v\lambda_{v,c(v)}.
\]

The four underlying matching products in the order
\((P_0,P_1,P_2,R)\) are \((-3,1,1,1)\). Hence

\[
                         F_c=\Lambda_c(-3+1+1+1)=0       \tag{9}
\]

for all 729 colourings. This proves the first part of (2).

Let

\[
              u_a=\prod_{uv\in P_a}x_{uv}^{aa}.          \tag{10}
\]

The factor \(\lambda_{0,0}=-1/3\) cancels the unique factor
\(h_{12}=-3\) in \(u_0\), while all factors of \(u_1,u_2\) are one.
Therefore

\[
                         u_0=u_1=u_2=U=1,                \tag{11}
\]

proving the rest of (2).

Evaluation is a ring homomorphism. Equations (9) imply that it annihilates
the whole ideal \(I_{\mathrm{mix}}\), including every multiplier and every
degree. Equation (11) then proves (3), as well as properness of the
localized ideal \(I_{\mathrm{mix}}R[U^{-1}]\).

## 3. Why the double-dimer class is genuinely isolated

Fix \(a\). The exponent vector of \(u_a^2\) assigns multiplicity two to
the three decorated occurrences of \(P_a\) and zero to every other
occurrence. If matching monomials \(m_Mm_N\) have this exponent vector,
both \(M\) and \(N\) must use only those three underlying edges. Since they
already form a perfect matching, necessarily

\[
                              M=N=P_a.                   \tag{12}
\]

This is stronger than saying that component switches do not help: (12)
says there is no second ordered pair anywhere in the complete six-site
matching set that belongs to the same decorated monomial class.

For the three colours together, \(U^2\) is therefore the unique six-matching
term with its decorated exponent vector. Its nonzero value cannot cancel
inside its fixed-union class. The cancellation \(P^2=0\) at the evaluation
instead occurs only after terms with different double-dimer unions are
collected. Any viable source-ideal recursion must retain exactly that global
coefficient coupling.

## 4. Exact audit

[verify_double_dimer_selected_state_counterfunctional.py](../computations/verify_double_dimer_selected_state_counterfunctional.py)
enumerates all fifteen perfect matchings and all 729 colourings over
\(\mathbb Q\). It checks (9), the three normalizations (11), nonvanishing of
every selected cell, and the uniqueness assertion (12) by enumeration of all
225 ordered matching pairs in each colour.
