# Chart 25 lifts exactly through off-carrier degree three

Let `K` be generated by the 216 coordinates outside the 36-coordinate
carrier of chart 25.  There is an exact characteristic-zero identity

\[
                    H_0H_1H_2\in I_{\rm mix}+K^4.
\]

Thus the one-generator carrier identity now lifts through the complete
degree-zero, degree-one, degree-two, and degree-three layers of the full
252-variable source.  The frozen certificate uses 1,634 stabilizer-orbits
of mixed Macaulay columns: 396 begin in `K`-degree two and 1,238 begin in
degree three.  Its coefficients have denominator lcm two and take only

\[
 \pm\tfrac12,\ \pm1,\ \pm\tfrac32,\ \pm2,\ \pm\tfrac52.
\]

## Why the coupled lift matters

If the exact degree-two solution is frozen and only new degree-three
columns are allowed, the continuation fails modulo 1009.  Its closure has
17,752 row orbits, 37,337 column orbits, rank 16,809, and a 1,041-coordinate
remainder.  This is not a genuine obstruction.

The degree-two matrix has 3,690 columns and rank 2,052, hence a
1,638-dimensional kernel.  In the source-faithful coupled degree-two/three
block, the old columns contribute 3,482 pivots.  The first 2,052 are the
degree-two image and the remaining

\[
                         3482-2052=1430
\]

are next-order images of degree-two kernel directions.  This is the exact
chart-25 Bockstein rank.  Those 1,430 directions remove the apparent fixed
tail obstruction.

The complete coupled census is

\[
\begin{array}{c|r|r}
&\text{row orbits}&\text{column orbits}\\ \hline
K^2/K^3&2264&3690\\
K^3/K^4&27440&55798.
\end{array}
\]

The block rank is 27,904.  The same pivot structure, rank, Bockstein rank,
and zero remainder occur independently modulo 1009, 1013, and 1019.
Modular support extraction followed by CRT reconstructs the half-integral
1,634-column solution.  The final theorem does not rely on those modular
tests: the permanent verifier expands the rational certificate on every
actual row and checks exact cancellation over `Fraction`.

## Discrete-Morse interpretation

The earlier shared-leading-row census predicted algebraic Morse diamonds.
The Bockstein calculation now measures their higher effect: a difference
of columns can cancel its leading degree-two row while retaining a useful
degree-three tail.  Treating each filtration layer independently destroys
precisely this information.  The correct proof object is therefore a
filtered contraction, or equivalently its transferred differential, not a
sequence of unrelated Macaulay rank tests.

This is still a finite-order chart result, not full chart-25 saturation.
The next layer must either repeat the source-faithful construction with all
degree-two/three kernel freedom or be controlled by a general acyclic
matching/contracting-homotopy lemma.
