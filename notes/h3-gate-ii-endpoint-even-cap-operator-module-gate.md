## 1. The desired character is endpoint-even

Use corner order `(1,w,s,sw)`.  The two characters relevant here are

```text

chi_ws = (1,-1,-1, 1).
```

Endpoint reversal exchanges `1<->s` and `w<->sw`.  Therefore

```text

s chi_ws = -chi_ws.
```

The known physical Cartan prism is the second, endpoint-odd line.  The
missing pointed Gate-II character is the first, endpoint-even line.  This is
why trying to manufacture an absolute odd PSQJet attacks the wrong parity
for the current PAComp packet.
