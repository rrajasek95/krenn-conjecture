## 1. Verdict

The one support orbit left at the minimal off-\(\Sigma\) layer is empty.
Combined with the support-cover audit,

\[
 \boxed{\text{an exact D1 source must have at least seven active cells
 outside }\Sigma.}
\]

This closes all D1 supports with \(m\leq6\), where \(m\) counts nonzero,
(E1)-admissible aggregate cells outside \(\Sigma\).  It does not address
\(m\geq7\), so it does not close D1 or Krenn's conjecture.

## 3. Independent six-generator unit certificate

There is an even smaller proof.  Put

\[
\begin{aligned}
B&=A_{01}(b,b)A_{24}(b,b)A_{36}(b,b)A_{57}(b,b),\\
C&=A_{04}(c,c)A_{15}(c,c)A_{23}(c,c)A_{67}(c,c).
\end{aligned}
\]

Six generators of the frozen input are

\[
\begin{array}{rcl}
B-1&=&0,\\
C-1&=&0,\\
A_{15}(c,c)A_{36}(b,b)A_{47}(a,a)&=&0,\\
A_{46}(a,a)A_{57}(b,b)&=&0,\\
A_{45}(a,a)A_{67}(c,c)&=&0,\\
A_{47}(a,a)A_{56}(a,a)+A_{46}(a,a)A_{57}(a,a)
 +A_{45}(a,a)A_{67}(a,a)-1&=&0.
\end{array}
\]

The first two are the \(b^8,c^8\) output equations.  The third is one of the
six-site Lemma-F purity equations, the next two are mixed residue-purity
equations, and the last is pure-a residue purity.

In the quotient by the first two equations, every factor of \(B\) and \(C\)
is a unit.  Equations 3--5 therefore force

\[
A_{47}(a,a)=A_{46}(a,a)=A_{45}(a,a)=0,
\]

and equation 6 becomes \(-1=0\).  No localization generator is used.
