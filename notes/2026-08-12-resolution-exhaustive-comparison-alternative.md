# The comparison should be taken in the canonical exhaustive cone

## 1. Why explicit endpoint membership is the wrong target

The order-six construction and the five non-Euler polars now have explicit
representatives, but they do not have the same stabilizer character.  For
each deleted face (v), their character difference is the sum of seven
local colour roots.  The corresponding seven-site covariance cube is
target-zero and has boundary

\[
                         L_v-D_v,                       \tag{1}
\]

where (L_v) is the all-output polar endpoint and (D_v) is the
all-derivation endpoint.  The remaining question was phrased as proving
that (D_v) equals the appropriate face (O_v) of the canonical
order-six totalization.

That equality is stronger than the proof needs.  The useful object is the
difference

\[
                         z_v=D_v-O_v.                   \tag{2}
\]

in a complete physical relative complex.  If (2) is a boundary, it supplies
the desired gluing.  If it is not a boundary, a dual class detecting it is
the desired separator.  Thus an explicit membership calculation should be
replaced by an exhaustive mapping-cone alternative.

## 2. Canonical source complex

Let (R) be the polynomial ring on the decorated physical edge
coordinates, let (I) be the ideal generated by all complete labelled
source coefficients, and let

\[
                      B=\operatorname {Bar}_R(R/I)
\]

be the normalized bar resolution.  Apply the multigraded
Hasse/principal-parts functor and its alternating cosimplicial totalization.
For the fixed word and repeated (P_3\sqcup K_2) degree, denote the resulting
finite complex by (S_g).

This choice has three important properties.

1. It is exhaustive by construction: every polynomial multiple of every
   complete source row and every relation among such multiples appears in
   the normalized bar resolution.
2. It is finite in the selected grade.  Every decorated edge and every bar
   generator has positive site degree, so both polynomial degree and bar
   length are bounded by the fixed multidegree.
3. Hasse translation is an algebra map.  Therefore the source ideal is
   stable under every face map, and the Boolean coproduct gives the required
   alternating signs.  No additional higher-face census is needed.

Adjoin the physical output rows—literal boundary, (D), (W), target,
ordinary residue, and anchor incidence—as mapping-cone summands.  Retain the
relative Kähler ridge (-d\Omega_v), whose contractions are

\[
 \iota_{\eta_z}(-d\Omega_v)=1+\delta_{vz}u_z/t,
 \qquad
 \iota_\sigma(-d\Omega_v)=-q_{pq}^{22}.                \tag{3}
\]

Call the resulting finite augmented complex (C_g).  The protected
readouts are chain maps on the endpoint-odd summand: this is exactly the
Cartan-prism augmentation theorem.  Equation (3) is a Kähler differential,
so its compatibility is (d^2\Omega_v=0); its strict commutation with the
complete order-six block has already been proved.

Consequently (C_g), rather than a hand-selected inventory of old physical
columns, is the correct exhaustive object in which to test (2).

The first-flat bounded calculation confirms that this enlargement is
load-bearing.  The complete old order-six plus repeated full-nine matrix has
an exact zero-normalized bridge, but its pure repeated aggregate vanishes on
the entire homogeneous kernel.  Thus the primitive comparison cannot be
obtained by changing the order-six representative or adding another old
repeated polynomial row.  The normalized bar/relative cells in `C_g` are
genuinely new source types, not merely a convenient resolution of a class
already present in the old matrix.

The corresponding bounded cokernel class now has a literal representative.
Each repeated component has a homogeneous dual supported on six private
matching coordinates plus the pure aggregate.  The five componentwise
classes lie in distinct face grades and pass every currently constructed
diagonal stabilizer kernel.  For the canonical faces-`(3,5)` component this
dual annihilates the exact first-flat order-six bridge matrix.  Hence the
first candidate for (4) is no longer a coarse quotient functional: it is a
fine-grade homogeneous six-term physical matching covector.  What remains
is only the **relative** part of the exhaustive cone.  Every complete
one-chart repeated boundary map is injective, so any higher absolute
differential landing there vanishes by `d1*d2=0`.  The doubled chart kernel
is exactly the anti-diagonal labelled-column module and is killed by every
chart-neutral augmentation.  The natural Tate kernel is
coefficient-sum-zero inside each complete label fibre.  Thus no absolute
bar/source syzygy, chart copy, or natural Tate completion can kill the
six-term class.  It remains to show that the class annihilates the relative
augmented generators of `C_g`, or identify the first such generator that
kills it; the latter generator is the desired boundary branch.

## 3. Resolution-exhaustive alternative

Let (d:C_{g,1}\to C_{g,0}) be the relevant boundary and suppose (z_v) is
the cycle (2), with the prism edge and ridge terms included.  Over the
characteristic-zero source field, exactly one of the following holds.

### Boundary branch

If (z_v=dM_v), then adding the prism to (M_v) identifies (O_v) with
(L_v).  Endpoint oddization gives ordinary residue (-\delta), zero
(D,W,
\operatorname {tgt},\operatorname {ainc}), and the ridge gives (3).
This is the physically typed comparison column required by the rootless
polar map and by the residual-(q) landing.

Different choices differ by (ker d).  If physical anchor/terminal
incidence is nonzero on that kernel, normalization gives the already proved
relative-generator branch.  If it vanishes there, the comparison is
zero-indeterminate and the five-polar Fredholm alternative applies.

### Cokernel branch

If (z_v\notin\operatorname {im}d), elementary duality gives

\[
 \lambda_v d=0,
 \qquad
 \lambda_v(z_v)=1.                                    \tag{4}
\]

To obtain the advertised physical reading from (4), one must use the
generator-level comparison to prove two facts: the relevant order-six face
is the boundary face of the canonical totalization in this same augmented
grade, and every prism edge except its all-output endpoint maps to a physical
source/bar boundary.  Under those two facts, (4) reads one on the physical
polar endpoint (L_v) and zero on every admitted physical correction.  The
five polar characters are independent, so the five functionals can then be
normalized facewise and summed to give the pentagon aggregate separator.

This is the point at which earlier local covectors failed: their matrices
omitted higher source cells and the eta/sigma rows.  Exhaustivity is built
into (C_g), but physical typing is still a theorem about the generator-level
map to the augmented rows.  Only after that theorem is checked does (4)
become a genuine physical dual rather than a quotient-level diagnostic.

The proof of the alternative is just finite-dimensional linear algebra:
either (z_v) belongs to the boundary image, or a functional on the quotient
(C_{g,0}/\operatorname {im}d) detects its nonzero class.

## 4. What remains to verify, rather than enumerate

This reframing removes the need to decide endpoint membership by a growing
support census.  To turn it into the final proof, only the following
generator-level assertions need explicit verification.

1. The all-derivation vertex (D_v) and the order-six face (O_v) are placed
   in the same full source word and repeated grade after the seven covariance
   labels are retained.  They need not be equal.
2. The map from the normalized bar/principal-parts model to physical
   boundary, (W), target, residue, and anchor rows is a chain map on the
   generating source rows.  Multiplicativity then handles all bar multiples.
3. The Kähler ridge (3) is the physical terminal row on this cone.  Its
   already proved eta/sigma laws and order-six commutation reduce this to the
   endpoint/bar generators.
4. In the boundary branch, the primitive order-six face must satisfy the
   one-sided transverse landing theorem.  In the cokernel branch, (4) must
   be identified with the already specified rootless/pentagon terminal
   separator.

Items 1--3 are a finite generator-family audit, not a case enumeration over
source support.  Item 4 is the separate source-typed tight-set/orbit theorem.

## 5. Consequence for the proof architecture

The conjecture-level local step can now be organized as

\[
\begin{array}{c}
 \text{canonical order-six class}\ +\
 \text{five covariance prisms}\ +\
 \text{Kähler ridge}
\end{array}
\longrightarrow
\begin{cases}
 \text{physical comparison carrier},\\
 \text{physical polar separator},\\
 \text{kernel-relative generator}.
\end{cases}                                           \tag{5}
\]

Every outcome in (5) advances the proof.  The comparison-carrier branch is
passed to one-sided transverse landing; the other two are the existing
rootless Fredholm exits.  Thus explicit endpoint gluing is no longer a
standalone conjectural lemma.  The load-bearing remaining theorem is the
physical chain-map typing on generator families, followed by transverse
landing.

## Scope

This note supplies the canonical exhaustive complex and the exact
boundary/cokernel/kernel alternative.  It does not assert, without checking,
that the current informal physical readouts already define the required
chain map on every generator family.  It also does not prove the one-sided
rank landing.  Those are now the two precise verification targets.
