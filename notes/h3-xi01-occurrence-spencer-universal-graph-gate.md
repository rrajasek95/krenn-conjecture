# The canonical occurrence resolution transports `Xi_01`; it does not fill it

## Outcome

The exact first missing Spencer/root object can be resolved
presentation-safely, but the resolution produces a retained carrier rather
than an absolute boundary.

In the missing-`0`/doubled-`S` sector there are 45 collision monomials and
90 parent-labelled repairs.  The incomplete root row

\[
 \Xi_{01}
 =a_{PS}{\partial\over\partial a_{P0}}
  -a_{S1}{\partial\over\partial a_{01}}              \tag{1}
\]

has 15 parent occurrences with coefficient `+1`, 15 with coefficient
`-1`, and 60 zeros.  Parent collection sends it to the nonzero 24-term
residual `R_01` of `b40cebc`.

The smallest rank-level graph is

\[
                         d\gamma_\Xi=\Xi_{01}-\tau_\Xi. \tag{2}
\]

It preserves `H0`, but one aggregate carrier cannot retain the 120 distinct
root/reinsertion-labelled PP flags.  The smallest literal occurrence lift
therefore uses one carrier for each of the 30 active parents:

\[
                  d\beta_i=c_i-t_i,qquad
                  d\sum_i w_i\beta_i=\Xi_{01}-\tau_{01},
 \quad \tau_{01}=\sum_iw_it_i.                       \tag{3}
\]

This also preserves `H0` and leaves `tau_01` nonzero.  Adding carrier graphs
for the other 60 zero-weight parents gives the full universal occurrence
resolution without changing that conclusion.

The canonical parent-to-collision cylinder is even sharper.  If
`pi:{1,...,90}->{1,...,45}` forgets which repair produced a collision, put

\[
                         d\theta_i=c_i-k_{\pi(i)}.     \tag{4}
\]

Then

\[
                 \boxed{d\Theta_\Xi=\Xi_{01}-R_{01}}. \tag{5}

Thus (4) makes only the kernel of collection absolute.  It cannot kill a
row with nonzero collected image.  The pullback of `R_01/24` along the
two-parent fibers annihilates every column in (4) and the symmetric
collision row, while reading one on both `Xi_01` and `R_01`.  This is an
exact certificate that the canonical cylinder retains `R_01`.

An added physical column `d eta_R=R_01` would make `Xi_01` absolute by
(5), but that column lowers the collected `H0` from 44 to 43.  It is the
new rank-raising cell requested by the proof, not something generated by
the occurrence resolution.

## 1. The parent row and its collection

Write the possible neighbours of doubled `S` as

```text
P, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5.
```

A collision is determined by an unordered pair of these neighbours and a
perfect matching on the remaining four vertices.  Hence there are

\[
                       {6\choose2}\,3=45              \tag{6}
\]

collision monomials.  Either `S` arm can be reversed to an edge from the
missing vertex `0`, so there are 90 parent-labelled repairs.

Equation (1) assigns weight

```text
+1 if the repaired source edge is p0=P0,
-1 if the repaired source edge is q01=01,
 0 for the other four possible source neighbours.
```

Each selected neighbour occurs with five possible sibling neighbours and
three matchings on the other four sites.  This gives the exact parent
histogram

```text
+1: 15,  -1: 15,  0: 60.
```

The parent-even row is one on all 90 coordinates.  Therefore

\[
                   \widetilde\lambda={\Xi_{01}\over30} \tag{7}

kills the even row and reads one on `Xi_01`.

Collection adds the two parent weights in each collision fiber.  This is
the signless incidence formula `c_u+c_v` from `a047c8f`.  Three fibers have
parents of weights `+1,-1` and cancel; the result has

```text
+1: 12,  -1: 12,  0: 21.
```

It is exactly `R_01`, and

\[
                         \lambda={R_{01}\over24}       \tag{8}

kills the coefficient-two symmetric collision row and reads one on the
residual.

This also explains why a fiberwise parent anti-diagonal and the complete
`Xi_01` row behave differently.  On each of the three cancelling fibers,
the anti-diagonal lies in `ker(pi)` and becomes absolute in (4).  Across all
90 parents, however, (1) has the nonzero quotient image `R_01`; equation
(5) is forced.

## 2. Minimal graph and exact `H0`

Treat the existing parent-even collision row as the one old boundary in
the 90-coordinate occurrence presentation.  Its degree-zero quotient is

\[
                              H_0=90-1=89.             \tag{9}

The rank-minimal graph (2) has 91 coordinates and two boundary columns:
the extended parent-even row and `Xi_01-tau_Xi`.  Its quotient dimension is
again

\[
                              91-2=89.                 \tag{10}

Both `Xi_01` and `tau_Xi` survive and represent the same class.  The
functional which is `Xi_01/30` on the old occurrence coordinates and one
on `tau_Xi` kills both boundaries and reads one on either representative.

For occurrence naturality, add the 30 carrier coordinates `t_i` and the 30
monic graphs in (3).  Together with the parent-even row they have rank 31
in 120 coordinates, so

\[
                             120-31=89.                \tag{11}

The extended functional `Xi_01/30` on both the `c_i` and corresponding
`t_i` coordinates proves that neither side of (3) is absolute.  Adding all
60 zero-weight graph pairs produces 180 coordinates and rank 91, again
leaving `H0=89`.  This full version is universal for arbitrary
parent-labelled rows; the active version is the minimal one needed by
`Xi_01` and its 120 PP flags.

For the canonical collection cylinder (4), use coordinates

```text
90 parent occurrences + 45 collected collisions.
```

The 90 graph columns are independent.  Adding the parent-even row gives
rank 91, hence

\[
                             135-91=44.                \tag{12}

This equals the old collected-sector quotient `45-1=44`; thus (4) is
presentation-safe when viewed as a mapping cylinder over the collected
target.  It deliberately collapses the larger parent-labelled quotient
from 89 dimensions to 44.  Equation (5) and the extended dual (8) show that
`Xi_01` remains nonzero there.

Adjoining `R_01` as an absolute boundary raises the rank to 92 and gives
`H0=43`.  Calling this step “canonical resolution” would hide the one new
rank which the physical construction must actually supply.

## 3. The 120 first-PP flags

Only the 30 nonzero parent occurrences contribute to `Xi_01`.  Each
collision monomial has four edges, so its complete first boundary contains

```text
120 root/reinsertion-labelled flags
 60 of topology 3K2
 60 of topology P3+K2.
```

They split more finely as follows.

| removed edge | number | face type | role |
|---|---:|---|---|
| varied target arm | 30 | `3K2` | first unary restriction |
| sibling `S` arm | 30 | `3K2` | other path/unary cofactor |
| disjoint tail edge | 60 | `P3+K2` | tail restriction/reinsertion |

With the response word, source parent, target arm, root trigger, removed
edge, and reinsertion arrow retained, all 120 flags are distinct.  The
minimal face graph is simply

\[
                         d(PP\beta_i)=f_i-s_i          \tag{13}

on every flag.  Its 240 coordinates and 120 monic columns leave a
120-dimensional relative face quotient.  A top-only declaration
`d eta=tau_01` without compatible versions of (13) is not a chain map.

If the parent and root/reinsertion trigger are forgotten, the 120 flags
collect to 108 face/removal coordinates.  Twelve have coefficient zero,
and the remaining 96 have

```text
+1: 48,  -1: 48;
3K2: 48, P3+K2: 48.
```

This is exactly the first PP boundary of the collected residual.  The
weighted PP version of (4) is therefore

\[
                         d(PP\Theta_\Xi)=PP\Xi_{01}-PP R_{01}. \tag{14}

Again the canonical cylinder transports the nonzero image; it does not
fill it.

## 4. First unary and reinsertion faces

Deleting the varied target arm exposes the cleanest unary boundary.  The
15 positive `p0 -> D` occurrences give the complete matching row on

```text
S,1,2,3,4,5,
```

while the 15 negative `q01 -> s1` occurrences give minus the complete row
on

```text
P,S,2,3,4,5.
```

Thus this face is the signed difference of two disjoint complete 15-term
unary rows.  It does not cancel.  More importantly, the physical Spencer
face remembers whether reinsertion is `p0 -> D` or `q01 -> s1`.
Identifying the coefficient rows after erasing that arrow does not provide
the required reinsertion-natural carrier.

The 60 tail-removal faces behave similarly.  Removing a labelled tail edge
and reinserting that exact edge returns the top occurrence, so the retained
carrier must satisfy the same square.  Their forward local type is the
known `DSQ` response cofactor; the response-to-cap word/fine transport is
not supplied by the occurrence graph.

After the opposite root, the first shared unary/reinsertion defect is the
selected block

\[
                             q_{01}H_{2345},            \tag{15}

three of the fifteen matchings in the complete unary row.  The minimal
graph

\[
                          d\gamma_q=q_{01}H_{2345}-t_q \tag{16}

preserves `H0=14`.  The functional taking `1/3` on the selected three,
`-1/12` on the other twelve, and one on `t_q` kills the complete unary row
and (16), while reading one on both retained representatives.  Therefore
the first unary/reinsertion step is relative for the same reason as the
collision top.

## 5. The full unsigned-transvection shortcut

There is a useful formal alternative.  Replace (1) by the full unsigned
vertex transvection

\[
                         X=\sum_{u\ne0,S}a_{Su}
                              {\partial\over\partial a_{0u}}.   \tag{17}

On the complete hafnian, both parents of every collision contribute with
coefficient `+1`.  Hence `X(H)` is the symmetric collision row with
coefficient two on all 45 monomials.  On the selected local channel

\[
                   A=Dq_{01},qquad B=p_0s_1,

the opposite unsigned transvection satisfies

\[
                              YX(A)=A+B.               \tag{18}

So (18) is the desired signless mate at the formal coefficient level.

It is not an absolute physical root square.  Every physical coefficient
row is a perfect matching and has augmented-vertex degree

```text
(1,1,1,1,1,1,1,1),
```

whereas the symmetric collision row has degree

```text
(1,2,0,1,1,1,1,1).
```

The exhaustive physical-word inventory of `ba087c6` has zero projection
to this sector.  Differentiating the hafnian polynomial by (17) identifies
the missing top; it does not make that top a source boundary.  Its complete
first PP boundary is larger than the `Xi_01` packet:

```text
180 flags = 90 3K2 + 90 P3+K2.
```

The `P3+K2` half remains in response word `11:110000` and is not the
canonical AugP2 word/fine cell.  Therefore the unsigned shortcut replaces
the centered `Xi_01/R_01` obstruction by the parent-even symmetric
collision cell and its 180-face physical descent.  If that entire symmetric
cell were independently constructed, (18) would indeed be a positive
route.  The transvection and canonical occurrence cylinder do not construct
it.
