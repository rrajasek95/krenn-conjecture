# The support-28 occurrence guard is one design orbit, and its diagonal torus is empty

## Result

Consider the full (K_8) occurrence CNF at support size (28), with the
distinguished edge (01) supported in exactly colours (1,2).  The exact
orbit-blocking audit in
`computations/analyze_n8_global_support28_design_orbits_and_torus.py` proves:

1. all satisfying supports form one orbit under the order-(2880) group
   generated by the setwise stabilizer of (01) and the colour swap
   (1\leftrightarrow2);
2. that orbit has size (720), hence stabilizer order (4);
3. a canonical representative is the doubled (K_4) one-factorization
   design described below; and
4. the diagonal coefficient fibre over every support in the orbit is empty
   over every field of characteristic different from (2).

The last claim has a three-binomial Laurent-unit certificate.  It is stronger
than a numerical rank or Fredholm test and does not use any pure, four-term,
or six-term equation.

## Canonical design

Take four supervertices with left sites

\[
 L=(0,1,2,3)
\]

and paired right sites

\[
 R=(4,6,7,5).
\]

The pair edges (L_iR_i=04,16,27,35) have all three colours.  Colour the
edges of the abstract (K_4) by its one-factorization

\[
 01,23\mapsto0,\qquad 03,12\mapsto1,\qquad 02,13\mapsto2.
\]

For (i\ne j), the two cross-shore edges (L_iR_j,L_jR_i) carry the
singleton factor colour of (ij), whereas each same-shore edge
(L_iL_j,R_iR_j) carries its two-colour complement.  Thus the (28) graph
edges split as

\[
 12\text{ singleton}+12\text{ double}+4\text{ triple},
\]

giving (48) nonzero diagonal variables.  Each vertex-colour pair is
incident to four supported cells.  Each colour graph has (16) edges and
(24) perfect matchings.

The orbit statement is exhaustive but solver-backed: the checker generates
the literal CNF, verifies a returned model with the source audit, blocks all
(720) transforms of its support, and obtains UNSAT.  This is deliberately
separate from any generated CNF/DRUP certificate.

## Exact diagonal coefficient equations

For every supported cell introduce a unit (x_{ij}^{c}).  For a word
(w\in\{0,1,2\}^{8}), the diagonal equation is

\[
 \sum_{\substack{M\text{ a perfect matching}\\
                   w_i=w_j\text{ and }c=w_i\text{ supported on }ij
                   \text{ for every }ij\in M}}
       \prod_{ij\in M}x_{ij}^{w_i}
 =
 \begin{cases}
 1,&w\text{ is pure},\\
 0,&w\text{ is mixed}.
 \end{cases}
\]

There are (309) nontrivial equations: (204) mixed binomials, (54)
mixed four-term equations, (48) mixed six-term equations, and three pure
(24)-term equations.  The (204\times48) binomial character matrix has
rational rank (27).

## Three-binomial unit certificate

The following three literal mixed-word equations occur in the canonical
support:

\[
\begin{aligned}
w=11110022:&\quad
x_{01}^{1}x_{23}^{1}x_{45}^{0}x_{67}^{2}
+x_{02}^{1}x_{13}^{1}x_{45}^{0}x_{67}^{2}=0,\\
w=11212100:&\quad
x_{01}^{1}x_{24}^{2}x_{35}^{1}x_{67}^{0}
+x_{05}^{1}x_{13}^{1}x_{24}^{2}x_{67}^{0}=0,\\
w=10110122:&\quad
x_{02}^{1}x_{14}^{0}x_{35}^{1}x_{67}^{2}
+x_{05}^{1}x_{14}^{0}x_{23}^{1}x_{67}^{2}=0.
\end{aligned}
\]

Every displayed factor is a unit by the fixed support.  Cancelling the
common factors gives

\[
 x_{01}^{1}x_{23}^{1}=-x_{02}^{1}x_{13}^{1},\qquad
 x_{01}^{1}x_{35}^{1}=-x_{05}^{1}x_{13}^{1},\qquad
 x_{02}^{1}x_{35}^{1}=-x_{05}^{1}x_{23}^{1}.
\]

Multiply the first and third ratios and divide by the second.  The Laurent
monomial is (1), while the sign is (-1).  Hence (1=-1), impossible in
characteristic different from (2).  Equivalently, the three binomial
character rows have the exact signed dependence

\[
 d_{11110022}-d_{11212100}+d_{10110122}=0
\]

but the corresponding coefficient sum is odd.

## Scope

This closes the diagonal coefficient fibre over every support satisfying the
specified support-(28), target-support-(12) occurrence guard.  It does not
classify smaller supports, supports with a different target support size,
off-diagonal tensor weights, or the unrestricted Krenn problem.  The SAT
guard itself is only an occurrence shadow; the unit certificate proves that
its sole support orbit cannot be promoted to an actual diagonal solution.
