## 4. Literal first HPL correction

The factorization has a rank-one contractible realization over the
normalized face ring.  Introduce one acyclic pair (u\mapsto v) and a
terminal generator (x):

\[
 d_0u=v,\qquad h(v)=u,\qquad
 \delta x=bv.                                            \tag{7}
\]

Then

\[
                I(x)=(1+h\delta)^{-1}x=x-bu              \tag{8}
\]

is an exact cycle.  Give (x) the naive physical readout (E), the
four-vector in (2), and give (u) the multiplier vector

\[
 a(u)=2\bigl(
 x_{23}^{00}x_{57}^{00},
 x_{23}^{00},x_{57}^{00},1\bigr).                       \tag{9}
\]

Equations (2), (8), and (9) give coefficientwise

\[
                       aI(x)=E-b\,a(u)=0.               \tag{10}

Thus the correction has exactly the abstract form

global hafnian/path-forest contraction.  The local calculation specifies
the value any such contraction must have.

## 5. General one-spoke and triangular-exposure lemmas

The calculation isolates a reusable elementary lemma.  Let (S) be the
localized coordinate ring of a terminal face, let (z) be one off-path
coordinate, and let (g) be a mixed source coefficient.  Suppose

\[
                           g=uz,\qquad u\in S^\times.     \tag{11}
\]

If a physical readout (E(z)) satisfies

\[
                         E(z)-E(0)\in zT                \tag{12}
\]

for an (S)-module (T), then its entire one-spoke variation is a source
boundary:

\[
 E(z)-E(0)
   ={E(z)-E(0)\over z},u^{-1}g.                        \tag{13}
\]

The quotient in (13) is polynomial by (12).  This is exactly the local value
of the first augmented correction.  In the selected chart (u=1),
(g=H_{01000111}), and (E(0)=0), so (13) is (2).

There is a correspondingly precise multi-spoke target.  Order the invisible
coordinates (z_1,\ldots,z_m).  It is enough to find mixed coefficients
(g_j) satisfying

\[
 g_j=u_jz_j+f_j,qquad
 u_j\in S^\times,qquad
 f_j\in(z_1,\ldots,z_{j-1}).                            \tag{14}
\]

Induction then gives

\[
              (g_1,\ldots,g_m)=(z_1,\ldots,z_m),        \tag{15}
\]

so every readout variation in the off-path ideal has a source-boundary
correction obtained by triangular division.  Thus the concrete remaining
terminal problem is not merely to find one exposing equation for every
spoke separately.  It is to find a compatible triangular exposure order on
each terminal chart, and to make those divisions agree under base exchange
so that they assemble into one filtered contraction.  Failure of a diagonal
unit (u_j) is exactly where a geometric open/closed split may be required.

This triangular condition is sufficient, not asserted necessary.  The exact
checker proves only its (m=1) instance.

## 6. Minimality and scope

With no retained source scalar, the two assignments (t=0) and (t=1),
with all seven terminal variables equal to one, have the same Hamilton row,
the same direct block, and the same activity.  Their cap errors are
respectively zero and ((2,2,2,2)).  Therefore a monomial-only readout, or
any zero-dimensional augmentation of it, cannot work.  Equation (1) shows
that one scalar suffices.  Hence the source-data dimension for this frozen
one-spoke family is exactly one.

The conclusion is deliberately bounded in three ways.

1. Coordinates outside the displayed face are set to zero.
2. Support coordinates are inverted (and normalized to one).
3. Only the single perturbation (x_{02}^{00}) is varied.

The result proves that the original four-term witness does not survive the
smallest source-faithful augmentation on its own face.  A terminal theorem
still needs a uniform construction over all invisible spokes and all
terminal rows, compatible with base exchange and source-label
identification.
