# The concentrated unary-top identity lifts through order four

## Outcome

The exact source-provenant identity from
[`n8-lemma-e-unary-top-offdiagonal-filtered-lift.md`](n8-lemma-e-unary-top-offdiagonal-filtered-lift.md)
extends through off-diagonal order four.  With all 135 internal cells retained,

\[
 F_{01}(1111)F_{23}(2222)H(000000)
 \in I+\mathcal F^{\geq5},                              \tag{1}
\]

where `I` is generated by the 285 literal compatible normalized zero
coefficient rows and `F` counts off-diagonal-colour cells in the target fine
multidegree.  This is an exact statement over `QQ`, not only a modular
reconnaissance.

It still does not prove full membership: orders five through seven and the
passage from concentrated spokes to multisite endpoint stars remain open.

## The 157-coordinate Schur calculation

The direct order-four block consists of source multiples whose minimum
off-diagonal order is already four.  Its exact census is

```text
order-four rows:             14,617
direct source columns:       30,812
rank modulo 1,000,003:       14,460
direct quotient dimension:     157.
```

The checker then replays the surjective order-at-most-three block, but carries
only the 157 quotient coordinates of each order-four tail.  Its 31,182 source
columns have 11,118 low pivots and 20,064 low dependencies.  The dependent
columns induce rank 136 in the 157-dimensional quotient.  Thus the complete
truncated matrix has

```text
rows:                 25,735
literal columns:      61,994
rank over QQ:         25,714
cokernel over QQ:         21.
```

The actual target lift has 148 nonzero quotient-tail coordinates before
reachable reduction and remainder zero afterward.  This answers the
load-bearing question left by the order-seven counterguard: the target is
not merely disjoint from the unreachable top classes; its first reachable
order-four tail is killed by literal low-kernel corrections.

## Why the conclusion is exact over `QQ`

The modular Schur pivots exhibit a `25,714`-row minor nonzero modulo
`1,000,003`, hence the rational rank is at least `25,714`.  Back-substitution
reconstructs 21 independent full left annihilators over `ZZ`.  Every
coefficient is `+1` or `-1`; their supports are

```text
24,16,16,28,26,12,24,24,24,16,24,
48,48,16,28,24,26,12,24,48,48.
```

The checker pairs these covectors with all 61,994 literal integer source
columns and obtains zero exactly.  They give the matching rank upper bound.
It also pairs every covector with the pure target and obtains zero.  Since
the 21 covectors form the complete rational left cokernel, the target lies in
the rational column span, proving (1).

No selected matching term, support torus, cell inversion, or anonymous
abstract row enters this calculation.  The multipliers are all complementary
site-colour token matchings and retain their original top/cofactor source
labels.

## Scope and next gate

The result covers arbitrary support and cancellation in the full internal
`3 x 3` cells, but only after fixing the concentrated ordered holes `(01)`
and `(23)` and their singular spokes.  It neither constructs a normalized
source nor rules out the remaining one-bad packet.

The next bounded gate is the same reachable-tail Schur construction at
order five.  A target remainder outside its exact cokernel would stop the
concentrated identity; zero remainder would advance (1) to
`F^{>=6}`.  The 11 order-seven primitive-cycle classes from
