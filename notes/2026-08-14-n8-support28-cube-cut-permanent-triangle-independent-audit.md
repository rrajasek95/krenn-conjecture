# Independent audit: the support-28 guard has a three-row Laurent unit

## Outcome

The pair-target support-28 SAT model printed by
`search_n8_global_occurrence_cnf.py` is not a diagonal coefficient point.
Its mixed coefficient ideal is the unit ideal after localizing the 48
declared nonzero cells.  Three literal mixed hafnian rows already give the
unit; the pure normalizations are not needed.

The exact checker is
`computations/audit_n8_support28_cube_cut_permanent_triangle_unit_independent.py`.

## 1. Canonical support

The solver support has all 28 graph edges live, 16 cells in each colour, and
cell-support histogram

```text
singleton 12, double 12, triple 4.
```

Assign the vertices the following points of \(\mathbb F_2^3\):

```text
0=000  1=011  2=010  3=001  4=110  5=111  6=100  7=101.
```

Then the literal support has the simple description

\[
q^c_{uv}\ne0 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad u_c\ne v_c.       \tag{1}
\]

Thus every colour graph is a complete \(K_{4,4}\), cut by one cube
coordinate.  In particular the target edge 01 carries exactly colours 1 and
2.

Under the occurrence-level target stabilizer generated by swapping vertices
0 and 1, permuting vertices 2 through 7, and swapping colours 1 and 2, this
support has orbit size 720 and stabilizer order 4.  Its lexicographically
least edge-mask signature is

```text
(6,1,2,3,4,5,7, 7,4,5,2,3,1, 3,2,5,4,6,
 1,6,7,5, 7,6,4, 1,3, 2).
```

One map from the printed representative to that canonical representative
fixes 0 and 1, fixes the colours, and sends the old tail `(2,3,4,5,6,7)` to
`(3,5,4,7,2,6)`.

As a cross-check against the parallel four-supervertex/one-factorization
normal form, a second target-stabilizer action sends the old tail to
`(5,7,2,4,6,3)` with no endpoint or colour swap.  The resulting support is
termwise identical to that design representative, not merely equal in its
edge-size histogram.

The direct potentially nonzero-word histogram agrees with the SAT model:

```text
multiplicity 0:1332, 2:204, 4:54, 6:48, 24:3.
```

So this is a genuine occurrence guard: it has no mixed singleton.

## 2. The exact coefficient fibre

Let

\[
R=\mathbb Q[(q^c_{uv})^{\pm1}:c\in\operatorname{supp}(uv)]
\]

and let \(I_{\rm mix}\subset R\) be generated by the literal mixed
coefficients of the eight-site diagonal decorated hafnian.  Equivalently, for
a word \(w\), its coefficient is

\[
F_w=\sum_{M\text{ compatible with }w}
       \prod_{uv\in M}q^{w_u}_{uv}.                         \tag{2}
\]

This is the correct coefficient fibre: all and only the SAT-declared cells
are units.  A source point would additionally require the three pure
coefficients to equal 1, but the contradiction below occurs already in
\(I_{\rm mix}\).

Set

\[
a=q^0_{04},\quad b=q^0_{05},\quad c=q^0_{06},\qquad
d=q^0_{14},\quad e=q^0_{15},\quad f=q^0_{16}.                \tag{3}
\]

All six are units by (1).  Three literal mixed rows expand to

\[
\begin{aligned}
F_{00110022}&=u(ae+bd), &u&=q^1_{23}q^2_{67},\\
F_{00210102}&=v(af+cd), &v&=q^1_{35}q^2_{27},\\
F_{00112002}&=w(bf+ce), &w&=q^1_{23}q^2_{47}.
\end{aligned}                                                \tag{4}
\]

Every displayed prefactor is also a support unit.  These expansions are not
projections: the checker enumerates all 105 perfect matchings for each word
and finds exactly the two displayed terms.

Write the three parenthesized permanents as \(E_{45},E_{46},E_{56}\).
The elementary permanent-triangle identity is

\[
cE_{45}+bE_{46}-aE_{56}=2bcd.                                \tag{5}
\]

In terms of the actual source rows, with denominators cleared, this is

\[
cvwF_{00110022}+buwF_{00210102}-auvF_{00112002}
   =2bcduvw.                                                   \tag{6}
\]

The right side is twice a support monomial.  It is a unit in \(R\) in
characteristic zero.  Hence

\[
1\in I_{\rm mix}R,                                            \tag{7}
\]

and the diagonal coefficient fibre of this support is empty.  Over a field
of characteristic two (5) is not a unit certificate; that restriction is
essential and matches the intended complex setting.

## 3. Reusable support criterion

The argument uses only a local support pattern.  Fix a colour, two row
vertices \(r,s\), and three column vertices \(x,y,z\).  Suppose

1. all six cells \(q^c_{rj},q^c_{sj}\), \(j\in\{x,y,z\}\), are nonzero; and
2. for each pair among \(x,y,z\), the complementary four vertices split into
   one supported edge of each of the other two colours.

The three corresponding `4+2+2` mixed words force the three permanents of the
same \(2\times3\) matrix to vanish.  Identity (5) then makes the localized
mixed ideal the unit ideal.  Call this a **permanent triangle**.

The support-28 cube-cut guard contains 96 permanent triangles, exactly 32 in
each colour.  Therefore its exclusion is highly redundant.  This criterion
can be added as a purely support-level forbidden pattern in the next global
SAT pass; it is stronger than singleton avoidance but still has a literal
three-row coefficient certificate behind it.
