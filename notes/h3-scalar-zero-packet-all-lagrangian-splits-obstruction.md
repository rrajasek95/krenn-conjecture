# Every Lagrangian split of the fixed scalar-zero lift fails a mixed row

## Result

Fix the six-dimensional latent response lift used by the exact scalar-zero
guard in commit `8e49a4c`.  Let

\[
 L=\langle p_0,p_1,p_2,t_0,t_1,t_2\rangle
\]

and let `J` be the nondegenerate symmetric form with

\[
 J(p_i,p_j)=J(t_i,t_j)=0,\qquad J(p_i,t_j)=\delta_{ij}.
                                                               \tag{1}
\]

There is no complementary maximal-`J`-isotropic split `L=P+S`, with any
choice of bases of `P` and `S`, whose wordwise cross blocks satisfy all nine
physical pair rows

\[
 C_w(P,S)=D_w-\operatorname {haf}(q)_w a                 \tag{2}
\]

for one common direct matrix `a`.  Thus the failure found in `8e49a4c` is
not an accident of its displayed `P,S` split.

The obstruction uses only two pure rows and one mixed row.  It is a
basis-invariant rank-one polarization identity, sharper than a search for a
common invariant Lagrangian of the algebra generated by `J^{-1}C_w`.

The exact checker is
`computations/verify_h3_scalar_zero_packet_all_lagrangian_splits_obstruction.py`.

## 1. The latent bilinear forms

Retain the response factors from the previous guard:

```text
u0=x00+x40,       v0=x01+x11,
u1=x10+x30,       v1=x02+x22,

p0=u0+i v0,       t0=(u0-i v0)/2,
p1=u1+i v1,       t1=(u1-i v1)/2,
p2=x20,            t2=-x50.
```

They are linearly independent.  Their two coordinate triples are
complementary maximal isotropics for (1), and their response lift is
`sum p_i t_i`.

For every six-letter word `w`, define the symmetric bilinear form

\[
 C_w(u,v)=[u v q^{[2]}]_w,qquad u,v\in L.              \tag{3}
\]

The checker constructs all 729 forms directly from the six decorated edges
of `q`.  Exactly 25 are nonzero.  In the ordered basis
`(p0,p1,p2,t0,t1,t2)`, put

\[
 \ell_0=(0,2,0,0,1,0),\qquad
 \ell_2=(0,2,0,0,-1,0).                                \tag{4}
\]

Three of the word forms obey the exact identities

\[
\begin{aligned}
 C_{000000}&={1\over2}\ell_0\otimes\ell_0,\\
 C_{222222}&=-{1\over2}\ell_2\otimes\ell_2,\\
 C_{002101}&={i\over4}
  (\ell_0\otimes\ell_2+\ell_2\otimes\ell_0).
                                                               \tag{5}
\end{aligned}
\]

Equivalently, their only nonzero `p1,t1` blocks are

\[
 C_{000000}=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&1/2\end{pmatrix},\quad
 C_{222222}=\begin{pmatrix}-2&1\\1&-1/2\end{pmatrix},\quad
 C_{002101}=\begin{pmatrix}2i&0\\0&-i/2\end{pmatrix}.
                                                               \tag{6}
\]

The full matrices and the factorizations in (5) are replayed over exact
Gaussian rationals.

## 2. The pure rows normalize the restrictions

Suppose a split and bases satisfied (2).  At the pure words the common
quadratic has zero hafnian, while the target matrices are the corresponding
diagonal matrix units.  Hence

\[
 C_{000000}(P,S)=E_{00},\qquad
 C_{222222}(P,S)=E_{22}.                                \tag{7}
\]

Write `x_c=ell_c|P` and `y_c=ell_c|S` in the chosen bases.  By (5), (7)
becomes

\[
 {1\over2}x_0y_0^{\mathsf T}=E_{00},\qquad
 -{1\over2}x_2y_2^{\mathsf T}=E_{22}.                  \tag{8}
\]

A rank-one outer product can equal a matrix unit only when both factors
lie on its row and column axes.  Therefore there are nonzero scalars
`lambda,mu` such that

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
 x_0=\lambda e_0,&y_0=2\lambda^{-1}e_0,\\
 x_2=\mu e_2,&y_2=-2\mu^{-1}e_2.
\end{array}                                             \tag{9}
\]

No use has been made of the displayed split.  Equations (8)--(9) hold for
every split, isotropic or otherwise, that has the two required pure cross
blocks.

## 3. The mixed row gives a Laurent unit

Restrict the third identity in (5) to `P x S` and substitute (9).  Two
off-diagonal entries are forced:

\[
 (C_{002101})_{02}=-{i\lambda\over2\mu},\qquad
 (C_{002101})_{20}={i\mu\over2\lambda}.                \tag{10}
\]

They lie in different matrix positions, so cannot cancel.  More
invariantly,

\[
 4(C_{002101})_{02}(C_{002101})_{20}=1.                \tag{11}
\]

This Laurent-unit identity is independent of both normalization scalars.

But `002101` is mixed and the checker verifies

\[
 D_{002101}=0,\qquad \operatorname {haf}(q)_{002101}=0. \tag{12}
\]

Thus (2) requires `C_002101(P,S)=0`, contradicting (11).  The direct matrix
`a` cannot help because its multiplier vanishes in this word.

This proves the all-split obstruction.  Maximal isotropy and complementarity
are part of the physical factorization dictionary, but the final
contradiction does not need them: the two pure cross blocks alone already
force the forbidden mixed block.

## 4. Scope

The theorem exhausts every complementary maximal-`J`-isotropic split and
every basis choice of the **fixed** minimal rank-six lift in `8e49a4c`.
It therefore closes the exact ambiguity left by the displayed `K=I` split.

It does not classify inequivalent rank-six completions obtained by changing
the same-site diagonal blocks that disappear in the site-square-zero
physical quotient.  Such a completion changes the latent space and the
forms `C_w`, so it is not a Lagrangian re-splitting of this `L`.  Excluding
those completions requires a separate completion-rigidity theorem or a
direct full-nine solve.  This distinction is load-bearing: the present
certificate is coordinate-free on `L`, not a claim that `L` is the unique
rank-six lift of the contracted response.
