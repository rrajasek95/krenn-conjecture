# Exact audit of the n=8 toric-binomial search

This note audits `computations/search_n8_toric_binomial_lazy_cegar.py`.
The search is over supports for which every nonconstant fibre has either zero
or two perfect-matching monomials.  Cell weights are arbitrary elements of
the complex torus; they are not restricted to signs.

## Laurent consistency

For exponent-difference rows `d_i`, the mixed equations are

\[
  x^{d_i}=-1. 
\]

Adjoin a sign coordinate and let `L` be the integer row lattice generated by
`(d_i,1)` and `(0,2)`.  The equations are consistent over the complex torus
if and only if `(0,1)` is not in `L`.  Indeed, membership gives an integer
dependency among the `d_i` whose coefficient sum is odd.  Conversely, Smith
normal form of `D` solves `D theta = (1/2)1 (mod Z)` exactly whenever no such
dependency exists.  Thus the augmented row-HNF test is both necessary and
sufficient.

The script's HNF test and its independent SymPy Smith reconstruction were
compared on 12,502 exact integer matrices.  These include every matrix with
at most four rows, at most two columns, and entries in `{-1,0,1}`, followed by
5,000 seeded random rectangular matrices with entries in `[-4,4]`.  In every
consistent case the reconstructed rational phases were converted to a common
root order and every equation was checked by integer modular arithmetic.
The checker is `computations/verify_n8_toric_lattice_engine.py`.

## Nonzero constant fibres

When extra pure terms are allowed, let `G=(Z^m+Z epsilon)/L`.  Consistency
means that the image of `epsilon` is a nonzero element of order two.  The
coordinate ring of the mixed solution set is

\[
  C[G]/(epsilon+1).
\]

The row-HNF representative has last coordinate zero or one.  Terms with the
same first coordinates and opposite last coordinate therefore combine with
opposite signs.  This is exactly the reduction performed by
`reduced_constant_product`.  The product of the three pure-fibre Laurent
polynomials is nonzero after this reduction if and only if it is a nonzero
element of the coordinate ring.  That ring is reduced: it is a group algebra
of a finitely generated abelian group over characteristic zero, with one
order-two character component selected.  Consequently a nonzero product is
nonzero at some complex torus point, so all three pure fibres are
simultaneously nonzero there.  Conversely, a zero reduced product vanishes
at every mixed-equation solution.

This establishes the exact semantics of `SURVIVOR TORIC`.  Such a survivor
still needs a fixed-data reconstruction and exhaustive coefficient check
before it can be reported as a counterexample.

As a separate implementation-level check, the signed quotient reduction was
compared with exhaustive evaluation on 1,000 random full-rank Laurent systems
in one or two variables.  Cramer's rule bounds every solution denominator by
`2|det(D)|`, so these tests enumerate the complete complex solution set.  All
root sums were decided exactly modulo the appropriate cyclotomic polynomial.
The quotient product was nonzero if and only if an enumerated point made all
three factors nonzero in every case.  See
`computations/verify_n8_toric_constant_product.py`.

The same audit also exercised the independent constructive specialization in
`computations/reconstruct_n8_toric_witness.py`: all 687 full-rank random
instances with nonzero quotient product produced exact weights of the form
`zeta^r * b^p`, and every zero-product instance was rejected.  A further 500
rank-deficient systems in two to four variables specifically exercise the
free-torus path; all 488 nonzero cases were reconstructed, with every returned
family evaluated independently and exactly modulo its cyclotomic polynomial,
and all 12 zero-product cases rejected.  Two deterministic cases additionally
force the component enumerator to skip a vanishing torsion branch and force
the integer specialization to reject base two before succeeding at base
three.  For an n=8 survivor this routine enumerates the finite Smith torsion
components, uses an injective mixed-radix specialization on the free torus,
and then checks all `3^8` fibres.  A vertex-zero gauge divides by the three
certified nonzero pure sums and gives constant coefficients exactly one.  The
exhaustive witness checker also verifies term by term that every perfect
matching contains exactly one vertex-zero cell carrying the coefficient
coloring's vertex-zero color, so the common normalization factor is checked
explicitly.

## Orbit-40 monomial boundary

The 28-cell orbit-40 seed chooses one diagonal cell on every edge of `K_8`.
Direct enumeration of all `3^8` colourings and all 105 perfect matchings gives
the fibre distribution

\[
  \{1:1,\;2:38,\;4:1,\;24:1\}.
\]

Thus every one of its 38 mixed nonempty fibres is binomial.  Nevertheless its
38 exponent rows contain exactly 48 three-row relations with all coefficients
in `{+1,-1}`.  Each has odd coefficient sum, so multiplying its three
binomial equations yields `1=-1`.  Every circuit uses ten distinct selected
cells.  Therefore this boundary support admits no arbitrary-complex phase
assignment.  The exact checker is
`computations/verify_n8_toric_orbit40_boundary.py`.

The CEGAR search now finds all such unit-coefficient triangle circuits by a
quadratic hash lookup and learns all their sound ten-cell (or smaller) support
guards at once.

The 48 boundary guards lie in two disjoint orbits under arbitrary vertex and
colour relabeling.  Each orbit has 30,240 elements, so they yield 60,480
target-independent ten-cell clauses.  These are preloaded by default and all
later circuit guards are deduplicated against them.  The independent checker
`computations/verify_n8_toric_diagonal_triangle_orbits.py` derives the two
representatives afresh from the 48 checked circuits, enumerates both full
`S_8 x S_3` orbits, verifies their sizes and disjointness, and checks exact
equality with the encoder's generated clause set.

After that preload, four further model-specific triangle guards were captured.
Each ten-cell guard, considered as a support by itself, has exactly three
nonempty fibres; all three are binomial and their exponent rows form exactly
one odd unit-coefficient circuit.  Up to `S_8 x S_3`, these give three new
orbits of sizes 30,240, 60,480, and 30,240.  The first contains a genuinely
off-diagonal endpoint-colour cell; the other two are new diagonal schemas.
They are pairwise disjoint and disjoint from the first 60,480 guards.  Thus
the default production preload now has 181,440 clauses.  The independent
checker `computations/verify_n8_toric_additional_triangle_orbits.py` recovers
the three binomial fibres directly from each representative's ten cells,
checks the odd circuit, enumerates all relabelings, and checks exact equality
with `global_triangle_guards()`.

A dense 83-cell structural checkpoint exposed a further twelve-cell guard.
Three of its mixed binomials have coefficient signs `(-1,+1,+1)` in an exact
zero row relation.  The guard's full `S_8 x S_3` orbit is regular of size
241,920 and disjoint from all 181,440 prior guards; preloading that entire
orbit is unnecessary.  Its orbit under the fixed orbit-40 target
automorphism group has only 48 members, all distinct, and removing forced
target cells leaves seven-literal support clauses.  The full orbit contains a
larger but still inexpensive target-relevant slice: 28,800 guards contain at
least three forced cells.  After removing those units they remain distinct,
with 384 clauses of length seven, 4,608 of length eight, and 23,808 of length
nine.  Production preloads this complete high-overlap slice, which contains
the 48-element target-automorphism orbit.  The remaining full-orbit overlap
counts are also recorded by the verifier.  `computations/verify_n8_toric_dense_target_triangle.py`
derives the three terms and relation directly, checks containment in the
preserved checkpoint, enumerates both orbits and the high-overlap slice
independently, and verifies exact equality with production.

For the extra-constant search, a consistent mixed lattice can still force the
product of the three pure sums to vanish.  The encoder installs exact Boolean
indicators for all 315 pure matching terms.  Its zero-product nogood guards
(i) every cell used by the current mixed binomials and (ii) the exact
present/absent assignment of those 315 pure terms.  If a future valid
zero-or-two support falsified this clause, it would retain both terms of every
current mixed binomial; it cannot add a third term, so it retains all current
Laurent equations.  It also retains the three pure Laurent polynomials.
Therefore the same quotient-product calculation is still zero.  This proves
the term-level cut sound while allowing every irrelevant support bit to vary.
The independent truth-table checker
`computations/verify_n8_toric_zero_product_nogood.py` exhausts both the
matching-term equivalence CNF and the guarded-clause semantics.

Before learning this clause the encoder minimizes both the mixed row set and
the set of pure colours, explicitly testing every single row across every
minimum-cardinality vanishing colour set first.  This last qualification is
important: committing to the first colour set before scanning can miss a
one-row certificate belonging to a later colour.  A small exact regression in
`verify_n8_toric_zero_product_nogood.py` exercises that case.  On a captured
36-cell phase-consistent chart (94 mixed binomials and pure fibre sizes 24, 2,
2), the original deletion heuristic found a valid alternative three-row
certificate with a ten-cell guard.  The singleton scan finds a stronger
one-row certificate: after canceling common matching cells, its exponent
difference is literally the exponent difference of the two pure terms.  Thus
the mixed equation makes the pure terms opposites.  The three-row certificate
and its 15,120 relabelings remain exactly checked but are subsumed and are not
preloaded.

The first minimal hybrid guard consists of six support cells plus the exact
status of 105 pure matching indicators, for 111 literals.  Its full
`S_8 x S_3` orbit has 7,560 distinct schemas.  The independent checker
`computations/verify_n8_toric_one_row_pure_zero_orbit.py` verifies the exponent
identity, the `[1,1]` opposite-sign quotient class, the orbit size, and exact
equality with `global_one_row_pure_zero_schemas()`.

For a fixed target, however, that global orbit contains only 144 relevant
schemas.  A complete classification starts with the forced pure matching
`P`: the other pure matching `Q` must share one or two edges with `P`; the
mixed pair uses the same alternating cycle and an arbitrary shared perfect
matching on the common-edge vertices, whose colors may be assigned in every
nonconstant way.  There are exactly 9,408 such target-compatible schemas:
8,640 have six-cell guards and 768 have seven-cell guards.  Thus 9,264 were
not present in the original orbit.

For a four-cycle row there are three disjoint opposite-sign pure matching
pairs, one for each complement matching.  Therefore exact pure fibres of
sizes four or six can also vanish as unions of complete pairs.  Including all
such unions gives the genuinely complete one-row family of 35,328 schemas,
with pure-size distribution 9,408 / 17,280 / 8,640 for sizes two / four / six.
After forced cells are removed the guards remain distinct and have sizes two
through four.  Only 204 exact pure-fibre assignments occur.  Production
factors them through shared existential triggers: exact fibre presence forces
its trigger, while every associated support guard forces it false.
Existential elimination gives the original clauses exactly, and all 35,328
guard clauses have lengths only 3--5.  Compared with direct term-status
clauses the encoding saves 3,617,364 literal occurrences.  The generalized
projection is truth-table checked in
`verify_n8_toric_zero_product_nogood.py`.

The independent inverse enumeration in
`computations/verify_n8_toric_all_target_one_row.py` starts from every mixed
matching pair, then reconstructs all pure quotient pairs directly from each
row signature, proving exact set equality with the 35,328-schema production
generator.  It also checks a preserved 83-cell full-formula structural chart
with 3,687 mixed binomials and pure sizes `(2,2,2)`: the old global orbit has
no hit, while the complete target family has five, each rechecked directly in
the chart.  This supplies a concrete regression showing that the additional
schemas are operational rather than merely formal.
