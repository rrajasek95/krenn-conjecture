# Augmented source contraction: living attack sketch

Audit date: 2026-08-11.

This note records the proof attack as evidence changes it.  It distinguishes
proved local mechanisms, conditional interfaces, and tempting implications
already ruled out by physical guards.

## 1. Revised master principle

The conjecture is not governed by an ordinary matching graph alone.  The
working source object has two layers.

### Presentation layer

For one complete endpoint star, let `C0` be generated by literal matching
occurrences

```text
(source row, fine word, endpoint ports, physical matching, decorated tail).
```

A genuine exchange `1`-cell is present only when two occurrences differ by
one physical `C4` and retain the identical decorated complementary tail and
the opposite determinant orientation.  Complete coefficient rows give
attaching relations among occurrences and target symbols.  They become
filled higher cells only after an explicit source-labelled exchange-chain
lift is constructed.

### Correction/readout layer

For an augmented physical correction map

\[
                         \widehat J:L\longrightarrow E,              \tag{1}
\]

and a distinguished physical readout `q` on `L`, there is a common linear
alternative:

- if `q` detects `ker(Jhat)`, a normalized kernel class is terminal;
- if `q` kills `ker(Jhat)`, the readout descends through the image and the
  appropriate Fredholm/separator alternative applies.

In Theorem A, a detected same-star kernel is an anchor-safe support
contraction.  In Theorems B/C, a detected physically typed kernel is the
relative generator.  This is the genuine common principle.

The nonproportional Fitting branch in A is different.  A nonzero exterior
minor certifies injectivity/nonproportionality; it is not terminal homology
and does not itself give distinct heads, deleted-star rank three, or a clean
cap.  A separate source-labelled comparison must turn it into a physical
carrier, followed by rank landing and termination.

## 2. Exact evidence from the minimal disconnected packet

The four-base packet

```text
A=01|23|45, B=01|24|35, K=02|15|34, L=05|12|34
```

has two flat physical `C4` components.  Common `q` alone permits two
independent coefficient lines, so local flatness does not connect them.

The full unary and diagonal rows change the answer:

1. On the exact four-base support, an integral three-row identity gives a
   source unit.
2. Every extension forces one of nine physical `C4` bridges or two `C6`
   separators.
3. One `C6` meets both crossed holes.  The sole silent separator is
   `03|14|25`.
4. Complete response rows turn that silent separator into a typed crossed
   chord, a direct-cap exit, or one chordless diagonal response lock.
5. Zero response fibres do not close the lock: an exact rational guard has
   injective five-lock map and no complementary wedge.
6. Every genuine fixed-port bright completion nevertheless supplies a
   source unit or a nonanchor active carrier.
7. On the nine selected private core-port coefficients, proportional full
   columns absorb; the remaining reciprocal branch is a nonzero two-port
   Fitting/Hall class.  Surplus nonproportional columns and its rank landing
   remain open.

This strongly supports **contraction or witness**, but refutes the simpler
claim that every source row merely adds a graph edge.

## 3. First global-to-local reduction

Use the flip distance

\[
 \delta(M,N)=\sum_C(|C|/2-1).
\]

The theorem in `matching-base-first-separator-distance.md` proves that a
minimum-distance unjoined same-word occurrence differs from the joined
typed component by one alternating cycle, and that any supported
distance-three shortening chord contradicts minimality.

Therefore the hierarchy of genuine obstructions is:

```text
delta=1: physical C4 with missing source typing;
delta=2: chordless C6 with unresolved mixed transgression faces;
delta>=3: the same first-transgression problem, not new cycle topology.
```

The old phrase “chord-or-Hall theorem” obscured this.  The first missing
input is a source-labelled lift at `delta=1`; after that it is a
first-transgression selection theorem at `delta=2`.

## 4. Current candidate theorem for Theorem A

The strongest formulation consistent with all guards is:

> Let `J` be a connected typed component in the complete literal
> unary-plus-four-response presentation of one endpoint star in a
> maximum-anchor, minimum-support packet.  For a first nonzero occurrence
> outside `J`, one of the following holds:
>
> 1. a source-certified typed exchange joins it to `J`;
> 2. a nonzero typed curvature minor gives a physical carrier;
> 3. a complete-column kernel gives an anchor-safe support contraction;
> 4. a complete row produces an outside/nonanchor arm, direct cap, or
>    diagonal Hall reselection;
> 5. the remaining attaching relation is a source-valid Hall/Fitting or
>    five-lock class with an explicitly named terminal readout.

Items 1--4 are proved in important local packets.  Item 5 is only a
reduction unless an additional landing theorem converts the class to
four-good rank or a decreasing support move.

## 5. Evidence against over-unification

The following implications are false or unproved and must not enter the
master theorem silently:

- physical `C4` adjacency implies a typed complete-column exchange;
- a complete coefficient row automatically supplies a filled `2`-cell;
- coefficientwise flatness with identically empty support is vertex gauge;
- a nonzero Fitting determinant is terminal homology;
- a nonzero Fitting determinant gives four-good/clean landing;
- the nine selected private core-port coefficients classify surplus endpoint
  columns;
- the special silent-`C6` private-row mechanism selects a chord for a
  generic `C6`;
- a formal derived filler in B/C is already a physical anchor or cap.

These guards are evidence for a two-stage architecture rather than against
the overall program:

```text
source presentation/exhaustivity
            then
physical readout and landing.
```

## 6. Immediate proof attacks

### A1. Typed `C4` lift

Given a minimum-distance physical `C4` separator, use the full opposite
diagonal and crossed rows to produce the same decorated tail in the opposite
determinant orientation, or route an endpoint/offanchor/Hall term.  This is
the first unresolved implication even before long cycles.

### A2. First `C6` transgression

For a chordless `C6`, the punctured-face functional has four signed terms:
one desired pure chord and three mixed faces.  Use all five complete tensors
to route or kill the three mixed faces of one shortening base.  The special
silent packet proves this is possible in one nontrivial chart; the generic
statement is open.

### A3. Hall/Fitting landing

For the reciprocal core-port block, identify the common Fitting covector
with a literal rank-restoring response base, a same-star kernel, or a Hall
triangle/`K2,2` relation.  A mere nonzero minor is insufficient.

### A4. Active rank and potential

After a carrier is obtained, promote possible rank profile `(2,2,3,3)` to
four-good rank or lower a well-founded measure.  A candidate measure begins
with

```text
(number of typed source components,
 minimum separator flip distance,
 unresolved lock/Fitting rank,
 endpoint support).
```

No claim of monotonicity is made yet.

### BC. Physically typed comparison

Independently construct the derived-to-physical augmented comparison for
the non-Euler filler.  The shared kernel/readout alternative is already
formal; physical `W`, target, residue, fine grade, and anchor incidence are
the missing typing.  On the dense cyclotomic face-zero stratum the comparison
must additionally carry the complete indexed normal face.

## 7. How evidence will update this sketch

Every new calculation should answer one of four questions:

1. Does it create a certified source cell, or only physical adjacency?
2. Does it prove contraction, produce a curvature/Fitting witness, or leave
   an unfilled attaching relation?
3. Is the witness already terminal under a physical readout, or does it
   still need rank landing?
4. Which coordinate of the proposed global potential strictly decreases?

Results that answer none of these questions should not enlarge the proof
frontier.
