## All-order implication and missing lemma

The last three generic-$L$ obstruction orders now exhibit a stable shape:
Q30/Q33 are a unimodular pair times one scalar bend equation, and the new
pure H0 coefficient is a multiple of that same scalar equation.  The monic
new-bend coefficient is precisely the Hensel mechanism one would want for
indefinite formal lifting.

This pattern is evidence for, but not yet proof of, an all-order lemma.  The
missing step is a source-level identity in the completed local ring showing
for every order that:

1. the mixed obstruction cokernel stays rank one and is represented by the
   Q30/Q33 unimodular pair;
2. the newest free $z_{46}$ bend enters its scalar obstruction with a unit
   coefficient; and
3. the corresponding H0 and H1 pure coefficients lie in that scalar
   obstruction ideal.

Proving these three statements inductively would replace further order-by-
order expansion with an all-orders local membership theorem.  Without that
identity, the next exact fallback is the fourth normal graph, mixed strict
order eight, and H1 degree ten/H0 degree eleven.
