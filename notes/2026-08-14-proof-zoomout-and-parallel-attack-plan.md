# Proof zoom-out and parallel attack plan

## Executive conclusion

The conjecture is not yet proved, but the remaining work has a clean
hierarchy. The global induction is short once a uniform physical
comparison-or-terminal theorem is supplied. The present bottleneck is not
merely the four-coordinate relation

```text
z = (1, 1, -1, -1).
```

It is the construction, with literal source labels and all protected
readouts, of the response-Kodaira--Spencer to cap-`r0` comparison whose
local mixed face is measured by `z`. After that local construction one must
still prove its all-order prolongation and identify every non-lift with an
actual source terminal.

## The intended proof spine

1. **Palette reduction [P].** Any counterexample with at least three colours
   restricts to an exact ternary source.
2. **Minimal representative [P, with local generation qualifications].**
   Choose minimum even order, then maximum protected anchors and minimum
   occupied support.
3. **Six-site base case [P].** No exact ternary source exists at `N = 6`.
4. **Branch-complete comparison `PAComp(h)` [O].** At `N = 2h+2 >= 8`, the
   complete physical source complex must yield either an accepted physical
   terminal or an active clean cap.
5. **Clean-pair descent [P].** An active clean cap reconstructs an exact
   ternary source on `N-2` sites.
6. **Minimality.** The descended source contradicts minimum order; iterating
   reaches the six-site contradiction.
7. **Lower construction.** The known binary sources give `k_max(N)=2`.

Only Step 4 contains new proof mathematics on the global spine. Step 5 now
has a paper proof, a standalone symbolic checker, and an independent
from-scratch audit.

## Decision rule

Do not call a coefficient identity, a target-safe shadow, or a finite local
cokernel the missing theorem. A successful result must either:

- construct an actual source-labelled chain map with the full literal
  grading and augmented readouts; or
- prove an exhaustive comparison to the actual physical source complex and
  extend the dual to its accepted terminal quotient.

Everything else is valuable fencing, but not closure of the conjecture.
