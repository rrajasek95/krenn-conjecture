> Every primitive physical relative-C1 generator with word `01211222`, one
> of the six literal Gamma fine labels, repeated shape `P3+K2`, literal DQ/PS
> operation tag, root path AB or AC, and all protected augmented rows is

Equivalently, there is no independent same-grade physical column with
nonzero \(\delta\cdot(B-\mathrm{Eq})\).  This includes physical
occurrence-local source provenance for the two target-cone objects and the

relative-C1 generator.  Constructing such a descent would fill the Eq class.
Proving no such primitive descent exists would promote the declared terminal
to the accepted physical Fredholm terminal.
