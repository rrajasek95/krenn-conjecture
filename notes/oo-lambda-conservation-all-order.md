# The output-cascade separator is an all-order conservation law

Exact theorem for the committed physical polynomial codomain.  The
order-four cokernel covector from
[`oo-complete-order4-spencer-output-cascade.md`](oo-complete-order4-spencer-output-cascade.md)
is structural: it vanishes on every polynomial combination of the physical
full-nine row, denominator, Hasse, chart, and split-cap generators, and
therefore on the image of every later differential landing in that same
codomain.  Thus enumerating generic order five cannot produce the missing
relative generator there.  This is not a proof of the curved overlap theorem
or Krenn's conjecture: a genuinely enlarged source resolution can evade the
conservation law.

## 1. The augmentation and separator

Let \(R\) be the committed labelled-edge polynomial ring, including the
homogenizer \(u\), cap parameter \(Y\), and curvature \(\kappa\).  Write
\(\epsilon:R\to R_0\) for evaluation of every labelled physical edge at
zero, retaining \(u,Y,\kappa\).  For a physical degree-one chain \(x\), its
edge-zero Eq boundary is divisible by \(u\).  Define

\[
 \operatorname {Aug}(x)=(E,W,T,R)
 =\left(\frac{\epsilon(d_{\rm Eq}x)}u,
        \epsilon(d_wx),\epsilon(\operatorname {tgt}x),
        \epsilon(\operatorname {ores}x)\right).
\]

The four entries named \(T,R\) here are target and ordinary-residue
coordinates, not the source generators with the same conventional letters.
The separator is

\[
             \Lambda(E,W,T,R)=YE+W+YT-R.                 \tag{1}
\]

The forced divisibility follows from the literal row convention

\[
       F_\alpha\big|_{\rm edges=0}
          =-\operatorname {tgt}(r_\alpha)u,              \tag{2}
\]

and from the cap generators having no Eq boundary.

## 2. Generator calculation

The physical degree-one module is generated by the full-nine rows together
with the split-cap generators \(T,\rho\).  In the selected fine target
coordinate, the only nonzero full-nine target row is the pure word; the
other 6560 rows augment to zero.  The complete nonzero generator table is

\[
\begin{array}{c|rrrr|c}
 &E&W&T&R&\Lambda\\ \hline
 r_0&-1&0&1&0&0\\
 T&0&-Y&1&0&0\\
 \rho&0&1&0&1&0\\
 \text{physical Hasse/denominator top}&-1&Y&0&0&0.
\end{array}                                               \tag{3}
\]

The mixed row \(r_m\) augments to zero.  The last line is \(r_0-T\), so
it is not a new physical generator.  The checker exhausts all \(3^8=6561\)
literal rows and verifies (2), rather than assuming the table abstractly.

For every \(f\in R\), evaluation is a ring map and hence

\[
       \operatorname {Aug}(fx)=\epsilon(f)\operatorname {Aug}(x),
 \qquad
       \Lambda\operatorname {Aug}(fx)
          =\epsilon(f)\Lambda\operatorname {Aug}(x).     \tag{4}
\]

Therefore (3) proves \(\Lambda\operatorname {Aug}(M_1)=0\) for the
entire physical degree-one module with arbitrary polynomial coefficients.
This is the load-bearing all-order statement; the finite face audits below
pin the claimed Hasse/denominator/chart objects to that literal module.

## 3. Hasse, Spencer, denominators, and charts

The exact checker reruns the frozen order-four baseline and then verifies
the separator on:

* all 15 literal four-direction Hasse cubes and all 16 faces of each,
  both for the diagonally projected chains and their response chains;
* all 15 denominator columns, all 15 selection cubes, and all four internal
  faces, for 900 denominator face probes in total; and
* the committed two-chart parity ledger: the chart-odd comparison is
  boundaryless, targetless, and residue-free, while the chart-even part is
  the same conserved physical cap graph.

Every nonzero physical Hasse top has coordinates \((-1,Y,0,0)\).  Every
denominator face is a polynomial multiple of \(r_0-T\); the 15 Kronecker
tops reproduce the same vector.  Lower Hasse/Spencer faces and all response
faces also satisfy (1) exactly.  Thus face extraction and chart comparison
do not introduce a generator outside the physical module.

## 4. Closure under all later differentials

Let \(C_\bullet\) be any continuation of the **same committed physical
resolution**, so the degree-one target remains \(M_1\).  A later source
generator has differential

\[
                         dz=\sum_i f_i g_i,              \tag{5}
\]

where the \(g_i\) are the degree-one physical generators and \(f_i\in R\).
Equations (3)--(4) give

\[
                  \Lambda\operatorname {Aug}(dz)=0.      \tag{6}
\]

No order bound is used.  Polynomial multiplication, Hasse/Spencer
prolongation followed by physical diagonal projection, chart comparison,
and every differential whose image stays in this codomain preserve the
separator.  The desired relative source would require

\[
              q=(0,\kappa Y,0,0),\qquad \Lambda(q)=\kappa Y. \tag{7}
\]

On the active \(\kappa Y\ne0\) open this is a unit, contradicting (6).
Consequently **no order-five or higher enumeration in the same physical
codomain can supply \(n_c\)**.

## 5. Localization scope

The proof survives a localization \(S^{-1}R\) precisely when the
edge-zero map extends to it: each \(\epsilon(s)\), \(s\in S\), must be
invertible in the localized target.  This includes localization at
\(\kappa\), \(Y\), or \(\kappa Y\), so it includes the active curved/good
open used in (7).  It does **not** include localization at a labelled edge,
at \(H_m\), or at any source polynomial killed by edge-zero evaluation.
Those denominators have zero image and the augmentation, hence this
separator proof, is undefined there.  This distinction is necessary: it
does not turn the polynomial-source theorem into a claim about arbitrary
rational source operations.

## 6. Where conservation first fails

The free prolonged fourth-Spencer object already has an upstairs class

\[
        \kappa(s_I-T):\qquad
        \operatorname {Aug}_{\rm formal}=(0,\kappa Y,0,0),
        \quad \Lambda=\kappa Y.                          \tag{8}
\]

It is not an element of the committed physical degree-one codomain.
Physical diagonal projection adds exactly the Eq defect and produces

\[
             (-\kappa,\kappa Y,0,0),\qquad\Lambda=0.     \tag{9}
\]

Thus the first failure of conservation is not a mysterious generic
order-five face: it is the known **formal order-four** prolongation before
physical descent.  The complete order-four census proves that no existing
physical order-\(\le4\) column realizes (8).  To reopen the lane one must
add a genuine source-resolution generator whose physical image has the
formal coordinates (8) without the diagonal Eq term in (9), or change to a
localization on which the edge-zero augmentation does not exist.  Merely
adding later faces built from the present rows cannot work.
