# Component IV requires a new denominator-relative source row

Research boundary only.  This does not prove the full overlap theorem,
`SP-CLEAN-BRIDGE`, or Krenn's conjecture.

## Outcome

The missing Component-IV chain

\[
 d n_c=\kappa Yw,\qquad
 \operatorname{tgt}(n_c)=\operatorname{ores}(n_c)=0       \tag{1}
\]

cannot be constructed from the existing physical EqSystem rows, cap rows,
or any polynomial Koszul/Bianchi/full-word identity among them.  This is not
merely a failure of the fourth-Hasse candidate or of a bounded multiplier
search.

In the ordinary physical row-plus-cap module, write a chain as

\[
 n=\sum_\alpha A_\alpha r_\alpha+aT+b\rho,
\]

where the (r_\alpha) run over all (3^8=6561) literal EqSystem rows and
the coefficients are arbitrary polynomials in the labelled source cells.
The target and residue conditions in (1) force (b=0), (a=-\kappa), and
the target-weighted sum of the (A_\alpha) to be (kappa).  The EqSystem
boundary condition forces

\[
                       \sum_\alpha A_\alpha F_\alpha=0.
\]

Every hafnian part has edge degree four.  At edge degree zero, the
homogenizing monomial (u) occurs only in the pure row
(F_0=H_0-u), with coefficient (-1).  Extracting that monomial from the
last equation says the same target-weighted sum is zero.  Since (kappa)
is a unit on the active chart, this is impossible.

Consequently, increasing the multiplier degree or adding a Bianchi/Koszul
syzygy among the same row generators cannot help: its image is still a
polynomial combination in this module.  Localizing at a source equation is
not allowed, because that equation vanishes on every physical source.

before invoking the all-degree extraction.  On each of the five deletion
faces there are 48 literal columns in each chart.  Their ranks are

\[

 \qquad
 \operatorname{rank}[C_{pq}\ C_{pr}]=48.
\]

coefficient rank and physical-target rank are both zero.  All 3,645 raw
denominator terms on each face miss this fine degree.  Thus the complete
natural next source-degree inventory contains no candidate with the

\[
