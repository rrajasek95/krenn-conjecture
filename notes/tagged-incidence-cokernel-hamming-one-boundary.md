# Tagged response incidence: exact cokernel and sharp row boundaries

## 1. Outcome

Let \(W\) be the six residual sites of an \(h=3\) deleted-pair chart and
write the literal full-nine rows as

\[
 d_{ij}q^{[3]}+p_is_jq^{[2]}=\delta_{ij}X_i.                 \tag{1}
\]

At \(x\in W\), put \(L_x=\bigoplus_{y\ne x}V_y\), decompose
\(q=q_0+q_x\) with \(q_x\in V_x\otimes L_x\), and define the physical
incidence map

\[
 Q_x:V_x^*\longrightarrow L_x,\qquad
 Q_x(\lambda)=(\lambda\otimes1)q_x.                         \tag{2}
\]

For a selected off-diagonal row \((a,b)\), write
\(p_a=p_{a,x}+\bar p_a\), \(s_b=s_{b,x}+\bar s_b\), and set

\[
 \beta_x(\lambda)
 =\lambda(p_{a,x})\bar s_b+\lambda(s_{b,x})\bar p_a.         \tag{3}
\]

Let \(I_x\) be the ideal in the off-\(x\) site algebra generated by
\(\operatorname {im}Q_x\).  After reduction modulo \(I_x\), the complete
all-word selected row implies the colon relation

\[
 \boxed{
 [\beta_x(\lambda)]q_0^{[2]}=0
 \quad\text{in }
 \left({\cal A}_{W\setminus x}/I_x\right)_5.}
                                                                    \tag{4}
\]

It does not formally imply
\([\beta_x(\lambda)]=0\in\operatorname{coker}Q_x\).  That step is
injectivity of multiplication by \(q_0^{[2]}\) on the relevant
degree-one quotient.

The primary sharp boundary is an exact all-word \(8/9\) packet.  It is a
literal eight-site two-color source augmented by dead third-label endpoint
edges.  On the selected six-site chart it has

* \(d=E_{02}\), hence selected off-diagonal scalar \(\alpha=1\);
* good endpoint-star triples and literal shared-star/Segre factorization;
* all six off-diagonal rows and the complete \(00,11\) diagonal rows on
  all \(729\) residual words; and
* \(\beta_0(e_0^*)=z_4^0\) while \(Q_0=0\).

Its sole full-nine residual is \(-X_2\) in the \(22\)-row.  Therefore the
missing \(22\) diagonal row is necessary beyond this guard.  The guard does
not prove that adjoining that row is sufficient.

A second integral packet has \(d_{01}=1\), good endpoint stars, and all
nine rows correct on all \(27\) pure coefficients and all \(324\)
Hamming-one coefficients, but retains a nonzero tagged cokernel class.
Its first selected-row failure is already Hamming two.  This locates the
first omitted mixed detector rather than giving an all-word boundary.

For comparison, the existing dense K6 one-anchor packet satisfies six
complete off-diagonal rows and the complete \(00\) diagonal row, has
separated Hall selectors and \(d_{10}=-1/10\), and also retains a tagged
cokernel class.  It is cited below rather than reconstructed.

On two adjacent charts, literal shared-source compatibility identifies
the two cross-site classes in a common double quotient, but leaves one
common torsor mode.  The overlap controls the difference channel, not the
sum channel.

## 2. Exact one-site quotient

Every term of \(q_x,p_{a,x},s_{b,x}\) occupies site \(x\).  Hence
\(q_x^{[2]}=0\), and the \(V_x\)-linear coefficients are

\[
 [q^{[3]}]_{V_x}=q_xq_0^{[2]},                              \tag{5}
\]

and

\[
 [p_as_bq^{[2]}]_{V_x}
 =\left(p_{a,x}\bar s_b+s_{b,x}\bar p_a\right)q_0^{[2]}
  +(\bar p_a\bar s_b)q_xq_0.                                \tag{6}
\]

Contracting site \(x\) by \(\lambda\in V_x^*\) in the selected
off-diagonal row gives

\[
 \left(d_{ab}Q_x(\lambda)+\beta_x(\lambda)\right)q_0^{[2]}
 +(\bar p_a\bar s_b)Q_x(\lambda)q_0=0.                      \tag{7}
\]

Reducing (7) modulo \(I_x\) proves (4).  Equivalently, the desired lift
requires injectivity, on the relevant class, of

\[
 m_{q_0^{[2]}}:
 L_x/\operatorname{im}Q_x\longrightarrow
 ({\cal A}_{W\setminus x}/I_x)_5.                           \tag{8}
\]

Cancelling the matching power assumes precisely this missing statement.
For a diagonal row the same calculation retains its target normal on the
right.  The other eight rows therefore provide coupled normal identities,
but goodness of the two star triples alone says nothing about the kernel
of (8).

### 2.1 Local classes, a global derivation, and averaging are different

There are three increasingly strong assertions.

1. For one tag \(\lambda\), the class
   \([\beta_x(\lambda)]\) vanishes.
2. The complete map \(\beta_x:V_x^*\to L_x\) factors through \(Q_x\),
   with one linear local lift for all tags.
3. The six local lifts obey the edgewise compatibility equations of one
   global site derivation.  In the symmetric normalization its first
   variation is \(2p_as_b\).  Its second variation contains the additional
   terms \(q_{xy}(\lambda_x,\lambda_y)\), which local cokernel vanishing
   does not control.

Thus even sitewise vanishing for all tags needs an integrability theorem
before it becomes the physical rank-two deformation required by the cubic
update.

There is a canonical reinsertion identity, but it is only Euler
bookkeeping.  If \({\cal E}_x\) restores the \(x\)-slot and sums over a
basis of tags, then

\[
 \sum_x{\cal E}_x(Q_x)=2q,\qquad
 \sum_x{\cal E}_x(\beta_x)=2p_as_b.                         \tag{9}
\]

The six cokernels in (8) are different quotients.  Equation (9) does not
define a sum of their classes without additional comparison maps
\(\rho_x\).  Proving that a selector-weighted sum through such maps
vanishes is a new chain-map or saturation assertion, not a consequence of
Euler's identity.

### 2.2 A compatible derivation would be sufficient

The integrability target is exact.  Suppose the local lifts assemble,
after the harmless symmetric rescaling, to a derivation \(\delta\) of the
site algebra such that

\[
                         \delta q=R:=p_as_b,\qquad
                         \delta R=0.                       \tag{9a}
\]

For general \(h\), put

\[
                         Q_j=R^{[j]}q^{[h-j]}
                         \qquad(0\le j\le h).
\]

The divided-power Leibniz rule gives

\[
                         \delta Q_j=(j+1)Q_{j+1}.          \tag{9b}
\]

The selected off-diagonal row is \(\alpha Q_0+Q_1=0\).
Applying \(\delta^k\), for \(0\le k\le h\), gives

\[
                         \alpha Q_k+(k+1)Q_{k+1}=0,       \tag{9c}
\]

after division by \(k!\), with \(Q_{h+1}=0\).  Since
\(\alpha\ne0\), the \(k=h\) equation gives \(Q_h=0\), and descending in
(9c) gives \(Q_{h-1}=\cdots=Q_0=0\).  Hence every nonlinear divided-power
layer vanishes.

Thus a compatible derivation satisfying (9a) really would close the
rank-two update.  Local tagged cokernel vanishing supplies only part of
\(\delta q=R\); edge compatibility, the second-variation terms, and
\(\delta R=0\) are the remaining integrability gates.

## 3. Primary all-word eight-row guard

Start with the eight-site binary cycle on
\(\{p,q,0,1,2,3,4,5\}\).  Its color-zero matching is

\[
 p0\mid q1\mid23\mid45,
\]

and its color-one matching is

\[
 p5\mid q0\mid12\mid34.
\]

All endpoint colors in these two matchings equal the displayed color.
Add three unit cells:

\[
 pq:(0,2),\qquad p2:(2,2),\qquad q4:(2,0).                \tag{G1}
\]

The ordered pair on \(pq\) records the colors at \(p,q\); for \(p2\) and
\(q4\), the first entry is the endpoint color at \(p\) or \(q\), and the
second is the residual endpoint color.

After deleting \(p,q\), the literal residual data are

\[
\begin{aligned}
 q&=(23)_0+(45)_0+(12)_1+(34)_1,\\
 (p_0,p_1,p_2)&=(z_0^0,z_5^1,z_2^2),\\
 (s_0,s_1,s_2)&=(z_1^0,z_0^1,z_4^0),\\
 d&=E_{02}.
\end{aligned}                                             \tag{G2}
\]

Site \(0\) is isolated in \(q\), so \(q^{[3]}=0\).  Among the nine
response products, exactly two have a nonzero \(q^{[2]}\)-cofactor:

\[
 p_0s_0q^{[2]}=X_0,\qquad p_1s_1q^{[2]}=X_1.              \tag{G3}
\]

Indeed their complementary matchings are \(23\mid45\) and
\(12\mid34\), respectively.  Every other endpoint pair has zero
complementary matching.  Consequently the complete all-word ledger is

\[
 d_{ij}q^{[3]}+p_is_jq^{[2]}
 =\begin{cases}
 X_0,&(i,j)=(0,0),\\
 X_1,&(i,j)=(1,1),\\
 0,&\text{otherwise}.
 \end{cases}                                              \tag{G4}
\]

Thus all six off-diagonal rows, including the selected \(02\)-row, and
the two diagonal rows \(00,11\) hold on all \(729\) words.  Relative to
the ternary full-nine target, the complete residual ledger has exactly one
nonzero entry:

\[
 \left[d_{22}q^{[3]}+p_2s_2q^{[2]}-X_2\right]_{222222}=-1.
                                                               \tag{G5}
\]

The three forms in each star of (G2) are linearly independent.  More
explicitly, the local rows at

\[
 (0,0),(5,1),(2,2)\quad\text{for \(p\)},\qquad
 (1,0),(0,1),(4,0)\quad\text{for \(s\)}
\]

are the two coordinate selector matrices \(I_3\).  Every Segre rectangle
is literal because the responses are the shared products \(p_is_j\).
The direct orientation is exactly \(d_{02}=1,d_{20}=0\).

For the selected \((a,b,c)=(0,2,0)\), at site \(0\),

\[
 \beta_0(e_0^*)=z_4^0,\qquad Q_0=0,                       \tag{G6}
\]

so its cokernel class is nonzero even on the pure-\(0\) projection.  At
the same time (4) holds because every two-matching of the residual path
\(12,23,34,45\) covers site \(4\), and hence
\(z_4^0q_{\bar0}^{[2]}=0\).  This is a literal colon-kernel witness, not
a cancellation of nonzero matching terms.  At
the other response endpoint,

\[
 \beta_4(e_0^*)=z_0^0,\qquad
 \operatorname{im}Q_4
   =\operatorname{span}\{z_5^0,z_3^1\},                   \tag{G7}
\]

and this class is nonzero as well.

The cells in (G1) do not create another perfect matching; equivalently,
the all-word ledger (G4) checks every possible boundary coloring.
Therefore this is a physical \(8/9\) source-row guard, not a direct-sum
or restricted-support presentation.  It is not a ternary GHZ source
precisely because of (G5).

## 4. Pure-plus-Hamming-one detector boundary

Use sites \(0,\ldots,5\), colors \(0,1,2\), and variables \(z_x^c\).
Put

\[
 q=\sum_{c=0}^2
 \left(z_0^cz_1^c+z_2^cz_3^c+z_4^cz_5^c\right),
 \qquad d=E_{01}.                                          \tag{10}
\]

The nonzero local coefficient vectors of the first star are

\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
(x,c)&(p_0,p_1,p_2)_{x,c}\\ \hline
(0,0)&(1,0,0)\\
(2,1)&(-1,1,0)\\
(3,0)&(1,-1,0)\\
(4,2)&(-1,0,0)\\
(5,2)&(0,0,1),
\end{array}                                                \tag{11}
\]

and those of the second star are

\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
(x,c)&(s_0,s_1,s_2)_{x,c}\\ \hline
(1,0)&(1,-1,0)\\
(2,1)&(0,1,0)\\
(3,0)&(0,1,0)\\
(3,1)&(0,1,0)\\
(4,2)&(0,0,1)\\
(5,2)&(0,1,0).
\end{array}                                                \tag{12}
\]

Both displayed row collections span \(\mathbb C^3\), so both global star
maps are injective.  On a pure color-\(c\) word, the only response edge
which can be completed by \(q^{[2]}\) is the fixed pair assigned to that
color.  Its response matrix is, respectively,

\[
 E_{00}-E_{01},\qquad E_{11}-E_{01},\qquad E_{22}-E_{01}. \tag{13}
\]

The pure hafnian of \(q\) is one, so adding \(d=E_{01}\) gives \(E_{cc}\).
Thus all \(27\) pure row coefficients are correct.

At a one-defect word, a response occupying an unbroken fixed pair leaves
the broken pair for \(q\).  A response on the broken pair vanishes
termwise except on pair \(23\) between colors \(0\) and \(1\).  There the
two orientations cancel by the literal outer-product identity

\[
 (-1,1,0)(0,1,0)^{\mathsf T}
 +(1,-1,0)(0,1,0)^{\mathsf T}=0.
\]

The direct hafnian also vanishes because one fixed pair is broken.  Hence
all \(36\cdot9=324\) Hamming-one row coefficients are correct.

Select \((a,b,c,x)=(0,1,0,0)\).  Then \(d_{01}=1\) and

\[
 \beta_0(e_0^*)
 =-z_1^0+z_2^1+z_3^0+z_3^1+z_5^2,                       \tag{14}
\]

whereas

\[
 \operatorname{im}Q_0
 =\operatorname{span}\{z_1^0,z_1^1,z_1^2\}.               \tag{15}
\]

The cokernel class has the pure-\(0\) term \(z_3^0\) and is nonzero even
after projecting every off-\(x\) site to the tagged pure color.  At the
other selected endpoint,

\[
 \beta_1(e_0^*)=-z_0^0+z_2^1-z_3^0+z_4^2,
\]

which is nonzero modulo
\(\operatorname{im}Q_1=\operatorname{span}\{z_0^0,z_0^1,z_0^2\}\).
In the natural common one-form ambient quotient, the private coordinates
\(z_3^1\) in the first class and \(z_4^2\) in the second show that no
nontrivial scalar-weighted sum of the two classes vanishes.

This packet is not all-word exact.  Its first failure is Hamming two:

\[
\begin{aligned}
 [000011]\bigl(\text{\(00\)-row residual}\bigr)&=1,\\
 [000022]\bigl(\text{selected \(01\)-row residual}\bigr)&=-1.
\end{aligned}                                             \tag{15a}
\]

Thus the next minimal detector for this packet is already the Hamming-two
selected/off-diagonal layer; one need not wait for the pair-uniform
Hamming-four word \(001122\).

## 5. Cross-check: the dense seven-row all-word guard

Proposition 5.1 of
[the K6 source-provenance guard](k6-lefschetz-source-provenance-guard.md)
gives the literal all-word matrix identity

\[
 d q^{[3]}+ps^{\mathsf T}q^{[2]}=E_{00}X_0.                \tag{16}
\]

Thus all six off-diagonal rows and the \(00\) diagonal row hold on every
one of the \(3^6\) words.  The packet has separated Hall selector
matrices, both with nonzero determinant.  Its only missing declarations
are the \(11\) and \(22\) diagonal targets.

Use the selected cell \((a,b)=(1,0)\), whose direct scalar is
\(d_{10}=-1/10\ne0\), and tag physical color \(c=0\) at \(x=0\).
The displayed star formulas in that note give

\[
\begin{aligned}
 p_1&=z_0^1-\tfrac12z_0^0-\tfrac16z_1^0-\tfrac12z_2^0,\\
 s_0&=-z_0^0+\tfrac13z_1^0+z_5^0.
\end{aligned}                                             \tag{17}
\]

Therefore

\[
 \beta_0(e_0^*)=\tfrac12(z_2^0-z_5^0).                    \tag{18}
\]

The internal quadratic is the complete rank-one graph on the physical
word \(110000\).  Hence

\[
 \operatorname{im}Q_0
 =\mathbb C(z_1^1+z_2^0+z_3^0+z_4^0+z_5^0),              \tag{19}
\]

and (18) is not in that line.  Consequently

\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{gathered}
 \text{six complete off-diagonal rows}
 +X_0+\text{separated selectors}\\
 \not\Longrightarrow [\beta_x(c)]=0.
 \end{gathered}}                                          \tag{20}
\]

Unlike Section 4, equation (16) consists of actual all-word equations for
all seven supplied rows.  It is still not a full-nine source: the omitted
rows are exactly \(0=X_1\) and \(0=X_2\).

In the active scalar subalgebra, the failure of cancellation is immediate.
On the five sites off \(0\), \(q_0\) is the complete unit graph.
Multiplication by \(q_0^{[2]}\) sends every coordinate one-form to the
same top coefficient.  Differences such as \(z_2^0-z_5^0\) lie in the
kernel of (8), while the physical incidence image is the sum line.

## 6. What an adjacent chart identifies

Use exposed sites \(p,q,r\), put
\(D={\cal V}\setminus\{p,q,r\}\), and write

\[
 P=A_{pq},\qquad R=A_{pr},\qquad T=A_{qr}.                 \tag{21}
\]

On \(D\), let \(x_i,y_j,t_k\) be the star rows from \(p,q,r\).
In the \(pq\)-chart, at its residual cross site \(r\), direct expansion
of the selected response gives

\[
 \beta^{pq}_{r}(k)=R_{ik}y_j+T_{jk}x_i,                    \tag{22}
\]

and the corresponding physical \(q\)-incidence form is \(t_k\).  In the
\(pr\)-chart, at residual cross site \(q\),

\[
 \beta^{pr}_{q}(j)=P_{ij}t_k+T_{jk}x_i,                    \tag{23}
\]

and its incidence form is \(y_j\).  Therefore literal shared-source
compatibility implies

\[
 \boxed{
 [\beta^{pq}_{r}(k)]=[\beta^{pr}_{q}(j)]=T_{jk}[x_i]
 \quad\text{in }L_D/(\mathbb Ct_k+\mathbb Cy_j).}          \tag{24}
\]

The same formula can be stated using the complete incidence images in the
denominator.  It is the degree-one form of the adjacent normal identity

\[
 L_{pq;r}-L_{pr;q}
 =-(h-1)(P_{ij}t_k-R_{ik}y_j),                             \tag{25}
\]

whose \(h=3\) normalization is the \(-2\delta\) row in the adjacent-cycle
ledger.

Thus the overlap kills the difference of the two classes.  The common
mode \(T_{jk}[x_i]\) is a torsor parameter.  After imposing the two
all-word normal rows, it lies in the intersection of their two colon
kernels.  The exact two-chart question is whether the remaining diagonal
anchors make that intersection zero.

The direct-sum/source-grade packet in
[the adjacent h=3 cycle note](adjacent_full_nine_h3_cycle_transgression.md)
shows that the known Bianchi and Euler rows retain precisely this common
sum channel.  Since that packet is not a complete all-label source, it
guards deletion of the common mode but does not decide the full-nine
intersection theorem.

## 7. Six-witness averaging does not supply the lift

The symbols \(\beta_x\) in
[the six-set beta-overlap jet note](six-set-beta-overlap-jet.md) are
five-site annihilating functionals, not the response-incidence rows (3).
Their exact average produces the global functional \(4\Lambda_S\), but
the two-crossing sector survives with multiplicity four and becomes an
external Hessian term.  Four- and six-crossing jets retain the remaining
cokernel residue.

Thus that averaging theorem transports local witness classes into higher
sectors; it does not select an integrable zero lift.  It defines neither a
comparison map among the six quotients in (8) nor a selector-weighted
vanishing of (3).

## 8. Exact audit and remaining claim

The dependency-free
[eight-row checker](../computations/verify_tagged_incidence_cokernel_eight_row_guard.py)
enumerates all \(8\cdot729\) supplied coefficients, verifies that (G5) is
the sole ninth-row residual, checks \(d=E_{02}\), both good stars, all
literal Segre rectangles, both augmented incidence ranks, and exact
mutations of a cofactor edge, a dark endpoint, and each third-label star.

The separate
[Hamming-one checker](../computations/verify_tagged_incidence_cokernel_hamming_one_boundary.py)
enumerates all \(27+324\) admitted coefficients, checks the first
Hamming-two residuals (15a), both star ranks, the two augmented incidence
ranks, the weighted-sum private coordinates, and exact direct/cancellation
mutations.

Both new checkers pass normally, with optimization enabled, and under
isolated no-site mode.  The existing
[all-word checker](../computations/verify_k6_one_anchor_lefschetz_guard.py)
independently enumerates all \(9\cdot3^6\) coefficients of (16).

The unproved physical statement is now precise:

\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{gathered}
 \text{In a full-nine source, does the ninth row absent from (G4) force}\\
 \ker m_{q_0^{[2]}}\cap
 \{[\beta_x(\lambda)]:\lambda\in V_x^*\}=0
 \quad\text{for every }x,\\
 \text{or force the common two-chart torsor class (24) to vanish?}
 \end{gathered}}
\]

Even a positive answer must still be upgraded from local factorization to
a compatible global site derivation and must control its second variation.
No admitted pure, Hamming-one, seven-row, eight-row, Bianchi, Euler, or
six-witness averaging identity currently supplies those additional steps.
