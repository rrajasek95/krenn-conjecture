# The order-six GammaJet collision contraction: strict same-edge gate

The canonical shared-site contraction does **not** yet define a physical
response-to-cap map on the complete order-six target.  Its coefficient shadow
is exact on the 148 shared-one-site coordinates and the normalized full-star
average is exact on all 90 parent matchings, but the literal 159-coordinate
target has eleven additional same-edge directions.  Those directions are
canonically null only one stage higher, in the labelled `D3` source
resolution.  No current physical constructor carries that `D3` homotopy, or
the residual DQ/PS Beck--Chevalley face, into the AugP2/K_Eq cap packet.

The executable certificate is
`computations/verify_h3_order6_gammajet_collision_contraction_same_edge_gate.py`.
It pins the current 8,580-column order-six constructor and all source packets
used below.

## 1. Literal inventory and rank theorem

After retaining the source columns and then projecting only the pair row, the
site-repeating target has 159 coarsened coordinates.  Before coarsening there
are 271 `(pair, word/fine shift)` occurrences: all 159 occur in word
`11111111`, and 112 also occur in word `11211211`.  The 159 coordinates split
canonically as

\[
159=148+7+4.
\]

The three summands are:

1. 148 pairs of decorated edges sharing exactly one site.  These have a
   canonical missing/doubled `P3` spine and are the domain of the proposed
   `P3+K2` collision contraction;
2. seven identical decorated-edge pairs, whose correct integral target is
   `gamma_2(x_e)`;
3. four colour-distinct pairs supported on the same uncoloured edge, whose
   target is `gamma_1(x_e^a) gamma_1(x_e^b)`.

The first omitted row in the pinned ordering is
`(2, ((0,7,1,1),(0,7,1,1)))`, abbreviated `(07:11)^2`.  It is hit by 345
literal columns and occurs in both source words, so it is not an unused or
accidentally adjoined coordinate.

Let `S=D2 ker(D0,D1)` for the complete 8,580-column block.  Exact modular
elimination at both pinned primes gives

\[
\operatorname{rank}(S\to\mathbf F^{159})=153,
\qquad
153=142+7+4.
\]

Here 142 is the rank on the 148 shared-site rows, while the two same-edge
summands have ranks seven and four.  Therefore forgetting the same-edge target
leaves an eleven-dimensional kernel on the constrained image.  This is a
strict chain-map failure before any putative `e_C A e_R` operation landing is
defined.

## 2. The honest strict repair and the derived qualification

The smallest coefficient-level strict repair is exactly eleven rows: seven
divided squares and four mixed same-edge products.  The existing order-six
constructor already has the right divided-Hasse normalization.  If a
direction occurs with multiplicity `m`, its diagonal `D2` coefficient is
`C(m,2)` because unordered direction positions are chosen.  The mixed
coefficient is the product of the two direction multiplicities.  Thus the
repair restores rank 153 without a factor-of-two adjustment.

This does **not** imply that eleven new cap objects are homologically
mandatory.  On the augmented 5-simplex of six labelled derivative slots, the
face dimensions and boundary ranks are

\[
(\dim L_1,\dim L_2,\dim L_3)=(6,15,20),\qquad
(\operatorname{rank}d_1,\operatorname{rank}d_2,
\operatorname{rank}d_3)=(1,5,10).
\]

The pair-cycle dimension is ten and equals `rank(d3)`, so `H1` at the pair
layer is zero.  Passing to repeated-slot coinvariants is exact over
`Q` by Reynolds averaging, with `gamma_2` as the diagonal basis.  Hence the
eleven same-edge `D0/D1` cycles are `D3`-exact in the canonical labelled
source resolution.  A **derived** comparison may send them to zero, but only
if it includes the corresponding labelled `D3` homotopy.  The constructed
full-star family currently contains the site-0 collision triangles, not the
complete `D3 -> AugP2` comparison.  Dropping the eleven rows without either
that homotopy or the strict divided-power target is invalid.

Even a complete source-side `D3` nullhomotopy is not sufficient unless its
image has the correct absolute Eq component.  The normalized base change
`t=H0-u=0` makes the evident coefficient top map a chain map, but its cone has
homology `(H0,H1,H2)=(1,0,0)`, generated by the surviving absolute `e_Eq`
row.  A relative cell `dK=t e_Eq` becomes an `H1` cycle after that base change,
with homology `(1,1)`; only an absolute decorated preimage `dK=e_Eq` makes the
two-term cap packet acyclic.  Thus the physical `D3` comparison must either
produce that absolute Eq preimage or prove that Eq is contractible in the full
enriched target.

Topologically the strict repair also cannot be silently identified with the
present cap.  Every selected AugP2 occurrence is `P3+K2`, with three distinct
uncoloured edges and site-degree profile `(2,1,1,1,1)`.  A same-edge divided
square has profile `(2,2)`, or `(2,2,1,1)` after adjoining a disjoint remote
`K2`.  It is not a `P3+K2` occurrence.

## 3. What the full star does prove

For one root, the 21 unordered star pairs have output rank seven.  Every one

the `1/6` full-star average is exactly the complete parent augmentation.  The

\[

\]

and the 1,020 deleted-factor squares admit presentation-safe relative

All 24 branches are genuine `P3` branches and none supplies a same-edge row.

Thus the full-star theorem is an exact theorem in the common coefficient base

## 4. DQ/PS as a restriction--insertion square

The groupoid-cardinality shadow behaves exactly as hoped.  For one pair, the
two ordered DQ/PS branches and the two root sections form a `K_{2,2}` packet.
Its four edge boundaries have rank three and their unique relation is

\[
(1,-1,1,-1).
\]

After forgetting operation labels, the 21-pair star is complete and its `1/6`
average gives the identity on the parent augmentation.  This is an `H0` or
groupoid-cardinality statement, not by itself a homotopy-Cartesian theorem.  In
the literal source operation category the unique `K_{2,2}` cycle has no
response-to-cap Beck--Chevalley filler.  The current restriction, reinsertion,
and objectwise K_Eq constructors provide the edges, not this mixed face.
Consequently the coefficient incidence test passes, while the physical square
is **not proved homotopy Cartesian**.  Its excess class is the already isolated
`(H0-u)e_Eq` face.

This also rules out the apparent K_Eq bypass.  Applying `I_i D_j` first returns
the original parent matching in the response copy of the common base.  The
existing K_Eq action is objectwise, so applying it there stays in
`e_R A e_R`; it does not produce a component in `e_C A e_R`, a tied cap
`B=Eq` landing, or cap target normalization.  Declaring the output to be cap
is precisely the missing mixed Beck--Chevalley/K_Eq face under another name.

The first literal mismatch is visible before any coarse B/Eq test: the
canonical response face has word `11110000`, while cap `r0` has word
`01211222`; six sites and all six selected fine degrees change, and the cap
word is outside the existing response D4 cube.  The packaging quotient has
rank two from the collision and objectwise K_Eq edges, rank three only after
the mixed face, and rank four after the labelled ridge face.

## 5. Protected rows and exact remaining constructor

\[

## 6. Intrinsic versus presentation-level obstruction

The eleven same-edge rows are intrinsic to this strict `D2` presentation, but
their obstruction is not intrinsic to the original polynomial solution
problem: they are `D3`-exact before mapping to cap.  Likewise
`Hom(e_R,e_C)=0`, the missing `K_{2,2}` face, and orthogonal word/operation
idempotents say that the selected enriched category does not yet contain the
desired comparison; they do not by themselves say anything negative about
the existence of a polynomial solution.

The reduced Eq discrepancy becomes intrinsic only if its detector is defined
directly on actual EqSystem coefficients.  Therefore a weaker theorem than a
literal physical comparison would suffice for the terminal proof: construct
the Gamma observable `Psi=delta.(B-Eq)` in the actual EqSystem/Macaulay (or
cotangent) quotient and prove that every exact solution satisfies `Psi=0`.
The required terminal right-hand side has nonzero `Psi`, so this scalar
solution-level Fredholm identity would close the obstruction without adding a
response-to-cap source generator or a full chain map.  Until that descent of
`Psi` is proved, the current result is a sharp gate on the enriched proof
route, not an intrinsic no-solution theorem.
