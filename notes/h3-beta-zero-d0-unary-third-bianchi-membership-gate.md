# The beta-zero selected `D0` branch is one protected membership

## Outcome

In the intrinsic `beta=0` trace-Cartan branch, the collapsed rows see only
the complementary root direction `D2`; the missing selected direction is
the one-dimensional quotient generated by `[D0]`.  The genuine unary row,
its full-nine incidence theorem, the third-cofactor hierarchy, and the
third collision-Bianchi coefficient do **not** yet construct a physical
cell mapping to `[D0]` in the required word and adjacent Rees grade.

They reduce the branch to one exact map.  Let `Z` be the module of
selected order-three source chains killed by

* the lower, unwanted-word, ordinary-residue, and anchor-correction
  readouts;
* source descent;
* the endpoint-ridge readout; and
* source-word mismatch.

Let

\[
                \theta:Z\longrightarrow Q_0=S[D_0]             \tag{1}
\]

be the remaining selected-root target coefficient.  The beta-zero branch
closes exactly when

\[
                         1\in\theta(Z).                          \tag{2}
\]

Over a field, failure of (2) is exactly the normalized dual `[D0]^*` that
kills every protected physical chain and reads one on the desired cell.
Over a general coefficient ring the primary object is the image ideal
`theta(Z)`; a proper image produces the dual after reduction modulo a
maximal ideal containing it.

## Unary incidence is not the selected target nullhomotopy

The genuine unary equation

\[
                         q^{[3]}=X_0                              \tag{3}
\]

and the full-nine four-cover imply that selected colour zero is incident at
all six residual sites, with at least two sites full in all three target
axes.  This is a statement in the `q`-star ideal.  It neither selects one
matching occurrence nor gives a source chain in the mixed `D0` target word
and exact adjacent Rees grade.  Thus existence of the order-three
principal part is strictly weaker than the same-grade target nullhomotopy
required by (2).

At `beta=0`, the literal diagonal rows satisfy

\[
              J_2=2J_1,\qquad J_*=0,                            \tag{4}
\]

and have typed root coordinates `(0,*)` in the `(D0,D2)` basis.  The
desired two-sided defect is `D0+D2`; after the known `D2` row is quotiented,
the target of (1) is therefore exactly rank one, not a coarse unidentified
trace remainder.

## The product-rule top is formal, not source-valid

For a selected unary matching `M={e1,e2,e3}`, the genuine third cofactor is

\[
 \partial_{e_1}\partial_{e_2}\partial_{e_3}
       (q_{e_1}q_{e_2}q_{e_3})=J_M=1.                            \tag{5}
\]

The squarefree order-three product rule has face census `1,3,3,1`.  In the
complete Hasse prolongation its proper-face tail can be combined with the
exceptional target-normalized unary lift.  In row order

\[
        (\operatorname{lower},\operatorname{ainc},W,
          \operatorname{target},\operatorname{ores}),
\]

the two exact coarse rows are

\[
 C_{\rm tail}=(-1,-1,0,0,0),\qquad
 x=(1,-1,0,0,0),                                      \tag{6}
\]

so

\[
             \frac12(C_{\rm tail}+x)=(0,-1,0,0,0).    \tag{7}
\]

Thus the cross-term really does cancel target and ordinary residue while
retaining the required physical anchor incidence.  The obstruction is not
one of those three readouts.  The selected connecting functional is one on
the formal tail and zero on `x`, hence it is `1/2` on (7).  Equivalently,
the same full-row unit that creates the formal top sends the underived
source equation `H_m=0` to `1`.  Therefore (7) does not descend to the
physical source quotient and supplies no selected mixed-`D0` upper label.

This also explains why the polynomial identity

\[
              3(H-u)-\sum_eq_eH_e+3uJ_M=0             \tag{8}
\]

does not settle the branch.  It identifies the first algebraically capable
coefficient, but interpreting `uJ_M` as a boundary would declare the
missing physical lower face.

## The full-row and endpoint obstructions

For a direct-free mixed source word, (5) is literally the fourfold
full-row coefficient

\[
          J_M=\partial_{a_{pq}^{22},a_{xv}^{0m_v},N}H_m=1.       \tag{9}
\]

The complete formal Hasse totalization is square-zero and has protected
tail signature

\[
       (\operatorname{ainc},W,\operatorname{target},
         \operatorname{ores})=(-1,0,0,0).                        \tag{10}
\]

Physical comparison with the zero-endpoint Bianchi cube nevertheless
leaves the primitive ridge

\[
 \Omega_v=(a_{pq}^{22}-a_{pq}^{00})
          -(a_{xv}^{0m_v}-a_{xv}^{00}).                          \tag{11}
\]

Across the five deleted sites the `Omega_v` span rank five inside the
rank-six endpoint-ridge space, and no selected midpoint source word is hit.
A single adjacent comparison edge transfers (11) rather than killing it.
These are literal source-labelled failures, not missing signs in the
formal cube.

## Why Dold--Kan does not supply the cell

In normalized chains on the standard four-simplex, all 125 degenerate top
strings vanish and have zero normalized boundary.  Declaring `J_M` to be a
degeneracy therefore also kills the proposed `uJ_M` attachment.  The unique
nondegenerate four-simplex survives, but has all five nondegenerate proper
faces.  In the physical labelled cube those faces include (11) and the
source-descent unit.

Dold--Kan normalization is an equivalence for an already constructed
simplicial source module.  It cannot adjoin a new physical syzygy or turn an
operator that does not preserve the source ideal into one.  Hence the
third cofactor is a formal Spencer/Hasse top until a genuine source-labelled
lower cell is exhibited.

## The third-Bianchi coefficient is an occurrence carrier

The dormant collision cell `W_02` is invisible through orders zero, one,
and two for all `3^6=729` colourings.  On the marked matching

\[
                         02\mid13\mid45                         \tag{12}
\]

it first appears at order three with coefficient one.  This proves an
evaluated occurrence coefficient and is the correct degree at which a
higher occurrence split can awaken.  It does not define the target map in
(1): identical unary incidence and third-Bianchi occurrence data are
compatible with target-lift rank zero or one.  The distinction is between
an evaluated occurrence covector and a physical source chain with a
specified `D0` target.

## Exact next theorem and relation to the active gates

A positive result now needs one source-valid endpoint-word-changing
Cartan/Spencer or higher occurrence-splitting cell that lands in the exact
selected final word/order-three grade, kills both the descent unit and
`Omega_v`, and maps by a unit to `[D0]`.  Producing that cell proves (2);
proving its absence produces the dual branch of (1).

This has the same architecture as the remaining Gate-I/Gate-II typing
