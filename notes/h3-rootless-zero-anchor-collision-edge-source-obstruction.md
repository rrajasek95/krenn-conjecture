# The zero-anchor collision edges fail first at a reduced pure-Eq face

## Outcome

The repeated-site \(P_3\sqcup K_2\) edge \(E_v\) is not merely an abstract
support type. The committed denominator-marked principal-parts squares give
its exact response-cancelling ridge boundary. For adjacent faces \(v,w\),
the strict two-chart symbol has

\[
 dE_v^{\mathrm{strict}}=-r_v+r_w,\qquad
 (\widehat w,\operatorname{tgt},\operatorname{ores},
   \operatorname{ainc})=(0,0,0,0).                    \tag{1}
\]

Thus the common-\(q\) S-pair and its signs are already correct. The failure
occurs when the strict symbol is descended to the physical source. The
literal order-four PP/Bianchi cube has

\[
 dE_v^{\mathrm{phys}}
   =-r_v+r_w+\delta_v(H_0-u)e_{\mathrm{Eq}},            \tag{2}
\]

where, in cyclic order,

\[
 (\delta_0,\ldots,\delta_4)
    =(a-b,c-d,e-a,b-c,d-e).                            \tag{3}
\]

No correction in the complete bounded source inventory cancels the last
term in (2) while keeping physical anchor incidence, \(W\), target, and
ordinary residue zero. In the decisive quotient the primitive dual

\[
              \boxed{\text{pure Eq}+\operatorname{ainc}}             \tag{4}
\]

kills every admitted correction and reads \(-\delta_v\) on the required
reduced Eq face.

The first missing source cell is therefore exact:

\[
 dC_v=-\delta_v(H_0-u)e_{\mathrm{Eq}},\qquad
 (\operatorname{ainc},\widehat w,\operatorname{tgt},
   \operatorname{ores})(C_v)=(0,0,0,0).               \tag{5}
\]

Then \(E_v=E_v^{\mathrm{phys}}+C_v\) has (1). The cell \(C_v\) is a
zero-anchor descent correction, not the independent primitive anchor cell
required later by the augmented pentagon.

The five corrections (5) have no new degree-five sign obstruction: their
Tate-weighted boundaries sum to zero identically. Hence (5) is the earliest
physical construction gate.

## 1. Exact bounded correction module

Project to the selected pure Eq label and retain physical anchor incidence,
\(W\), target, and ordinary residue. In the row order

\[
       (\text{pure Eq},\operatorname{ainc},W,
          \operatorname{tgt},\operatorname{ores}),
\]

\[
\begin{array}{c|rrrrr}

   \operatorname{tgt}&\operatorname{ores}\\ \hline
 r_0&1&-1&0&1&0\\
 T&0&0&-1&1&0\\
 \rho&0&0&1&0&1.
\end{array}                                             \tag{6}
\]

The normalization \(Y=1\) in (6) is harmless; any nonzero \(Y\) gives the
same separator. The desired reduced face has vector

\[
                         (-1,0,0,0,0).                 \tag{7}
\]

The covector (4) kills all three columns in (6) and reads \(-1\) on (7).
The exact rank rises from three to four, with a unimodular four-row minor.
Thus the obstruction is primitive over the integers and survives scalar
extension or the allowed monomial localizations.

The complete polynomial source inventory does not add a hidden pure-Eq
column:

* in each cubic repeated-site degree the 288 literal full-nine multiplier
  columns have injective physical boundary;

  differences, with all readouts zero;

Mixed-row corrections occupy different labelled Eq coordinates and cannot
cancel the selected \((H_0-u)e_{\mathrm{Eq}}\) term coefficientwise.

to a hand-picked three-column ansatz.

Two nearby derived faces do not change the conclusion. The completed

## 2. The PP edge and the source-validity defect

On the five-cycle specialization,

\[
 (h_1,h_3,h_5,h_2,h_4)=(bd,ad,ac,ce,be).
\]

For the first edge, multiplication gives

\[
                      a h_1-b h_3=0.                  \tag{8}
\]

This is the correct companion cancellation in the repeated degree \(abd\),
and it produces the ridge boundary \(-r_1+r_3\). The strict chart
PP/Bianchi square has no remaining coarse readout.

Physical diagonal descent of its two order-four cubes leaves

\[
                       (a-b)(H_0-u)e_{\mathrm{Eq}}.    \tag{9}
\]

Adding a multiple of \(r_0\) cancels (9), but (6) shows that it
simultaneously introduces anchor and target. The target cap and split
residue can redistribute target, \(W\), and ordinary residue; neither
changes the pure-Eq-plus-anchor conservation (4). A source-valid zero-anchor
edge therefore requires the genuinely reduced face (5).

The same calculation applies cyclically to all five edges. Each
\(\delta_v\) is a primitive nonzero polynomial. Its vanishing at
\(a=b=c=d=e=1\) does not give a universal source cell; it only says the
physical defect disappears on that proper specialization.

## 3. Degree-five compatibility is already correct

The natural Tate multipliers are

\[
                      ce,\ be,\ bd,\ ad,\ ac.
\]

They obey the literal identity

\[
\begin{split}
 &ce(a-b)+be(c-d)+bd(e-a)\\
 &\qquad{}+ad(b-c)+ac(d-e)=0.                          \tag{10}
\end{split}
\]

Thus, if the five reduced faces \(C_v\) in (5) were constructed, their
weighted boundary would already satisfy the degree-five \(d^2=0\)
condition. The degree-five Tate top introduces no earlier obstruction and
cannot repair an individual \(\delta_v\) face: a chain complex requires
\(d^2E_v=0\) for each edge before the top cell is attached.

This separates the two missing physical types cleanly:

1. the zero-anchor repeated-site edge needs the reduced pure-Eq correction
   (5);
2. the five edges then use the already correct degree-five compatibility;
3. the primitive vertex anchor with incidence \(-1\) remains a separate
   augmented-pentagon cell.
