# Centered occurrence shear and the relative Tate completion

## Result

There is a positive formal construction after the single relative
comparison cell is admitted.  If

\[
 d\epsilon=L,
 \qquad L=z^Tu,
 \qquad \mathbf1^Tz=0,
\]

then the centered occurrence shear extends recursively over a full
Koszul--Tate/cofibrant resolution of the physical occurrence monomial
algebra.  There is no later abstract obstruction, and the same argument is
valid over `k[beta]`.

This is not yet a finite physical source theorem.  The extension resolves
the relative quotient `A/(L)`, not the original physical algebra `A`, and
the minimal commutative Tate resolution is infinite.  The first concrete
failure of a quadratic-only construction is already large: there are 5,160

## 1. The recursive relative lift

Let

\[
 S=k[u_M]_{M=1}^{90},
 \qquad A=S/I\subset k[p,s,q]
\]

be the physical occurrence semigroup algebra, and let `Q -> A` be a
semi-free Tate resolution.  The free occurrence shear is the derivation

\[
 \delta_0(u_M)=-L,
 \qquad L=z^Tu.
\]

For every polynomial relation `r in I`, the chain rule gives

\[
 \delta_0(r)
 =-L\,\partial_{\mathbf1}r,
 \qquad
 \partial_{\mathbf1}=\sum_M\frac{\partial}{\partial u_M}.       \tag{1}
\]

Adjoin one degree-one relative cell `epsilon` with `d epsilon=L`.  If
`e_r` is a degree-one Tate generator with `d e_r=r`, define

\[
 \delta(e_r)=-\epsilon\,\partial_{\mathbf1}r.                    \tag{2}
\]

Equations (1)--(2) give `[d,delta](e_r)=0`.  Centering is also essential:

\[
 \delta(L)=-L\sum_Mz_M=0,
\]

so one may take `delta(epsilon)=0`.

The algebra `A` is a subalgebra of the polynomial domain `k[p,s,q]`, hence
is a domain.  For nonzero `L`, multiplication by `L` is injective on `A`.
Consequently

\[
 H_i\bigl(Q\otimes K(L)\bigr)=0\quad(i>0),
 \qquad
 H_0\bigl(Q\otimes K(L)\bigr)=A/(L).                \tag{3}
\]

Now proceed through the Tate filtration.  At a generator `x` of homological
degree at least two, assume `delta` has been defined below `x` and commutes
with `d` there.  Then `delta(d x)` is a positive-degree cycle.  By (3) it
has a filler, which defines `delta(x)`.  This proves existence at every
stage.  The choices are noncanonical, but a homogeneous resolution gives
homogeneous fillers.

The identical proof works after base change to `k[beta]`: `A[beta]` is a
domain and `L` remains regular.  The formal Tate recursion introduces no
`beta` torsion and no extra Bockstein obstruction.

## 2. The extension is genuinely relative

Adjoining `d epsilon=L` kills the class of `L` in degree zero.  Therefore
the construction is a resolution of `A/(L)`, not an automorphism or a
cofibrant replacement of the unchanged physical algebra `A`.  This is the
derived form of the strict toric obstruction in `df3787b` and `05a62d0`.

For the first quadratic minor `r_2`, (1) reads

\[
 \delta_0(r_2)=-L(B-A)(x-y),
\]

and (2) is exactly the previously isolated face

\[
 d\bigl(-\epsilon(B-A)(x-y)\bigr)
 =-L(B-A)(x-y).
\]

Thus the conormal is not a second independent generator after a
multiplicative physical `epsilon` exists.  Conversely, a merely linear
occurrence-module section does not supply (2).

## 3. Exact size of the physical toric presentation

The exponent matrix of the ninety occurrence monomials uses 27 physical
variables and has rank 20.  Hence

\[
 \operatorname{height}I=90-20=70.
\]

In degree two there are 4,095 source monomials and 3,870 distinct physical
images.  Every nontrivial fibre has size two, giving exactly

\[
 \dim I_2=225
\]

minimal independent quadratic binomials.  Since `225>70`, the toric algebra
is not a complete intersection.

The next degree is already not generated by those quadrics.  In degree
three the exact counts are

```text
source monomials                         125580
distinct physical images                100520
dim I_3                                  25060
rank of S_1 I_2                          19900
new cubic generators I_3/(S_1 I_2)       5160
linear syzygies among the 225 quadrics     350
```

Every quadratic-disconnected cubic fibre has two isolated monomials.  The
lexicographically first is

\[
 r_3=u_0u_4u_{13}-u_1u_3u_{14},                     \tag{4}
\]

where

```text
0  = (0,1;23|45)        1  = (0,1;24|35)
3  = (0,2;13|45)        4  = (0,2;14|35)
13 = (0,5;13|24)        14 = (0,5;14|23).
```

The two sides of (4) are the same literal `p,s,q` monomial.  No sequence of
quadratic fibre moves connects them.  Its directional derivative is

\[
 k_3=u_4u_{13}+u_0u_{13}+u_0u_4
     -u_3u_{14}-u_1u_{14}-u_1u_3,
\]

so its shear defect is `-L k_3`.  The same relative cell fills it:

\[
 d(-\epsilon k_3)=-Lk_3.                             \tag{5}
\]

This illustrates the general recursion rather than adding a new primitive
comparison hypothesis.

## 4. Finite versus infinite conclusions

The non-complete-intersection witness implies that the standard minimal
commutative Tate/acyclic closure cannot terminate after finitely many Tate
variables.  A finite free resolution exists as an `S`-module, but it is not
the multiplicative cofibrant source object needed for the product-rule,
bar, and principal-parts construction.

Therefore:

- every fixed homological truncation is finite and the relative shear can
  be extended across it;
- the universal all-coherence construction is an infinite filtered Tate
  completion;
- the construction does not by itself produce finitely many literal
  physical source columns with target, anchor, `q`, cap/ridge, eta, and
  sigma values.

The highest-leverage remaining theorem is consequently still finite and
physical: construct a source-labelled, multiplicative AugP2/E14 image of
`epsilon` through exactly the coherence depth consumed by PAComp.  Formal
Tate theory then guarantees that no new algebraic obstruction appears at
later stages, but it cannot replace that augmented placement theorem.

## Scope

This is exact for the canonical `h=3` occurrence semigroup over a
characteristic-zero field and after flat base change to `k[beta]`.  It does
not construct the physical `epsilon`, choose equivariant fillers, bound the
coherence depth required by PAComp, or assign augmented physical readouts to
the infinite Tate generators.
