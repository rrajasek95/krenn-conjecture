# The literal `EqSystem`/`AugP2` presentation does not determine essential surjectivity

## Outcome

Starting from the official `EqSystemN 8 3` variables and relations, and then
adjoining **only** the response, cap/`AugP2`, fixed-window and bar constructors
that are presently callable in the repository, gives a finite source-derived
presentation.  It is not the previously declared `Gamma_*` grammar.

That literal presentation is operation-disconnected:

\[
 \operatorname{Hom}^0(\mathrm{response},\mathrm{cap})=0,
 \qquad
 \operatorname{Hom}^1(\mathrm{response},\mathrm{cap})=0.       \tag{1}
\]

In particular, it contains neither `Phi_KS,r0` nor any of the eight `kappa`
instances.  The standard mapping-cylinder API fails at its input edge with
`MissingPhysicalArrow`.  Thus there is not yet a comparison functor from the
natural `Phi` bicomplex to an actual source presentation: its first undefined
image is `Phi` itself.

Freely adjoining one normalized, source-labelled `Phi` family produces the
expected finite comparison.  Modulo canonical and `chi`-dark cells, its
off-diagonal degree-one quotient has the eight standard word-separated
generators

```text
0012  0102  0110  0111  0122  0212  1112  2112,
```

and all have tied boundary `(v_i,v_i)` in `(B,Eq)`, hence normalized
`Psi`-charge zero.  This is an exact theorem for the **freely adjoined
completion**.  It is not a derivation of `Phi` from `EqSystem`.

The input data admit a second completion with the same literal restriction
and one additional bright off-diagonal atom.  Therefore they do not logically
determine essential surjectivity, and the current local covector cannot yet be
promoted to the accepted Macaulay/Fredholm terminal.

## 1. What the official source actually presents

The official system has

\[
 {8\choose2}3^2=252
\]

canonically oriented weight variables and one relation for each of the
`3^8=6561` colour words.  Each relation is a degree-four sum of the `105`
perfect-matching monomials, with target one on the three pure words and zero
on the other `6558` words.  Its canonical first Tate stage is

\[
 P[e_\iota],\qquad |e_\iota|=1,\qquad
 d e_\iota=\operatorname{pmSum}(\iota)-\operatorname{target}(\iota). \tag{2}
\]

This source records the eight-site word and polynomial degree.  It does **not**
record any of the following:

- a response/cap operation parent;
- a fine `t*q_(v,N)` occurrence;
- the repeated grade `P3+K2`;
- a selected window/occurrence idempotent; or
- an `AugP2` face type.

Thus `EqSystem` is a polynomial predicate, not an enriched operation category.
There is no source-defined slot called
`Hom(response,cap)` in (2).  Choosing a free resolution of (2) can exhaust its
coefficient/Macaulay syzygies, but cannot decide the contents of an undefined
off-diagonal Hom space.

## 2. The smallest presentation generated by current constructors

The executable constructor audit calls every implemented API rather than
reading a family registry.  It produces `128` primitives:

| operation object | primitive count |
|---|---:|
| response | 1 |
| cap | 26 |
| fixed window | 100 |
| covariance bar | 1 |

All are objectwise loops.  The free composition graph therefore has only the
four diagonal edges.  Inductively, every path is again diagonal, proving (1)
for the generated closure.

At the exact cap packet, the callable image has rank `23/27`, its `B/Eq`
projection has rank `7/8`, and `Psi` kills all `128` entries.  There are `25`
literal `Gamma_*` cap entries and zero literal `kappa` entries.

The obvious Macaulay shortcut is already included.  Multiplication of the
selected `b01` coefficient by cap `r0` has Leibniz boundary

\[
 d(b_{01}r_0)=db_{01}\,r_0+b_{01}E.                 \tag{3}
\]

But both factors in (3) carry the cap parent.  It is a `cap -> cap` operation,
not the response-selected `db01`, and its projection to the mixed
response-to-`AugP2` grade is zero.  Macaulay multiplication does not create
the missing off-diagonal matrix unit.

The result is sharp but deliberately limited:

> The smallest presentation generated by the literal data presently in the
> repository has no off-diagonal primitive.  It is too small to state, let
> alone prove, the desired physical essential-surjectivity theorem.

This differs from proving that a full physical source has no off-diagonal
primitive.  No full enriched source presentation has yet been defined.

## 3. The canonical `Phi` completion

Now adjoin, as new data, a normalized family

\[
 \Phi_{KS,r0}:\mathrm{response}\longrightarrow\mathrm{cap}.    \tag{4}
\]

The unique normalized ungraded chain-map shape sends
`epsilon_s -> r0` and `c_f -> -E`.  In the free multiplicative/cellular
completion of (4), the two binary features are word change and `K_Eq`:

| word | `Eq` | normal form |
|---:|---:|---|
| 0 | 0 | canonical/local |
| 1 | 0 | dark cross-word cylinder |
| 0 | 1 | dark objectwise `K_Eq` |
| 1 | 1 | standard `kappa` interchange |

There are eight one-root word idempotents, so the last line gives eight
independent quotient classes.  This is a finite comparison functor: by the
four-feature normal form, the natural `Phi` bicomplex is identified with the
freely generated off-diagonal subcategory of the completion.

Every strict product has

\[
 \Pi_{B/Eq}(d\kappa_i)=(v_i,v_i),
 \qquad
 \Psi(d\kappa_i)=0.                                  \tag{5}
\]

Hence there is no ninth class **inside this chosen completion**.  Equation
(5) does not establish that the chosen completion is the actual physical
source: the word/fine/repeated/operation-labelled construction of (4) and its
proper target, `q`, anchor, residue, `W`, ridge, eta and sigma faces remains
open.

## 4. Exact two-completion counterguard

Let `P_Phi` denote the completion above.  Its relative quotient has rank
eight.  Form another enriched completion by adjoining a primitive

\[
 \omega_{0102}:\mathrm{response}\longrightarrow\mathrm{cap},
 \qquad
 \Pi_{B/Eq}(d\omega)=(\delta,0),
 \qquad \delta=(1,1,-1,-1),                         \tag{6}
\]

with zero official-`EqSystem`, callable-constructor, target, `q`, anchor,
ordinary-residue, `W` and ridge shadow.  Then

\[
 \operatorname{rank} Q(P_\Phi)=8,
 \qquad
 \operatorname{rank} Q(P_\Phi+\omega)=9,
 \qquad
 \chi(d\omega)=\delta\cdot(B-Eq)=4.                \tag{7}
\]

The two completions restrict identically to all `252` official variables, all
`6561` official relations and all `128` callable constructor outputs and
differentials.  Equations (6)--(7) therefore prove that those data alone do
not imply essential surjectivity onto the eight `kappa` classes.

This is an underdetermination counterguard, not a physical counterexample.
The atom `omega` is **not** asserted to arise from a decorated GHZ source.
Conversely, the audit found no actual source-provenant bright exotic among
the current constructors.

## 5. Relation to the Macaulay/Fredholm terminal

The rank-`7/8` `B/Eq` block and its normalized covector are not by themselves
an accepted terminal.  The accepted finite certificate requires one map

\[
 J_{\rm phys,\Gamma_*}:C_{\rm phys,\Gamma_*}
       \longrightarrow Y_{\rm phys,\Gamma_*}         \tag{8}
\]

which is exhaustive for physical off-diagonal primitives, retains every
target, `W`, residue, `M`, anchor, `q`, ridge, eta and sigma row, and contains
the literal physical candidate `b` in the same codomain.  Only then may one
prove

\[
 \Psi J_{\rm phys,\Gamma_*}=0,
 \qquad \Psi(b)=1,                                   \tag{9}
\]

and invoke the existing kernel-generator/Fredholm alternative.

For `P_Phi`, (5) supplies the only currently missing projected calculation.
If a source theorem identifies the full map (8) with that completion and
identifies its literal right-hand side, then (9) gives the accepted terminal.
For `P_Phi+omega`, (6) raises the projected rank from seven to eight and fills
the local class, so `Psi` does not descend.

Thus the terminal lane has not ended at a local cokernel.  It has been reduced
to the precise source datum on which terminal promotion depends.

## Sharp remaining work package

One theorem must supply all of the following as a single enriched construction:

1. a literal word/fine/repeated/operation-labelled `Phi_KS,r0`, including its
   first `db01` and central `Eq` faces;
2. a minimality/essential-surjectivity proof that every physical
   `Hom^1_Gamma*(response,cap)` primitive is canonical, `chi`-dark, or one of
   the eight `kappa` interchanges; and
3. the fully augmented comparison with the literal physical right-hand side
   needed in (8)--(9).

A derivation of (1) from the current APIs cannot replace this theorem: it says
the current presentation is disconnected, not that the physical completion is
uniquely the dark `Phi` completion.
