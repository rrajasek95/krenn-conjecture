# The nine Segre pair units are not a hereditary graph certificate

## Outcome

The exact two-cell units of `6f251bb` do not yield a vertex-cover theorem
for simultaneous support on the seventeen active Segre deformation
coordinates.  There are two independent obstructions.

1. The primary critical graph contains a unique four-cycle whose quadratic
   source terms admit a nonzero rational cancellation.
2. A pinned exact source lift for one unit edge acquires a nonzero residue
   after an adjacent active coordinate is adjoined.

Thus “the support contains a unit edge” is not hereditary.  The generators
themselves change when more physical cells are turned on.  A valid global
proof needs transition maps between source certificates, not only the graph
of nonzero second derivatives.

## 1. Exact graph structure

The seventeen vertices are the sixteen first-order coordinates from
`e180f0c` plus the hidden quadratic coordinate `12:02`.  The nine critical
edges split into

* a six-vertex, six-edge component with one cycle;
* the star (K_{1,3}) centred at `15:01`; and
* seven isolated vertices.

The unique cycle is

```text
03:10 -- 12:02 -- 05:10 -- 14:02 -- 03:10.
```

The remaining two edges in that component are the leaves

```text
02:10 -- 14:02,
04:10 -- 12:02.
```

This census is already a warning: seven one-cell units do not appear in the
primary quadratic graph at all, so an edge-cover argument cannot even see
their certificate transitions.

## 2. A coefficient-consistent critical cycle

Write

\[
 x=q_{03}(1,0),\quad z=q_{05}(1,0),\quad
 u=q_{12}(0,2),\quad v=q_{14}(0,2).
\]

Set all 45 diagonal variables to one and all other deformation coordinates
to zero.  The full six-row functional of `43cac9a` restricts to

\[
 \boxed{
       \Phi_{C_4}=4x+3v+4xv+4zv-2xu-2zu.
 }                                                       \tag{1}

At the nonzero rational torus point

\[
                         (x,z,v,u)=(1,1,1,15/4),        \tag{2}

equation (1) vanishes.  Hence the four edge transgressions do not define a
positive or odd-holonomy obstruction around the cycle.  Their coefficients
can cancel while every cycle coordinate remains nonzero.

This does not satisfy all top rows and is not a one-bad packet.  It is the
sharp counterguard to a theorem using only the primary critical graph.

## 3. Exact non-heredity of a pinned source lift

Take the exact unit chart

```text
03:10 + 12:02.
```

The pinned `liftstd` certificate of `6f251bb` is a literal source-row
combination equal to (2).  Now adjoin the adjacent cycle vertex

```text
14:02.
```

Re-evaluate the **same source rows and the same multipliers** in the enlarged
physical quadratic.  Exact reconstruction gives

\[
                         2+R,                           \tag{3}

not (2), where

\[
 R=q_{14}(0,2)\,P\,Q,                                  \tag{4}

\[
 P=d_{05}d_{23}+d_{03}d_{25}+d_{02}d_{35},             \tag{5}

and (Q) is a nonzero sixteen-term diagonal/carrier polynomial.  The residue
has 48 monomials.  At the all-ones specialization,

\[
                         P=3,\qquad Q=4,\qquad R=12.   \tag{6}

So the contamination is not a formal possibility or a cancellation hidden
by factorization.  One additional graph vertex destroys the old pair
certificate literally.  Another certificate may still close the enlarged
chart, but that requires new source algebra; it does not follow from the
old edge unit.

## 4. Why a vertex cover is insufficient

For ideals generated by complete source fibres, adjoining a cell does not
merely enlarge an ideal in a fixed polynomial ring.  It adds matching terms
to the existing generators.  If

\[
                    1=\sum a_i f_i
\]

on a smaller chart, then on a larger chart the same lift reads

\[
                    1+\sum a_i\Delta f_i.
\]

Equations (3)--(6) are an exact instance with nonzero remainder.  Therefore
the usual monotonic inference “a unit subchart implies every superchart is
unit” is false for source support.

The right global object would be a source-labelled transition complex:
every certificate residue must either reduce by another certificate, form
an odd/valuation-holonomy unit, or strictly lower a well-founded matching
order.  The nine graph edges alone supply none of these transition rules.

## 5. The 59-direction scope guard

Of the 76 non-diagonal deformation directions, 59 are absent from the full
primary six-row functional to every order.  This does **not** prove they are
harmless.  They can still enter the alternate one-cell lifts or the nine
pair lifts, exactly as `14:02` enters (3) for the selected edge certificate.

Accordingly a theorem on the seventeen-vertex primary graph would remain
local even if every graph cycle were closed.  Globalization additionally
requires certificate stability or transition identities against those 59
directions.

## 6. Verification and scope

The standard-library checker
[`verify_n8_one_bad_segre_critical_graph_nonheredity.py`](../computations/verify_n8_one_bad_segre_critical_graph_nonheredity.py)
pins `43cac9a` and `6f251bb`, reconstructs the graph and the cycle polynomial
(1), verifies (2), then reruns the pinned pair lift inside the enlarged
source ring and verifies the factorization (3)--(6).

This is an exact source-labelled S-pair counterguard.  It is not a broader
support census, not a coefficient-feasible full top packet, and not a Krenn
counterexample.
