# Exact triangular exposure of 96 terminal off-path coordinates

## 1. Outcome

The one-spoke correction on the selected chart-26 Hamilton row extends to
all 96 non-support coordinates on off-path edges incident to terminal
endpoint 2 or 5.

There are 148 mixed hafnian rows with a support-unit linear coefficient in
one of these coordinates.  Their unit-incidence matrix is one-hot.  Exact
triangular peeling exposes 80 coordinates in four rounds of sizes

```text
18, 30, 29, 3.
```

The sixteen raw pivots that remain are exactly the non-support decorations
of edges 24 and 56.  Their unit coefficients contain an alternating-cycle
factor, but a second mixed row supplies its nonconstant part.  A two-row
Bezout subtraction exposes every one of the sixteen without any additional
localization.  Consequently all 96 coordinates belong to the mixed source
ideal after inverting the twelve chart-support coordinates.

On this 96-coordinate face, the complete symbolic clean-cap error has
6,078,888 terms in all 729 residual output words.  Every term contains one
of the exposed coordinates.  The error therefore belongs to the localized
mixed source ideal.  This is simultaneous multi-spoke membership, not a
collection of one-coordinate specializations.

The result is deliberately face-local.  Six path-edge decorations are the
first clean-error directions outside the face.  They are exposed after
quotienting by the 96-coordinate ideal, but adjoining them simultaneously
creates a quadratic alternate-matching system with no initial raw
triangular pivot.  That 102-coordinate cycle system is the next terminal
block; it is not solved here.

## 2. The rings and the 96-coordinate face

Let \(U\) be the set of twelve chart-26 support coordinates, and let \(P\)
be the seven decorated coordinates in the selected Hamilton row

```text
04237475b8cfea.
```

Its uncoloured path is

```text
2 - 3 - 6 - 4 - 0 - 1 - 7 - 5,
```

with endpoints \(p=2\) and \(q=5\).  Let \(Z\) consist of all coordinates
which

1. are not in \(U\cup P\);
2. lie on an uncoloured edge not in this Hamilton path; and
3. lie on an edge incident to 2 or 5.

There are 96 such coordinates.  This includes the eight non-support
decorations of the direct endpoint edge 25.  Work in the quotient by every
coordinate outside \(U\cup P\cup Z\), and localize at the product of the
coordinates in \(U\).  The checker then normalizes the support units to one.
The seven variables in \(P\) remain symbolic and are not inverted.

Write \(J\) for the ideal generated by all mixed hafnian coefficients in
this ring.

## 3. The support-boundary identity minor

There is a useful larger local calculation before introducing the terminal
row.  At the support boundary, retain all 240 coordinates outside \(U\).
The mixed hafnian linearization has exactly 358 nonzero rows.  Every such row
has one entry, equal to 1.  The number of available unit rows per coordinate
is

```text
number of rows       1    2    3
number of coordinates 130  102   8.
```

Selecting the lexicographically first row for every coordinate gives a
literal \(240\times240\) identity minor over \(\mathbb Z\).  In particular,
this is an exact characteristic-zero certificate, not a modular rank
observation.

If \(\mathfrak m\) is the ideal of the 240 off-support coordinates, the
selected equations have the form

\[
                         g_i=z_i+O(\mathfrak m^2).
\]

Thus \(\mathfrak m=J+\mathfrak m^2\), and Nakayama gives
\(\mathfrak m=J\) in the local ring at the support boundary.  The selected
pivot-map digest is

```text
f6ab60cf4db636bc9c7d80dc6f2863399fb8591b23fc593dc4b7c640d41c228d.
```

This local certificate explains why every individual spoke has a mixed
unit exposure.  It does not by itself give polynomial triangular division
along a nonzero terminal row.

## 4. Raw triangular exposure on the terminal row

On the 96-coordinate face there are 148 support-unit mixed rows.  Each row
has exactly one unit-linear coordinate.  The distribution of available
rows per coordinate is

```text
number of rows       1   2   3
number of coordinates 52  36   8.
```

For a candidate pivot \(z_j\), retain a row only if every non-pivot
monomial contains some candidate coordinate other than \(z_j\).  Such a row
has the form

\[
              g_j=z_j+f_j,\qquad
              f_j\in (z_1,\ldots,z_{j-1})
\]

once every dependency hyperedge meets the already exposed set.  Taking all
currently eligible coordinates simultaneously gives the four rounds

\[
                         18,quad30,quad29,quad3.
\]

This proves that the resulting eighty-coordinate ideal is contained in
\(J\).  The frozen source-labelled triangular digest is

```text
638f147df0e2b02fae2e5d86ee7da180a928ee1364b9573f51960feca9fff554.
```

No raw support-unit row can expose any of the remaining sixteen.  Each has
only one unit row, and that row has a self-contaminating term involving the
pivot and two Hamilton-row variables.  Hence 80 is maximal for this precise
raw monomial-unit triangular rule.

## 5. The sixteen-coordinate cycle block is Bezout-exact

Let

\[
 A=x_{01}^{11},\qquad D=x_{36}^{11},\qquad
 B=x_{04}^{22},\qquad C=x_{17}^{22}.
\]

Modulo the ideal of the eighty exposed coordinates, every non-support
decoration \(z=x_{24}^{ab}\), with \((a,b)\ne(1,1)\), has the two literal
mixed equations

\[
\begin{aligned}
 U_{ab}&=H_{11a1b111}=(1+AD)z,\\
 V_{ab}&=H_{00a1b111}=Dz.
\end{aligned}
\]

Therefore

\[
                         z=U_{ab}-A V_{ab}.               \tag{1}
\]

Likewise, every non-support decoration \(z=x_{56}^{ab}\), with
\((a,b)\ne(2,2)\), satisfies

\[
\begin{aligned}
 U'_{ab}&=H_{22222ab2}=(1+BC)z,\\
 V'_{ab}&=H_{12111ab2}=Cz,
\end{aligned}
\]

and hence

\[
                         z=U'_{ab}-B V'_{ab}.              \tag{2}
\]

The raw sixteen-by-sixteen unit-pivot block has diagonal factors \(1+AD\)
and \(1+BC\).  Treating those factors as an open-chart determinant would
miss the stronger fact: the coefficient ideals contain

\[
              (1+AD,D)=(1),\qquad (1+BC,C)=(1).
\]

Equations (1)--(2) are polynomial Bezout identities.  They require neither
inverting a Hamilton-row variable nor inverting the binomial cycle factors.
Together with the first eighty pivots, they prove

\[
                              (Z)\subseteq J.              \tag{3}
\]

The extra terms suppressed in (1)--(2) all contain one of the first eighty
coordinates, so the identities lift from their displayed quotient to (3).

The two binomials come from alternating \(C_4\) matching exchanges.  For
edge 24 the two complementary matchings on vertices \(0,1,3,6\) are the
support matching \((03)(16)\) and row matching \((01)(36)\).  For edge 56
the corresponding exchange on \(0,1,4,7\) compares \((07)(14)\) with
\((04)(17)\).  The second source row is exactly what resolves this first
cycle defect.

## 7. The first block outside the solved face

Among all 233 coordinates outside \(U\cup P\), exactly 45 change the
all-one clean error when added one at a time.  Thirty-nine belong to the
solved 96-coordinate face.  The other six are

\[
 x_{23}^{10},x_{23}^{11},x_{23}^{12},qquad
 x_{57}^{20},x_{57}^{21},x_{57}^{22}.                    \tag{4}
\]

After quotienting by the 96 off-path coordinates, the three variables
\(z=x_{23}^{1b}\) satisfy

\[
 H_{221b2222}=(1+BC)z,qquad H_{211b2111}=Bz,
\]

so \(z=H_{221b2222}-C H_{211b2111}\).  Similarly the three variables
\(z=x_{57}^{2b}\) satisfy a pair with coefficients \(1+AD\) and \(D\).
Thus the six-coordinate linear block is Bezout-exact in that quotient.

This quotient result does **not** close the simultaneous 102-coordinate
face.  When the path variables and off-path variables are present together,
alternate matchings create quadratic terms.  Three frozen examples are

\[
\begin{aligned}
H_{01000111}
 &=x_{02}^{00}+x_{05}^{01}x_{27}^{01},\\
H_{22102222}
 &=(1+BC)(x_{23}^{10}+x_{26}^{12}x_{35}^{02}),\\
H_{11111210}
 &=(1+AD)(x_{57}^{20}+x_{27}^{10}x_{45}^{12}).
\end{aligned}                                             \tag{5}

For all 102 candidates, the raw unit-dependency hypergraph has zero initial
strict pivots.  Equations (5) identify the precise nonlinear matching-cycle
system that must be contracted next.  The solved result of this note is the
96-coordinate off-path face, not arbitrary simultaneous terminal lifts.
