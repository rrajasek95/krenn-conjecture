# Chart 25 lifts exactly through off-carrier degree two

Let `K` be the monomial ideal generated by the 216 coordinates outside the
36-coordinate carrier of chart 25.  Averaging the one-generator chart
identity over the order-eight support stabilizer, and then adding 275 exact
mixed Macaulay orbit-columns, gives

\[
             H_0H_1H_2\in I_{\rm mix}+K^3.
\]

This is an exact rational identity in the full 252-variable source.  It is
strictly stronger than the earlier restricted-carrier result
`H_0H_1H_2 in I_mix + K`: the degree-zero factorization, the structurally
empty degree-one layer, and the complete degree-two tail now all lift.

## Exact quotient calculation

The stabilizer-averaged degree-two residual has 736 actual rows in 132 row
orbits.  Closing those rows under every mixed Macaulay column whose minimum
`K`-degree is two produces 2,264 row orbits and 3,690 column orbits.  The
eight closure increments are

\[
(272,382),(514,692),(634,1118),(426,918),
(140,344),(110,144),(36,78),(0,14).
\]

The matrix has rank 2,052 modulo 1009, and the fixed residual is already in
its image.  Deterministic Gaussian elimination over the rationals then
finds a correction on 275 column orbits.  Its coefficients are

\[
\begin{array}{c|rrrrrr}
q&1&-1&1/2&-1/2&1/4&-1/4\\ \hline
\#&34&11&70&57&52&51,
\end{array}
\]

so the correction denominator lcm is only four.  A full, unquotiented
replay reconstructs the pure target and every actual column orbit and
checks that no degree-two row remains.  No degree-zero or degree-one kernel
adjustment is needed for existence at this layer.

## Discrete-Morse signal

Order the invariant rows and columns lexicographically and call the least
row in a column its leading row.  Of the 3,690 column orbits, 2,988 lie over
a leading row shared with another column; 906 leading rows are shared, and
one is shared by 26 columns.  There are also 920 columns supported on a
single invariant row.

This is the incidence pattern expected from algebraic discrete-Morse
diamonds: differences of columns with a shared leading row remove a layer
without changing the preceding layer.  The census is evidence for that
mechanism, not yet a proof that the entire chart complex admits an acyclic
matching or that charts 25 and 26 are its only critical cells.
