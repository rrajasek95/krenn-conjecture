# The aggregate comparison is universally exact

## Theorem

The five covariance--Spencer bridge edges identify the five face vertices
modulo one aggregate `H0`.  Both constructions which meet there have the
same normalization:

- the primitive order-six face has coefficient one; and
- every physical non-Euler marked polar has coefficient one.

Consequently their facewise difference has zero aggregate `H0`.  The
positive-total-degree universal Spencer complex is contractible by the Euler
homotopy

\[
 H={\iota_E\over \deg_{\rm poly}+\deg_{\rm ext}}.
\]

Therefore the order-six/covariance/polar aggregate difference is a boundary
in the universal Hasse--Spencer complex.  There is no remaining universal
higher-face obstruction.

## Proof sketch

Let `V=Q^5` be generated by the five face endpoints.  The cyclic bridge
boundaries span

\[
              \ker(\varepsilon:V\to\mathbf Q),\qquad
              \varepsilon(x)=\sum_vx_v.               \tag{1}
\]

The rank is four and the lattice is saturated.  Thus equality of the two
augmentations is the only degree-zero condition needed to glue their five
vertices.  Their normalized augmentation vectors are both

\[
                         (1,1,1,1,1).                 \tag{2}
\]

Subtracting removes (2).  What remains has positive Spencer degree, where
the Euler identity `dH+Hd=1` gives an explicit universal homotopy.  The
commuting-ridge theorem permits the Kähler terminal face to be carried along
this homotopy without creating a new universal source term.

## What remains physical

The Euler homotopy need not descend through the quotient retaining literal
source labels, repeated grade, `W`, target, ordinary residue, anchor, eta,
and sigma.  Its failure is a class in the relative mapping cone of the
universal-to-physical comparison.  This gives the exact exhaustive
alternative:

1. the relative class is a boundary, producing the physical aggregate
   comparison cell;
2. the physical terminal detects it, in which case normalization gives the
   required relative generator; or
3. the terminal kills relative homology, hence descends and enters the
   already proved Fredholm separator alternative.

This is not a choice between proof strategies.  It is the long-exact-
sequence trichotomy for one canonical aggregate class.

## Frontier change

The comparison problem is no longer:

```text
guess five endpoint fillers and enumerate their higher Hasse faces.
```

It is:

```text
prove that the physical augmented readouts form a chain map on one
universal aggregate homotopy, or evaluate the resulting relative class.
```

The literal bridge audit shows why this step is genuinely relative: its two
first transformed pieces are independent modulo the old repeated component.
The universal theorem says those pieces are nevertheless coherently filled
before physical descent.  Both facts are needed.

## Scope

This proves universal exactness and locates every remaining obstruction in
physical relative homology.  It does not prove descent of `W`, anchor,
eta/sigma, or the marked-sector terminal, construct a physical aggregate
cell, or prove one-sided transverse landing.
