# Common-radical cap reduction and its exact quartic tail

## Scope

This note continues only branch (iii) of the fixed-pair bright quotient in
`shared-reciprocal-two-bad-bright-pairing-radical-dichotomy.md`: the two old
bright lifts span the common radical line and the complementary target class
is nonzero.  It does not address branch (i), where the target class already
lies in the target-free kernel-product image, or branch (ii), where one old
bright target projection vanishes.

The source is the complete 184-variable, 3,645-row common-provenance packet
frozen in `shared-reciprocal-two-bad-common-radical-provenance-system.md`.
That packet gives, with

\[
  L=\Phi(P),\qquad R=L(22222),
\]

the exact source identity

\[
  D_{22}R=1.
\]

Thus both `D22` and `R` are units.  We retain this localization literally;
we do not impose the stronger, unjustified simultaneous specialization
`D22=R=1`.

## Hafnian cap and tail

Adjoin `U` with `U D22=1` and put

\[
  r=U Q_tR_t
\]

in the site-square-zero algebra.  The `F22` rows say

\[
  D_{22}Pq^{[2]}+P Q_tR_tq=X_t.
\]

Polarization therefore gives the exact localized congruence

\[
 D_{22}P(q+r)^{[2]}
   =X_t+U H_0,
 \qquad
 H_0=P(Q_tR_t)^{[2]}
    =2P Q_t^{[2]}R_t^{[2]}.
\]

The checker expands this identity before localization and verifies that the
difference is generated by the literal `F22` row and `U D22-1`, coefficient by
coefficient on all 243 five-site words.  The tail has 144 nonzero word
coefficients and 2,016 sparse terms.  Its frozen digest is

`72a88c13eb8203a963fbf31f28fb428ea174582e396f7ccc04dca04828f38f5b`.

Both `Qt` and `Rt` have their only target projection at the same site.  Hence
`H0` has zero raw all-target coefficient: its two `Qt` and two `Rt` slots
cannot occupy four distinct target sites.  This does **not** show that `H0`
vanishes as a tensor or belongs to `im(Phi)`.

## Exact elimination of the direct block

For a non-target family write

\[
 F_{jk}(w)=D_{jk}L(w)+B_{jk}(w).
\]

Every direct-block scalar disappears from the literal source determinant

\[
 R F_{jk}(w)-L(w)F_{jk}(22222)
   =R B_{jk}(w)-L(w)B_{jk}(22222).
\]

There are `8*242=1936` such rows.  For the target family the source constant
changes the determinant to

\[
 R F_{22}(w)-L(w)F_{22}(22222)
   =R B_{22}(w)+L(w),
\]

giving another 242 rows.  The checker reconstructs all 2,178 determinants
from the committed equations, not from an abstract template.  Their reduced
right-hand sides have digest

`a429ec84dec1682ed345893af577ad63c1a08b8c64cbc7c2385d946dafc8770b`.

This is the smallest exact residual obtained here: after eliminating all nine
direct-block scalars, the obstruction to the Schur-cap lift is precisely the
quartic tensor `2 U P Qt^[2] Rt^[2]`.  The present artifact neither kills that
tail using the other eight full families nor supplies a common-provenance
point.  Proving `H0=0`, proving `H0 in im(Phi)`, or finding a source point with
a surviving tail is the remaining branch-(iii) gate.
