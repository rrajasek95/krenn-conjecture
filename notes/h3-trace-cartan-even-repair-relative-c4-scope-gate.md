# The even repair shares the coarse relative-C4 type, but not yet its tail

## Result

The `tau_plus` repair and the two `tau_minus` repairs require the same coarse
source signature:

```text
occurrence augmentation = 1,
physical ainc = W = target = ordinary residue = 0.
```

At that coarse level, a single protected-zero, augmentation-one relative
`C4` primitive is enough.  The known exact `M_v`/collision directions span
the full augmentation-zero hyperplane of the six pure columns, so a unit
`B_i` can be translated algebraically to

\[
                         r={B_1+B_4\over2}.             \tag{1}
\]

But current theorems do not make this translation physical in the omitted
`tau_plus` source grade.  Exhausting the local `C4` replacements shows that
none lands directly on `B1` or `B4`, and the old `tau_minus` shared cells
live in a different repeated-edge grade.  The exact unifying hypothesis is
therefore a **tail-covariant, same-grade primitive relative-C4 family**, not
the coarse anchor generator alone.

## Complete local `C4` census

For every one of the sixteen maximal 13-of-15 `tau_plus` site collapses,
take the omitted \(\rho\)-pair and enumerate every matching differing from
each omitted matching by one alternating `C4`.  Exactly four replacements
per label remain loop-free and land in the canonical six-column target.
Their target sets are always

```text
one omitted label: {B0,B3} or {B2,B5},
its rho mate:      the complementary set.
```

Across all sixteen maps the direct targets are precisely
`B0,B2,B3,B5`.  No local replacement lands on either deficient fixed column
`B1` or `B4`.

There is also a literal grade mismatch.  The possible omitted `tau_plus`
labels have repeated edges

```text
01, 04, 12, 24,
```

while the `tau_minus` shared-loop packet has repeated edge `02`.  Thus even
symmetrizing a future repeated-`02` primitive does not automatically define
a source cell on the `tau_plus` labels.

## Why the target algebra nevertheless looks unified

Let \(E=\mathbf Q^6\) be the pure-column target and let
\(\epsilon(x)=\sum_i x_i\).  The exact `M_v` and clean-collision directions
span

\[
                       \ker\epsilon,\qquad\dim=5.       \tag{2}
\]

Both a direct `C4` unit \(B_i\) and (1) have augmentation one.  Hence

\[
                         r-B_i\in\ker\epsilon.          \tag{3}
\]

The checker verifies (3) for all four direct target types.  Consequently a
physical protected-zero `C4` primitive in the actual omitted-label grade,
together with an exact same-grade realization of the correction (3), would
close the even repair.  This is the precise sense in which one uniform
relative family could close both parity gates.

Equation (3) alone is not a chain construction.  The augmentation-zero
directions must carry the same word, fine grade, repeated-edge grade, root
decoration, and Rees order as the primitive being translated.

## Why the anchor-indeterminacy alternative does not select the tail

The rootless target-normalization theorem constructs the coarse lift

\[
             x=(1,-1,0,0,0)
\]

in rows `(occurrence,ainc,W,target,ores)`.  It gives the exact dichotomy:

1. nonzero physical-anchor indeterminacy yields a primitive relative
   generator immediately;
2. zero indeterminacy does not produce the desired coarse
   \(U=(1,0,0,0,0)\).

The first case is a valid proof exit and can remove the need to finish
`tau_plus` on that branch.  It is not a construction of `iota`.  The coarse
projection forgets the six-column tail: (1) and, for example, `B0` both map
to the same `U`, and the fibre has dimension five.  Even a physical cyclic
package `A` giving coarse `U=A/5` would leave that tail undetermined.

The normal-jet `C5` theorem has the same limitation.  One primitive vertex
anchor completes each rank-four incidence block and prolongs functorially,
but that primitive family is still conditional and has no map to the six
`B_i` columns.

## Exact frontier

One theorem would unify the remaining cells:

> In every omitted collision grade, a protected-zero,
> occurrence-augmentation-one relative `C4` primitive exists, is covariant
> under the physical word/fine/repeated-edge transports, and accepts the
> same-grade augmentation-zero translations generated by exact
> `M_v`/collision cells.

Applied to an omitted `tau_plus` representative, translate its unit image by
(3) to `(B1+B4)/2`; equivariance supplies the mate.  Applied in repeated
grade `02`, it gives the fixed and paired `tau_minus` repairs.  Without this
tail law, the coarse `U` and Fredholm alternatives do not identify the cells.
