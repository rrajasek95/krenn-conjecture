# Larger projective blocks obey delete-or-Hasse, not automatic deletion

## The exact support-lowering statement

For a multiaffine source map `F` and an occupied-coordinate direction
`xi`, the literal line expansion is finite:

\[
 F(x+t\xi)=F(x)+tJ_xF(\xi)+
            \sum_{r\geq2}t^rF_{[r]}(x;\xi).          \tag{1}
\]

Consequently an anchor-safe Jacobian kernel direction gives an exact
support-deleting line only when **all** its higher divided-Hasse faces
vanish.  At a minimum occupied-support source the correct alternative is

```text
all higher faces vanish  -> choose t to kill an occupied coordinate;
first H_r is nonzero     -> a physical higher-face packet is exposed.
```

The affine spoke theorem `4f7f104` is unchanged: its variables
`q23,q24,q25` all meet site `2`, so no matching occurrence contains two of
them.  Every higher face in (1) is identically zero.  What fails is applying
that integration argument again to arbitrary coefficient factors inside a
larger same-word block.

## Minimal multiaffine counterguard

The two-equation system

\[
                         ab=1,\qquad a+b=2            \tag{2}
\]

at `(a,b)=(1,1)` is already decisive.  Both Jacobian rows are `(1,1)`, so
`xi=(1,-1)` is a nonzero occupied-coordinate tangent.  But

\[
 (1+t)(1-t)-1=-t^2,\qquad (1+t)+(1-t)-2=0.           \tag{3}
\]

Thus the tangent does not integrate to an affine source line.  Eliminating
`b=2-a` gives

\[
                         ab-1=-(a-1)^2,
\]

so the fibre ring is `Q[epsilon]/(epsilon^2)`: it has a tangent but only one
reduced point, and neither coordinate vanishes there.  This is the smallest
possible obstruction—two occupied variables, a normalized product, and a
three-term projective block (two variable occurrences plus its scalar
term).  Minimum support alone cannot remove the quadratic face.

This counterguard is deliberately an algebraic logical test, not a claim
that every such double point occurs in the complete GHZ source.  Its role is
to rule out the proposed proof step.  Physical matching structure decides
whether the first higher face exits or is fillable.

## First literal diagonal matching seed

The smallest physical larger block is a residual `C4` with three matching
occurrences.  Put

```text
q01[00]=q23[00]=q45[00]=1,

q23[11]q45[11] =  1,
q24[11]q35[11] =  1,
q25[11]q34[11] = -2.
```

All displayed cells are diagonal.  The pure-zero target has the unique
matching `01|23|45`, value one, so `01` is a literal coloop.  The complete
target scan before the remaining colour normalizations is exactly

| word | occurrences | value |
|---|---:|---:|
| `000000` | 1 | 1 |
| `000011` | 1 | 1 |
| `001100` | 1 | 1 |
| `001111` | 3 | 0 |

The last row is the larger projective block

\[
 q_{01}^{00}
 (q_{23}^{11}q_{45}^{11}+q_{24}^{11}q_{35}^{11}
                         +q_{25}^{11}q_{34}^{11})=0. \tag{4}
\]

Varying the two factors in its first occurrence as `1+t,1-t` kills the
linear face but leaves `-t^2`.  This is the literal diagonal `C4` version of
the obstruction in (3), and agrees with the `C4` branch of the complete
second-Hasse classification `1480f7d`.

The two nonzero mixed rows in the table are retained, rather than silently
discarded: they are exactly the first complete-row recurrence generated by
trying to place (4) next to the normalized coloop target.

## Complete minimal three-colour recurrence

A minimal pure-one normalization cannot use a matching containing `01`.
Adding `q01[11]` multiplies the zero residual sum in (4), so the three such
choices still have target value zero.  Each of the other twelve cross
matchings has a unique literal coefficient normalization; old cells are
never overwritten.  Pure colour two has all fifteen unit-matching choices.

Hence the smallest diagonal three-occurrence obstruction cannot survive its
first minimum-support three-colour target completion: it enters the physical
offdiagonal active-fan branch or destroys the chosen coloop.

## What remains

This gives the usable induction interface

```text
occurrence-incompatible kernel -> exact support deletion;
compatible kernel              -> first nonzero Hasse packet;
minimal three-occurrence C4     -> complete-row exit after all minimal
                                    three-colour target completions.
```

It does not yet prove the arbitrary-size induction.  Extra same-word
occurrences can supply mates for the private rows counted above.  The next
step must show that such a mate lowers occupied support or advances the same
recurrence, or construct the chart-complete `C2+/C4/P2` Spencer comparison
already isolated by the H2 work.  Minimum support and multiaffinity alone do
not supply that comparison.
